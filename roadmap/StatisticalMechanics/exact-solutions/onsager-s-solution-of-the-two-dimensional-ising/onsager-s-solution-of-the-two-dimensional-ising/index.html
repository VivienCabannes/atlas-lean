    A. Finite volume

    TheoremHigh-temperature expansion#✓fully proved

    StatMech.Onsager.main_high_temperature_expansion

    High-temperature expansion (van der Waerden 1941). On a finite graph, Z(β) = 2^|V| (cosh β)^|E| X(tanh β) with X the even-subgraph generating function.

    theorem main_high_temperature_expansion (beta : ℝ) :
    ons_Z G beta =
      (2 : ℝ) ^ Fintype.card V * (Real.cosh beta) ^ G.edgeFinset.card *
        ons_X G (Real.tanh beta)
    noncomputable def ons_Z (β : ℝ) : ℝ := isingZ G β 0

    (beta : ℝ) (hbeta : 0 ≤ beta) (hV : 0 < Fintype.card V) :

    Onsager's formula (L. Onsager, Crystal statistics. I. A two-dimensional model with an order-disorder transition, Phys. Rev. 65, 1944). For the zero-field nearest-neighbor Ising model with unit coupling on the L × L torus, the pressure log Z_L(β) / L² converges, for every β ≥ 0, to log 2 + (1 / 8π²) ∫∫_{[-π, π]²} log (cosh² 2β - sinh 2β (cos k₁ + cos k₂)).

    The limit runs over all L (the sequence is 0 for L ≤ 2, which does not affect it). Negative β (antiferromagnetic coupling) is not covered: the right-hand side is even in β, but on odd tori the corresponding limit involves frustrated configurations. Non-analyticity at β_c is not part of the statement.
