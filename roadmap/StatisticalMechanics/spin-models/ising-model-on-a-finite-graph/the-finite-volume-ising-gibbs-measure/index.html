    DefinitionIsing Hamiltonian on a finite graph#✓fully proved

    StatMech.Ising.hamiltonian

    The Ising Hamiltonian H(s) = -∑_{xy ∈ E(G)} σ_x σ_y - h ∑_x σ_x on a finite graph G with unit coupling and external field h. See also CondensedMatter.IsingModel.energy and StatMech.Ising.hamiltonian_eq_energy.

    def hamiltonian (h : ℝ) (s : ConfigSpace V) : ℝ :=
  - (∑ e ∈ G.edgeFinset, bond s e) - h * ∑ x, spin s x
