    TheoremExponent of the general linear group over a prime field#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.generalLinearGroup_exponent_eq_lcm_eq_cyclotomicProduct

    Let \(p\) be a prime and let \(m \ge 1\). The exponent of the group \(\mathrm{GL}_m(\mathbb{F}_p)\) of invertible \(m \times m\) matrices over \(\mathbb{F}_p = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\) is

    \[p^{\lceil \log_p m \rceil} \cdot \operatorname{lcm}(p - 1, p^2 - 1, \dots, p^m - 1),\]

    and this number equals \(p^{\lceil \log_p m \rceil} \prod_{k=1}^{m} \Phi_k(p)\), where \(\Phi_k\) denotes the \(k\)-th cyclotomic polynomial.

    theorem generalLinearGroup_exponent_eq_lcm_eq_cyclotomicProduct
    (p m : ℕ) (hp : p.Prime) (hm : 0 < m) :
    Monoid.exponent (Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup (Fin m) (ZMod p)) =
        p ^ Nat.clog p m * (Finset.range m).lcm (fun i => p ^ (i + 1) - 1) ∧
      ((p ^ Nat.clog p m * (Finset.range m).lcm (fun i => p ^ (i + 1) - 1) : ℕ) : ℤ) =
        (p : ℤ) ^ Nat.clog p m *
          ∏ k ∈ Finset.Icc 1 m, (Polynomial.cyclotomic k ℤ).eval (p : ℤ)

    TheoremJordan's theorem on finite linear groups#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.JordanLinearGroupsWanted.jordan_linear_groups

    For every natural number \(n\) there is a natural number \(J\) such that every finite subgroup \(G\) of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{C})\) contains an abelian subgroup \(A\) that is normal in \(G\) and has index \([G : A] \le J\).

    public theorem jordan_linear_groups
    (n : ℕ) : ∃ J : ℕ, ∀ (G : Subgroup (Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup (Fin n) Complex)),
      Finite ↥G →
        ∃ (A : Subgroup (Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup (Fin n) Complex)),
          A ≤ G ∧
          (∀ g ∈ G, ∀ a ∈ A, g * a * g⁻¹ ∈ A) ∧
          (∀ x ∈ A, ∀ y ∈ A, x * y = y * x) ∧
          A.relIndex G ≤ J
    Sources
    Sources
    • C. Jordan, J. Reine Angew. Math. 84 (1878); Lean states complex GL_n specialization GL_n(ℂ) while general theorem holds over any char-zero field with bound depending only on n.

    TheoremSelberg's lemma#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.LinearGroupFinitenessWanted.selberg_finiteIndex_torsionFree

    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero and let \(n\) be a natural number. Every finitely generated subgroup \(G\) of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(K)\) has a subgroup \(H\) of finite index in \(G\) that is torsion-free: the identity is the only element of finite order in \(H\).

    theorem selberg_finiteIndex_torsionFree
    {K : Type*} [Field K] [CharZero K] {n : ℕ}
    (G : Subgroup (Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup (Fin n) K))
    (hFG : Group.FG G) :
    ∃ H : Subgroup G, H.FiniteIndex ∧ IsMulTorsionFree H
    Sources

    DefinitionStable sets of linear automorphisms#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.StableSeriesShared.HasStableSeries

    Let \(V\) be a vector space over a field \(K\). A set \(S\) of \(K\)-linear automorphisms of \(V\) is stable when there are a natural number \(m\) and subspaces \(0 = W_0 \le W_1 \le \cdots \le W_m = V\) of \(V\) such that \(g(w) - w \in W_i\) for every \(i < m\), every \(g \in S\) and every \(w \in W_{i+1}\).

    def HasStableSeries {K V : Type} [Field K] [AddCommGroup V] [Module K V]
    (S : Set (V ≃ₗ[K] V)) : Prop :=
  ∃ (m : ℕ) (W : Fin (m + 1) → Submodule K V), W 0 = ⊥ ∧ W (Fin.last m) = ⊤ ∧
    (∀ i : Fin m, W i.castSucc ≤ W i.succ) ∧
    ∀ i : Fin m, ∀ g ∈ S, ∀ w ∈ W i.succ, g w - w ∈ W i.castSucc
