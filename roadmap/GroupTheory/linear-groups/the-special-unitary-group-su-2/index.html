    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SU2Longitude.longitudeSubgroup

    The longitude subgroup of \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) is the subgroup of those elements of \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) that are the diagonal rotation through \(\theta\) for some real number \(\theta\).

    It is the longitude \(\mathrm{Long}_i\) at the equator point \(i\).

    DefinitionLongitude map#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SU2Longitude.longitudeMap

    The longitude map is the group homomorphism from the circle group \(\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}\), written multiplicatively, to the longitude subgroup of \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) that sends the class of a real number \(\theta\) to the diagonal rotation through \(\theta\), that is, to \(\cos\theta \cdot I + \sin\theta \cdot \operatorname{diag}(i, -i) = \operatorname{diag}(e^{i\theta}, e^{-i\theta})\), where \(I\) is the \(2 \times 2\) identity matrix. It is well defined because diagonal rotations are \(2\pi\)-periodic.
