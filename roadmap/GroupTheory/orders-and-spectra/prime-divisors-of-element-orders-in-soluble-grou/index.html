    Prime divisors of element orders in soluble groups

    DefinitionLinear bound on the prime divisors of a finite soluble group (Kourovka 14.66)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SolublePrimeDivisorsLinearBoundKourovka14Problem66Wanted.solublePrimeCountLinearInElementPrimeCount

    The statement, posed by A. Moretó, that there are natural numbers \(a\) and \(b\) such that for every finite soluble group \(G\) and every natural number \(m\), if the order of every element of \(G\) has at most \(m\) distinct prime divisors, then \(|G|\) has at most \(am + b\) distinct prime divisors.

    Equivalently, \(|\pi(G)| \le a\,\nu(G) + b\) for every finite soluble group \(G\), where \(\pi(G)\) is the set of primes dividing \(|G|\) and \(\nu(G)\) is the largest number of distinct primes dividing the order of a single element of \(G\).

    def solublePrimeCountLinearInElementPrimeCount : Prop :=
  ∃ a b : ℕ, ∀ (G : Type) [Group G] [Finite G], Group.IsSolvable G → ∀ m : ℕ,
    (∀ g : G, (orderOf g).primeFactors.card ≤ m) → (Nat.card G).primeFactors.card ≤ a * m + b
