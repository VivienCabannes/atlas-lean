    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SolvableLERFBaumslagSolitarKourovka16Problem2Wanted.IsSeparableSubgroup

    A subgroup \(H\) of a group \(G\) is separable when for every element \(x \in G\) with \(x \notin H\) there are a finite group \(F\) and a group homomorphism \(f \colon G \to F\) with \(f(x) \notin f(H)\).

    def IsSeparableSubgroup {G : Type} [Group G] (H : Subgroup G) : Prop :=
  ∀ x : G, x ∉ H → ∃ (F : Type) (_ : Group F) (_ : Finite F) (f : G →* F), f x ∉ H.map f

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SolvableLERFBaumslagSolitarKourovka16Problem2Wanted.solvableLERFIffNoBaumslagSolitar_refuted

    There is a finitely generated solvable group \(G\) for which the following two conditions are not equivalent: \(G\) is locally subgroup-separable; there is no \(n > 1\) such that \(G\) has a subgroup isomorphic to the Baumslag–Solitar group \(\mathrm{BS}(1, n)\).

    Button's counterexample is the solvable ascending HNN extension of \(\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} = \langle u, v \rangle\) by the injective endomorphism \(u \mapsto u^5 v^{-1}\), \(v \mapsto u^2\): it is not locally subgroup-separable but contains no \(\mathrm{BS}(1, m)\) with \(m \ne 0, \pm 1\).

    public theorem_wanted solvableLERFIffNoBaumslagSolitar_refuted : ¬ solvableLERFIffNoBaumslagSolitar
