    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.LamplighterGroupAlgebraOreKourovka12Problem47Wanted.Lamplighter

    For a natural number \(p\), the lamplighter group \(\mathbb{Z}/p \wr \mathbb{Z}\) is the semidirect product \(\left(\bigoplus_{i \in \mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Z}/p\right) \rtimes \mathbb{Z}\), in which \(n \in \mathbb{Z}\) acts on the base group by the \(n\)-th power of the shift \(f \mapsto f(\cdot - 1)\).

    abbrev Lamplighter (p : ℕ) : Type :=
  Multiplicative (ℤ →₀ ZMod p) ⋊[zpowersHom (MulAut (Multiplicative (ℤ →₀ ZMod p))) (shift p)]

    DefinitionNon-zero-divisor#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.LamplighterGroupAlgebraOreKourovka12Problem47Wanted.IsNonZeroDivisor

    An element \(s\) of a ring \(R\) is a non-zero-divisor when it is neither a left nor a right zero divisor: for every \(z \in R\), \(zs = 0\) implies \(z = 0\) and \(sz = 0\) implies \(z = 0\).

    def IsNonZeroDivisor {R : Type*} [Ring R] (s : R) : Prop :=
  (∀ z : R, z * s = 0 → z = 0) ∧ ∀ z : R, s * z = 0 → z = 0

    DefinitionRight Ore ring#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.LamplighterGroupAlgebraOreKourovka12Problem47Wanted.IsOreRing

    A ring \(R\) satisfies the right Ore condition for its non-zero-divisors, so that it has a classical right ring of quotients, when for all \(s, t \in R\) with \(s\) a non-zero-divisor there are \(x, y \in R\) with \(x\) a non-zero-divisor and \(sy = tx\).

    For group algebras the left and right conditions agree.

    def IsOreRing (R : Type*) [Ring R] : Prop :=
  ∀ s t : R, IsNonZeroDivisor s → ∃ x y : R, IsNonZeroDivisor x ∧ s * y = t * x
    def IsNonZeroDivisor {R : Type*} [Ring R] (s : R) : Prop :=
  (∀ z : R, z * s = 0 → z = 0) ∧ ∀ z : R, s * z = 0 → z = 0

    DefinitionKourovka Problem 12.47: some lamplighter group algebra is Ore#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.LamplighterGroupAlgebraOreKourovka12Problem47Wanted.lamplighterGroupAlgebraOre

    There are a field \(k\) and a prime \(p\) such that the group algebra \(k[\mathbb{Z}/p \wr \mathbb{Z}]\) of the lamplighter group \(\mathbb{Z}/p \wr \mathbb{Z}\) satisfies the right Ore condition for its non-zero-divisors, that is, has a classical right ring of quotients.

    Kourovka 12.47 (P. A. Linnell) asks this for a given field and prime; the existential form makes its refutation cover every field and every prime.

    def lamplighterGroupAlgebraOre : Prop :=
  ∃ (k : Type) (_ : Field k) (p : ℕ), p.Prime ∧ IsOreRing (MonoidAlgebra k (Lamplighter p))
    def IsOreRing (R : Type*) [Ring R] : Prop :=
  ∀ s t : R, IsNonZeroDivisor s → ∃ x y : R, IsNonZeroDivisor x ∧ s * y = t * x

    TheoremLamplighter group algebras are not Ore#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.LamplighterGroupAlgebraOreKourovka12Problem47Wanted.lamplighterGroupAlgebraOre_refuted

    For every field \(k\) and every prime \(p\), the group algebra \(k[\mathbb{Z}/p \wr \mathbb{Z}]\) of the lamplighter group \(\mathbb{Z}/p \wr \mathbb{Z}\) does not satisfy the right Ore condition for its non-zero-divisors: there are \(s\) and \(t\) in it, with \(s\) a non-zero-divisor, such that \(sy = tx\) holds for no \(x\) and \(y\) with \(x\) a non-zero-divisor.

    Resolved false: Linnell, Lück and Schick (Theorem 3, with W. Dicks's argument) prove that \(kG\) is not an Ore ring for every field \(k\) and every group \(G\) containing \(H \wr \mathbb{Z}\) with \(H \ne 1\) finite, in particular for \(G = \mathbb{Z}/p \wr \mathbb{Z}\) and every prime \(p\); the Kourovka Archive answers 12.47 "No, it does not".
