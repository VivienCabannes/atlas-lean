    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.DPiHallIntersectionKourovka18Problem31Wanted.piCore

    Let \(\pi\) be a set of natural numbers. For a group \(G\), the \(\pi\)-core \(O_\pi(G)\) is the subgroup of \(G\) generated by all normal \(\pi\)-subgroups of \(G\).

    DefinitionThree Hall π-subgroups meeting in the π-core (Kourovka 18.31)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.DPiHallIntersectionKourovka18Problem31Wanted.threeHallSubgroupsIntersectInCore

    For every set \(\pi\) of natural numbers and every finite \(D_\pi\)-group \(G\), there are Hall \(\pi\)-subgroups \(H_1\), \(H_2\), \(H_3\) of \(G\), not necessarily distinct, with \(H_1 \cap H_2 \cap H_3 = O_\pi(G)\), the \(\pi\)-core of \(G\).

    This is the question of E. P. Vdovin and D. O. Revin; only the primes in \(\pi\) matter.

    def threeHallSubgroupsIntersectInCore : Prop :=

      IsHallPiSubgroup π H₃ ∧ H₁ ⊓ H₂ ⊓ H₃ = piCore π G
