    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.FiniteStateConjugacyProblemKourovka16Problem34Wanted.IsTreeAut

    For \(d \in \mathbb{N}\), the vertices of the \(d\)-regular rooted tree are the finite words over the alphabet \(\{0, \dots, d-1\}\). A permutation \(f\) of this set of words is a tree automorphism when, for all words \(u\) and \(v\), writing \(|u|\) for the length of \(u\) and \(uv\) for the concatenation of \(u\) and \(v\), the word \(f(u)\) has length \(|u|\) and the first \(|u|\) letters of \(f(uv)\) form \(f(u)\).

    def IsTreeAut {d : ℕ} (f : Equiv.Perm (List (Fin d))) : Prop :=
  ∀ u v : List (Fin d), (f u).length = u.length ∧ (f (u ++ v)).take u.length = f u

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.FiniteStateConjugacyProblemKourovka16Problem34Wanted.finiteStateConjugacyDecidable

    The proposition that every finitely generated group of finite-state automorphisms of a regular rooted tree has decidable conjugacy problem: for all \(d, k \in \mathbb{N}\) and all finite-state automorphisms \(g_0, \dots, g_{k-1}\) of the \(d\)-regular rooted tree, some algorithm decides, given two words in the \(g_i\) and their inverses, whether the elements they spell are conjugate in the group generated by \(g_0, \dots, g_{k-1}\).

    DefinitionAutomorphisms of the regular rooted tree#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.FiniteStateIrreducibleGeneratorsKourovka16Problem86Wanted.IsTreeAutomorphism

    For a natural number \(d\), the \(d\)-regular rooted tree has as vertices the finite words over the alphabet \(\{0, \dots, d-1\}\); write \(vw\) for the concatenation of words \(v\) and \(w\). The root is the empty word and the children of a word \(v\) are the words \(vi\) for letters \(i\). A permutation \(g\) of the words is a tree automorphism when for all words \(v\) and \(w\) there is a word \(u\) of the same length as \(w\) with \(g(vw) = g(v)u\).

    For a permutation of the words this says that \(g\) preserves lengths and the prefix order, so it is an automorphism of the tree as a graph.

    def IsTreeAutomorphism {d : ℕ} (g : Equiv.Perm (List (Fin d))) : Prop :=
  ∀ v w : List (Fin d), ∃ u : List (Fin d), u.length = w.length ∧ g (v ++ w) = g v ++ u

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.FiniteStateIrreducibleGeneratorsKourovka16Problem86Wanted.finiteStateAutomorphisms

    For a natural number \(d\), consider the \(d\)-regular rooted tree whose vertices are the finite words over the alphabet \(\{0, \dots, d-1\}\). The group of finite-state automorphisms of this tree is the subgroup of the group of all permutations of the words generated by the tree automorphisms that have only finitely many distinct sections.

    These automorphisms are closed under products and inverses, so the subgroup consists of exactly them.
