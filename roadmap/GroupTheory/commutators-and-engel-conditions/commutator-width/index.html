    DefinitionProducts of at most \(n\) commutators (Kourovka 14.15)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.AutFreeGroupCommutatorLengthKourovka14Problem15Wanted.IsProductOfCommutators

    Let \(G\) be a group and \(n\) a natural number. An element \(z \in G\) is a product of at most \(n\) commutators when there are \(m \le n\) and elements \(x_1, y_1, \dots, x_m, y_m \in G\) with \(z = [x_1, y_1] \cdots [x_m, y_m]\), where \([x, y] = xyx^{-1}y^{-1}\); for \(m = 0\) the empty product is the identity.

    def IsProductOfCommutators {G : Type*} [Group G] (n : ℕ) (z : G) : Prop :=
  ∃ l : List (G × G), l.length ≤ n ∧ (l.map fun p => p.1 * p.2 * p.1⁻¹ * p.2⁻¹).prod = z

    TheoremCommutator length in Aut(Fₙ) is unbounded#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.AutFreeGroupCommutatorLengthKourovka14Problem15Wanted.autFreeGroupCommutatorLengthUnbounded_holds

    Let \(n \ge 3\) and let \(F_n\) be the free group on \(n\) generators. For every natural number \(k\) there is an element of the derived subgroup of \(\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\) that is not a product of at most \(k\) commutators \(xyx^{-1}y^{-1}\) with \(x, y \in \operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\). Equivalently, the supremum \(k_n\) of the commutator lengths of the elements of the derived subgroup of \(\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\) is infinite.

    This answers Kourovka Problem 14.15 (V. G. Bardakov): \(\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)\) is acylindrically hyperbolic (Genevois–Horbez), and acylindrically hyperbolic groups have infinite commutator width (Bestvina–Bromberg–Fujiwara).

    DefinitionCommutator width of the derived subgroup#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.CommutatorWidthRatioKourovka16Problem8Wanted.commutatorWidth

    For a group \(G\) with derived subgroup \(G'\), the commutator width \(w(G')\) is the least positive integer \(m\) such that every element of \(G'\) is a product of at most \(m\) commutators \(aba^{-1}b^{-1}\) with \(a, b \in G\); it is \(0\) when no such \(m\) exists.

    noncomputable def commutatorWidth (G : Type*) [Group G] : ℕ :=
  sInf {m : ℕ | 0 < m ∧ ∀ g ∈ commutator G, IsProductOfCommutators m g}

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.CommutatorWidthRatioKourovka16Problem8Wanted.maxCommutatorWidthRatioIsOneSixth

    The proposition that for every finite non-abelian group \(G\) the commutator width \(w(G')\) of its derived subgroup \(G'\) satisfies \(w(G')/|G| \le 1/6\), and that for the symmetric group \(S_3\) equality holds: \(w(S_3')/|S_3| = 1/6\).
