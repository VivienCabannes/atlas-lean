    A group \(G\) has spread at least \(k\), for a natural number \(k\), when for every family \(x_1, \dots, x_k\) of nontrivial elements of \(G\), not necessarily distinct, there is an element \(y \in G\) such that the subgroup \(\langle x_i, y \rangle\) generated by \(x_i\) and \(y\) is all of \(G\) for every \(i\).

    def HasSpreadAtLeast (G : Type u) [Group G] (k : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∀ x : Fin k → G, (∀ i, x i ≠ 1) →
    ∃ y : G, ∀ i, Subgroup.closure {x i, y} = ⊤

    TheoremThere is a group of spread exactly one#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.Kourovka21Problem38Wanted.conjecture_holds

    There exists a group \(G\) of spread exactly \(1\): for every nontrivial \(x \in G\) there is \(y \in G\) with \(\langle x, y \rangle = G\), but for every natural number \(m > 1\) there are nontrivial elements \(x_1, \dots, x_m \in G\) such that no single \(y \in G\) satisfies \(\langle x_i, y \rangle = G\) for all \(i\). This answers Kourovka Problem 21.38 affirmatively.

    The Nilradical note A group of spread one (17 September 2026, Kourovka Notebook site), Theorem 1, shows that the subgroup \(\{f \in F : f'(0^+) = f'(1^-)\}\) of Thompson's group \(F\) is infinite and has spread exactly one. The note is not refereed; the Kourovka Notebook (arXiv:1401.0300v49) marks Problem 21.38 solved (AI solution confirmed by S. Corson).

    public theorem_wanted conjecture_holds : conjecture
    def conjecture : Prop :=
  ∃ (G : Type u) (_ : Group G), HasSpreadExactly G 1

    DefinitionSpread of a group#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SpreadOfAFiniteGroupWanted.SpreadOfAFiniteGroup.s

    Let \(G\) be a group. The spread \(s(G)\) of \(G\) is the supremum in \(\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\) of the natural numbers \(k\) such that for any non-trivial elements \(x_1, \dots, x_k\) of \(G\) (not necessarily distinct) there exists \(y \in G\) such that the subgroup \(\langle x_i, y \rangle\) generated by \(x_i\) and \(y\) is all of \(G\) for every \(i\). In particular \(s(G) = \infty\) when there is no largest such \(k\).

    noncomputable def s (G : Type*) [Group G] : ℕ∞ :=
  sSup ((↑) '' {k : ℕ | ∀ x : Fin k → G, 1 ∉ Set.range x → ∃ y, ∀ i, Subgroup.closure {x i, y} = ⊤})

    TheoremBurness–Guralnick–Harper spread theorem#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.SpreadOfAFiniteGroupWanted.SpreadOfAFiniteGroup.theorem_1

    Let \(G\) be a finite group. Then the spread \(s(G)\) is at least \(2\) if and only if every proper quotient of \(G\) is cyclic, that is, \(G/N\) is cyclic for every non-trivial normal subgroup \(N\) of \(G\).

    public theorem_wanted theorem_1 (G : Type*) [Group G] [Finite G] :
    s G ≥ 2 ↔ ∀ (N : Subgroup G) [N.Normal] [Nontrivial N], IsCyclic (G ⧸ N)
