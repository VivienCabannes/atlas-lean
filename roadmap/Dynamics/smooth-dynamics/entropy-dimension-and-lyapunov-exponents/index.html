    DefinitionHausdorff dimension of a measure#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.dimMeasure

    For a measure \(\mu\) on an extended metric space \(M\) equipped with a σ-algebra, the Hausdorff dimension of \(\mu\) is the infimum, in \([0, \infty]\), of the Hausdorff dimensions of the measurable sets \(s \subseteq M\) with \(\mu(M \setminus s) = 0\).

    noncomputable def dimMeasure {M : Type*} [EMetricSpace M] [MeasurableSpace M]
    (μ : Measure M) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  sInf {d : ℝ≥0∞ |
    ∃ s : Set M, MeasurableSet s ∧ μ sᶜ = 0 ∧ dimH s = d}

    StructureMeasurable partition modulo null sets#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.IsMeasurablePartition

    Let \(\mu\) be a measure on a measurable space \(M\). A finite family \(P\) of subsets of \(M\) is a measurable partition of \(M\) modulo \(\mu\)-null sets when every member of \(P\) is measurable, the complement of the union of the members of \(P\) has \(\mu\)-measure \(0\), and any two distinct members \(A \ne B\) of \(P\) satisfy \(\mu(A \cap B) = 0\).

    structure IsMeasurablePartition {M : Type*} [MeasurableSpace M]
    (μ : Measure M) (P : Finset (Set M)) : Prop where
  measurable : ∀ A ∈ P, MeasurableSet A
  cover : μ (⋃ A ∈ P, A)ᶜ = 0
  disjoint : ∀ A ∈ P, ∀ B ∈ P, A ≠ B → μ (A ∩ B) = 0

    DefinitionShannon entropy of a finite family of sets#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.partitionEntropy

    For a measure \(\mu\) on a measurable space \(M\) and a finite collection \(P\) of subsets of \(M\), the entropy of \(P\) is \(H_\mu(P) = \sum_{A \in P} -\mu(A) \log \mu(A)\), where \(\log\) is the natural logarithm, with the convention \(0 \log 0 = 0\). Here \(\mu(A)\) is taken as a real number, so a set of infinite measure contributes \(0\).

    It is applied to finite measurable partitions \(P\).

    noncomputable def partitionEntropy {M : Type*} [MeasurableSpace M]
    (μ : Measure M) (P : Finset (Set M)) : ℝ :=
  ∑ A ∈ P, Real.negMulLog (μ A).toReal

    DefinitionIterated join of a partition#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.iteratedJoin

    For a map \(T : M \to M\), a finite family \(P\) of subsets of \(M\) and \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), the iterated join \(\bigvee_{k=0}^{n-1} T^{-k} P\) is the finite family of all sets \(\bigcap_{k=0}^{n-1} T^{-k}(A_k)\) with \(A_0, \dots, A_{n-1} \in P\), where \(T^{-k}(A)\) is the preimage of \(A\) under the \(k\)-th iterate \(T^k\). For \(n = 0\) it is \(\{M\}\).

    noncomputable def iteratedJoin {M : Type*} (T : M → M)
    (P : Finset (Set M)) (n : ℕ) : Finset (Set M) :=
  (Fintype.piFinset (fun _ : Fin n => P)).image
    (fun f : Fin n → Set M => ⋂ k : Fin n, T^[(k : ℕ)] ⁻¹' f k)

    DefinitionEntropy of a map relative to a partition#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.entropyW

    Let \(\mu\) be a measure on a measurable space \(M\), let \(T : M \to M\) be a map and let \(P\) be a finite family of subsets of \(M\). The entropy of \(T\) relative to \(P\) is

    \[h_\mu(T, P) = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} H_\mu\Big(\bigvee_{k=0}^{n-1} T^{-k} P\Big),\]

    where \(\bigvee_{k=0}^{n-1} T^{-k} P\) is the iterated join of \(P\) under \(T\); the Shannon entropy of a finite family \(Q\) of sets is \(H_\mu(Q) = \sum_{A \in Q} -\mu(A) \log \mu(A)\), with \(0 \log 0 = 0\) and with \(\mu(A)\) replaced by \(0\) when \(\mu(A) = \infty\); and the limit superior is taken to be \(0\) when it is \(+\infty\) or \(-\infty\).

    noncomputable def entropyW {M : Type*} [MeasurableSpace M]
    (μ : Measure M) (T : M → M) (P : Finset (Set M)) : ℝ :=
  Filter.limsup
    (fun n : ℕ => partitionEntropy μ (iteratedJoin T P n) / n) atTop
    noncomputable def partitionEntropy {M : Type*} [MeasurableSpace M]
    (μ : Measure M) (P : Finset (Set M)) : ℝ :=
  ∑ A ∈ P, Real.negMulLog (μ A).toReal

    DefinitionKolmogorov–Sinai entropy#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.kolmogorovSinaiEntropy

    Let \(\mu\) be a measure on a measurable space \(M\) and let \(T : M \to M\) be a map. The Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy \(h_\mu(T)\) is the supremum of the entropies \(h_\mu(T, P)\) of \(T\) relative to \(P\) over all finite measurable partitions \(P\) of \(M\) modulo \(\mu\)-null sets, taken to be \(0\) when these entropies are not bounded above.

    noncomputable def kolmogorovSinaiEntropy {M : Type*} [MeasurableSpace M]
    (μ : Measure M) (T : M → M) : ℝ :=
  sSup {h | ∃ P : Finset (Set M), IsMeasurablePartition μ P ∧ entropyW μ T P = h}

    DefinitionUpper Lyapunov exponent#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.lyapunovUpperAt

    For a map \(T : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2\) of the Euclidean plane and a point \(x \in \mathbb{R}^2\), the upper Lyapunov exponent of \(T\) at \(x\) is

    \[\overline{\lambda}(T, x) = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \log \|D(T^n)_x\|,\]

    where \(D(T^n)_x\) is the derivative at \(x\) of the \(n\)-th iterate \(T^n\), taken to be \(0\) when \(T^n\) is not differentiable at \(x\); \(\|\cdot\|\) is the operator norm; \(\log 0 = 0\); and the limit superior is taken to be \(0\) when it is \(+\infty\) or \(-\infty\).

    noncomputable def lyapunovUpperAt
    (T : EucPlane → EucPlane) (x : EucPlane) : ℝ :=
  Filter.limsup
    (fun n : ℕ => Real.log ‖fderiv ℝ (T^[n]) x‖ / n) atTop

    For a map \(T : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2\) of the Euclidean plane and a point \(x \in \mathbb{R}^2\), the lower Lyapunov exponent of \(T\) at \(x\) is

    \[\underline{\lambda}(T, x) = -\limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \log \|(D(T^n)_x)^{-1}\|,\]

    DefinitionHarmonic mean of Lyapunov exponents#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.harmonicMeanLyapunov

    For real numbers \(\lambda_1\) and \(\lambda_2\), this is the harmonic mean \(\frac{2 \lambda_1 (-\lambda_2)}{\lambda_1 - \lambda_2}\) of \(\lambda_1\) and \(-\lambda_2\), taken to be \(0\) when \(\lambda_1 = \lambda_2\).

    noncomputable def harmonicMeanLyapunov (lam1 lam2 : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  2 * lam1 * (-lam2) / (lam1 - lam2)

    TheoremYoung's entropy–dimension–Lyapunov formula (planar case)#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.entropy_dimension_lyapunov

    Let \(T\) be a \(C^2\) diffeomorphism of the Euclidean plane \(\mathbb{R}^2\): \(T\) has a two-sided inverse \(T^{-1}\) and both \(T\) and \(T^{-1}\) are \(C^2\). Let \(K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2\) be a compact set with \(T(K) = K\), and let \(\mu\) be a Borel probability measure on \(\mathbb{R}^2\) with \(\mu(\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus K) = 0\) that is \(T\)-invariant and ergodic for \(T\). Let \(\lambda_1\) and \(\lambda_2\) be the \(\mu\)-integrals of the upper and the lower Lyapunov exponent of \(T\). Then the Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy of \(T\) with respect to \(\mu\) is

    \[h_\mu(T) = \frac{\dim(\mu) \cdot H}{2},\]

    where \(\dim(\mu)\) is the Hausdorff dimension of \(\mu\) and \(H = \frac{2 \lambda_1 (-\lambda_2)}{\lambda_1 - \lambda_2}\) is the harmonic mean of \(\lambda_1\) and \(-\lambda_2\), taken to be \(0\) when \(\lambda_1 = \lambda_2\).

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.LaiSangYoungWanted.Dynamics.pesin_formula

    Let \(T\) be a \(C^2\) diffeomorphism of the Euclidean plane \(\mathbb{R}^2\), that is, a \(C^2\) map with a \(C^2\) two-sided inverse. Let \(K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2\) be a compact set with \(T(K) = K\), and let \(\mu\) be an ergodic \(T\)-invariant Borel probability measure on \(\mathbb{R}^2\) with \(\mu(\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus K) = 0\). Suppose that the Hausdorff dimension of \(\mu\) equals \(2\) and that \(\int \lambda_1 \, d\mu = -\int \lambda_2 \, d\mu\). Then \(h_\mu(T) = \int \lambda_1 \, d\mu\).
