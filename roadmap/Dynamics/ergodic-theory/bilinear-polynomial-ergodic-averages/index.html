    MathlibExt.Dynamics.PointwiseErgodicTheoremsWanted.PointwiseErgodicTheorems.Lacunary

    For a real number \(\Lambda \ge 0\), a sequence \((a_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\) of nonnegative real numbers is \(\Lambda\)-lacunary if \(a_{n+1} / a_n > \Lambda\) for every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), where division by \(0\) gives \(0\); in particular every term of a \(\Lambda\)-lacunary sequence is positive.

    def Lacunary (Λ : ℝ≥0) (a : ℕ → ℝ≥0) : Prop := ∀ n, a (n + 1) / a n > Λ

    DefinitionVariational norm#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.PointwiseErgodicTheoremsWanted.PointwiseErgodicTheorems.variationalNorm

    For a real number \(r \ge 0\) and a sequence \(a = (a_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\) of complex numbers, the \(r\)-variational norm of \(a\) is the element of \([0, \infty]\)

    \[\|a\|_{\mathbb{V}^r} = \sup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} |a_n| + \sup_{J \in \mathbb{N}} \sup_{N_0 \le N_1 \le \dots \le N_J} \Big( \sum_{j=0}^{J-1} |a_{N_{j+1}} - a_{N_j}|^r \Big)^{1/r},\]

    where the inner supremum runs over nondecreasing finite sequences of natural numbers \(N_0 \le N_1 \le \dots \le N_J\) and the empty sum (\(J = 0\)) is \(0\). For \(r = 0\) the whole term \(\big( \sum_{j=0}^{J-1} |a_{N_{j+1}} - a_{N_j}|^r \big)^{1/r}\) is replaced by the number of indices \(j < J\) with \(a_{N_{j+1}} \ne a_{N_j}\).

    noncomputable def variationalNorm (r : ℝ≥0) (a : ℕ → ℂ) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  (⨆ n, ‖a n‖ₑ) + ⨆ (J : ℕ) (N : ℕ → ℕ) (_ : Monotone N),
    ENNReal.ofReal ‖(WithLp.toLp r (fun (i : Fin J) ↦ a (N (i + 1)) - a (N i)))‖

    DefinitionBilinear polynomial ergodic averages#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Dynamics.PointwiseErgodicTheoremsWanted.PointwiseErgodicTheorems.A

    Let \(X\) be a measurable space and let \(T : X \to X\) be a measurable bijection with measurable inverse. For a real number \(N \ge 0\), polynomials \(P_1, P_2 \in \mathbb{Z}[t]\), functions \(f, g : X \to \mathbb{C}\) and a point \(x \in X\), the bilinear average is the complex number

    \[A_N^{P_1, P_2}(f, g)(x) = \frac{1}{\lfloor N \rfloor} \sum_{n=1}^{\lfloor N \rfloor} f(T^{P_1(n)} x)\, g(T^{P_2(n)} x),\]

    where \(T^k\) for \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\) is the \(k\)-th power of \(T\) in the group of bijections of \(X\) (so negative powers are iterates of \(T^{-1}\)), and \(A_N^{P_1, P_2}(f, g)(x) = 0\) when \(\lfloor N \rfloor = 0\).

    noncomputable def A (T : 𝓧 ≃ᵐ 𝓧) (N : ℝ≥0) (P₁ : ℤ[X]) (P₂ : ℤ[X]) (f g : 𝓧 → ℂ) (x : 𝓧) : ℂ :=
  (Nat.floor N : ℝ)⁻¹ * ∑ n ∈ Icc 1 (Nat.floor N),
    f ((T ^ (P₁.eval (n : ℤ)) : 𝓧 ≃ 𝓧) x) * g ((T ^ (P₂.eval (n : ℤ)) : 𝓧 ≃ 𝓧) x)

    Let \((X, \mu)\) be a \(\sigma\)-finite measure space and let \(T : X \to X\) be a measurable bijection with measurable inverse that preserves \(\mu\). Let \(P \in \mathbb{Z}[t]\) have degree at least \(2\), and let \(p_1, p_2 \in (1, \infty)\) and \(p \in [0, \infty]\) satisfy \(p_1^{-1} + p_2^{-1} = p^{-1}\) and \(p^{-1} \le 1\). For a real number \(N \ge 0\), functions \(f, g : X \to \mathbb{C}\) and \(x \in X\) let

    \[A_N(f, g)(x) = \frac{1}{\lfloor N \rfloor} \sum_{n=1}^{\lfloor N \rfloor} f(T^n x)\, g(T^{P(n)} x),\]

    with \(A_N(f, g)(x) = 0\) when \(\lfloor N \rfloor = 0\), where \(T^k\) for \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\) is the \(k\)-th power of \(T\) in the group of bijections of \(X\). Then for all \(f \in L^{p_1}(\mu)\) and \(g \in L^{p_2}(\mu)\) with values in \(\mathbb{C}\) there is a function \(h : X \to \mathbb{C}\) in \(L^p(\mu)\) such that \(\|A_N(f, g) - h\|_{L^p(\mu)} \to 0\) as the real number \(N\) tends to \(\infty\).

    This is Theorem 1.17(i) of Krause, Mirek and Tao.
