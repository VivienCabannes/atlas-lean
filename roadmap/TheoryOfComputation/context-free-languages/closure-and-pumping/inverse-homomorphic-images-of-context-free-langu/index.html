    Cslib.Language.insSymbol

    Let \(g\) be a context-free grammar over \(T'\) and let \(T\) be an alphabet. Each symbol of \(g\) is translated to a string of symbols with terminals in \(T \sqcup T'\) and nonterminals those of \(g\) together with two new nonterminals \(S\) and \(D\): a terminal \(b \in T'\) becomes the marker \(D\) followed by \(b\), and a nonterminal \(A\) of \(g\) becomes \(A\).

    def insSymbol : Symbol T' g.NT → List (Symbol (T ⊕ T') (Option (Option g.NT)))
  | .terminal b => [.nonterminal none, .terminal (.inr b)]
  | .nonterminal A => [.nonterminal (some (some A))]

    AbbreviationMarker-insertion grammar#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.insGrammar

    Let \(g\) be a context-free grammar over \(T'\) with start symbol \(A_0\), and let \(T\) be a finite alphabet. The marker-insertion grammar of \(g\) is the context-free grammar over \(T \sqcup T'\) whose nonterminals are those of \(g\) together with a new start symbol \(S\) and a marker \(D\), and whose rules are: for each rule \(A \to \alpha\) of \(g\), the rule \(A \to \alpha'\), where \(\alpha'\) is \(\alpha\) with \(D\) inserted in front of each terminal; the rule \(S \to A_0 D\); the rule \(D \to \varepsilon\); and the rule \(D \to a D\) for each \(a \in T\).

    noncomputable abbrev insGrammar : ContextFreeGrammar (T ⊕ T') where
  NT := Option (Option g.NT)
  initial := some none
  rules :=
    g.rules.image (fun r => ⟨some (some r.input), r.output.flatMap (insSymbol (T := T) g)⟩) ∪
      ({⟨some none, [.nonterminal (some (some g.initial)), .nonterminal none]⟩, ⟨none, []⟩} ∪
        Finset.univ.image fun a : T => ⟨none, [.terminal (.inl a), .nonterminal none]⟩)

    DefinitionValuation for the marker-insertion grammar#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.insValuation

    Let \(g\) be a context-free grammar over \(T'\) and let \(T\) be an alphabet. This valuation assigns a language over \(T \sqcup T'\) to each nonterminal of \(g\) and to the two new nonterminals \(S\) and \(D\) of the marker-insertion grammar of \(g\): to the marker \(D\) the words with no letter from \(T'\); to the new start symbol \(S\) the words whose letters from \(T'\) form a word of the language of \(g\); and to a nonterminal \(A\) of \(g\) the words whose letters from \(T'\) form a word that \(g\) derives from \(A\).

    def insValuation (g : ContextFreeGrammar T') : Option (Option g.NT) → Language (T ⊕ T')
  | none => {u | projRight u = []}
  | some none => {u | projRight u ∈ g.language}
  | some (some A) => {u | projRight u ∈ languageFrom g A}
