    TheoremContext-free languages are closed under homomorphic images#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.IsContextFree.homImage

    Let \(T\) and \(T'\) be alphabets, \(L\) a context-free language over \(T\), and \(\varphi\) a map sending each letter of \(T\) to a word over \(T'\). Then the language of all words \(\varphi(a_1) \cdots \varphi(a_n)\) with \(a_1 \cdots a_n \in L\) is context-free.

    DefinitionSubstituting the symbols of a grammar#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.Substitution.substSymbol

    Let \(g\) be a context-free grammar over \(T\) with nonterminals \(N_g\), and let \(e\) assign to each letter \(a \in T\) an encoded grammar \(e(a)\) over \(T'\). Each symbol of \(g\) becomes a nonterminal in \(N_g \sqcup (\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N})\): a nonterminal \(B\) of \(g\) becomes \(B\) in the left part, and a terminal \(a\) becomes the pair \((k, n)\), where \(k\) is the number of \(a\) (the position, counted from \(0\), of the first occurrence of \(a\) in the list of terminals occurring in the productions of \(g\), or the length of that list when \(a\) does not occur in it) and \(n\) is the start nonterminal of \(e(a)\).

    def substSymbol : Symbol T g.NT → Symbol T' (g.NT ⊕ (ℕ × ℕ))
  | .terminal a => .nonterminal (.inr (code g a, (e a).start))
  | .nonterminal B => .nonterminal (.inl B)

    Cslib.Language.Substitution.val

    Let \(g\) be a context-free grammar over \(T\) with nonterminals \(N_g\), let \(e\) assign to each letter \(a \in T\) an encoded grammar \(e(a)\) over \(T'\), and let \(s\) assign to each letter \(a \in T\) a language \(s(a)\) over \(T'\). Each element of \(N_g \sqcup (\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N})\), the nonterminals of the substitution grammar, is assigned a language over \(T'\). A nonterminal \(B \in N_g\) is assigned the substitution of \(s\) into the language generated by \(g\) from \(B\). A pair \((k, n)\) is assigned, when \(k\) is less than the length of the list of terminals occurring in the productions of \(g\) and \(a\) is the entry at position \(k\) of that list (counted from \(0\)), the language generated from the nonterminal \(n\) by the grammar that \(e(a)\) denotes, and the empty language otherwise.

    def val : g.NT ⊕ (ℕ × ℕ) → Language T'

  | .inr (k, n) =>
      match decode g k with
      | some a => languageFrom (e a).toCFG n
      | none => 0
