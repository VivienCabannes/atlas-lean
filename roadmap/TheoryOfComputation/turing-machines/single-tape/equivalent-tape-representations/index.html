    DefinitionCell view of a two-way tape#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Turing.BiTape.toTape

    A CSLib tape \(t\) over an alphabet \(\Gamma\) has a head cell and two halves, each a finite list of cells not ending in a blank, where a cell is a symbol of \(\Gamma\) or the blank. Its cell view is the Mathlib tape over cells, with the blank as default symbol, whose head is the head cell of \(t\) and whose left and right halves are the halves of \(t\), blank past their ends. So the cell at position \(i \in \mathbb{Z}\) of the view is the cell \(i\) places to the right of the head of \(t\) (to the left when \(i < 0\)).
