    DefinitionThe clocked universal program#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Turing.Universal.Clocked.prog

    The program of the clocked universal machine gives an instruction for every label. Numbers sit on stacks as counters of units (the bit true), and a cell is a counter followed by a marker (the bit false).

    Tape update. \(\mathsf{act0}\) empties \(Q\) and \(\mathsf{act1}\) moves the counter \(N\) onto \(Q\). \(\mathsf{act2}\) pops \(A\) and goes to \(\mathsf{mvL0}\) if \(A\) was empty, else to \(\mathsf{act3}\), which pops \(A\) and goes to \(\mathsf{mvR0}\) if \(A\) was empty, else to \(\mathsf{wr0}\); so the action \(0\) moves left, \(1\) moves right and \(a + 2\) writes \(a\). \(\mathsf{wr0}\) empties \(H\) and \(\mathsf{wr1}\) moves the counter \(A\) onto \(H\). A left move (\(\mathsf{mvL0}\) to \(\mathsf{mvL3}\)) pushes a marker on \(R\), moves the units of \(H\) onto \(R\), moves the units on top of \(L\) onto \(H\) and pops one more symbol of \(L\); a right move (\(\mathsf{mvR0}\) to \(\mathsf{mvR3}\)) does the same with \(L\) and \(R\) exchanged. Writes and moves end at \(\mathsf{fin}\), which pops the clock \(Ck\) and goes to \(\mathsf{tmo}\) if it was empty, else to \(\mathsf{sc0}\).

    Scan. \(\mathsf{sc0}\) goes to \(\mathsf{nf}\) if \(C\) is empty, else to \(\mathsf{rq0}\). Then \(\mathsf{rq0}\), \(\mathsf{rq1}\) move the units on top of \(C\) onto \(T\) and pop the next symbol of \(C\); \(\mathsf{rq2}\) compares \(T\) with \(Q\) unit by unit, parking the popped units on \(U\) and \(V\); \(\mathsf{cqA}\), \(\mathsf{cqB}\) move \(U\) back onto \(T\) and \(V\) back onto \(Q\); \(\mathsf{sq0}\), \(\mathsf{sq1}\) move \(T\) onto \(B\), push a marker on \(B\), and go to \(\mathsf{ra0}\) if the two counters were equal, else to \(\mathsf{sk3a}\). The labels \(\mathsf{ra0}\) to \(\mathsf{sa1}\) do the same with \(H\) in place of \(Q\), and go to \(\mathsf{rd0}\) on equality, else to \(\mathsf{sk2a}\). From \(\mathsf{sk3a}\), \(\mathsf{sk2a}\) or \(\mathsf{sk1a}\) the program moves three, two or one further cells of \(C\) onto \(B\) (the units, then the next symbol replaced by a marker) and returns to \(\mathsf{sc0}\). The labels \(\mathsf{rd0}\) to \(\mathsf{rd3}\) move the units on top of \(C\) onto both \(N\) and \(B\), pop the next symbol of \(C\) and push a marker on \(B\); \(\mathsf{rd4}\) to \(\mathsf{rd7}\) do the same with \(A\) in place of \(N\) and go to \(\mathsf{rs}\). Finally \(\mathsf{rs}\) and \(\mathsf{nf}\) move all of \(B\) back onto \(C\), one symbol at a time, and go to \(\mathsf{act0}\) and \(\mathsf{hlt}\) respectively.

    Parse. \(\mathsf{pc0}\), \(\mathsf{pc1}\) move the units on top of \(X\) onto \(Ck\) and pop the next symbol of \(X\). Then, while the top of \(X\) is a unit, \(\mathsf{px0}\) to \(\mathsf{px2}\) move the units on top of \(X\) onto \(P\), pop the next symbol of \(X\) and push a marker on \(P\); otherwise \(\mathsf{px3}\) pops \(X\), \(\mathsf{px4}\) moves all of \(P\) onto \(R\), \(\mathsf{px5}\), \(\mathsf{px6}\) move the units on top of \(R\) onto \(H\) and pop the next symbol of \(R\), and \(\mathsf{pk0}\) moves all of \(X\) onto \(C\) and goes to \(\mathsf{sc0}\).

    Exits. \(\mathsf{tmo}\) pushes a marker on \(O\) and goes to \(\mathsf{done}\). \(\mathsf{hlt}\) pushes a marker on \(R\), \(\mathsf{o1}\) moves the units of \(H\) onto \(R\), \(\mathsf{o2}\) moves all of \(R\) onto \(P\), \(\mathsf{o3}\) pops the markers on top of \(P\), \(\mathsf{o4}\) pushes one marker on \(P\) unless \(P\) is empty, \(\mathsf{o5}\) moves all of \(P\) onto \(O\), and \(\mathsf{o6}\) pushes a unit on \(O\) and goes to \(\mathsf{done}\), where the program halts.
