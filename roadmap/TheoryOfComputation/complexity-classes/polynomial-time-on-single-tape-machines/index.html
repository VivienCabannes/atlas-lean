    TheoremThe decider within time#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Turing.SingleTapeTM.OfTM2.deciderTM_outputsWithinTime

    Let \(M\) be a finite multi-stack machine with finite stack alphabets, whose input alphabet is identified with the bits by \(e\) and whose output alphabet by \(e_1\), and let \(C\) be the emulation constant of \(M\). Let \(w\) be a bit list, \(b\) a bit and \(t\) a natural number. Suppose \(M\), started with its input stack holding the bits of \(w\) in reverse order followed by \(\mathrm{true}\) (read through \(e^{-1}\)), is after exactly \(t\) steps in the halting configuration whose output stack holds just \(e_1^{-1}(b)\), whose other stacks are empty and whose internal state is the initial one. Then the single-tape decider of \(M\), started on the tape of input-bit cells of \(w\), halts on the one-cell tape holding the input-bit cell of \(b\) within \(C t (|w| + 1 + C t + 1) + 4\) steps.

      some (haltList tm [e₁.symm b])) :
    (deciderTM tm e e₁).OutputsWithinTime (w.map (inCell tm e)) [inCell tm e b]
      (emulationConstant tm * t * (w.length + 1 + emulationConstant tm * t + 1) + 4)
