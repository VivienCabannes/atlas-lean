    DefinitionThe ε-NFA for the homomorphic image#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.HomImage.εnfa

    Let \(M\) be a DFA over a finite alphabet \(T\) with start state \(q_0\), transition function \(\delta\) and accepting states \(F\), let \(\varphi\) map letters of \(T\) to words over \(T'\), and let \(N\) be the maximum of the lengths \(|\varphi(a)|\) over \(a \in T\). The ε-NFA over \(T'\) has as states the states of \(M\) together with the triples \((q, a, i)\) with \(q\) a state of \(M\), \(a \in T\) and \(0 \le i < N\). Write \(\mathrm{next}(q, a, i)\) for \((q, a, i + 1)\) if \(i + 1 < |\varphi(a)|\) and for \(\delta(q, a)\) otherwise. From a state \(q\) of \(M\), reading \(b\) leads to \(\mathrm{next}(q, a, 0)\) for each letter \(a\) whose image starts with \(b\), and an ε-move leads to \(\delta(q, a)\) for each letter \(a\) with \(\varphi(a)\) empty. From \((q, a, i)\), reading \(b\) leads to \(\mathrm{next}(q, a, i)\) if the letter of \(\varphi(a)\) at position \(i\) (counted from \(0\)) is \(b\), and there are no ε-moves. The only start state is \(q_0\) and the accepting states are those in \(F\).

    def εnfa : εNFA T' (State φ σ) where
  step
    | .inl q, some b => {s | ∃ a, (φ a)[0]? = some b ∧ s = advance φ M q a 0}
    | .inr (q, a, i), some b => {s | (φ a)[i.val]? = some b ∧ s = advance φ M q a i}
    | .inl q, none => {s | ∃ a, φ a = [] ∧ s = .inl (M.step q a)}
    | .inr _, none => ∅
  start := {.inl M.start}
  accept := {s | ∃ q ∈ M.accept, s = .inl q}

    InductiveShuffle of two words#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.IsShuffle

    The relation "\(w\) is a shuffle of \(u\) and \(v\)" on words over \(\alpha\) is defined inductively: the empty word is a shuffle of the empty word and the empty word, and if \(w\) is a shuffle of \(u\) and \(v\), then for every letter \(a\) the word \(aw\) is a shuffle of \(au\) and \(v\), and a shuffle of \(u\) and \(av\). Thus \(w\) interleaves the letters of \(u\) and of \(v\), keeping their order within each word.

    inductive IsShuffle : List α → List α → List α → Prop
  | nil : IsShuffle [] [] []
  | left {u v w : List α} (a : α) : IsShuffle u v w → IsShuffle (a :: u) v (a :: w)
  | right {u v w : List α} (a : α) : IsShuffle u v w → IsShuffle u (a :: v) (a :: w)

    DefinitionThe ε-NFA for a substitution#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Language.RegularSubstitution.εnfa

    Let \(M\) be a DFA over \(T\) with states \(\sigma\), and for each letter \(a \in T\) let \(N_a\) be a DFA over \(T'\) with states \(\tau_a\).
