    Cslib.Language.minimizedDFA

    Let \(M\) be a DFA accepting the language \(L\). The minimized DFA of \(M\) has as states the reachable residuals of \(M\), that is, the languages accepted by \(M\) from the states it reaches from its start state. Its start state is \(L\); on a letter \(a\) it moves from a state \(K\) to the left quotient \(\{z : az \in K\}\); and its accepting states are those that contain the empty word.
