    Cslib.Language.alphabetPreimageMorphism_ker_le

    Let \(f \colon \Sigma \to \Gamma\) be a map between alphabets, \(L\) a language over \(\Gamma\) with syntactic monoid \(M(L)\), and \(\varphi \colon \Sigma^* \to M(L)\) the alphabet preimage morphism, which sends a word \(w\) to the class in \(M(L)\) of the word \(f^*(w)\) obtained by applying \(f\) to each letter of \(w\).
