    TheoremBounded monotone rounds on lists close off#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Decidability.iterate_closed

    Let \(f\) map lists of natural numbers to lists of natural numbers and be monotone for list inclusion: \(f(G) \subseteq f(G')\) whenever \(G \subseteq G'\), where \(G \subseteq G'\) means that every entry of \(G\) is an entry of \(G'\). If \(B\) is a list with \(f(G) \subseteq B\) for every list \(G\), and \(n\) is the length of \(B\), then \(f(f^n([\,])) \subseteq f^n([\,])\), where \(f^n\) is the \(n\)-fold iterate of \(f\) and \([\,]\) is the empty list.

    theorem iterate_closed {f : List ℕ → List ℕ} (hmono : ∀ G G', G ⊆ G' → f G ⊆ f G')
    (B : List ℕ) (hB : ∀ G, f G ⊆ B) : f (f^[B.length] []) ⊆ f^[B.length] []
