    StructureParity game#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Games.ParityGame

    A parity game on a type of positions consists of an owner for each position, which is one of the two players, a move relation between positions, where the owner of \(v\) may move from \(v\) to \(w\) when \(v\) is related to \(w\), and a natural-number priority for each position, such that every position has at least one move (there are no dead ends).

    As in the usual infinite-play model, the absence of dead ends means that both players always have positional strategies.

    structure ParityGame (Pos : Type*) where
  /-- The player choosing the move at each position. -/
  owner : Pos → Player
  /-- The moves: `Move v w` iff the owner of `v` may move from `v` to `w`. -/
  Move : Pos → Pos → Prop
  /-- The priority of each position. -/
  priority : Pos → ℕ
  /-- Every position has a move. -/
  serial : ∀ v, ∃ w, Move v w
    inductive Player where
  /-- The player who wins plays whose maximal recurring priority is even. -/
  | even
  /-- The player who wins plays whose maximal recurring priority is odd. -/
  | odd
  deriving DecidableEq, Repr
