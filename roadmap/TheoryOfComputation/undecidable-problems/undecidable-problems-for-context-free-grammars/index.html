    Cslib.Decidability.exists_not_tm2Decidable_overlap

    There is an alphabet \(T\), with decidable equality and a primitive recursive encoding into \(\mathbb{N}\), such that no multi-stack machine decides (in the sense of TM2-decidability), for a pair \((G_1, G_2)\) of encoded context-free grammars over \(T\), whether some word lies in both \(L(G_1)\) and \(L(G_2)\), where \(L(G)\) is the language of \(G\).

    \(T\) is not required to be finite. This is Sipser's problem \(\mathit{OVERLAP}_{\mathit{CFG}}\).

    theorem exists_not_tm2Decidable_overlap :
    ∃ (T : Type) (_ : Primcodable T) (_ : DecidableEq T),
      ¬TM2Decidable fun g : EncodedCFG T × EncodedCFG T ↦
        ∃ w, w ∈ g.1.language ∧ w ∈ g.2.language
