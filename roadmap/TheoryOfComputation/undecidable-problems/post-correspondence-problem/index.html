    Let \(P\) be a finite list of dominoes. A solution of \(P\) is a nonempty sequence \(d_1, \dots, d_k\) of dominoes, each occurring in \(P\) (repetitions allowed), such that the concatenation of the top words of \(d_1, \dots, d_k\) equals the concatenation of their bottom words.
