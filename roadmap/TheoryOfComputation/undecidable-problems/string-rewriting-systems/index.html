    Cslib.Decidability.Rewriting.Step

    Let \(R\) be a string rewriting system, given as a list of rules. A word \(w\) rewrites to a word \(w'\) in one step of \(R\) when there are a rule \((l, r)\) of \(R\) and words \(u\) and \(v\) with \(w = u l v\) and \(w' = u r v\).

    inductive Step (R : List (Rule α)) : List α → List α → Prop
  | mk {l r : List α} (u v : List α) : (l, r) ∈ R → Step R (u ++ l ++ v) (u ++ r ++ v)

    Cslib.Decidability.Rewriting.toMPCP

    Let \((R, x, y)\) be an instance of the reachability problem over an alphabet \(\alpha\), where \(R = [(l_1, r_1), \dots, (l_k, r_k)]\), and write \(\#\) for the separator and \(\diamond\) for the end marker of the extended alphabet \(\mathrm{Sym}(\alpha)\), in which letters of \(\alpha\) are read as letters.     TheoremThe backward direction of the MPCP reduction#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Decidability.Rewriting.derives_of_top_eq

    Let \((R, x, y)\) be a reachability instance and read words over \(\alpha\) as words of letters. Let \(A\) be a sequence of dominoes of the MPCP instance of \((R, x, y)\). For all words \(v, s, t\) over \(\alpha\), if \(s\) rewrites to \(t\) under \(R\) in zero or more steps and the top of \(A\) equals \(v \star t\) followed by the bottom of \(A\), where \(\star\) is the row separator, then \(s v\) rewrites to \(y\) under \(R\) in zero or more steps.

    Reading the dominoes left to right, \(s\) is the part of the current row already matched, with bottoms \(t\), and \(v\) is the rest of the row still to be matched on top.

    theorem derives_of_top_eq : ∀ (A : List (Domino (Sym α))), (∀ c ∈ A, c ∈ toMPCP I) →
    ∀ (v s t : List α), Derives I.1 s t → top A = lift v ++ star :: (lift t ++ bottom A) →
      Derives I.1 (s ++ v) I.2.2
    abbrev Domino (α : Type*) := List α × List α
