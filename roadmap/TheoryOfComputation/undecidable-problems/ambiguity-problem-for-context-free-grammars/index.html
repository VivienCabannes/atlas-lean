    Let \(P = [(t_0, b_0), \dots, (t_{m-1}, b_{m-1})]\) be a list of dominoes over an alphabet \(\alpha\), with top words \(t_i\) and bottom words \(b_i\). The ambiguity grammar of \(P\) is the encoded context-free grammar over the terminals \(\alpha \sqcup \mathbb{N}\) (letters and indices) with nonterminals \(S = 0\), \(A = 1\), \(B = 2\), start symbol \(S\), and the productions \(S \to A\) and \(S \to B\) together with, for every \(i < m\), the productions \(A \to t_i\, A\, i\), \(A \to t_i\, i\), \(B \to b_i\, B\, i\) and \(B \to b_i\, i\), in which the index \(i\) is a terminal.

    def ambGrammar (P : List (Domino α)) : EncodedCFG (α ⊕ ℕ) :=
  ⟨0, (0, [ntSym 1]) :: (0, [ntSym 2]) :: (sideRules Prod.fst 1 P ++ sideRules Prod.snd 2 P)⟩

    Let \(P\) be a list of dominoes over \(\alpha\), let \(\sigma\) assign a word over \(\alpha\) to each domino, let \(A\) be a natural number, and for \(i \in \mathbb{N}\) write \(w_i\) for \(\sigma\) applied to the \(i\)-th domino of \(P\) (the domino with both words empty when \(i\) is past the end of \(P\)).
