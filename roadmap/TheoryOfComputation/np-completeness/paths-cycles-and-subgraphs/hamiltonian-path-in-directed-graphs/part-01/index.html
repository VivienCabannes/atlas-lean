    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.IsHamPath

    Let \(E\) be a list of pairs of natural numbers, viewed as the digraph on \(0, \dots, n - 1\) whose arcs are the pairs in \(E\) with both ends below \(n\). A list \(p\) is a Hamiltonian path from \(s\) to \(t\) when it lists each of the vertices \(0, \dots, n - 1\) exactly once, starts at \(s\), ends at \(t\), and each two consecutive entries of \(p\) form an arc.

    def IsHamPath (n : ℕ) (es : List (ℕ × ℕ)) (s t : ℕ) (p : List ℕ) : Prop :=
  p.Nodup ∧ p.length = n ∧ (∀ v ∈ p, v < n) ∧ p.head? = some s ∧ p.getLast? = some t ∧
    p.IsChain (Path.arc n es)
    def arc (n : ℕ) (es : List (ℕ × ℕ)) (u v : ℕ) : Prop := (u, v) ∈ es ∧ u < n ∧ v < n

    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.diamondEdges

    Let \(\varphi\) be a CNF formula with \(m\) clauses, and write \(T(i)\) for the top of diamond \(i\) and \(R(i, l)\) for its row vertex \(l\). The arcs of diamond \(i\) for variable \(x\) are \(T(i) \to R(i, 0)\), \(T(i) \to R(i, 3m)\), \(R(i, 0) \to T(i + 1)\) and \(R(i, 3m) \to T(i + 1)\); for every \(l < 3m\), both \(R(i, l) \to R(i, l + 1)\) and \(R(i, l + 1) \to R(i, l)\); and for every clause \(j < m\), the arcs \(R(i, 3j + 1) \to j\) and \(j \to R(i, 3j + 2)\) if \(x\) occurs positively in clause \(j\), and the arcs \(R(i, 3j + 2) \to j\) and \(j \to R(i, 3j + 1)\) if \(x\) occurs negatively in clause \(j\). Here \(j\) is the vertex of clause \(j\).

    def diamondEdges (φ : CNF) (i x : ℕ) : List (ℕ × ℕ) :=
  let m := φ.length
  [(hpT m i, hpR m i 0), (hpT m i, hpR m i (3 * m)), (hpR m i 0, hpT m (i + 1)),
    (hpR m i (3 * m), hpT m (i + 1))] ++
  (List.range (3 * m)).flatMap (fun l => [(hpR m i l, hpR m i (l + 1)),
    (hpR m i (l + 1), hpR m i l)]) ++

    (if (x, true) ∈ φ.getD j [] then [(hpR m i (3 * j + 1), j), (j, hpR m i (3 * j + 2))]
      else []) ++
    (if (x, false) ∈ φ.getD j [] then [(hpR m i (3 * j + 2), j), (j, hpR m i (3 * j + 1))]
      else [])

    DefinitionSipser's HAMPATH instance of a CNF formula#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.toHam

    Let \(\varphi\) be a CNF formula with \(m\) clauses, numbered \(0, \dots, m - 1\), and let \(x_0, \dots, x_{k-1}\) be its distinct variables, in the order of their last occurrences in \(\varphi\) (clauses read in order, and the literals of each clause in order). Put \(T_i = m + i(3m + 2)\) and \(R_{i,l} = T_i + 1 + l\). The result is the encoded PATH instance with vertices \(0, \dots, T_k\), source \(T_0\), target \(T_k\), and, for each \(i < k\), the following arcs: the frame arcs \(T_i \to R_{i,0}\), \(T_i \to R_{i,3m}\), \(R_{i,0} \to T_{i+1}\) and \(R_{i,3m} \to T_{i+1}\); the row arcs \(R_{i,l} \to R_{i,l+1}\) and \(R_{i,l+1} \to R_{i,l}\) for every \(l < 3m\); and, for every clause \(j < m\), the arcs \(R_{i,3j+1} \to j\) and \(j \to R_{i,3j+2}\) if the literal \(x_i\) occurs in clause \(j\), and the arcs \(R_{i,3j+2} \to j\) and \(j \to R_{i,3j+1}\) if the literal \(\neg x_i\) occurs in clause \(j\).

    def toHam (φ : CNF) : List Bool :=
  Path.encodeP (hpT φ.length 0) (hpT φ.length (hamVars φ).length)
    (hpT φ.length (hamVars φ).length + 1) (hamEdges φ)
