    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.HamVerify.prog

    The verifier is the program of the multi-stack machine with the stacks and labels of the Hamiltonian path verifier, every stack over the alphabet \(\mathrm{Sym}\), and a Boolean internal variable, given below label by label. Write \(\mathrm{bit}(x)\) for the bit symbol of a boolean \(x\). Write \((a, n)\), \((a, s)\), \((c, n)\) and \((b, t)\) for the four counter comparisons, and \(\alpha(i)\) and \(\beta(i)\) for the first and the second counter of comparison \(i\). Every unary reader, unary mover, bit-moving loop and transfer below has the label it sits at as its own label; the bit-moving loops read bit symbols as bits and every other symbol as a marker, and push \(\mathrm{bit}(x)\) for a bit \(x\). The transfer from a stack \(k\) onto a list of stacks with next label \(m\) pops \(k\); if it popped a symbol, it pushes \(\mathrm{bit}(\mathrm{true})\) onto each listed stack and returns to its own label, and otherwise it goes to \(m\). An emptiness test of a stack "to \(e\), else \(e'\)" goes to \(e\) if the stack is empty and to \(e'\) otherwise. A transfer also stores in the Boolean variable whether its pop found a symbol, and an emptiness test stores there whether the stack is empty.

    • \(\mathsf{rdS}\), \(\mathsf{rdT}\), \(\mathsf{rdN}\): the unary reader from \(X\) onto \(S\), \(T\), \(N\) respectively, with next labels \(\mathsf{rdT}\), \(\mathsf{rdN}\), \(\mathsf{vE}\) respectively.
    • \(\mathsf{vE}\): the emptiness test of \(X\) to \(\mathsf{vEb}\), else \(\mathsf{vEU}\). \(\mathsf{vEU}\) and \(\mathsf{vEV}\): the unary mover from \(X\) to \(E\), with next labels \(\mathsf{vEV}\) and \(\mathsf{vE}\) respectively. \(\mathsf{vEb}\): the bit-moving loop from \(E\) to \(X\) with next label \(\mathsf{vW}\).
    • \(\mathsf{vW}\): the emptiness test of \(W\) to \(\mathsf{vWb}\), else \(\mathsf{vWU}\). \(\mathsf{vWU}\): the unary mover from \(W\) to \(Z\) with next label \(\mathsf{vW}\). \(\mathsf{vWb}\): the bit-moving loop from \(Z\) to \(W\) with next label \(\mathsf{loop}\).
    • \(\mathsf{loop}\): the emptiness test of \(W\) to \(\mathsf{cmp}(c, n)\), else \(\mathsf{rdA}\). \(\mathsf{rdA}\): the unary reader from \(W\) onto \(A\) with next label \(\mathsf{cmp}(a, n)\). \(\mathsf{hd}\): the emptiness test of \(C\) to \(\mathsf{cmp}(a, s)\), else \(\mathsf{scan}\).
    • For each comparison \(i\), \(\mathsf{cmp}(i)\) is one round of counter comparison of \(\alpha(i)\) and \(\beta(i)\), with scratch stacks \(R\) (for \(\alpha(i)\)) and \(Q\) (for \(\beta(i)\)), both units \(\mathrm{bit}(\mathrm{true})\), own label \(\mathsf{cmp}(i)\), and \(\mathsf{cr}(i, <)\), \(\mathsf{cr}(i, =)\), \(\mathsf{cr}(i, >)\) as its labels \(\mathit{lt}\), \(\mathit{eq}\) and \(\mathit{gt}\). For each ordering \(o\), \(\mathsf{cr}(i, o)\) is the transfer from \(R\) onto \(\alpha(i)\) with next label \(\mathsf{crb}(i, o)\), and \(\mathsf{crb}(i, o)\) is the transfer from \(Q\) onto \(\beta(i)\) whose next label is the exit label of the counter comparison \(i\) with outcome \(o\).
    • \(\mathsf{scan}\): the emptiness test of \(X\) to \(\mathsf{no}\), else \(\mathsf{ceU}(\mathrm{true})\). For each boolean \(\mathit{ok}\): \(\mathsf{ceU}(\mathit{ok})\) is the step of the unary comparison loop with source \(X\), save stack \(E\), counter \(B\), label families \(\mathsf{ceU}\) and \(\mathsf{rsU}\), and verdict \(\mathit{ok}\); \(\mathsf{rsU}(\mathit{ok})\) is the transfer from \(R\) onto \(B\) with next label \(\mathsf{ceV}(\mathit{ok})\); \(\mathsf{ceV}(\mathit{ok})\) is the step of the unary comparison loop with source \(X\), save stack \(E\), counter \(A\), label families \(\mathsf{ceV}\) and \(\mathsf{rsV}\), and verdict \(\mathit{ok}\); and \(\mathsf{rsV}(\mathit{ok})\) is the transfer from \(R\) onto \(A\) with next label \(\mathsf{scB}\) if \(\mathit{ok}\) is true and \(\mathsf{scan}\) otherwise. \(\mathsf{scB}\): the bit-moving loop from \(E\) to \(X\) with next label \(\mathsf{nd}\).
    • \(\mathsf{nd}\): the emptiness test of \(W\) to \(\mathsf{ndB}\), else \(\mathsf{cbN}(\mathrm{true})\). For each boolean \(\mathit{ok}\): \(\mathsf{cbN}(\mathit{ok})\) is the step of the unary comparison loop with source \(W\), save stack \(Z\), counter \(A\), label families \(\mathsf{cbN}\) and \(\mathsf{rsN}\), and verdict \(\mathit{ok}\); and \(\mathsf{rsN}(\mathit{ok})\) is the transfer from \(R\) onto \(A\) with next label \(\mathsf{no}\) if \(\mathit{ok}\) is true and \(\mathsf{nd}\) otherwise. \(\mathsf{ndB}\): the bit-moving loop from \(Z\) to \(W\) with next label \(\mathsf{incC}\).
    • \(\mathsf{fin}\): the emptiness test of \(C\) to \(\mathsf{no}\), else \(\mathsf{cmp}(b, t)\). \(\mathsf{incC}\): the counter increment pushing \(\mathrm{bit}(\mathrm{true})\) onto \(C\) and going to \(\mathsf{dB}\). \(\mathsf{dB}\): the transfer from \(B\) onto no stack (it drains \(B\)) with next label \(\mathsf{mA}\). \(\mathsf{mA}\): the transfer from \(A\) onto \(B\) with next label \(\mathsf{loop}\).
    • \(\mathsf{yes}\) and \(\mathsf{no}\): halt.
