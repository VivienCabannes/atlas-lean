    Cslib.Complexity.CookLevin.Hardness.encX

    Given a finite multi-stack Turing machine \(M\) and a symbol \(a\) of its output-stack alphabet, this is the encoding of \(M\) that numbers the stacks and the pairs (optional label, state) through their finite enumerations, and whose effective alphabet on each stack \(k\) is the set of symbols some statement of \(M\) can push on \(k\) (plus the whole input alphabet if \(k\) is the input stack), with \(a\) added if \(k\) is the output stack.
