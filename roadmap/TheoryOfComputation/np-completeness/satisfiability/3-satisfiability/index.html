    • \(\mathrm{fb}(s, x)\): pop \(U\) until it is empty, pushing a unit on \(Z\) and on \(W\) per popped symbol, then go to \(\mathrm{fc}(s, x)\).
    • \(\mathrm{fc}(s, x)\): pop \(W\) until it is empty, pushing a unit on \(U\) per popped symbol, then go to \(\mathrm{fd}(s, x)\).
    • \(\mathrm{fd}(s, x)\): pop \(Z\) until it is empty, prepending \(\text{true}, \text{false}\) to \(O\) per popped symbol, then go to \(\mathrm{fe}(s, x)\).
