    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.EmitColor.dup_run

    Let \(s\) be a counter, \(c\) a stack different from the stack of \(s\) and from \(W\), and \(x\) a symbol of \(c\). Suppose the instruction at label \(l_1\) pops the stack of \(s\) until it is empty, pushing \(x\) onto \(c\) and the unit symbol onto \(W\) for each popped symbol, then goes to \(l_2\); and the instruction at \(l_2\) pops \(W\) until it is empty, pushing the unit symbol of \(s\) onto the stack of \(s\) for each popped symbol, then goes to \(\ell\). Then for every \(n\), every flag and all stacks \(S\) in which the stack of \(s\) holds \(n\) unit symbols of \(s\) and \(W\) is empty, the emitter goes from label \(l_1\) with stacks \(S\) to label \(\ell\) in exactly \(2n + 2\) steps, with some flag and the stacks \(S\) except that \(n\) copies of \(x\) are pushed onto \(c\).

    theorem dup_run (s : Src) {c : K} (x : Γ c) (hc : c ≠ s.k) (hcW : c ≠ .W) {l₁ l₂ next : Lab}
    (h₁ : prog l₁ = xfer s.k [⟨c, x⟩, ⟨.W, Sym.cl⟩] l₁ l₂)
    (h₂ : prog l₂ = xfer .W [⟨s.k, s.unit⟩] l₂ next) (val : ℕ) (v : Bool) (S : ∀ k, List (Γ k))
    (hS : S s.k = cnt s.unit val) (hW : S .W = []) :
    ∃ v', Run prog (2 * val + 2) ⟨some l₁, v, S⟩
      ⟨some next, v', update S c (cnt x val ++ S c)⟩
