    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.emitColor

    The emitter whose stacks, alphabets, labels, program and start label are those of the 3-COLOR emitter machine, which exits at the machine's final label, reads the tokens of a CNF formula from stack \(T\) and writes its output bits on stack \(O\) (both symbol maps being the identity), and has step bound \(B(n) = 500 (n + 1)^4\) for \(n\) tokens. Its output for a CNF formula \(\varphi\) is the encoded 3-COLOR instance of \(\varphi\), and for an undecodable input the encoding of the complete graph \(K_4\).
