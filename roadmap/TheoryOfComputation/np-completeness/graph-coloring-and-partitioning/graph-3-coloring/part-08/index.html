    Cslib.Complexity.Karp.ColVerify.walk1_run

    Let \(k\) be a stack of the 3-COLOR verifier other than the color stack \(P\) and the scratch stack \(T\), \((a, b)\) a pair of bits, and \(\ell\) a label whose statement is the step of the walk towards the farther endpoint on the counter \(k\), holding the color \((a, b)\) and continuing at \(\ell\). Let \(j\) be a natural number, \(\mathit{pre}\) and \(\mathit{post}\) lists of color codes, \(s\) a bit and \(S\) an assignment of a list of symbols to every stack. Suppose that \(S\) holds \(j\) unit symbols on \(k\), the bits of \(\mathit{post}\) on \(P\) (each code \((a', b')\) contributing \(a'\) then \(b'\), the first code on top), and the bits of \(\mathit{pre}\), flattened the same way, in reverse order on \(T\). Started at \(\ell\) with internal state \(s\) and stacks \(S\), the verifier reaches within at most \(j + 1\) steps, with some internal state and stacks \(S'\), the label \(\mathsf{back}\) if \(j\) is less than the length of \(\mathit{post}\) and the code at position \(j\) of \(\mathit{post}\) differs from \((a, b)\), and the label \(\mathsf{no}\) otherwise.     St .T = (flat pre).reverse.map Sym.bit →
