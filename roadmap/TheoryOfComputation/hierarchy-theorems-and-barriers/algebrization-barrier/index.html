    DefinitionMultilinear extension of a Boolean function#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.Algebrization.multilinearExt

    Identify the booleans true and false with \(1\) and \(0\). Let \(F\) be a field, \(n\) a natural number and \(f : \{0,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}\). The multilinear extension of \(f\) at a point \(x = (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1}) \in F^n\) is

    \[\sum_{a \in \{0,1\}^n} f(a) \prod_{i=0}^{n-1} \ell_{a_i}(x_i),\]

    where \(f(a) \in \{0, 1\}\) is read in \(F\), \(\ell_1(t) = t\) and \(\ell_0(t) = 1 - t\).

    noncomputable def multilinearExt {n : ℕ} {F : Type*} [Field F]
    (f : (Fin n → Bool) → Bool) (x : Fin n → F) : F :=
  ∑ a : Fin n → Bool, (if f a then 1 else 0) *
    ∏ i : Fin n, (if a i then x i else 1 - x i)

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.Algebrization.extAnswer

    Let \(n\) be a natural number, let \(p\) be a monic irreducible polynomial of degree \(n + 1\) over \(\mathbb{F}_2 = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), let \(F = \mathbb{F}_2[X]/(p)\) with \(\alpha\) the class of \(X\), let \(B\) be a function from bitstrings to booleans, and let \(\tilde B_n\) be the multilinear extension over \(F\) of the restriction of \(B\) to inputs of length \(n\). Given a list \((e_0, e_1, \ldots)\) of bitstrings and an index \(j\) with \(0 \le j \le n\), let \(e_{i,k} \in \{0, 1\}\) be the bit of \(e_i\) at position \(k\), counting from \(0\), where a missing list entry or a missing bit counts as \(0\), and let \(x \in F^n\) be the point with coordinates \(x_i = \sum_{k=0}^{n} e_{i,k} \alpha^k\) for \(i < n\). The answer bit is true exactly when the coefficient of \(\alpha^j\) in \(\tilde B_n(x)\), written in the basis \(1, \alpha, \dots, \alpha^n\) of \(F\) over \(\mathbb{F}_2\), equals \(1\).

    The index \(j\) ranges over \(0, \dots, n\) only: an out-of-range index must be rejected by the caller, not reduced modulo \(n + 1\).

    noncomputable def extAnswer {p : Polynomial (ZMod 2)} [Fact (Irreducible p)] (hp : p.Monic)
    (n : ℕ) (_hdeg : p.natDegree = n + 1) (B : List Bool → Bool)
    (pt : List (List Bool)) (j : Fin p.natDegree) : Bool :=
  decide (((AdjoinRoot.powerBasisAux' hp).repr
    (multilinearExt (baseAt B n)
      (fun i => AdjoinRoot.mk p (∑ k : Fin p.natDegree,
        Polynomial.monomial k.val
          (if (pt.getD i.val []).getD k.val false then (1 : ZMod 2) else 0))))
    j = 1))

    DefinitionAlgebraic extension oracles#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.Algebrization.IsAlgebraicExtension

    Let \(B\) and \(O\) be oracles, that is, functions from bitstrings to booleans. Then \(O\) is an algebraic extension of \(B\) when there are polynomials \(\pi_0, \pi_1, \pi_2, \ldots\) over \(\mathbb{F}_2 = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), each \(\pi_n\) monic and irreducible of degree \(n + 1\), with the following two properties, where \(F_n\) is the field \(\mathbb{F}_2[X]/(\pi_n)\), \(\alpha_n\) is the class of \(X\) in \(F_n\), and \(\tilde B_n\) is the multilinear extension over \(F_n\) of the restriction of \(B\) to inputs of length \(n\). First, the map sending a bitstring \(u\) to the coefficient bitstring of \(\pi_{|u|}\) with parameter \(|u|\) (whose bits say which of the coefficients of \(X^0, \dots, X^{|u|+1}\) equal \(1\)) is polynomial-time computable. Second, let \(y\) be a bitstring. If \(y\) decodes, under the canonical bitstring encoding of triples, to a triple \((u, (e_0, \dots, e_{m-1}), j)\) of a bitstring \(u\), a list of bitstrings and a natural number \(j\) such that, writing \(n = |u|\), we have \(m = n\), every \(e_i\) has length \(n + 1\) and \(j \le n\), then \(O(y)\) is true exactly when the coefficient of \(\alpha_n^j\) in \(\tilde B_n(x)\), written in the basis \(1, \alpha_n, \dots, \alpha_n^n\) of \(F_n\) over \(\mathbb{F}_2\), equals \(1\). Here \(x \in F_n^n\) is the point with coordinates \(x_i = \sum_{k=0}^{n} e_{i,k} \alpha_n^k\), where \(e_{i,k} \in \{0, 1\}\) is the bit of \(e_i\) at position \(k\), counting from \(0\). Otherwise, in particular when \(y\) does not decode, \(O(y)\) is false.

    Every malformed query is answered false, so an oracle machine cannot route around the algebraic structure through invalid encodings, and an out-of-range \(j\) does not wrap around to coordinate \(0\).

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.Algebrization.aaronson_wigderson_algebrization

    Call an oracle a function from bitstrings to booleans. There are oracles \(B\) and \(\tilde B\) such that \(\tilde B\) is an algebraic extension of \(B\) and \(\mathsf{P}^B = \mathsf{NP}^{\tilde B}\). There are also oracles \(B\) and \(\tilde B\) such that \(\tilde B\) is an algebraic extension of \(B\) and \(\mathsf{NP}^B\) is not contained in \(\mathsf{P}^{\tilde B}\).
