    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.LocalHamiltonianQMA.initState

    For a bitstring \(x = (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})\), with true read as \(1\) and false as \(0\), numbers \(m\) and \(a\), and a vector \(\psi \in \mathbb{C}^{2^m}\) (the witness on \(m\) qubits), the initial state is the tensor product of the computational basis state of \(x\), the vector \(\psi\) and the all-zero state on \(a\) ancilla qubits, in this order. It is the vector on \(\{0, \dots, 2^{n+m+a} - 1\}\) whose value at \(s\) is \(\psi(\lfloor s / 2^n \rfloor)\) if \(s < 2^{n+m}\) and \(s \bmod 2^n = \sum_{k < n} x_k 2^k\), and \(0\) otherwise. So \(x\) occupies the \(n\) low-order bits of the index, the witness the next \(m\) bits and the ancillas the \(a\) high-order bits.

    def initState (x : List Bool) (m a : ℕ) (ψ : Fin (2 ^ m) → ℂ) :
    Fin (2 ^ (x.length + m + a)) → ℂ :=
  tensorProd (tensorProd (basisState x) ψ) (zeroState a)
    def tensorProd {a b : ℕ} (φ : Fin (2 ^ a) → ℂ) (ψ : Fin (2 ^ b) → ℂ) :
    Fin (2 ^ (a + b)) → ℂ :=
  fun s => φ ⟨s.val % 2 ^ a, Nat.mod_lt _ (by positivity)⟩ *
    ψ ⟨s.val / 2 ^ a, by
      have h : s.val < 2 ^ b * 2 ^ a := by
        calc s.val < 2 ^ (a + b) := s.isLt
          _ = 2 ^ a * 2 ^ b := by rw [pow_add]
          _ = 2 ^ b * 2 ^ a := by rw [Nat.mul_comm]

    DefinitionQMA for promise problems#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.LocalHamiltonianQMA.QMAPromise

    A pair \((\Pi_{\mathrm{yes}}, \Pi_{\mathrm{no}})\) of sets of bitstrings is in QMA when there are polynomials \(m\), \(a\) and \(s\) with natural-number coefficients and a polynomial-time computable map sending each bitstring \(x\) to a list of encoded 2-local quantum gates such that, for every bitstring \(x\), writing \(N = |x| + m(|x|) + a(|x|)\): the list has at most \(s(|x|)\) gates, each well-formed on \(N\) qubits; if \(x \in \Pi_{\mathrm{yes}}\), some state \(\psi\) on \(m(|x|)\) qubits with squared norm \(1\) makes the acceptance probability of the circuit, applied to the tensor product of the basis state of \(x\), \(\psi\) and the all-zero state on \(a(|x|)\) qubits, at least \(2/3\); and if \(x \in \Pi_{\mathrm{no}}\), every state \(\psi\) on \(m(|x|)\) qubits with squared norm \(1\) makes this acceptance probability at most \(1/3\).

    No condition is imposed on bitstrings outside \(\Pi_{\mathrm{yes}} \cup \Pi_{\mathrm{no}}\).

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.LocalHamiltonianQMA.LocalTerm

    A 2-local Hamiltonian term is encoded as a triple \((i, j, e)\) of natural numbers \(i\) and \(j\), the qubits it acts on, and a list \(e\) of pairs of encoded rational numbers, the real and imaginary parts of the entries of its \(4 \times 4\) matrix in row-major order.

    The encoding itself does not require \(e\) to have \(16\) entries or the matrix to be Hermitian; the qubit bounds, the number of entries and positive semidefiniteness (hence Hermiticity) are part of the validity of a term.

    DefinitionEmbedding of a 2-local term into \(n\) qubits#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.LocalHamiltonianQMA.embedTerm

    Let a 2-local term act on qubits \(i\) and \(j\), and let \(M\) be the \(4 \times 4\) complex matrix encoded by its list of entries, with rows and columns indexed by pairs of bits. Its embedding into \(n\) qubits is the \(2^n \times 2^n\) complex matrix whose entry at basis indices \(s, t \in \{0, \dots, 2^n - 1\}\) is \(M_{(s_i, s_j), (t_i, t_j)}\) if \(s_k = t_k\) for every \(k \ne i, j\), and \(0\) otherwise, where \(s_k = \lfloor s / 2^k \rfloor \bmod 2\) is bit \(k\) of \(s\).

    When \(i \ne j\) and \(i, j < n\), this is \(M\) acting on qubits \(i\) and \(j\), tensored with the identity on the other qubits.

    noncomputable def embedTerm (n : ℕ) (term : LocalTerm) :
    Matrix (Fin (2 ^ n)) (Fin (2 ^ n)) ℂ :=
  fun s t =>
    if (∀ k, k ≠ term.1 → k ≠ term.2.1 → qBit k s.val = qBit k t.val) then
      (matrixOfEntries term.2.2) (qBit term.1 s.val, qBit term.2.1 s.val)
        (qBit term.1 t.val, qBit term.2.1 t.val)
    else 0

    MetaMathlibExt.Complexity.KeyTheorems.LocalHamiltonianQMA.localTermValid

    A 2-local term acting on qubits \(i\) and \(j\) with entry list \(e\) is valid on \(n\) qubits when \(i < j < n\), the list \(e\) has exactly \(16\) entries, each entry has nonzero denominators in the encodings of both its real and its imaginary part, the \(4 \times 4\) complex matrix \(M\) encoded by \(e\) is positive semidefinite, and \(\sum_{p, q} |M_{pq}|^2 \le 1\), that is, \(M\) has Frobenius norm at most \(1\).

    TheoremThe 2-local Hamiltonian problem is QMA-complete#●ready to prove

    The 2-local Hamiltonian promise problem, given by its yes-instances and no-instances, is in QMA, and every promise problem in QMA whose sets of yes-instances and no-instances are disjoint sets of bitstrings has a polynomial-time reduction to it.

    Here an instance encodes a number \(n\) of qubits, a list of \(m\) valid 2-local terms on \(n\) qubits and encoded rationals \(a\) and \(b\) with \(b - a \ge 1/(m + 1)\); it is a yes-instance when the Hamiltonian of the terms on \(n\) qubits is Hermitian with ground energy at most \(a\), and a no-instance when it is Hermitian with ground energy at least \(b\).
