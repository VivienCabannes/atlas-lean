    MetaMathlibExt.toeplitzHessenbergDet_genFun

    Let \(R\) be a commutative ring and \(a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots\) a sequence in \(R\). Write \(\det(a_0; a_1, \dots, a_n)\) for the determinant of the \(n \times n\) Toeplitz–Hessenberg matrix whose entry in row \(i\) and column \(j\) is \(a_{i+1-j}\) for \(j \le i + 1\) and \(0\) above the superdiagonal. In the ring of formal power series over \(R\) let \(f(x) = \sum_{n \ge 1} \det(a_0; a_1, \dots, a_n)\, x^n\) and \(H(x) = \sum_{i \ge 1} a_i (-a_0)^{i-1} x^i\). Then \(f = H + H f\).

    When \(a_0\) is invertible, \(H(x) = -g(-a_0 x)/a_0\) with \(g(x) = \sum_{i \ge 1} a_i x^i\), and the identity is the paper's \(f(x) = (-g(-a_0 x)/a_0)/(1 + g(-a_0 x)/a_0)\). The form \(f = H + H f\) needs no hypothesis on \(a_0\); for \(a_0 = 0\) it gives \(\det(0; a_1, \dots, a_n) = a_1^n\).

    MetaMathlibExt.toeplitzHessenbergDet_fussCatalan

    Write \(\det(a_0; a_1, \dots, a_n)\) for the determinant of the \(n \times n\) Toeplitz–Hessenberg matrix whose entry in row \(i\) and column \(j\) is \(a_{i+1-j}\) for \(j \le i + 1\) and \(0\) above the superdiagonal. For natural numbers \(p, r, n\) let \(A_n(p, r) = \frac{r}{pn + r} \binom{pn + r}{n}\) be the generalized Fuss–Catalan number, taken to be \(0\) when \(pn + r = 0\). Then for all natural numbers \(p, r\) and every \(n \ge 1\),

    \[\det(1; A_1(p, r), \dots, A_n(p, r)) = (-1)^{n-1} \frac{r}{n} \binom{pn - r - 1}{n - 1}\]

    in \(\mathbb{Q}\), where the binomial coefficient has an integer, possibly negative, upper index: \(\binom{x}{k} = x (x - 1) \cdots (x - k + 1)/k!\).

    This includes \(p = r = 0\), which the paper's standing assumption excludes; there every entry \(A_i(0, 0)\) is \(0\) and both sides vanish.

    TheoremToeplitz–Hessenberg determinants with Catalan entries#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.toeplitzHessenbergDet_catalan

    Write \(\det(a_0; a_1, \dots, a_n)\) for the determinant of the \(n \times n\) Toeplitz–Hessenberg matrix whose entry in row \(i\) and column \(j\) is \(a_{i+1-j}\) for \(j \le i + 1\) and \(0\) above the superdiagonal (so \(a_0\) fills the superdiagonal and \(a_1, \dots, a_n\) the first column); for \(n = 0\) it is \(1\). Let \(C_n\) be the \(n\)-th Catalan number, \(C(x) = \sum_{n \ge 0} C_n x^n \in \mathbb{Q}[[x]]\), and \(K_m = C_{m+2} - C_{m+1}\) for \(m \ge -1\), so that \(K_{-1} = 0\). Then the following hold. (1) \(\sum_{n \ge 1} \det(-1; C_1, C_3, \dots, C_{2n-1})\, x^{2n} = x^2 C(x)^2 C(-x)^2\). (2) \(\bigl(\sum_{n \ge 0} \det(-1; C_2, \dots, C_{n+1})\, x^n\bigr) \bigl(2 - C(x)^2\bigr) = 1\). (3) For every \(n \ge 1\), \(\det(1; K_{-1}, K_0, \dots, K_{n-2}) = (-1)^{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} C_i\). (4) For every \(n \ge 1\), \(\det(1; K_0, K_1, \dots, K_{n-1}) = (-1)^{n-1} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} C_i\). (5) \(\bigl(\sum_{n \ge 1} \det(-1; K_0, K_1, \dots, K_{n-1})\, x^n\bigr) \bigl(1 - x C(x)^3\bigr) = x C(x)^3\).

    Thus the determinants in (2) have generating function \(1/(2 - C(x)^2)\) and those in (5) have generating function \(x C(x)^3/(1 - x C(x)^3)\), the generating function of OEIS A026674; the determinants in (1) form OEIS A066357.
