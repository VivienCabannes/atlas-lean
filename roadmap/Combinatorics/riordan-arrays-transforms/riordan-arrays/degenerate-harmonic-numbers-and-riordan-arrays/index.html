    DefinitionDegenerate harmonic terms#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.degenerateHarmonicTerm

    For real numbers \(\lambda\) and \(\alpha\) and a natural number \(n\), the degenerate harmonic term is \(0\) when \(n = 0\) and \((-1)^{n-1} \binom{\lambda}{n} / (\lambda \alpha^n)\) when \(n \ge 1\), where \(\binom{\lambda}{n} = \lambda(\lambda - 1)\cdots(\lambda - n + 1)/n!\).

    These are the coefficients of \(t^n\) in \(-\log_\lambda(1 - t/\alpha)\), where \(\log_\lambda(1 + t) = \sum_{n \ge 1} \binom{\lambda}{n} t^n / \lambda\); the series has no constant term. For \(n \ge 1\) the term is the paper's \(H^0_{n,\lambda}(\alpha)\); at \(n = 0\) the value \(0\) follows the paper's convention \(H^r_{0,\lambda}(\alpha) = 0\) and the generating function.

    noncomputable def degenerateHarmonicTerm (lam α : ℝ) (n : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  if n = 0 then 0 else (-1) ^ (n - 1) * Ring.choose lam n / (lam * α ^ n)

    DefinitionGeneralized degenerate harmonic numbers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.generalizedDegenerateHarmonic

    For real numbers \(\lambda\) and \(\alpha\) and a natural number \(n\), Dağlı's generalized degenerate harmonic number is

    \[ H_{n,\lambda}(\alpha) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{(-1)^{k-1} \binom{\lambda}{k}}{\lambda\, \alpha^k}, \]

    where \(\binom{\lambda}{k} = \lambda(\lambda - 1)\cdots(\lambda - k + 1)/k!\); in particular \(H_{0,\lambda}(\alpha) = 0\). At \(\alpha = 1\) these are the degenerate harmonic numbers \(H_{n,\lambda}\).

    noncomputable def generalizedDegenerateHarmonic (lam α : ℝ) (n : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  ∑ k ∈ Finset.Icc 1 n, (-1) ^ (k - 1) * Ring.choose lam k / (lam * α ^ k)

    DefinitionGeneralized degenerate hyperharmonic numbers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.generalizedDegenerateHyperharmonic

    For real numbers \(\lambda\) and \(\alpha\), the generalized degenerate hyperharmonic numbers \(H^r_{n,\lambda}(\alpha)\), indexed by natural numbers \(r\) (the order) and \(n\), are defined by recursion on \(r\). At order \(0\) they are the degenerate harmonic terms: \(H^0_{0,\lambda}(\alpha) = 0\) and \(H^0_{n,\lambda}(\alpha) = (-1)^{n-1} \binom{\lambda}{n} / (\lambda \alpha^n)\) for \(n \ge 1\), where \(\binom{\lambda}{n}\) is the generalized binomial coefficient. For every order \(r\), \(H^{r+1}_{n,\lambda}(\alpha) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} H^r_{k,\lambda}(\alpha)\).

    In particular \(H^r_{0,\lambda}(\alpha) = 0\) for \(r \ge 1\), and \(H^1_{n,\lambda}(\alpha)\) is the generalized degenerate harmonic number \(H_{n,\lambda}(\alpha) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^{k-1} \binom{\lambda}{k} / (\lambda \alpha^k)\).

    noncomputable def generalizedDegenerateHyperharmonic (lam α : ℝ) : ℕ → ℕ → ℝ
  | 0, n => degenerateHarmonicTerm lam α n
  | r + 1, n => ∑ k ∈ Finset.Icc 1 n, generalizedDegenerateHyperharmonic lam α r k

    DefinitionGeneralized degenerate harmonic numbers of a given rank#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.degenerateHarmonicRank

    For real \(\lambda\) and \(\alpha\), let \(\ell_0 = 0\) and \(\ell_m = (-1)^{m-1} \binom{\lambda}{m} / (\lambda \alpha^m)\) for \(m \ge 1\), where \(\binom{\lambda}{m}\) is the generalized binomial coefficient and a quotient by \(0\) is read as \(0\), and let \(L(t) = \sum_{m \ge 0} \ell_m t^m\) as a formal power series. For \(\lambda, \alpha > 0\) this is the expansion of \(-\log_\lambda(1 - t/\alpha)\), where \(\log_\lambda(1 + t) = ((1 + t)^\lambda - 1)/\lambda\) is the degenerate logarithm. For natural numbers \(n\) and \(r\), the generalized degenerate harmonic number of rank \(r\), written \(H_\lambda(n, r, \alpha)\), is the sum of the coefficients of \(t^0, t^1, \dots, t^n\) in \(L(t)^{r+1}\). Equivalently, \(\sum_{n \ge 0} H_\lambda(n, r, \alpha) t^n = L(t)^{r+1} / (1 - t)\).

    Expanding the power, \(H_\lambda(n, r, \alpha)\) is the sum of \(\ell_{k_0} \cdots \ell_{k_r}\) over all \(k_0, \dots, k_r \ge 1\) with \(k_0 + \cdots + k_r \le n\), which is the explicit definition in the source.

    noncomputable def degenerateHarmonicRank (lam α : ℝ) (n r : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  ∑ m ∈ Finset.range (n + 1),
    PowerSeries.coeff m (PowerSeries.mk (degenerateHarmonicTerm lam α) ^ (r + 1))
    noncomputable def degenerateHarmonicTerm (lam α : ℝ) (n : ℕ) : ℝ :=
