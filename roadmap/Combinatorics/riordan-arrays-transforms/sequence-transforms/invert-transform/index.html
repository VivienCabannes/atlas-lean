    MetaMathlibExt.bell_convolution_linear_recurrence_of_charZero

    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero and let \((c_m)_{m \ge 0}\) and \((y_n)_{n \ge 0}\) be sequences in \(K\) such that for every \(n \ge 0\)

    \[y_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} k! \sum_{j} \prod_{i=1}^{n+1} \frac{c_i^{j_i}}{j_i!},\]

    where the inner sum runs over all tuples \((j_1, \dots, j_{n+1})\) of integers with \(0 \le j_i \le n\), \(j_1 + j_2 + \dots + j_{n+1} = k\) and \(j_1 + 2 j_2 + \dots + (n+1) j_{n+1} = n\). Let \(r \ge 1\) be an integer, and for \(N \ge 0\) let \(y^{(r)}_N\) be the sum of \(y_{a_1} y_{a_2} \cdots y_{a_r}\) over all tuples \((a_1, \dots, a_r)\) of nonnegative integers with \(a_1 + \dots + a_r = N\). Then for every \(n \ge 1\)

    \[n \, y^{(r)}_n = \sum_{m=1}^{n} \bigl(n + m(r-1)\bigr) \, c_m \, y^{(r)}_{n-m}.\]

    The hypothesis says that \(y_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{k!}{n!} B_{n,k}(1!\,c_1, 2!\,c_2, \dots)\) with the partial Bell polynomials \(B_{n,k}\), that is, \(y\) is the INVERT transform of \(c\): as formal power series in \(z\), \(\sum_{n \ge 0} y_n z^n = 1/(1 - \sum_{m \ge 1} c_m z^m)\). The value \(c_0\) plays no role, and the theorem for real sequences is the case \(K = \mathbb{R}\).

      ∏ i, ((c (i.val + 1)) ^ (j i) / (((j i).factorial : ℕ) : K)))
    (n : ℕ) (hn : 1 ≤ n) :
    let conv : ℕ → K := fun N =>
      ∑ f ∈ Finset.univ.filter
        (fun f : Fin r → Fin (N + 1) => ∑ i, (f i).val = N),
      ∏ i, y ((f i).val);
    (n : K) * conv n = ∑ m ∈ Finset.Icc 1 n, ((n + m * (r - 1) : ℕ) : K) * c m * conv (n - m)

    DefinitionThe INVERT transform of an integer sequence#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.invertTransform

    The INVERT transform of an integer sequence \(a = (a_n)_{n \ge 0}\) is the integer sequence \(b = (b_n)_{n \ge 0}\) in which \(b_n\) is the coefficient of \(x^{n+1}\) in the inverse of \(1 - \sum_{m \ge 0} a_m x^{m+1}\) in \(\mathbb{Z}[[x]]\) (the inverse exists because the constant coefficient is \(1\)). In the one-based indexing of the source, \(a_n\) and \(b_n\) are the terms of index \(n + 1\).

    noncomputable def invertTransform (a : ℕ → ℤ) : ℕ → ℤ :=
  fun n => PowerSeries.coeff (n + 1) ((1 - PowerSeries.X * PowerSeries.mk a).invOfUnit 1)

    MetaMathlibExt.invertTransform_spec

    Let \(a = (a_n)_{n \ge 0}\) be an integer sequence and \(b = (b_n)_{n \ge 0}\) its INVERT transform, and put \(A(x) = \sum_{n \ge 0} a_n x^{n+1}\) and \(B(x) = \sum_{n \ge 0} b_n x^{n+1}\). Then \((1 - A(x))(1 + B(x)) = 1\) in \(\mathbb{Z}[[x]]\), that is, \(1 + B(x)\) is the inverse of \(1 - A(x)\).
