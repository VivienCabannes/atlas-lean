    TheoremEach component of the power digraph has exactly one cycle#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.powMap_component_unique_cycle

    Let \(p\) be a prime and \(k\) a natural number, and let \(G^k_p\) be the digraph on \(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\) with edges \(a \to a^k\), so that \(m\) steps lead from \(a\) to \(a^{k^m}\). Call \(a\) a cycle vertex if \(a^{k^r} = a\) for some \(r > 0\), and say that \(a\) and \(b\) lie in the same component if \(a^{k^m} = b^{k^n}\) for some \(m, n \ge 0\). Then every \(a\) has a cycle vertex \(a^{k^m}\) among its iterates, and if two cycle vertices \(a\) and \(b\) lie in the same component, then \(b = a^{k^m}\) for some \(m \ge 0\).       ∀ a b : ZMod p, (∃ r : ℕ, 0 < r ∧ a ^ k ^ r = a) → (∃ r : ℕ, 0 < r ∧ b ^ k ^ r = b) →
        (∃ m n : ℕ, a ^ k ^ m = b ^ k ^ n) → ∃ m : ℕ, a ^ k ^ m = b
