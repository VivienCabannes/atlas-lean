    DefinitionPosition function of a letter#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.letterPosition

    For an infinite binary word \(w = \ell_0 \ell_1 \cdots\), a letter \(\alpha\) and \(n \ge 1\), \(p_\alpha(n)\) is the position of the \(n\)-th occurrence of \(\alpha\) in \(w\), counting positions from \(0\); that is, \(p_\alpha(n)\) is the \(n\)-th smallest index \(i\) with \(\ell_i = \alpha\).

    By convention \(p_\alpha(n) = 0\) when \(\alpha\) occurs fewer than \(n\) times in \(w\), and \(p_\alpha(0) = p_\alpha(1)\).

    public noncomputable def letterPosition (w : ℕ → Bool) (α : Bool) (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  Nat.nth (fun i => w i = α) (n - 1)
