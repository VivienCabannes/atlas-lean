    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedThueMorse.shiftWord_mem

    Let \(L\) be a natural number and let \(t\) and \(b\) be letters of the alphabet \(\{0, 1, \dots, L - 1\}\).     DefinitionBlocks of the generalized Thue–Morse sequence#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedThueMorse.block

    Let \(L > 1\) and \(k > 1\) be natural numbers, take the letters \(0, 1, \dots, L-1\) (the elements of \(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}\)), and let \(\kappa\) assign a shift \(\kappa(j, n) \in \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}\) to each \(j \in \{1, \dots, k-1\}\) and each \(n \ge 0\). For a word \(w\) and \(t \in \mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z}\), let \(f^t(w)\) be the word obtained from \(w\) by adding \(t\) modulo \(L\) to every letter. The blocks \(A_n\) of the \((L, k, \kappa)\)-Thue–Morse sequence are the words given by \(A_0 = 0\) (a word of one letter) and \(A_{n+1} = A_n \, f^{\kappa(1, n)}(A_n) \, f^{\kappa(2, n)}(A_n) \cdots f^{\kappa(k-1, n)}(A_n)\).

    public def block (L k : Nat) (hL : 1 < L) (hk : 1 < k)
    (kappa : Fin (k - 1) → Nat → Fin L) : Nat → List (Fin L)
  | 0 => [⟨0, by omega⟩]
  | n + 1 =>
    let prev := block L k hL hk kappa n
    prev ++ List.flatten (List.map (fun j => shiftWord (kappa j n) prev) (List.finRange (k - 1)))
