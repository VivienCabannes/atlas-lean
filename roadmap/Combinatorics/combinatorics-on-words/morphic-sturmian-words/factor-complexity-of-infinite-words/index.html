    InfiniteWord.factor

    A right-infinite word over an alphabet \(\alpha\) is a sequence \(u = u_0 u_1 u_2 \cdots\) of letters \(u_n \in \alpha\). For natural numbers \(k\) and \(i\), the length-\(k\) factor of \(u\) at position \(i\) is the word \(u_i u_{i+1} \cdots u_{i+k-1}\), given as the map \(\{0, \ldots, k-1\} \to \alpha\), \(s \mapsto u_{i+s}\).

    def factor (u : ℕ → α) (k i : ℕ) : Fin k → α := fun s => u (i + s)

    InfiniteWord.IsFactor

    A finite list \(w\) over an alphabet \(\alpha\) is a factor of a right-infinite word \(u = u_0 u_1 u_2 \cdots\) over \(\alpha\) when, writing \(\ell\) for the length of \(w\), there is a position \(i \in \mathbb{N}\) with \(w = [u_i, u_{i+1}, \ldots, u_{i+\ell-1}]\). In particular, the empty list is a factor of every right-infinite word over \(\alpha\).

    DefinitionNonempty factors of an infinite word#✓fully proved

    InfiniteWord.IsNonemptyFactor

    Let \(u = u_0 u_1 u_2 \cdots\) be a right-infinite word over an alphabet \(\alpha\), viewed as a map \(\mathbb{N} \to \alpha\). A finite word \(w = w_0 w_1 \cdots w_{n-1}\) over \(\alpha\) is a nonempty factor of \(u\) when \(n \ge 1\) and there is a position \(i \in \mathbb{N}\) such that \(w_j = u_{i+j}\) for every \(j < n\).

    The empty word is excluded, following the convention in which a factor is given by its endpoints \(r \le s\).

    def IsNonemptyFactor (w : List α) (u : ℕ → α) : Prop :=
  w ≠ [] ∧ ∃ i, ∀ j (hj : j < w.length), w[j]'hj = u (i + j)

    DefinitionFactor complexity of an infinite word#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.wordFactorComplexity

    Let \(w = w_0 w_1 w_2 \cdots\) be an infinite word over a finite alphabet \(A\). For \(n \ge 0\) and a position \(i \ge 0\), the factor of \(w\) of length \(n\) at position \(i\) is the word \(w_i w_{i+1} \cdots w_{i+n-1}\). The factor complexity \(p_w(n)\) is the number of distinct factors of \(w\) of length \(n\), each word being counted once however often it occurs in \(w\).

    noncomputable def wordFactorComplexity {A : Type*} [Finite A]
    (w : ℕ → A) (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  Set.ncard (InfiniteWord.factorSet w n)
    def factorSet (u : ℕ → α) (k : ℕ) : Set (Fin k → α) :=
