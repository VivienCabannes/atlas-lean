    MetaMathlibExt.IsLyndonWord

    Let \(\alpha\) be a linearly ordered alphabet, and order the words over \(\alpha\) lexicographically. A word \(w\) over \(\alpha\) of length \(n\) is a Lyndon word if it is nonempty and, for every \(k\) with \(0 < k < n\), \(w\) is strictly smaller than its rotation by \(k\), the word obtained by moving the first \(k\) letters of \(w\) to the end.

    def IsLyndonWord {α : Type*} [LinearOrder α] (w : List α) : Prop :=
  w ≠ [] ∧ ∀ k, 0 < k → k < w.length → w < w.rotate k

    MetaMathlibExt.wittWordContent_cons

    Let \(r\) be a natural number, let \(w\) be a word over the alphabet \(\{0, \dots, r-1\}\), let \(i\) be a letter, and write \(c(u)\) for the content of a word \(u\) (the number of occurrences of each letter in \(u\)).     MetaMathlibExt.card_lyndonWordsOfContent_eq_wittMultigradedValue

    Let \(r\) be a natural number and let \(n_0, \dots, n_{r-1}\) be natural numbers with \(n = n_0 + \dots + n_{r-1} > 0\). Then the number of Lyndon words over this alphabet in which each letter \(i\) occurs exactly \(n_i\) times equals, as a rational number, Witt's multigraded value \(M(n_0, \dots, n_{r-1}) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{d \mid g} \mu(d) \binom{n/d}{n_0/d, \ldots, n_{r-1}/d}\), where \(g = \gcd(n_0, \dots, n_{r-1})\), the sum runs over the positive divisors \(d\) of \(g\), and \(\mu\) is the Möbius function.

    def wittMultigradedValue {r : ℕ} (degree : Fin r → ℕ) : ℚ :=
  let n := Finset.sum Finset.univ degree
