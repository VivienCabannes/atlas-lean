    TheoremNon-intersecting pairs of words#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.nonIntersectingWordPairs_card_eq_coeff

    Fix natural numbers \(k\), \(n \ge 1\) and \(r \ge 1\), and call a pair of words over an alphabet of \(k\) letters non-intersecting when no letter occurs in both. Write \([x^m] F\) for the coefficient of \(x^m\) in a formal power series \(F\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and \(S(m, j)\) for the Stirling numbers of the second kind. Let \(N\) be the number of non-intersecting pairs \((u, v)\) with \(u\) of length \(n\) and \(v\) of length \(r\). Then \(N = \sum_{j=1}^{k-1} \binom{k}{j} \, [x^n]\Bigl(j! \, x^j \bigl(\prod_{i=1}^{j} (1 - i x)\bigr)^{-1}\Bigr) \, [x^r]\bigl((k-j) x \, (1 - (k-j) x)^{-1}\bigr)\) and \(N = \sum_{j=1}^{k-1} \binom{k}{j} \, j! \, S(n, j) \, [x^r]\bigl((k-j) x \, (1 - (k-j) x)^{-1}\bigr)\).

    public theorem_wanted nonIntersectingWordPairs_card_eq_coeff (k n r : ℕ) (hn : 1 ≤ n) (hr : 1 ≤ r) :
    (((Finset.univ : Finset ((Fin n → Fin k) × (Fin r → Fin k))).filter fun p => Disjoint (Finset.univ.image p.1) (Finset.univ.image p.2)).card : ℚ) =
        ∑ j ∈ Finset.Icc 1 (k - 1), (k.choose j : ℚ) *
          coeff n ((j.factorial : ℚ⟦X⟧) * X ^ j * (∏ i ∈ Finset.Icc 1 j, (1 - (i : ℚ⟦X⟧) * X))⁻¹) *
          coeff r (((k - j : ℕ) : ℚ⟦X⟧) * X * (1 - ((k - j : ℕ) : ℚ⟦X⟧) * X)⁻¹) ∧
      (((Finset.univ : Finset ((Fin n → Fin k) × (Fin r → Fin k))).filter fun p => Disjoint (Finset.univ.image p.1) (Finset.univ.image p.2)).card : ℚ) =
        ∑ j ∈ Finset.Icc 1 (k - 1), (k.choose j : ℚ) * j.factorial * Nat.stirlingSecond n j *
          coeff r (((k - j : ℕ) : ℚ⟦X⟧) * X * (1 - ((k - j : ℕ) : ℚ⟦X⟧) * X)⁻¹)

    MetaMathlibExt.nonIntersectingWordPairs_sameLength_generatingFunction

    Let \(k\) be a natural number, let \(S(n, j)\) denote the Stirling numbers of the second kind, and for \(n \ge 1\) let \(h(n)\) be the number of pairs \((u, v)\) of words of length \(n\) over an alphabet of \(k\) letters such that no letter occurs in both. Then, as formal power series over \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(\sum_{n \ge 1} h(n) x^n = \sum_{n \ge 0} \sum_{j=1}^{k-1} \binom{k}{j} \, j! \, S(n, j) \, (k-j)^n x^n\).

      PowerSeries.mk fun n => ∑ j ∈ Finset.Icc 1 (k - 1),

    TheoremOverlap statistic on pairs of words#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.wordPairs_overlap_sum_eq_coeff

    Fix natural numbers \(k\), \(n \ge 1\), \(r \ge 1\) and a rational number \(q\). For words \(u\) and \(v\) over an alphabet of \(k\) letters, the overlap of \((u, v)\) is the number of positions of \(v\) whose letter occurs in \(u\). Write \([x^m] F\) for the coefficient of \(x^m\) in a formal power series \(F\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(S(m, j)\) for the Stirling numbers of the second kind, and \(c_j = qj + k - j\). Then the sum of \(q^{\text{overlap}}\) over all pairs \((u, v)\) with \(u\) of length \(n\) and \(v\) of length \(r\) equals \(\sum_{j=1}^{k} \binom{k}{j} \, [x^r]\bigl(c_j x \, (1 - c_j x)^{-1}\bigr) \, j! \, S(n, j)\).

    public theorem_wanted wordPairs_overlap_sum_eq_coeff (k n r : ℕ) (hn : 1 ≤ n) (hr : 1 ≤ r) (q : ℚ) :
    ∑ p ∈ (Finset.univ : Finset ((Fin n → Fin k) × (Fin r → Fin k))),
        q ^ (Finset.univ.filter fun i => p.2 i ∈ Finset.univ.image p.1).card =
      ∑ j ∈ Finset.Icc 1 k, (k.choose j : ℚ) *
        coeff r (PowerSeries.C (q * j + (k - j : ℕ)) * X *
          (1 - PowerSeries.C (q * j + (k - j : ℕ)) * X)⁻¹) *
        (j.factorial * Nat.stirlingSecond n j)

For words \(u\) and \(v\) over this alphabet, the overlap of \((u, v)\) is the number of positions of \(v\) whose letter occurs in \(u\). Write \([x^m] F\) for the coefficient of \(x^m\) in a formal power series \(F\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\). Let \(T\) be the sum of \(q^{\text{overlap}}\) over all pairs \((u, v)\) of weakly decreasing words, \(u\) of length \(n\) and \(v\) of length \(r\). Then \(T = \sum_{j=1}^{k} \binom{k}{j} \, [x^n]\bigl(x^j (1-x)^{-j}\bigr) \, [x^r]\Bigl((1-x)^{-(k-j)} - 1 + (1-x)^{-(k-j)} \sum_{m \ge 1} \binom{m+j-1}{m} q^m x^m\Bigr)\) and \(T = \sum_{j=1}^{k} \binom{k}{j} \, [x^n]\bigl(x^j (1-x)^{-j}\bigr) \, [x^r]\bigl(-1 + (1-x)^{-(k-j)} (1 - qx)^{-j}\bigr)\).
