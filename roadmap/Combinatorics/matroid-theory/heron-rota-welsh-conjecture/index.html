    DefinitionCoefficients of the characteristic polynomial of a matroid#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.MatroidCharacteristicPolynomialLogConcaveAMR156Problem0026Wanted.charPolyCoeff

    Let \(M\) be a finite matroid on the ground set \(E\) with rank function \(r\), and let \(\chi_M(\lambda) = \sum_{I \subseteq E} (-1)^{|I|} \lambda^{r(E) - r(I)}\) be its characteristic polynomial. For a natural number \(j\), this is the integer coefficient of \(\lambda^j\) in \(\chi_M\), that is, the sum of \((-1)^{|I|}\) over the subsets \(I \subseteq E\) with \(r(E) - r(I) = j\).

    noncomputable def charPolyCoeff {α : Type*} (M : Matroid α) [M.Finite] (j : ℕ) : ℤ :=
  ∑ I ∈ M.ground_finite.toFinset.powerset.filter
      (fun I : Finset α => finRank M M.E - finRank M (I : Set α) = j), (-1) ^ I.card
    noncomputable def finRank {α : Type*} (M : Matroid α) (X : Set α) : ℕ :=
  (M.eRk X).toNat

    DefinitionLog-concavity of the characteristic polynomial of a matroid, statement#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.MatroidCharacteristicPolynomialLogConcaveAMR156Problem0026Wanted.characteristicPolynomialLogConcave

    For every finite matroid \(M\) on a ground set \(E\) with rank function \(r\), let \(w_k(M)\) be the absolute value of the coefficient of \(\lambda^{r(E) - k}\) in the characteristic polynomial \(\chi_M(\lambda) = \sum_{I \subseteq E} (-1)^{|I|} \lambda^{r(E) - r(I)}\) when \(k \le r(E)\), and \(w_k(M) = 0\) when \(k > r(E)\). Then \(w_{k-1}(M) \, w_{k+1}(M) \le w_k(M)^2\) for every natural number \(k \ge 1\).

    This is part (a) of Stanley's Problem 25 (Read–Hoggar–Rota–Heron–Welsh).

    TheoremAdiprasito–Huh–Katz theorem (Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture)#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.MatroidCharacteristicPolynomialLogConcaveAMR156Problem0026Wanted.characteristicPolynomialLogConcave_holds

    For every finite matroid \(M\) on a ground set \(E\) with rank function \(r\), let \(w_k(M)\) be the absolute value of the coefficient of \(\lambda^{r(E) - k}\) in the characteristic polynomial \(\chi_M(\lambda) = \sum_{I \subseteq E} (-1)^{|I|} \lambda^{r(E) - r(I)}\) when \(k \le r(E)\), and \(w_k(M) = 0\) when \(k > r(E)\). Then \(w_{k-1}(M) \, w_{k+1}(M) \le w_k(M)^2\) for every natural number \(k \ge 1\).

    This is the conjecture of Heron, Rota and Welsh, which extends Read's unimodality conjecture and Hoggar's log-concavity conjecture for chromatic polynomials of graphs. History of the proof:

    • Huh proved it for matroids representable over a field of characteristic \(0\), which includes graphs, by identifying the coefficients with mixed multiplicities of a hyperplane arrangement (Milnor numbers of projective hypersurfaces and the chromatic polynomial of graphs, Theorem 3).
    • Huh and Katz extended it to matroids realizable over some field, by identifying the coefficients of the reduced characteristic polynomial with intersection numbers on a toric variety and applying the Khovanskii–Teissier inequality (Log-concavity of characteristic polynomials and the Bergman fan of matroids, Theorems 1.1 and 6.2).
    • Adiprasito, Huh and Katz proved it for all matroids (Theorem 9.9 (2)) by constructing the Chow ring of an arbitrary matroid and proving the hard Lefschetz theorem and the Hodge–Riemann relations for it (Theorem 1.4).

    DefinitionCoefficients \(\mu_k\) of the reduced characteristic polynomial#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.MatroidReducedCharacteristicPolynomialWanted.reducedCharPolyCoeff

    Let \(M\) be a finite matroid of rank \(r + 1\). When \(M\) is loopless, \(\chi_M(1) = 0\) and the reduced characteristic polynomial is $\(\overline{\chi}_M(\lambda) = \frac{\chi_M(\lambda)}{\lambda - 1} = \sum_{k=0}^{r} (-1)^k \mu_k(M) \, \lambda^{r-k}.\)$ Dividing by \(\lambda - 1\) turns coefficients into partial sums, so $\(\mu_k(M) = (-1)^k \sum_{i=0}^{k} [\lambda^{r+1-i}] \, \chi_M(\lambda),\)$ which is the Lean definition (it makes sense for every finite matroid; if \(M\) has a loop then \(\chi_M = 0\) and every \(\mu_k(M)\) is \(0\)). Adiprasito, Huh and Katz note that \(\mu_0(M) = 1\), that \(\mu_r(M) = |\mu_M(\emptyset, E)|\) is the absolute value of the Möbius number, and that \(\mu_k(M) = w_k(M) - w_{k-1}(M) + \cdots + (-1)^k w_0(M)\).

    noncomputable def reducedCharPolyCoeff {α : Type*} (M : _root_.Matroid α) [M.Finite] (k : ℕ) :
    ℤ :=
  (-1) ^ k * ∑ i ∈ Finset.range (k + 1), charPolyCoeff M (finRank M M.E - i)

    TheoremLog-concavity of the reduced characteristic polynomial#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.MatroidReducedCharacteristicPolynomialWanted.reducedCharPolyCoeff_logConcave

    For every finite matroid \(M\) of rank \(r + 1\) and every integer \(0 < k < r\), $\(\mu_{k-1}(M) \, \mu_{k+1}(M) \le \mu_k(M)^2 .\)$

    This is Proposition 9.8 and Theorem 9.9 (1) of Adiprasito, Huh and Katz, stated there for loopless matroids (with a loop every \(\mu_k\) vanishes). Their proof is by induction on the rank: for \(k < r - 1\) it passes to the truncation of \(M\), and for \(k = r - 1\) it writes the coefficients as mixed degrees \(\mu_k(M) = \deg(\alpha_M^{r-k}\beta_M^k)\) in the Chow ring and applies the inequality \(\deg(\ell_1^2 \ell_2^{r-2}) \deg(\ell_2^2 \ell_2^{r-2}) \le \deg(\ell_1 \ell_2^{r-1})^2\) for a nef class \(\ell_2\) (Lemma 9.6, a consequence of the Hodge–Riemann relations in degree one) to \(\ell_1 = \alpha_M\) and \(\ell_2 = \beta_M\). Since \(w_k(M) = \mu_k(M) + \mu_{k-1}(M)\), the absolute coefficients of \(\chi_M\) are the convolution of \((\mu_k)\) with \((1, 1)\), and a convolution of log-concave sequences is log-concave; this gives the Heron–Rota–Welsh conjecture.
