    MetaMathlibExt.constantTerm_linearLaurent_pow_digits

    Let \(p\) be a prime, let \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), \(\gamma\), \(a\), \(b\) be integers, and let \(n\) be a natural number whose base-\(p\) digits are \(d_0, \dots, d_s\) (there are none when \(n = 0\), and both products below are then \(1\)).
