    AbbreviationColored Motzkin steps of a given rank#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.ULDMotzkin.ULDMotzkinStep

    Let \(r\) and \(l\) be natural numbers and let \(u, d \colon \{0, \dots, r - 1\} \to \mathbb{N}\). The admissible steps of rank \(r\) are the up-steps \(U_j\) with \(0 \le j < r\) in one of \(u(j)\) colors, the level steps in one of \(l\) colors, and the down-steps \(D_j\) with \(0 \le j < r\) in one of \(d(j)\) colors: the disjoint union of the sets \(\{j\} \times \{0, \dots, u(j) - 1\}\) for \(j < r\), the set \(\{0, \dots, l - 1\}\), and the sets \(\{j\} \times \{0, \dots, d(j) - 1\}\) for \(j < r\).

    A step kind with zero colors contributes no steps. The steps \(U_j\) and \(D_j\) have jump size \(j + 1\), so no up- or down-step has jump size zero.

    abbrev ULDMotzkinStep (r : ℕ) (u : Fin r → ℕ) (l : ℕ) (d : Fin r → ℕ) : Type :=
  (Σ _j : Fin r, Fin (u _j)) ⊕ Fin l ⊕ (Σ _j : Fin r, Fin (d _j))

    MetaMathlibExt.ULDMotzkin.stepHeight

    The height change of an admissible step of rank \(r\) is the integer \(j + 1\) for an up-step \(U_j\) with \(0 \le j < r\), \(0\) for a level step, and \(-(j + 1)\) for a down-step \(D_j\) with \(0 \le j < r\), whatever its color.

    MetaMathlibExt.ULDMotzkin.pathHeight

    For a sequence \(p = (p_0, \dots, p_{n-1})\) of \(n\) admissible steps of rank \(r\) and a natural number \(k\), the height of \(p\) after \(k\) steps is the integer \(\sum_{i < \min(k, n)} h(p_i)\), where \(h\) is the height change of a step. For \(k \ge n\) it is the height of the whole sequence.

    def pathHeight (r : ℕ) (u : Fin r → ℕ) (l : ℕ) (d : Fin r → ℕ) (n : ℕ)

    MetaMathlibExt.ULDMotzkin.IsMotzkinPath

    Let \(r \ge 1\). A sequence \(p = (p_0, \dots, p_{n-1})\) of \(n\) admissible steps of rank \(r\) is a Motzkin path when its height after \(k\) steps is nonnegative for every \(k\) with \(0 \le k \le n\) and its height after \(n\) steps is \(0\).
