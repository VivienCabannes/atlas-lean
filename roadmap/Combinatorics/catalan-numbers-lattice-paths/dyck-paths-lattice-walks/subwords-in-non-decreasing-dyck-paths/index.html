    DefinitionValley heights#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.dyckValleyHeights

    For a finite sequence \(l\) of up steps \(U\) and down steps \(D\), the valley heights of \(l\) form the list with one entry for each factor \(DU\) of \(l\), from left to right: the number of \(U\) steps minus the number of \(D\) steps in \(l\) up to and including the \(D\) of that factor, taken to be \(0\) when negative.

    public def dyckValleyHeights (l : List DyckStep) : List ℕ :=
  ((List.range l.length).filter fun i => decide (l[i]? = some D ∧ l[i + 1]? = some U)).map
    fun i => (l.take (i + 1)).count U - (l.take (i + 1)).count D

    DefinitionNumber of occurrences of a subword#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.dyckSubwordCount

    For finite words \(w\) and \(l\) in the up step \(U\) and the down step \(D\), the number of occurrences of \(w\) in \(l\) is the number of suffixes of \(l\), including \(l\) itself and the empty suffix, that begin with \(w\). Thus every occurrence of \(w\) as a contiguous subword of \(l\) is counted, overlapping occurrences separately.

    public def dyckSubwordCount (w l : List DyckStep) : ℕ :=
  l.tails.countP fun t => w.isPrefixOf t

    MetaMathlibExt.nondecreasingDyckSubwordBivariateGF

    For a word \(w\) in the steps \(U\) and \(D\), let \(D_n\) be the set of non-decreasing Dyck paths of semilength \(n\), and let \(\#w(P)\) be the number of occurrences of \(w\) as a contiguous subword of a path \(P\), overlapping occurrences counted separately.
