    DefinitionDiagonal sequence of a partition#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.diagonalSequence

    Let \(\alpha\) be a partition with parts \(\alpha_1 \ge \alpha_2 \ge \dots \ge \alpha_t\), and set \(\alpha_i = 0\) for \(i > t\). Its diagonal sequence \(\delta(\alpha) = (d_k)_{k \ge 0}\) has \(d_0 = 0\) and, for \(k \ge 1\), \(d_k\) equal to the number of indices \(i\) with \(1 \le i \le k\) and \(\alpha_i + i - 1 \ge k\), that is, the number of cells of the Young diagram on its \(k\)-th diagonal from upper right to lower left. Equivalently, \(d_k\) is the number of \(i\) with \(1 \le i \le k\) such that at least \(i\) parts of \(\alpha\) are at least \(k + 1 - i\); the definition uses this form, with the parts given as a finite multiset of natural numbers.

    def diagonalSequence (s : Multiset ℕ) (k : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((Finset.Icc 1 k).filter fun i => i ≤ s.countP (k + 1 - i ≤ ·)).card
