    MetaMathlibExt.IsFixedColumnHook

    Let \(\lambda\) be a partition of \(n\), let \(m\) and \(s\) be natural numbers and let \(h\) be an integer. The partition \(\lambda\) has an \(h\)-fixed hook in column \(m\) at row \(s\) if the square \((s, m)\) lies in the Young diagram of \(\lambda\), that is, \(s \ge 1\) and \(1 \le m \le \lambda_s\), and its hook length is \(h_{s,m}(\lambda) = s + h\). In other words, the \(s\)-th term of the sequence \(h_{1,m}(\lambda), h_{2,m}(\lambda), \dots\) of hook lengths in column \(m\) is an \(h\)-fixed point.

    def IsFixedColumnHook {n : ℕ} (p : n.Partition) (m s : ℕ) (h : ℤ) : Prop :=
  1 ≤ s ∧ 1 ≤ m ∧ m ≤ partitionRowLen p s ∧ partitionHookLength p s m = s + h
