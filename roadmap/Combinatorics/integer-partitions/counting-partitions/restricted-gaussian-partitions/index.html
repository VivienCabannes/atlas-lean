    MetaMathlibExt.removeFirstNPos

    Let \(x\) and \(c\) be natural numbers and let \(l\) be a list of natural numbers. For \(c = 0\) the result is the pair of the empty list and \(l\). For \(c \ge 1\), if \(l\) has at least \(c\) entries equal to \(x\), the result is the pair of the list of entries different from \(x\) that come before the \(c\)-th entry equal to \(x\), in their order in \(l\), and the part of \(l\) after that entry. If \(l\) has fewer than \(c\) entries equal to \(x\), there is no result.

    def removeFirstNPos (x c : Nat) : List Nat → Option (List Nat × List Nat)
  | [] => if c = 0 then some ([], []) else none
  | y :: ys =>
    match c with
    | 0 => some ([], y :: ys)
    | n + 1 =>
      if y = x then removeFirstNPos x n ys

    MetaMathlibExt.applySplit

    For natural numbers \(q, a, b, c\) and a list \(l\) of natural numbers, the split replacement is defined exactly when \(l\) has at least \(c\) entries equal to \(a + b\). It removes the last \(c\) entries of \(l\) equal to \(a + b\) and, at the position of the first of these removed entries, inserts \(c\) copies of \(a\) followed by \(c q^a\) copies of \(b\); the entries of \(l\) before that position stay in front, and the entries after it that were not removed follow in their original order. For \(c = 0\) the list is unchanged.

    No sorting is performed.

    MetaMathlibExt.stageSegments

    Let \(q\) be a natural number, let \((d_0, m_0), \dots, (d_{\ell-1}, m_{\ell-1})\) be blocks, let \(C_0, C_1, \dots\) be chains of branch steps, let \(F_0, F_1, \dots\) be lists of natural numbers, and let \(i\) and \(k\) be natural numbers; blocks, chains and lists are indexed from \(0\). A step \((a, b, c)\) acts on a list by deleting its last \(c\) entries equal to \(a + b\) and putting \(c\) copies of \(a\) followed by \(c q^a\) copies of \(b\) where the first deleted entry was, after the entries before it and before the remaining entries after it (a step with \(c = 0\) changes nothing); it fails if the list has fewer than \(c\) entries equal to \(a + b\).

    The stage segments at \((i, k)\) form the list of \(\ell\) segments whose entry for block \(h\) is \(F_h\) if \(h < i\); the result of applying the first \(k + 1\) steps of \(C_i\) in order to the basic segment of block \(i\) if \(h = i\) (all steps of \(C_i\) if it has at most \(k + 1\), and none if there is no chain \(C_i\)); and the basic segment of block \(h\) if \(h > i\).     MetaMathlibExt.IsRestrictedGaussianPartition

    Let \(q\) and \(n\) be natural numbers and let \(G\) be a Gaussian partition. Then \(G\) is a restricted Gaussian partition for \(q\) and \(n\) when \(q\) is a prime power, \(n > 0\), and there exist blocks \((d_1, m_1), \dots, (d_\ell, m_\ell)\) and, for each block \(i\), a chain \(C_i\) of branch steps \((a, b, c)\), with the following properties.

    The blocks satisfy \(d_i \ge 1\) and \(m_i \ge 1\) for every \(i\), \(d_1 > d_2 > \dots > d_\ell\) and \(m_1 d_1 + \dots + m_\ell d_\ell = n\). With \(p_i = \sum_{h < i} m_h d_h\), the basic segment of block \(i\) is the list of \(\beta_i = \sum_{j=0}^{m_i - 1} q^{p_i + j d_i}\) copies of \(d_i\).

    Each chain \(C_i\) is empty or satisfies the following: its first step \((a, b, c)\) has \(1 \le b \le a\), \(a + b = d_i\), \(1 \le c \le q^{p_i + (m_i - 1) d_i} - 1\) and \(\beta_i \ne 1\), and each pair of consecutive steps \((a, b, c), (a', b', c')\) has \(a' + b' = a\), \(1 \le b' \le a'\) and \(1 \le c' \le c\).

    A step \((a, b, c)\) acts on a list of dimensions by deleting its last \(c\) entries equal to \(a + b\) and putting \(c\) copies of \(a\) followed by \(c q^a\) copies of \(b\) where the first deleted entry was, after the entries before it and before the remaining entries after it; it fails if the list has fewer than \(c\) entries equal to \(a + b\). Applying the steps of \(C_i\) in order to the basic segment of block \(i\) succeeds for every \(i\), with final segment \(F_i\).

    The concatenation of the basic segments is nonincreasing, the concatenation \(F_1 \cdots F_\ell\) is nonincreasing, and for every block \(i\) and every \(k\) smaller than the length of \(C_i\), the concatenation of \(F_1, \dots, F_{i-1}\), the result of applying the first \(k + 1\) steps of \(C_i\) to the basic segment of block \(i\), and the basic segments of blocks \(i + 1, \dots, \ell\) is nonincreasing. Finally, the dimension list of \(G\) is \(F_1 \cdots F_\ell\).

    Let \(q\) be a prime power and \(n > 0\).
