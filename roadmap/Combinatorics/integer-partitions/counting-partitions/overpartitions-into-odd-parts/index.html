    Let \(\mathrm{po}(j)\) be the number of overpartitions of \(j\) into odd parts. For every natural number \(n\), the sum \(\sum_k (-1)^k \, \mathrm{po}(n - k(3k+1)/2)\) over the integers \(k\) with \(k(3k+1)/2 \le n\) equals \((-1)^{\lceil m/2 \rceil}\) if \(n = m(3m+1)/2\) for some integer \(m\), and \(0\) otherwise.

    Such an \(m\) is unique, since the numbers \(m(3m+1)/2\) with \(m\) an integer are distinct.

    DefinitionBipartitions into distinct parts#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.distinctPartsBipartitionCount

    Let \(\operatorname{pd}(m)\) be the number of partitions of \(m\) into distinct parts. For a natural number \(n\), \(q(n)\) is the number of ordered pairs of partitions into distinct parts whose sizes add up to \(n\), that is, \(q(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \operatorname{pd}(k) \operatorname{pd}(n-k)\).

    Its generating function is \(\sum_{n \ge 0} q(n) x^n = (-x; x)_\infty^2\), where \((a; x)_\infty = \prod_{i \ge 0} (1 - a x^i)\).

    public def distinctPartsBipartitionCount (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ∑ k ∈ Finset.range (n + 1),
    (Nat.Partition.distincts k).card * (Nat.Partition.distincts (n - k)).card

    MetaMathlibExt.overpartitionOdd_odd_eq_two_mul_sum_overpartition

    Let \(\mathrm{po}(m)\) be the number of overpartitions of \(m\) into odd parts and \(\overline{p}(m)\) the number of overpartitions of \(m\).
