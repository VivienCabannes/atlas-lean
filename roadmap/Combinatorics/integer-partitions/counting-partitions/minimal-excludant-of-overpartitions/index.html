    DefinitionNumber of non-overlined copies of a part size#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.nonOverlinedCount

    For an overpartition \(O\) of \(n\) and a natural number \(a\), the number of non-overlined parts of size \(a\) in \(O\) is the multiplicity of \(a\) in the underlying partition of \(O\), minus \(1\) if \(a\) is overlined in \(O\) and minus \(0\) otherwise.

    This matches the convention that only the first occurrence of a part size may be overlined: an overpartition records each overlined size once.

    public def nonOverlinedCount {n : ℕ} (O : Nat.Overpartition n) (a : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  O.toPartition.parts.count a - if a ∈ O.overlines then 1 else 0

    DefinitionMinimal excludant of an overpartition with a multiplicity bound#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.leastGapOverpartition

    For natural numbers \(r \ge 1\) and \(n\) and an overpartition \(O\) of \(n\), \(\overline{\mathrm{mex}}_r(O)\) is the least positive integer \(k\) that occurs fewer than \(r\) times among the non-overlined parts of \(O\). Such a \(k\) exists, since \(n + 1\) is not a part of \(O\). For \(r = 0\), which the paper does not use, the value is \(0\) by convention.

    MetaMathlibExt.leastGapOverpartition · 16 lines 
    public def leastGapOverpartition (r : ℕ) {n : ℕ} (O : Nat.Overpartition n) : ℕ :=
  if hr : r = 0 then 0
  else
    Nat.find (show ∃ k : ℕ,
        1 ≤ k ∧ nonOverlinedCount O k < r from by
      have hrpos : 0 < r := Nat.pos_of_ne_zero hr
      have hnotmem : n + 1 ∉ O.toPartition.parts := by
        intro hmem
        have hle : n + 1 ≤ O.toPartition.parts.sum :=
          Multiset.le_sum_of_mem hmem
        rw [O.toPartition.parts_sum] at hle
        omega
      have hcount : O.toPartition.parts.count (n + 1) = 0 :=
        Multiset.count_eq_zero.mpr hnotmem
      exact ⟨n + 1, Nat.zero_lt_succ n, by
        simp [nonOverlinedCount, hcount, hrpos]⟩)

    DefinitionThree-colored distinct-part partitions#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.D3

    For a natural number \(n\), \(D_3(n)\) is the number of triples \((A, B, C)\) of subsets of \(\{1, \dots, n\}\) such that the sum of the elements of \(A\), the sum of the elements of \(B\) and the sum of the elements of \(C\) add up to \(n\). Here \(A\), \(B\), \(C\) are the parts of the three colors of a partition of \(n\) whose parts of each color are distinct.

    A size may occur in several colors but at most once in each, as in the product \(\prod_{m \ge 1} (1 + q^m)^3\).

    public def D3 (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((((Finset.Icc 1 n).powerset.product (Finset.Icc 1 n).powerset).product
    (Finset.Icc 1 n).powerset).filter fun p =>
      p.1.1.sum id + p.1.2.sum id + p.2.sum id = n).card

    DefinitionSums of minimal excludants over overpartitions#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.sigmaMexOverpartition

    For natural numbers \(r\) and \(n\), \(\sigma_r\overline{\mathrm{mex}}(n)\) is the sum, over all overpartitions \(\pi\) of \(n\), of \(\overline{\mathrm{mex}}_r(\pi)\), the least positive integer occurring fewer than \(r\) times among the non-overlined parts of \(\pi\); for \(r = 0\) the value \(\overline{\mathrm{mex}}_0(\pi)\) is \(0\) by convention, so \(\sigma_0\overline{\mathrm{mex}}(n) = 0\). We write \(\sigma\overline{\mathrm{mex}}(n) = \sigma_1\overline{\mathrm{mex}}(n)\).

    By direct enumeration, \(\sigma\overline{\mathrm{mex}}(n) = 1, 3, 6, 13, 24\) for \(n = 0, \dots, 4\), and \(\sigma_2\overline{\mathrm{mex}}(2) = 5\).

    public noncomputable def sigmaMexOverpartition (r n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ∑ O : Nat.Overpartition n, leastGapOverpartition r O

    TheoremIngham's Tauberian theorem#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.ingham_tauberian

    Let \(a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}\) be nonnegative and weakly increasing, let \(A : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}\) be a function, and let \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), \(C\) be real numbers with \(C > 0\). Suppose that for every complex \(q\) with \(|q| < 1\) the series \(\sum_{n \ge 0} a(n) q^n\) is summable with sum \(A(q)\), and that for every complex \(q\) with \(|q| > 1\) it is not summable. Suppose also that \(A(e^{-t}) \sim \alpha t^\beta e^{C/t}\) as \(t \to 0^+\) through positive reals, and that for every \(\Delta > 0\), \(A(e^{-z}) = O(|z|^\beta e^{C/|z|})\) as \(z \to 0\) within the cone \(\{z \in \mathbb{C} : \mathrm{Re}\, z > 0,\ |\mathrm{Im}\, z| \le \Delta\, \mathrm{Re}\, z\}\). Then, as \(n \to \infty\),

    \[a(n) \sim \frac{\alpha}{2\sqrt{\pi}} C^{(2\beta + 1)/4} n^{-(2\beta + 3)/4} e^{2\sqrt{Cn}}.\]

    Nothing is assumed about the series on the circle \(|q| = 1\), since radius of convergence one permits either behavior there.
