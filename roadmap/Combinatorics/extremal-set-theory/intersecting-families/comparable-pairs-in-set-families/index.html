    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.ComparablePairsSetFamilyErdosProblem777Wanted.comparablePairs

    For \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and a family \(F\) of subsets of \(\{0, \dots, n - 1\}\), the number of comparable pairs of \(F\) is the number of ordered pairs \((A, B)\) of members of \(F\) with \(A \subsetneq B\). It is the number of edges of the comparability graph \(G_F\) on \(F\), in which two distinct members are adjacent when one contains the other.

    noncomputable def comparablePairs {n : ℕ} (F : Finset (Finset (Fin n))) : ℕ :=
  ((F ×ˢ F).filter fun p => p.1 ⊂ p.2).card

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.ComparablePairsSetFamilyErdosProblem777Wanted.fewComparablePairsBelowTwoHalfPower_holds

    For every real \(\varepsilon > 0\) there is \(N\) such that for every \(n \ge N\), every family \(F\) of subsets of \(\{0, \dots, n - 1\}\) with \(|F| \le (2 - \varepsilon) 2^{n/2}\) has fewer than \(2^n\) comparable pairs, that is, ordered pairs \((A, B)\) of members of \(F\) with \(A \subsetneq B\).

    The bound is sharp: for even \(n\), a family of \(m = 2^{n/2 + 1}\) subsets formed by two stacked subcubes has at least \(2^n\) comparable pairs. According to erdosproblems.com/777 the affirmative answer follows from Theorem 1.4 and Corollary 1.5 of Alon, Das, Glebov and Sudakov.

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.ComparablePairsSetFamilyErdosProblem777Wanted.denseComparabilityFamilySmall_refuted

    It is not the case that for every real \(c > 0\) there is a real constant \(C\) such that for every \(n\) and every family \(F\) of subsets of \(\{0, \dots, n - 1\}\) with at least \(c |F|^2\) comparable pairs (ordered pairs \((A, B)\) of members of \(F\) with \(A \subsetneq B\)), we have \(|F| \le C \cdot 2^{n/2}\). Equivalently, there is a real \(c > 0\) such that for every real \(C\) some \(n\) and some family \(F\) of subsets of \(\{0, \dots, n - 1\}\) with at least \(c |F|^2\) comparable pairs have \(|F| > C \cdot 2^{n/2}\).

    Alon and Frankl's Example 6.1 with \(d = 1\) gives such families: \(m \gg n 2^{n/2}\) subsets of an \(n\)-element set with at least \(2^{-5} \binom{m}{2}\) comparable pairs (erdosproblems.com/777).
