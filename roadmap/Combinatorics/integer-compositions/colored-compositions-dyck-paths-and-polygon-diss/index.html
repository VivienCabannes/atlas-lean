    MetaMathlibExt.coloredCompositionCount

    Let \(\gamma = (\gamma_1, \gamma_2, \dots)\) be a sequence of natural numbers and let \(n, k\) be natural numbers. The number \(c_{n,k}(\gamma)\) of \(\gamma\)-colored compositions of \(n\) with \(k\) parts counts the compositions of \(n\) with exactly \(k\) parts in which each part equal to \(j\) carries one of \(\gamma_j\) colors: it is the sum, over all compositions \((a_1, \dots, a_k)\) of \(n\) into \(k\) positive parts, of \(\gamma_{a_1} \cdots \gamma_{a_k}\).

    public def coloredCompositionCount (γ : ℕ → ℕ) (n k : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ∑ c : Composition n with c.length = k, (c.blocks.map γ).prod

    DefinitionDiagonals of a convex polygon#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.polygonDiagonals

    For \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), label the vertices of the convex \((n+2)\)-gon \(0, 1, \dots, n+1\) in cyclic order. Its diagonals are the pairs \((a, b)\) of natural numbers with \(a + 2 \le b \le n + 1\) and \((a, b) \ne (0, n+1)\); the excluded pair is the side joining the vertices \(0\) and \(n+1\).

    public def polygonDiagonals (n : ℕ) : Finset (ℕ × ℕ) :=
  (Finset.range (n + 2) ×ˢ Finset.range (n + 2)).filter fun e =>
    e.1 + 2 ≤ e.2 ∧ e ≠ (0, n + 1)

    DefinitionNoncrossing sets of diagonals#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsNoncrossingDiagonalSet

    A finite set \(D\) of pairs of natural numbers is noncrossing when there are no \((a, b) \in D\) and \((c, d) \in D\) with \(a < c < b < d\). For diagonals \((a, b)\) with \(a < b\) of a convex polygon whose vertices are labelled by natural numbers in cyclic order, this says that no two diagonals of \(D\) cross in the interior of the polygon.

    public def IsNoncrossingDiagonalSet (D : Finset (ℕ × ℕ)) : Prop :=
  ∀ e ∈ D, ∀ f ∈ D, ¬ (e.1 < f.1 ∧ f.1 < e.2 ∧ e.2 < f.2)

    DefinitionSize of the polygon under a chord#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.dissectionFaceSize

    Let \(D\) be a finite set of pairs of natural numbers and let \(e = (a, b)\) be a pair of natural numbers. The face size of \(e\) in \(D\) is \(2\) plus the number of natural numbers \(v\) with \(a < v < b\) for which there is no pair \((c, d) \in D\) other than \(e\) with \(a \le c < v < d \le b\). When \(D\) is a noncrossing set of diagonals of the convex \((n+2)\)-gon with vertices \(0, \dots, n+1\) in cyclic order and \(e\) is a diagonal in \(D\) or the root side \((0, n+1)\), this is the number of vertices of the polygon of the dissection that lies directly under \(e\), on the side of the vertices \(a, \dots, b\): the endpoints \(a\) and \(b\) and the vertices between them that no other chord of \(D\) under \(e\) cuts off. Every polygon of the dissection lies directly under exactly one such chord.

    public def dissectionFaceSize (D : Finset (ℕ × ℕ)) (e : ℕ × ℕ) : ℕ :=
  2 + ((Finset.Ioo e.1 e.2).filter fun v =>
    ∀ f ∈ D, f ≠ e → ¬ (e.1 ≤ f.1 ∧ f.1 < v ∧ v < f.2 ∧ f.2 ≤ e.2)).card

    DefinitionColored polygon dissection count#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.coloredPolygonDissectionCount

    Let \(\gamma : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) and \(n, k \in \mathbb{N}\). For \(n \ge 1\), label the vertices of the convex \((n+2)\)-gon \(0, \dots, n+1\) in cyclic order, with root side \((0, n+1)\). Then \(p_{n,k}(\gamma)\) is the sum, over all sets \(D\) of \(k - 1\) pairwise noncrossing diagonals of this polygon, of the product, over the root side and the diagonals \(e\) of \(D\), of \(\gamma(j_e)\), where \(j_e + 2\) is the number of vertices of the polygon of the dissection lying directly under \(e\). Every polygon of the dissection lies directly under exactly one of these chords, so \(p_{n,k}(\gamma)\) counts the partitions of the rooted \((n+2)\)-gon into \(k\) polygons by \(k - 1\) noncrossing diagonals in which each \((j+2)\)-gon is colored in one of \(\gamma(j)\) ways. For \(k = 0\) the sum is empty, so \(p_{n,0}(\gamma) = 0\). For \(n = 0\) (a degenerate \(2\)-gon) the count is set to \(p_{0,k}(\gamma) = 0\), so \(\gamma(0)\) never enters.

    public def coloredPolygonDissectionCount (γ : ℕ → ℕ) (n k : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  if n = 0 then 0 else
  ∑ D ∈ (polygonDiagonals n).powerset with IsNoncrossingDiagonalSet D ∧ D.card + 1 = k,
    ∏ e ∈ insert (0, n + 1) D, γ (dissectionFaceSize D e - 2)
    public def polygonDiagonals (n : ℕ) : Finset (ℕ × ℕ) :=
  (Finset.range (n + 2) ×ˢ Finset.range (n + 2)).filter fun e =>
    e.1 + 2 ≤ e.2 ∧ e ≠ (0, n + 1)
