    DefinitionNumber of fixed tilings#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.fixedTilingCount

    Let \(\alpha\) (the cells) and \(T\) (the tile designs) be finite sets, let \(\varphi : \alpha \to \alpha\) be a map and let \(\sigma\) be a permutation of \(T\). The number of tilings fixed by \((\varphi, \sigma)\) is the number of maps \(F : \alpha \to T\) such that \(F(\varphi(c)) = \sigma(F(c))\) for every \(c \in \alpha\).

    This is the paper's fixed-point condition \(F(c \cdot s) = F(c) \cdot g\), with \(\varphi\) encoding the action on cells and \(\sigma\) the action on designs. No ambient group action is assumed, since each fixed-point formula involves a single symmetry.

    public def fixedTilingCount {α T : Type*} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
    [Fintype T] [DecidableEq T] (φ : α → α) (σ : Equiv.Perm T) : ℕ :=
  Fintype.card {F : α → T // ∀ c, F (φ c) = σ (F c)}

    DefinitionTile designs fixed by a power of a permutation#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.tileFixedCount

    For a finite set \(T\) of tile designs, a permutation \(\sigma\) of \(T\) and a natural number \(k\), this is the number of designs \(d \in T\) with \(\sigma^k(d) = d\).

    DefinitionOrbit of a cell under a permutation#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.permOrbit

    Let \(\alpha\) be a finite set, \(\varphi\) a permutation of \(\alpha\) and \(c \in \alpha\). The orbit of \(c\) under \(\varphi\) is the finite set \(\{\varphi^k(c) : k \in \mathbb{N}\}\) of images of \(c\) under the nonnegative powers of \(\varphi\). Since \(\alpha\) is finite, this is the orbit of \(c\) under the cyclic group generated by \(\varphi\).

    A fixed point \(c\) of \(\varphi\) has the singleton orbit \(\{c\}\); these one-cycles are kept.

    public noncomputable def permOrbit {α : Type*} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
    (φ : Equiv.Perm α) (c : α) : Finset α := by
  classical
  exact Finset.univ.filter fun d => ∃ k : ℕ, (φ ^ k) c = d

    DefinitionOrbits of a permutation#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.permOrbits

    Let \(\alpha\) be a finite set and \(\varphi\) a permutation of \(\alpha\). For \(c \in \alpha\) let \(O(c) = \{\varphi^k(c) : k \in \mathbb{N}\}\) be the orbit of \(c\) under \(\varphi\). The set of orbits of \(\varphi\) is the set \(\{O(c) : c \in \alpha\}\) of subsets of \(\alpha\), in which each orbit occurs once.

    Fixed points of \(\varphi\) contribute singleton orbits, which the paper's Theorem 20 needs.

    public noncomputable def permOrbits {α : Type*} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
    (φ : Equiv.Perm α) : Finset (Finset α) := by
  classical
  exact Finset.univ.image (permOrbit φ)

    DefinitionCylinder shift sum#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.cylinderShiftSum

    Let \(T\) be a finite set of tile designs, let \(n, m \ge 1\), let \(g\) be a map from the cells \(\mathbb{Z}/n \times \mathbb{Z}/m\) to themselves, and let \(\sigma\) be a permutation of \(T\). The cylinder shift sum of \(g\) and \(\sigma\) is the sum, over all shifts \(a \in \mathbb{Z}/n\), of the number of tilings \(F : \mathbb{Z}/n \times \mathbb{Z}/m \to T\) with \(F(g(x + a, y)) = \sigma(F(x, y))\) for every cell \((x, y)\).

    The shift is applied before \(g\), following the paper's right-action convention, and \(\sigma\) is the action of \(g\) on tile designs.

  ∑ a : ZMod n,
    fixedTilingCount (fun c => cellG (c.1 + a, c.2)) σ

    TheoremFixed tilings of two cells under the identity#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.fixedTilingCount_fin_two_identity

    Let the set of cells and the set of tile designs both be the two-element set \(\{0, 1\}\). The number of maps \(F : \{0, 1\} \to \{0, 1\}\) with \(F(\varphi(c)) = \sigma(F(c))\) for every cell \(c\), where \(\varphi\) is the identity map on cells and \(\sigma\) is the identity permutation of designs, equals \(4\); that is, all \(2^2\) tilings are fixed.

    This is a sanity check of the definition of fixed tilings, not one of the paper's enumerative theorems.

    fixedTilingCount (α := Fin 2) (T := Fin 2) id 1 = 4
