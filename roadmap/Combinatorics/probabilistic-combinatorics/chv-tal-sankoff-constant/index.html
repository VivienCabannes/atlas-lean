    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.BinaryChvatalSankoffWanted.expectedLCSLength

    Let \(L_n\) be the length of a longest common subsequence of two independent uniformly random binary strings of length \(n\). The binary Chvátal–Sankoff constant is the real number \(\liminf_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{E}[L_n]/n\).
