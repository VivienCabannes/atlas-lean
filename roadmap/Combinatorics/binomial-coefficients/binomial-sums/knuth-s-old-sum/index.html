    DefinitionGeneralized binomial coefficient of complex arguments#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.gammaBinom

    For complex numbers \(u\) and \(v\), the generalized binomial coefficient is

    \[ \binom{u}{v} = \frac{\Gamma(u+1)}{\Gamma(v+1)\,\Gamma(u-v+1)}, \]

    where \(\Gamma\) is taken to be \(0\) at the non-positive integers (its poles) and division by \(0\) gives \(0\). In particular \(\binom{u}{v} = 0\) whenever \(v\) or \(u - v\) is a negative integer, and at natural arguments \(\binom{u}{v}\) is the ordinary binomial coefficient.

    public noncomputable def gammaBinom (u v : ℂ) : ℂ :=
  Complex.Gamma (u + 1) / (Complex.Gamma (v + 1) * Complex.Gamma (u - v + 1))
