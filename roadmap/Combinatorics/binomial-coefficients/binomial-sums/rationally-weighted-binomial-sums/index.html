    TheoremScherk's identity with \(s\)-rising factorials#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.sum_risingStep_mul_coeff_eq_sum_hsuShiue_derivative

    Let \(s\) be a real number and let \(S(n, k)\) be the generalized Stirling numbers of Hsu and Shiue with parameters \((-s, 1, 0)\): a real array with \(S(0, 0) = 1\), \(S(n, 0) = 0\) for \(n \ge 1\), \(S(n, k) = 0\) for \(n < k\), and \(S(n+1, k+1) = S(n, k) + (k + 1 + ns) \, S(n, k+1)\) for all \(n, k \ge 0\). Let \(N\) and \(r\) be natural numbers, let \(a_0, \dots, a_N\) be real numbers, and let \(f(y) = \sum_{k=0}^{N} a_k y^k\), with \(i\)-th derivative \(f^{(i)}\). Then for every real number \(x > 0\),

    \[\sum_{k=0}^{N} k (k + s) \cdots (k + (r-1)s) \, a_k x^{k + rs} = \sum_{i=0}^{r} S(r, i) \, x^{rs + i} f^{(i)}(x),\]

    where the product \(k (k + s) \cdots (k + (r-1)s)\) has the \(r\) factors \(k + js\), \(0 \le j < r\), and is \(1\) when \(r = 0\).

    The powers of \(x\) have real exponents, which is why \(x > 0\) is assumed.

    ∑ k ∈ Finset.range (N + 1),
        (∏ j ∈ Finset.range r, ((k : ℝ) + j * s)) * a k * x ^ ((k : ℝ) + r * s) =
      ∑ i ∈ Finset.range (r + 1), S r i * x ^ ((r : ℝ) * s + i) *

    TheoremRising-factorial weighted sums via iterated antiderivatives#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.sum_coeff_mul_pow_div_ascFactorial_eq_antideriv

    Let \(N\) and \(s\) be natural numbers, \(a_0, \dots, a_N\) real numbers and \(f(y) = \sum_{k=0}^{N} a_k y^k\). Let \(I_0, I_1, I_2, \dots\) be functions \(\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) with \(I_0 = f\) such that for every \(j \ge 0\) the function \(I_{j+1}\) is differentiable on \(\mathbb{R}\) with derivative \(I_j\) (the constants of integration are arbitrary).
