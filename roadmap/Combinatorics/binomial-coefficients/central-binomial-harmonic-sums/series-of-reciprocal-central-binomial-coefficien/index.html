    MetaMathlibExt.IsPolyTFamily

    Let \(S\) and \(T\) be sequences of real polynomials indexed by the natural numbers, with terms \(S(l)\) and \(T(l)\). The pair \((S, T)\) satisfies this predicate when \(T(0) = 0\); for every \(l \ge 1\) the polynomial \(T(l)\) is nonzero and has degree \(l - 1\); and for every \(l \ge 1\) and every real \(x\) with \(0 < x < 4\), the function \(y \mapsto 2\sqrt{y}\,T(l)(y)\) is differentiable at \(x\) with derivative

    \[\frac{8x^l - S(l)(x)}{\sqrt{x}\,(4 - x)}.\]

    The term \(T(l)\) stands for the polynomial \(T_{l-1}\) of the source, so \(T(0) = 0\) encodes the convention \(T_{-1} = 0\), and the derivative condition expresses \(T_{l-1}(x)\) as \(\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\) times an antiderivative of the displayed integrand.

  T 0 = 0 ∧
    (∀ l : ℕ, 1 ≤ l → (T l).natDegree = l - 1 ∧ T l ≠ 0) ∧
    ∀ l : ℕ, 1 ≤ l → ∀ x : ℝ, 0 < x → x < 4 →
      HasDerivAt (fun y : ℝ => 2 * Real.sqrt y * Polynomial.aeval y (T l))
        (TIntegrand S l x) x

    MetaMathlibExt.hasDerivAt_arcsinAntiderivP_pow_mul_centralBinomReciprocalH

    Let \(l \ge 1\), let \(Q_l\) and \(C_l\) be Zurita's polynomial and constant, and let \(H\) be the closed form of the reciprocal central binomial series.
