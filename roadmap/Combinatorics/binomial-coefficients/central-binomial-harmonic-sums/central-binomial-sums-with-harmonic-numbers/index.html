    Central binomial sums with harmonic numbers

    DefinitionWeighted central binomial sum#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.centralBinomPowSum

    For a sequence \(a = (a_k)_{k \ge 0}\) of rational numbers and natural numbers \(r\) and \(n\), the weighted central binomial sum is the rational number

    \[S_r^a(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{2k}{k} 4^{-k} k^r a_k,\]

    with the convention \(0^0 = 1\).

    Taking \(a_k = 1\), \(a_k = O_k\) (the odd harmonic numbers), \(a_k = H_k\) and \(a_k = H_{2k}\) (the harmonic numbers) gives the paper's sums \(S_r(n)\), \(S_r^O(n)\), \(S_r^H(n)\) and \(S_r^{2H}(n)\).

    public def centralBinomPowSum (a : ℕ → ℚ) (r n : ℕ) : ℚ :=
  ∑ k ∈ range (n + 1), (k.centralBinom : ℚ) / 4 ^ k * (k : ℚ) ^ r * a k
