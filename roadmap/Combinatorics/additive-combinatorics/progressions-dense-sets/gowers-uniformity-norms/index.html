    Gowers uniformity norms

    The Gowers norms \(\|f\|_{U^d}\) of a function on a finite abelian group measure how far \(f\) is from being random with respect to the structures of "degree \(d - 1\)": \(\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}\) is the average of \(f\) over \(d\)-dimensional cubes \((x + \omega \cdot h)_{\omega \in \{0,1\}^d}\), with complex conjugates on the odd vertices. Gowers introduced them in his proof of Szemerédi's theorem (2001), as the right notion of pseudorandomness for counting progressions of length \(k = d + 1\): by the generalized von Neumann theorem, \(k\)-term progressions are controlled by \(U^{k-1}\). For \(d = 2\) they reduce to Fourier analysis, \(\|f\|_{U^2} = \|\hat f\|_{\ell^4}\), and a large \(U^2\) norm means correlation with a character; for \(d \ge 3\) an inverse theorem must use polynomial phases, which is the subject of higher-order Fourier analysis.

    The definition follows Green and Tao's recursion \(\|f\|_{U^{d+1}}^{2^{d+1}} = \mathbb{E}_h \|\Delta_h f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}\) and is shown equal to the cube average. Averages are normalised expectations; Gowers's original sums differ by powers of \(|G|\).

    DefinitionMultiplicative derivative#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.mulDeriv

    For a finite abelian group \(G\), a shift \(h \in G\) and \(f : G \to \mathbb{C}\), the multiplicative derivative of \(f\) in direction \(h\) is $\(\Delta_h f(x) = f(x)\,\overline{f(x + h)}.\)$ For a phase \(f = e(\varphi)\) it is \(e(\varphi(x) - \varphi(x + h))\), a discrete derivative of the phase. This is Gowers's difference function \(\Delta(f; k)(s) = f(s)\overline{f(s - k)}\) with \(k = -h\), and Green and Tao's \(T^h f \cdot \overline{f}\) up to complex conjugation; neither change affects the Gowers norms built from it.

    def mulDeriv (h : G) (f : G → ℂ) : G → ℂ := fun x => f x * conj (f (x + h))

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.gowersPow

    For \(f : G \to \mathbb{C}\) on a finite abelian group, the quantity \(\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}\) is defined by the recursion $\(\|f\|_{U^0} = \mathbb{E}_x f(x), \qquad \|f\|_{U^{d+1}}^{2^{d+1}} = \mathbb{E}_{h \in G} \|\Delta_h f\|_{U^d}^{2^d},\)$ where \(\Delta_h\) is the multiplicative derivative. This is formula (1.1) of Green and Tao, who state it as equivalent to their Definition 1.6, the average over cubes \(\mathbb{E}_{x, h_1, \dots, h_d} \prod_{\omega \in \{0,1\}^d} C^{|\omega|} f(x + \omega \cdot h)\); the equivalence is proved here. For \(d \ge 1\) the value is a nonnegative real number; for \(d = 0\) it is the complex mean of \(f\).

    noncomputable def gowersPow : ℕ → (G → ℂ) → ℂ
  | 0, f => 𝔼 x, f x
  | d + 1, f => 𝔼 h, gowersPow d (mulDeriv h f)

    DefinitionGowers uniformity norm#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.gowersNorm

    \(\|f\|_{U^d} = |\,\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}|^{1/2^d}\), the \(2^d\)-th root of the modulus of the \(2^d\)-th power. For \(d \ge 1\) this is the \(U^d(G)\) norm of Green and Tao (Definition 1.6). Gowers's original \(\|f\|_d\) uses sums instead of averages, so it is \(|G|^{(d+1)/2^d}\) times this one. For \(d = 0\) the page's convention gives \(|\mathbb{E} f|\), the modulus of Green and Tao's complex number \(\|f\|_{U^0} = \mathbb{E} f\).

    \(U^1\) is only a seminorm, since \(\|f\|_{U^1} = |\mathbb{E} f|\); for \(d \ge 2\), \(U^d\) is a norm (Gowers, Lemma 3.9; triangle inequality). For \(f = e(\varphi)\) the norm measures how far the \(d\)-th derivative of the phase is from vanishing: it is \(1\) when \(\varphi\) is a polynomial of degree less than \(d\).

    noncomputable def gowersNorm (d : ℕ) (f : G → ℂ) : ℝ :=
  ‖gowersPow d f‖ ^ ((1 : ℝ) / 2 ^ d)

    TheoremGowers norms as cube averages#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.gowersPow_eq_cubeAverage

    For every \(d \ge 0\) and \(f : G \to \mathbb{C}\), $\(\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d} = \mathbb{E}_{x \in G,\ h \in G^d} \prod_{\omega \in \{0,1\}^d} C^{|\omega|} f(x + \omega \cdot h),\)$ where \(C\) is complex conjugation, \(|\omega|\) is the number of coordinates of \(\omega\) equal to \(1\) and \(\omega \cdot h = \sum_i \omega_i h_i\). This is Definition 1.6 of Green and Tao; with the recursive definition it proves their remark that (1.1) is an equivalent definition. The configurations \((x + \omega \cdot h)_\omega\) are the cubes of dimension \(d\), so \(\|1_A\|_{U^d}^{2^d}\) is the number of cubes in \(A\) divided by \(|G|^{d+1}\).

    theorem gowersPow_eq_cubeAverage (d : ℕ) (f : G → ℂ) :
    gowersPow d f =
      𝔼 x, 𝔼 h : Fin d → G, ∏ ω : Fin d → Bool,
        (if Even (univ.filter fun i => ω i = true).card
          then f (x + ∑ i, if ω i then h i else 0)
          else conj (f (x + ∑ i, if ω i then h i else 0)))
    noncomputable def gowersPow : ℕ → (G → ℂ) → ℂ
  | 0, f => 𝔼 x, f x
  | d + 1, f => 𝔼 h, gowersPow d (mulDeriv h f)

    DefinitionGowers inner product#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.gowersInner

    For functions \(F_\omega : G \to \mathbb{C}\) indexed by \(\omega \in \{0,1\}^d\), $\(\langle (F_\omega)_\omega \rangle_{U^d} = \mathbb{E}_{x \in G,\ h \in G^d} \prod_{\omega} C^{|\omega|} F_\omega(x + \omega \cdot h),\)$ the left-hand side of Gowers's Lemma 3.8, with averages in place of sums and \(+\) in place of \(-\) in the shifts. When every \(F_\omega\) equals \(f\) it is \(\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}\), by the cube formula. For \(d = 2\) it is the \(U^2\) inner product up to the indexing of \(\{0,1\}^2\).

    (if Even (univ.filter fun i => ω i = true).card
      then F ω (x + ∑ i, if ω i then h i else 0)

    TheoremCauchy–Schwarz step for Gowers powers#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.sq_norm_gowersPow_le_and_real

    For every \(d \ge 0\) and \(f : G \to \mathbb{C}\), the number \(\|f\|_{U^{d+1}}^{2^{d+1}}\) is real and $\(\bigl|\,\|f\|_{U^d}^{2^d}\bigr|^2 \le \|f\|_{U^{d+1}}^{2^{d+1}}.\)$ The proof is by induction on \(d\) from the recursion (1.1): \(|\mathbb{E}_h a_h|^2 \le \mathbb{E}_h |a_h|^2\) applied to \(a_h = \|\Delta_h f\|_{U^{d-1}}^{2^{d-1}}\).     TheoremMonotonicity of the Gowers norms#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.gowersNorm_le_gowersNorm_succ

    For every \(d \ge 0\) and \(f : G \to \mathbb{C}\), $\(\|f\|_{U^d} \le \|f\|_{U^{d+1}}.\)$ This is (1.2) of Green and Tao, an immediate consequence of the recursion (1.1). Gowers's Lemma 3.4 is the same statement in the language of uniformity: a function that is \(\alpha\)-uniform of degree \(d\) is \(\alpha^{1/2}\)-uniform of degree \(d - 1\).     TheoremGowers–Cauchy–Schwarz inequality#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.norm_gowersInner_le

    For every \(d \ge 0\) and functions \(F_\omega : G \to \mathbb{C}\) indexed by \(\omega \in \{0,1\}^d\), $\(|\langle (F_\omega)_\omega \rangle_{U^d}| \le \prod_{\omega} \|F_\omega\|_{U^d}.\)$ This is Lemma 3.8 of Gowers, stated on \(\mathbb{Z}_N\) with sums; both sides scale by the same power of \(N\), and the statement here holds on every finite abelian group. Gowers calls it little more than the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality. The proof here is by induction on \(d\): Cauchy–Schwarz in the first coordinate of the cube, the induction hypothesis for the derivatives \(\Delta_t F_\omega\), and Hölder's inequality over the \(2^d\) faces. For \(d = 0\) and \(d = 1\) both sides are equal.

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.gowersNorm_add_le

    For every \(d \ge 0\) and \(f, g : G \to \mathbb{C}\), $\(\|f + g\|_{U^d} \le \|f\|_{U^d} + \|g\|_{U^d}.\)$ This is Lemma 3.9 of Gowers, stated for \(d \ge 2\) on \(\mathbb{Z}_N\), where it shows that \(\|\cdot\|_d\) is a norm; for \(d = 0, 1\) it is the triangle inequality for \(|\mathbb{E} f|\).     TheoremGowers norms dominate the mean#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.GowersNorm.norm_expect_le_gowersNorm

    For every \(d \ge 1\) and \(f : G \to \mathbb{C}\), \(|\mathbb{E} f| \le \|f\|_{U^d}\), by monotonicity from \(\|f\|_{U^1} = |\mathbb{E} f|\).

The restriction \(k \ge 2\) avoids the degenerate \(U^0\).
