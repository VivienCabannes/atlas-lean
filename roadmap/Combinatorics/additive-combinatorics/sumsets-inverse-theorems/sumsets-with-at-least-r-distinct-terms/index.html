    DefinitionSums with at least \(r\) distinct terms#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.atLeastDistinctSumset

    Let \(G\) be an additive commutative monoid, \(A\) a finite subset of \(G\) and \(h, r\) natural numbers. The sumset \(h^{(\ge r)}A\) is the set of all sums of \(h\) elements of \(A\), repetitions allowed, in which at least \(r\) distinct elements occur, that is, the elements \(\sum_{a \in B} x_a a\) with \(B \subseteq A\), \(|B| \ge r\), positive integers \(x_a\) and \(\sum_{a \in B} x_a = h\).

    For \(r = 1\) and \(h \ge 1\) it is the \(h\)-fold sumset \(hA\); for \(r = h\) it is the restricted sumset \(h^{\wedge}A\) of sums of \(h\) distinct elements of \(A\).

    def atLeastDistinctSumset {G : Type*} [AddCommMonoid G] [DecidableEq G] (h r : ℕ)
    (A : Finset G) : Finset G :=
  ((A.sym h).filter fun s : Sym G h => r ≤ (s : Multiset G).toFinset.card).image
    fun s : Sym G h => (s : Multiset G).sum
