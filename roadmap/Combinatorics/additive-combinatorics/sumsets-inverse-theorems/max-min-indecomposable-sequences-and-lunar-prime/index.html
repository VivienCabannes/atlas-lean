    DefinitionMax-min product#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.maxMinMul

    The max-min product of sequences \(g, h \colon \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) is the sequence \(g \otimes h\) with \((g \otimes h)_k = \max_{i + j = k} \min(g_i, h_j)\) for every \(k \in \mathbb{N}\), the maximum running over pairs of natural numbers \((i, j)\). This is the product of the max-min semiring \((\mathbb{N}, \max, \min)\) on coefficient sequences.

    public def maxMinMul (g h : ℕ → ℕ) : ℕ → ℕ :=
  fun k => (Finset.antidiagonal k).sup fun p => min (g p.1) (h p.2)

    MetaMathlibExt.IsMaxMinIndecomposable

    A coefficient sequence \(f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) is indecomposable (in the arXiv version: irreducible) when for all sequences \(g, h : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) with \(g \otimes h = f\), where \(\otimes\) is the max-min product, \(g\) or \(h\) is a max-min monomial, that is, a sequence with exactly one nonzero term.

    Factors range over all sequences \(\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\); for a natural number \(b\) and \(f\) with all terms less than \(b\), this is equivalent to factoring over \(\{0, \dots, b - 1\}\), by applying the digit map \(x \mapsto \min(x, b - 1)\).

    public def IsMaxMinIndecomposable (f : ℕ → ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∀ g h : ℕ → ℕ, maxMinMul g h = f → IsMaxMinMonomial g ∨ IsMaxMinMonomial h

    DefinitionLunar unit#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lunarUnit

    For a natural number \(b\), the lunar unit of base \(b\) is the coefficient sequence \(\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) whose constant term is \(b - 1\) and whose other terms are all \(0\), that is, the constant polynomial \(b - 1\) over the max-min semiring. The subtraction is truncated, so the lunar unit of base \(0\) is the zero sequence.

    For sequences with all terms less than \(b\), the lunar unit is the identity of the max-min product.

    public def lunarUnit (b : ℕ) : ℕ → ℕ :=
  fun k => if k = 0 then b - 1 else 0
