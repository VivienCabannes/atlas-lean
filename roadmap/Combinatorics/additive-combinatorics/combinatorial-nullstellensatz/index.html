    Combinatorial Nullstellensatz

    If a polynomial \(f\) over a field has degree \(\sum_i t_i\) and the coefficient of \(\prod_i x_i^{t_i}\) is nonzero, then \(f\) does not vanish on any grid \(S_1 \times \dots \times S_n\) with \(|S_i| > t_i\). Alon derives this from a structural statement: a polynomial vanishing on the grid is a combination \(\sum_i h_i g_i\) of the polynomials \(g_i = \prod_{s \in S_i}(x_i - s)\) with controlled degrees. Choosing \(f\) to encode a combinatorial configuration gives short proofs of the Chevalley–Warning theorem, the Cauchy–Davenport theorem, the Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture on sums of distinct elements, the Erdős–Ginzburg–Ziv theorem (through the permanent lemma), results on regular subgraphs and on covering the cube by hyperplanes, and the Alon–Tarsi theorem on list colourings. The presentation follows N. Alon, "Combinatorial Nullstellensatz", Combin. Probab. Comput. 8 (1999) 7–29.

    Mathlib proves both forms of the Nullstellensatz (MvPolynomial.combinatorial_nullstellensatz_exists_linearCombination and MvPolynomial.combinatorial_nullstellensatz_exists_eval_nonzero), the Cauchy–Davenport theorem for \(\mathbb{Z}/p\) (ZMod.cauchy_davenport, the paper's Theorem 3.2), the Chevalley–Warning theorem in its divisibility form (char_dvd_card_solutions_of_sum_lt) and the Erdős–Ginzburg–Ziv theorem for every modulus (ZMod.erdos_ginzburg_ziv, the paper's Proposition 8.2 for primes). The pages below restate the Nullstellensatz in the paper's notation, derive the paper's form of Chevalley–Warning, and prove, by the paper's polynomial arguments, the Erdős–Heilbronn bound (for the existing restricted sumset \(2^{\wedge}A\), MetaMathlibExt.restrictedSumset 2) and the Alon–Füredi theorem on covering the cube minus one vertex by hyperplanes.

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.CombinatorialNullstellensatz.exists_eq_sum_mul_of_vanishing

    Let F be a field, f a polynomial in the variables xᵢ (i ∈ σ, finitely many), Sᵢ nonempty finite subsets of F, and gᵢ = ∏_{s ∈ Sᵢ} (xᵢ - s). If f vanishes at every point of ∏ᵢ Sᵢ, then f = ∑ᵢ hᵢ gᵢ for polynomials hᵢ with deg hᵢ ≤ deg f - deg gᵢ (N. Alon, "Combinatorial Nullstellensatz", Combin. Probab. Comput. 8 (1999), Theorem 1.1). The degree bound is stated as deg hᵢ + deg gᵢ ≤ deg f, which is the same for hᵢ ≠ 0 and avoids truncated subtraction; the paper's "moreover" clause about subrings is not included.

    This restates Mathlib's MvPolynomial.combinatorial_nullstellensatz_exists_linearCombination in the paper's notation.

    theorem exists_eq_sum_mul_of_vanishing (S : σ → Finset F) (hS : ∀ i, (S i).Nonempty)
    (f : MvPolynomial σ F) (hf : ∀ x : σ → F, (∀ i, x i ∈ S i) → eval x f = 0) :
    ∃ h : σ → MvPolynomial σ F,
      (∀ i, h i = 0 ∨ (h i).totalDegree + (∏ s ∈ S i, (X i - C s)).totalDegree ≤
        f.totalDegree) ∧
      f = ∑ i, h i * ∏ s ∈ S i, (X i - C s)

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.CombinatorialNullstellensatz.exists_eval_ne_zero

    Let F be a field and f a polynomial in finitely many variables xᵢ (i ∈ σ) of total degree ∑ᵢ tᵢ, where the coefficient of the monomial ∏ᵢ xᵢ^{tᵢ} in f is nonzero.     Let K be a finite field and f_j (j ∈ s) polynomials in finitely many variables xᵢ (i ∈ σ) whose total degrees sum to less than the number of variables.     TheoremErdős–Heilbronn conjecture (Dias da Silva–Hamidoune theorem)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.ErdosHeilbronn.min_le_card_restrictedSumset

    If \(p\) is a prime and \(A\) a nonempty subset of \(\mathbb{Z}_p\), then the restricted sumset \(2^{\wedge}A = \{a + a' : a, a' \in A,\ a \ne a'\}\) satisfies $\(|2^{\wedge}A| \ge \min(p, 2|A| - 3).\)$ Natural-number subtraction makes the bound \(0\) when \(|A| = 1\), where the paper's \(2|A| - 3\) is negative. The bound is attained by arithmetic progressions; the test module checks \(A = \{0, 1, 2\} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}_7\), whose restricted sums are \(\{1, 2, 3\}\).

    The proof is the paper's. After passing to a subset one may assume \(2|A| - 3 \le p\). If the restricted sumset had at most \(m = 2|A| - 4\) elements, it would lie in a set \(C\) of exactly \(m\) elements, and \(Q = (x_1 - x_0) \prod_{c \in C} (x_0 + x_1 - c)\) would vanish on \(A \times (A \setminus \{a\})\). The coefficient of \(x_0^{|A|-1} x_1^{|A|-2}\) in \(Q\) is \(\binom{m}{|A|-1} - \binom{m}{|A|-2} = -\mathrm{Cat}(|A| - 2)\), a Catalan number up to sign, which \(p\) does not divide because \(m < p\), so the Combinatorial Nullstellensatz gives a point where \(Q \ne 0\).

    theorem min_le_card_restrictedSumset {p : ℕ} (hp : p.Prime) {A : Finset (ZMod p)}
    (hA : A.Nonempty) : min p (2 * A.card - 3) ≤ (MetaMathlibExt.restrictedSumset 2 A).card
    public def restrictedSumset {G : Type*} [AddCommMonoid G] [DecidableEq G] (h : ℕ)
    (A : Finset G) : Finset G :=
  (A.powersetCard h).image (fun B => ∑ a ∈ B, a)

    TheoremCovering the cube minus a vertex by hyperplanes (Alon–Füredi)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Additive.AlonFurediCover.le_card_of_covers

    Let \(F\) be a field and \(H_i = \{x \in F^n : \sum_j a_{ij} x_j = b_i\}\), \(i < m\), finitely many affine hyperplanes. If every vertex of the cube \(\{0, 1\}^n\) other than one vertex \(v\) lies on some \(H_i\), and \(v\) lies on none, then \(m \ge n\). The bound is tight: the \(n\) hyperplanes \(x_j = 1\) cover every vertex except \(0\) (the test module checks these hypotheses for every \(n\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\)).

    The paper states the theorem in \(\mathbb{R}^n\); the proof works over any field. The coefficient vectors \(a_i\) are not required to be nonzero: a degenerate "hyperplane" \(0 = b_i\) with \(b_i \ne 0\) is empty and does not help.

    The proof is the paper's: with \(\delta\) chosen so that \(P = \prod_i (a_i \cdot x - b_i) - \delta \prod_j (x_j - (1 - v_j))\) vanishes at \(v\), the polynomial \(P\) vanishes on the whole cube, while for \(m < n\) its coefficient of \(x_1 \cdots x_n\) is \(-\delta \ne 0\) and its degree is \(n\).

    theorem le_card_of_covers {m : ℕ} (a : Fin m → Fin n → F) (b : Fin m → F) (v : Fin n → F)
    (hv : ∀ j, v j = 0 ∨ v j = 1)
    (hcover : ∀ x : Fin n → F, (∀ j, x j = 0 ∨ x j = 1) → x ≠ v → ∃ i, ∑ j, a i j * x j = b i)
    (hmiss : ∀ i, ∑ j, a i j * v j ≠ b i) : n ≤ m
