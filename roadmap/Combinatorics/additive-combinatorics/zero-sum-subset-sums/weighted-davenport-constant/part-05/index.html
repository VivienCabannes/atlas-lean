    TheoremThree-term sums with prescribed square classes cover \(\mathbb{Z}_{p^r}\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.unit_square_class_three_sumset_eq_univ

    Let \(p \ge 7\) be a prime, \(r \ge 1\) and \(n = p^r\); write \(\mathbb{Z}_n = \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\), \(U(n)\) for its group of units, \(A_1 = U(n)^2\) for the set of squares of units and \(A_2 = U(n) \setminus U(n)^2\). For all units \(x_1, x_2, x_3\) of \(\mathbb{Z}_n\) and every \(f : \{1, 2, 3\} \to \{1, 2\}\), \(A_{f(1)} x_1 + A_{f(2)} x_2 + A_{f(3)} x_3 = \mathbb{Z}_n\): every \(z \in \mathbb{Z}_n\) equals \(a_1 x_1 + a_2 x_2 + a_3 x_3\) for some \(a_1 \in A_{f(1)}\), \(a_2 \in A_{f(2)}\), \(a_3 \in A_{f(3)}\).

    The hypothesis \(r \ge 1\) is needed: for \(r = 0\), \(\mathbb{Z}_1 = \{0\}\) has \(0 = 0^2\) as its only unit, so \(A_2\) is empty.

    public theorem_wanted unit_square_class_three_sumset_eq_univ (p r : ℕ) (hp : p.Prime)
    (h7 : 7 ≤ p) (hr : 0 < r) (x : Fin 3 → ZMod (p ^ r)) (hx : ∀ i, IsUnit (x i))
    (f : Fin 3 → Bool) (z : ZMod (p ^ r)) :
    ∃ a : Fin 3 → ZMod (p ^ r),
      (∀ i, IsUnit (a i) ∧ (a i ∈ unitSqSet (p ^ r) ↔ f i = true)) ∧ ∑ i, a i * x i = z

    TheoremThree unit terms force a square-weighted zero sum modulo \(p^r\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.unit_square_weighted_zero_sum_prime_power

    Let \(p \ge 7\) be a prime, \(r\) a natural number and \(n = p^r\), and let \(U(n)^2\) be the set of squares of units of \(\mathbb{Z}_n = \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\).     TheoremDavenport constant for the weights of Jacobi symbol one#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.jacobi_davenport_constant

    For a natural number \(n\) and \(A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\), let \(D_A(n)\) denote the least positive integer \(k\) such that every sequence \((x_1, \dots, x_k)\) in \(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\) has a nonempty set \(J\) of indices and weights \(a_i \in A\) for \(i \in J\) with \(\sum_{i \in J} a_i x_i = 0\). Let \(n\) be an odd squarefree natural number and let \(S(n)\) be the set of units \(x\) of \(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\) with Jacobi symbol \(\left(\frac{x}{n}\right) = 1\). If \(n\) is prime, then \(D_{S(n)}(n) = 3\). If \(n\) is not prime and every prime divisor of \(n\) is at least \(7\), then \(D_{S(n)}(n) = \Omega(n) + 1\), where \(\Omega(n)\) is the number of prime factors of \(n\) counted with multiplicity.

    public theorem_wanted jacobi_davenport_constant (n : ℕ) (hn : Odd n) (hsf : Squarefree n) :
    (n.Prime → IsLeast (davenportSet n (jacobiOneSet n)) 3) ∧
    (¬ n.Prime → (∀ p ∈ n.primeFactors, 7 ≤ p) →
      IsLeast (davenportSet n (jacobiOneSet n)) (Ω n + 1))

    MetaMathlibExt.jacobi_consecutive_constant

    For a natural number \(n\) and \(A \subseteq \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\), let \(C_A(n)\) denote the least positive integer \(k\) such that every sequence \((x_1, \dots, x_k)\) in \(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}\) has a block of consecutive terms \(x_s, \dots, x_t\) with \(s \le t\) and weights \(a_s, \dots, a_t \in A\) such that \(a_s x_s + \dots + a_t x_t = 0\).     TheoremLifting the Jacobi kernel into the Jacobi agreement set#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.jacobi_kernel_subset_image_jacobi_eq_legendre

    Let \(n\) be an odd natural number and let \(p\), \(p'\) be prime divisors of \(n\) with \(p\) coprime to \(n' = n/p\). For a residue \(x\) modulo \(N\) and an odd \(m\), write \((x/m)\) for the Jacobi symbol \((a/m)\) of the representative \(0 \le a < N\) of \(x\).
