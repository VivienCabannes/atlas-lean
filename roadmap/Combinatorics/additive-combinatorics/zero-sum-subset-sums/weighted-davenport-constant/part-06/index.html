    TheoremA block of consecutive coordinates orthogonal to \(a\) vectors of \(\mathbb{Z}_2^m\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.exists_consecutive_orthogonal

    Let \(\mathbb{Z}_2 = \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}\), let \(2^a \le m\) and let \(v_1, \dots, v_a \in \mathbb{Z}_2^m\), with coordinates indexed by \(\{0, \dots, m - 1\}\). Then there are indices \(s \le t\) in \(\{0, \dots, m - 1\}\) such that \(\sum_{j = s}^{t} (v_i)_j = 0\) in \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) for every \(i\); that is, the nonzero vector whose ones occupy exactly the positions \(s, \dots, t\) is orthogonal to every \(v_i\).

    public theorem_wanted exists_consecutive_orthogonal {a m : ℕ} (hm : 2 ^ a ≤ m)
    (v : Fin a → Fin m → ZMod 2) :
    ∃ s t : Fin m, s ≤ t ∧ ∀ i, ∑ j ∈ Finset.Icc s t, v i j = 0

    TheoremHalving consecutive unit-weighted zero-sum free sequences modulo a power of two#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.cExtremal_iff_halves

    Let \(s \ge 0\), \(n = 2^{s+1}\) and \(n' = 2^s\). Say that a sequence \(y_1, \dots, y_L\) in \(\mathbb{Z}_m\) has no consecutive unit-weighted zero-sum if there are no indices \(1 \le i \le j \le L\) and units \(a_i, \dots, a_j\) of \(\mathbb{Z}_m\) with \(a_i y_i + \dots + a_j y_j = 0\). For a sequence \(x_1, \dots, x_{n-1}\) in \(\mathbb{Z}_n\), let \(r_l \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}\) be the representative of \(x_l\) and let \(\bar x_l \in \mathbb{Z}_{n'}\) be the residue of \(\lfloor r_l / 2 \rfloor\). Then \(x_1, \dots, x_{n-1}\) has no consecutive unit-weighted zero-sum if and only if \(r_l\) is even for every \(l \ne n'\), \(r_{n'}\) is odd, and neither of the sequences \(\bar x_1, \dots, \bar x_{n'-1}\) and \(\bar x_{n'+1}, \dots, \bar x_{n-1}\) in \(\mathbb{Z}_{n'}\) has a consecutive unit-weighted zero-sum.
