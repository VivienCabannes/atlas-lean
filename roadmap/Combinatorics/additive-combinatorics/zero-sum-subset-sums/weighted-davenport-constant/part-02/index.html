    Let \(n \ge 2\) and let \(S(n)\) be the set of nonzero squares in \(\mathbb{Z}_n\). The sequence over \(\mathbb{Z}_n\) whose only term is \(0\) has an \(S(n)\)-weighted zero sum: there is a weight \(a \in S(n)\) with \(a \cdot 0 = 0\).

    MetaMathlibExt.squareSet_primePower_decomposition

    Let \(p\) be a prime, let \(r \ge 1\), and let \(l = \lfloor (r - 1)/2 \rfloor\). Write \(S(p^r)\) for the set of nonzero squares in \(\mathbb{Z}_{p^r}\), \(U(p^r)^2\) for the set of squares of units in \(\mathbb{Z}_{p^r}\), and \(p^{2k} U(p^r)^2 = \{p^{2k} x : x \in U(p^r)^2\}\). Then

    \[S(p^r) = \bigcup_{k=0}^{l} p^{2k} U(p^r)^2,\]

    public theorem_wanted squareSet_primePower_decomposition (p r : ℕ) (hp : p.Prime) (hr : 0 < r) :

    squareSet (p ^ r) = ⋃ k, pieces k ∧ Set.PairwiseDisjoint Set.univ pieces

    MetaMathlibExt.squareSet_constants_le_unitSquares_modSquare

    Square-weighted constants modulo \(p^r\), for \(p \ge 2\) and nonzero even \(r\), are at most unit-square constants modulo \(p^2\). Let \(p \ge 2\) be a natural number and let \(r\) be a nonzero even natural number.
