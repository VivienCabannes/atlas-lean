    DefinitionZero-based preferences of a word#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.stirlingPref

    Let \(w = w_1 \cdots w_{2n}\) be a word of natural numbers, read as the preferences of \(2n\) cars for spots numbered \(0, \dots, 2n - 1\). Car \(i \in \{1, \dots, 2n\}\) prefers the spot \((w_i - 1) \bmod 2n\), where \(w_i - 1\) is truncated at \(0\). When every \(w_i\) lies in \(\{1, \dots, 2n\}\), for example when \(w\) is a Stirling permutation of order \(n\), this is the spot \(w_i - 1\), which is spot \(w_i\) in the one-based numbering.

    public def stirlingPref {n : ℕ} (w : Fin (2 * n) → ℕ) (i : Fin (2 * n)) : Fin (2 * n) :=
  ⟨(w i - 1) % (2 * n), Nat.mod_lt _ i.pos⟩

    DefinitionParking spot of a car#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.stirlingParkSpot

    Let \(w = w_1 \cdots w_{2n}\) be a word of natural numbers. Car \(i\) prefers spot \(q_i = ((w_i - 1) \bmod 2n) + 1\), where \(w_i - 1\) is truncated at \(0\); when every \(w_i\) lies in \(\{1, \dots, 2n\}\), this is \(q_i = w_i\). Cars \(1, \dots, 2n\) arrive in order, and car \(i\) parks in the least spot \(p(i) \in \{q_i, \dots, 2n\}\) not taken by an earlier car, or in \(p(i) = q_i\) if there is no such spot. The parking spot of car \(i \in \{1, \dots, 2n\}\) is \(p(i)\).

    public def stirlingParkSpot {n : ℕ} (w : Fin (2 * n) → ℕ) (i : Fin (2 * n)) : ℕ :=
  (parkSpots (stirlingPref w) i).val + 1
    def parkSpots {n : ℕ} (f : Fin n → Fin n) : Fin n → Fin n :=
  fun i => (parkSpotsFrom (List.ofFn f) ∅).getD i.val i
