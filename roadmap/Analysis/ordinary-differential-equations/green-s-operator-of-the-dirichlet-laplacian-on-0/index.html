    MathlibExt.Analysis.ODE.GreensOperator.greensOperatorSqrt

    Let \(L^2([0, 1])\) be the real Hilbert space of square-integrable functions on \([0, 1]\), and let \((b_k)_{k \ge 1}\) be a Hilbert basis of \(L^2([0, 1])\) indexed by the positive integers with \(b_k(x) = \sqrt{2} \sin(k\pi x)\) for almost every \(x \in [0, 1]\).
