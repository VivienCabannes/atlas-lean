    TheoremChange of variables to spherical coordinates#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.MeasureTheory.Geometry.SphericalCoordinatesWanted.integral_comp_spherical_symm

    Let \(E\) be a real Banach space and let \(f \colon \mathbb{R}^3 \to E\) be any function. Then the integral of \(f\) over \(\mathbb{R}^3\) equals the integral over the set of \((r, \theta, \varphi) \in \mathbb{R}^3\) with \(r > 0\), \(0 < \theta < 2\pi\) and \(0 < \varphi < \pi\) of \(r^2 \sin\varphi \cdot f(r \sin\varphi \cos\theta,\, r \sin\varphi \sin\theta,\, r \cos\varphi)\), both integrals being Bochner integrals with respect to Lebesgue measure.

    No integrability hypothesis is assumed; as usual for Bochner integrals, a non-integrable function has integral \(0\). This is the three-dimensional analogue of the polar-coordinate change of variables.

    TheoremGreen's theorem#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.greens_theorem

    Let \(U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2\) be open, let \(D \subseteq U\) be bounded and measurable, and let \(L, M : \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) be continuously differentiable on \(U\). Let \(a\lt b\) and let \(\gamma=(\gamma_1,\gamma_2) : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^2\) be continuous on \([a,b]\), with \(\gamma(a)=\gamma(b)\), injective on \([a,b)\), with \(\gamma(t)\ne\gamma(a)\) for \(a\lt t\lt b\), with image \(\gamma([a,b])\) equal to the boundary of \(D\), and with \(\gamma(t)\in U\) for all \(t\in[a,b]\). Suppose there is a finite set \(S\subseteq[a,b]\) containing \(a\) and \(b\) such that \(\gamma\) is continuously differentiable on \([a,b]\setminus S\) and on \([u,v]\) for any two consecutive points \(u\lt v\) of \(S\), and such that for every \(t\in(a,b)\setminus S\) we have \(\gamma'(t)\ne0\) and, for some \(\delta>0\), \(\gamma(t)+\varepsilon(-\gamma_2'(t),\gamma_1'(t))\in D\) for all \(0\lt \varepsilon\lt \delta\) (so \(D\) lies to the left of the curve). Then

    \[ \int_a^b \bigl(L(\gamma(t))\,\gamma_1'(t)+M(\gamma(t))\,\gamma_2'(t)\bigr)\,dt =\int_D \Bigl(\frac{\partial M}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial L}{\partial y}\Bigr)\,d(x,y). \]

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Calculus.TaylorLittleoRemainderWanted.taylor_isLittleO_multivariable

    Let \(E\) and \(F\) be normed vector spaces over \(\mathbb{R}\), let \(n\) be a natural number, and let \(f : E \to F\) be \(n\) times continuously differentiable on a neighbourhood of a point \(x_0 \in E\). Write \(D^k f(x_0)\) for the \(k\)-th Fréchet derivative of \(f\) at \(x_0\), a continuous \(k\)-linear map from \(E^k\) to \(F\). Then, as \(x\) tends to \(x_0\),

    \[f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{1}{k!}\, D^k f(x_0)(x - x_0, \ldots, x - x_0) = o\bigl(\|x - x_0\|^n\bigr).\]
