    Submodule.topologicalAnnihilator

    Let \(E\) be a normed space over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\), with continuous dual \(E^*\). The topological annihilator of a subspace \(S\) of \(E\) is the subspace of \(E^*\) consisting of the continuous linear functionals \(f\) with \(f(x) = 0\) for all \(x \in S\).

    def topologicalAnnihilator (S : Submodule 𝕜 E) : Submodule 𝕜 (E →L[𝕜] 𝕜) where
