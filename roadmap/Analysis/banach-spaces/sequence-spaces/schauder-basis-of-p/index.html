    DefinitionThe unit vectors form an unconditional Schauder basis of ℓᵖ#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Normed.Lp.SchauderBasis.lpUnconditionalSchauderBasis

    Let \(\mathbb{K}\) be \(\mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\), let \(1 \le p < \infty\) and let \(\iota\) be a type. This is the unconditional Schauder basis of \(\ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})\) whose \(i\)-th basis vector is the unit vector \(e_i\), equal to \(1\) at \(i\) and to \(0\) elsewhere, and whose \(i\)-th coordinate functional is the evaluation \(x \mapsto x_i\). In particular every \(x \in \ell^p(\iota, \mathbb{K})\) is the limit in norm of the finite partial sums \(\sum_{i \in A} x_i e_i\) along the finite sets \(A \subseteq \iota\) directed by inclusion.
