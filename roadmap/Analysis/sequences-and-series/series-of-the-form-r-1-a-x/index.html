    For every real number \(x\) with \(|x| > 1\), we have \(x^k - 1 \ne 0\) and \(x^{k^2}(x^{2k} - 1) \ne 0\) for every integer \(k \ge 1\), the series \(\sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k+1}/(x^k - 1)\) and \(\sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k+1}(x^{2k} + 1)/(x^{k^2}(x^{2k} - 1))\) are summable, and \(\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{k+1}/(x^k - 1) = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{k+1}(x^{2k} + 1)/(x^{k^2}(x^{2k} - 1))\).

    theorem ramanujan_part1_ch6_entry13_ramanujanfactorial (x : ℝ) (hx : 1 < |x|) :
    let leftTerm := fun j : ℕ =>
      (-1 : ℝ) ^ (j + 2) / (x ^ (j + 1) - 1)
    let rightTerm := fun j : ℕ =>
      ((-1 : ℝ) ^ (j + 2) * (x ^ (2 * (j + 1)) + 1)) /
        (x ^ ((j + 1) ^ 2) * (x ^ (2 * (j + 1)) - 1))
    (∀ j : ℕ, x ^ (j + 1) - 1 ≠ 0) ∧
      (∀ j : ℕ,
        x ^ ((j + 1) ^ 2) * (x ^ (2 * (j + 1)) - 1) ≠ 0) ∧
      Summable leftTerm ∧ Summable rightTerm ∧
      ∑' j, leftTerm j = ∑' j, rightTerm j

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Ramanujan.Part1Ch6.Entry16FiniteSum.ramanujan_part1_ch6_entry16_finite_sum_general

    Let \(K\) be a field, let \(a, r, x \in K\) and let \(n \ge 0\) be an integer. Suppose that \(1 - a x^k \ne 0\) and \(1 - r x^{k-1} \ne 0\) for every integer \(k\) with \(1 \le k \le n\). Then \(\sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{r^k}{1 - a x^k} = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{(a r x^k)^k}{1 - a x^k} + \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{a^{k-1} \left( (r x^{k-1})^k - (r x^{k-1})^{n+1} \right)}{1 - r x^{k-1}}\). For \(n = 0\) all three sums are empty.

    theorem ramanujan_part1_ch6_entry16_finite_sum_general
    {K : Type*} [Field K] (a r x : K) (n : ℕ)
    (hax : ∀ k ∈ Icc 1 n, 1 - a * x ^ k ≠ 0)
    (hrx : ∀ k ∈ Icc 1 n, 1 - r * x ^ (k - 1) ≠ 0) :
    ∑ k ∈ Icc 1 n, r ^ k / (1 - a * x ^ k) =
      (∑ k ∈ Icc 1 n,
        (a * r * x ^ k) ^ k / (1 - a * x ^ k)) +
      ∑ k ∈ Icc 1 n,
        a ^ (k - 1) *
          ((r * x ^ (k - 1)) ^ k - (r * x ^ (k - 1)) ^ (n + 1)) /
            (1 - r * x ^ (k - 1))

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Ramanujan.Part1Ch6.Entry17Stirlingcoeffs.ramanujan_part1_ch6_entry17_stirlingcoeffs

    Let \(a, b, m, \nu, x\) be real numbers with \(|x| \le 1\), \(|\nu| < 1\) and \(m \ne 0\), such that \(|m \nu| < 1\) if \(|x| = 1\), and such that \(1 - m x^k \ne 0\) for every integer \(k \ge 0\) (here \(x^0 = 1\)). Then \(1 - \nu x^k \ne 0\) for every \(k \ge 0\), the series \(L = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(a + kb) \, \nu^k}{1 - m x^k}\), \(F = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(a + kb) (1 - m \nu x^{2k}) (m \nu x^k)^k}{(1 - m x^k)(1 - \nu x^k)}\) and \(G = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{(m \nu x^k)^{k+1}}{(1 - \nu x^k)^2}\) are summable, and \(L = F + \frac{b}{m} G\).
