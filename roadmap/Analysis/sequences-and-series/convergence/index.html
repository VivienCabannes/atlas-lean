    TheoremStolz–Cesàro theorem#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.stolz_cesaro

    Let \((a_n)_{n \ge 0}\) and \((b_n)_{n \ge 0}\) be sequences of real numbers such that \((b_n)\) is strictly increasing and \(b_n \to +\infty\) as \(n \to \infty\), and let \(L\) be a real number. If \(\frac{a_{n+1} - a_n}{b_{n+1} - b_n} \to L\) as \(n \to \infty\), then \(\frac{a_n}{b_n} \to L\) as \(n \to \infty\).

    theorem stolz_cesaro {a b : ℕ → ℝ} {L : ℝ}
    (hb_mono : StrictMono b)
    (hb_unbounded : Filter.Tendsto b Filter.atTop Filter.atTop)
    (hlim : Filter.Tendsto (fun n => (a (n + 1) - a n) / (b (n + 1) - b n))
      Filter.atTop (nhds L)) :
    Filter.Tendsto (fun n => a n / b n) Filter.atTop (nhds L)

    TheoremSummability of a rising-product series#✓fully proved

    Complex.summable_neg_pow_mul_pow_div_prod_add_mul

    Let \(b\), \(c\) and \(x\) be complex numbers such that \(c + kb \neq 0\) for every integer \(k \ge 1\). Then the series \(\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-b)^j x^{j+1}}{\prod_{k=1}^{j+1} (c + kb)}\) is summable.

    TheoremŚleszyński–Pringsheim convergence criterion#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.contFract_tendsto_of_pringsheim

    Let \(a_0, a_1, a_2, \dots\) and \(b_0, b_1, b_2, \dots\) be complex numbers with \(1 + |a_k| \le |b_k|\) for every \(k\). For \(n \ge 0\) let \(f_n\) be the \(n\)-th approximant of the continued fraction \(a_0 / (b_0 + a_1 / (b_1 + \cdots))\), that is \(f_0 = 0\) and \(f_n = a_0 / (b_0 + a_1 / (b_1 + \cdots + a_{n-1} / b_{n-1}))\) for \(n \ge 1\). Then there is a complex number \(v\) with \(|v| \le 1\) such that \(f_n \to v\) as \(n \to \infty\).

    Zero partial numerators are admitted.
