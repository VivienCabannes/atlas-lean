    TheoremParametric two-adic multisection#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.tsum_twoAdic_parametric_multisection

    Let \(q \in \mathbb{C}\) and let \(b : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}\) be a sequence (its value at \(0\) is not used). Suppose that the families \((q^j b_{(2n-1) 2^j})\) and \((q^j (q - 1) b_{2n \cdot 2^j})\), indexed by the pairs \((j, n)\) of natural numbers with \(n \ge 1\), are summable. Then

    \[\sum_{j \ge 0,\ n \ge 1} \left(q^j b_{(2n-1) 2^j} - q^j (q - 1) b_{2n \cdot 2^j}\right) = \sum_{m \ge 1} b_m.\]

    The two summability hypotheses imply that \((b_m)_{m \ge 1}\) is summable.

    public theorem_wanted tsum_twoAdic_parametric_multisection (q : ℂ) (b : ℕ → ℂ)
    (hodd : Summable fun x : ℕ × ℕ => q ^ x.1 * b ((2 * x.2 + 1) * 2 ^ x.1))
    (heven : Summable fun x : ℕ × ℕ => q ^ x.1 * (q - 1) * b ((2 * x.2 + 2) * 2 ^ x.1)) :
    ∑' x : ℕ × ℕ, (q ^ x.1 * b ((2 * x.2 + 1) * 2 ^ x.1)
        - q ^ x.1 * (q - 1) * b ((2 * x.2 + 2) * 2 ^ x.1))
      = ∑' m : ℕ, b (m + 1)

    TheoremTruncated two-adic regrouping#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.tsum_twoAdic_truncated_regroup

    For every natural number \(J\) and all sequences \(\varphi, c : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{C}\),

    \[\sum_{j \ge J,\ n \ge 1} \varphi(j) c_{(2n-1) 2^j} = \sum_{p \ge 1} \varphi(J + \nu_2(p)) c_{p 2^J},\]

    where \(\nu_2(p)\) is the exponent of the largest power of \(2\) dividing \(p\), the left side runs over the pairs \((j, n)\) of natural numbers with \(j \ge J\) and \(n \ge 1\), the right side runs over the positive integers \(p\), and each side is an unconditional sum, taken to be \(0\) when its family is not summable.

    public theorem_wanted tsum_twoAdic_truncated_regroup (J : ℕ) (φ c : ℕ → ℂ) :
    ∑' x : ℕ × ℕ, φ (x.1 + J) * c ((2 * x.2 + 1) * 2 ^ (x.1 + J))
      = ∑' p : ℕ, φ (J + padicValNat 2 (p + 1)) * c ((p + 1) * 2 ^ J)

    TheoremRegrouping the multiples of a power of 2 by their 2-adic factorization#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.tsum_twoAdic_high_regroup

    For every natural number \(J\) and every sequence \((c_m)_{m \ge 1}\) of complex numbers,

    \[\sum_{j \ge J,\, n \ge 1} c_{(2n-1) 2^j} = \sum_{p \ge 1} c_{p 2^J},\]

    where the left side is an unconditional sum over the pairs \((j, n)\). No summability is assumed: \((j, n) \mapsto (2n-1) 2^{j-J}\) is a bijection onto the positive integers, so the two families are summable together or not at all, and a non-summable family has sum \(0\) by convention.

    public theorem_wanted tsum_twoAdic_high_regroup (J : ℕ) (c : ℕ → ℂ) :
    ∑' x : ℕ × ℕ, c ((2 * x.2 + 1) * 2 ^ (x.1 + J)) = ∑' p : ℕ, c ((p + 1) * 2 ^ J)
