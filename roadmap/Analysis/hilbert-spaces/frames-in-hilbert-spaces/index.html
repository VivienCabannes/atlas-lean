    Let \(E\) be an inner product space over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\).     DefinitionFrame operator#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.FiniteFrame.frameOperator

    For a finite family \((f_i)_{i \in \iota}\) in an inner product space \(E\) over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\), the frame operator is the bounded linear operator \(S \colon E \to E\) given by \(S x = \sum_{i \in \iota} \langle f_i, x \rangle f_i\), the sum of the rank-one operators \(x \mapsto \langle f_i, x \rangle f_i\). The inner product is conjugate-linear in its first argument.

    noncomputable def frameOperator (f : ι → E) : E →L[𝕜] E :=
  ∑ i, rankOne 𝕜 (f i) (f i)

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.FiniteFrame.isUnit_frameOperator

    Let \(E\) be a Hilbert space over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\) and let \((f_i)_{i \in \iota}\) be a finite frame in \(E\) with frame bounds \(A\) and \(B\).
