    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.ConvexProjection.convexProjection

    For a subset \(K\) of a real inner product space \(F\) and a point \(x \in F\), the nearest-point projection \(P_K(x)\) is a chosen point of \(F\) such that, whenever \(K\) is nonempty, complete and convex, \(P_K(x) \in K\) and \(\|x - P_K(x)\| = \inf_{w \in K} \|x - w\|\); it is then the unique point of \(K\) nearest to \(x\). When \(K\) fails one of these hypotheses, the value of \(P_K(x)\) is unspecified.

  Classical.choose (exists_convexProjection_spec K x)
