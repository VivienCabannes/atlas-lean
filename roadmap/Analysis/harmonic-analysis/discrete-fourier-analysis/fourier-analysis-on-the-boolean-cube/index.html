    BooleanCube.boolFourierCoeff

    For \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), a function \(f : \{-1, 1\}^n \to \mathbb{R}\) and a set \(S \subseteq \{0, \dots, n-1\}\), the normalized Fourier coefficient of \(f\) at \(S\) is \(\hat f(S) = \frac{1}{|\{-1, 1\}^n|} \sum_{x \in \{-1, 1\}^n} f(x) \chi_S(x)\), where \(\chi_S(x) = \prod_{i \in S} x_i\).     BooleanCube.MultilinearDegreeLE

    A real-valued function \(f\) on the Boolean cube of dimension \(n\) has Fourier degree at most a natural number \(d\) when its Fourier coefficient \(\hat f(S)\) vanishes for every set \(S\) of coordinates with \(|S| > d\).

    BooleanCube.cubeBasis

    Let \(x\) be a point of the Boolean cube in \(n\) coordinates, indexed by \(i \in \{0, \dots, n-1\}\).     DefinitionMultilinear extension#✓fully proved

    BooleanCube.multilinearExtension

    Let \(f\) be a Boolean-valued function on the Boolean cube of dimension \(n\), and read each coordinate and each value as \(1\) for true and \(0\) for false. The real multilinear extension of \(f\) is the polynomial in \(\mathbb{R}[X_0, \dots, X_{n-1}]\)

    \[\sum_{x \in \{0,1\}^n} f(x) \prod_{i < n} \bigl(x_i X_i + (1 - x_i)(1 - X_i)\bigr).\]

    For each cube point \(x\) the product is its Lagrange basis polynomial: it evaluates to \(1\) at \(x\) and to \(0\) at every other point of \(\{0,1\}^n\). Hence the extension agrees with \(f\) on \(\{0,1\}^n\), and it is the unique polynomial of degree at most one in each variable that does.

    noncomputable def multilinearExtension {n : ℕ}
    (f : BoolCube n → Bool) : MvPolynomial (Fin n) ℝ := by
  classical
  exact
  ∑ x : BoolCube n,
    MvPolynomial.C (if f x then (1 : ℝ) else 0) *
      cubeBasis x
