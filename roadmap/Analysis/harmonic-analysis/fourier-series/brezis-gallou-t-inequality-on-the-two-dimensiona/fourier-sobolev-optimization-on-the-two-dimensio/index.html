    TorusSobolev.fourierCoefficient

    Let \(u : (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) be continuous and let \(k \in \mathbb{Z}^2\) be a frequency.     DefinitionGradient energy#✓fully proved

    TorusSobolev.gradientEnergy

    For a continuous function \(u : (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) with Fourier coefficients \(a_k\), the gradient energy is \(\sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}^2} |k|^2 \, |a_k|^2\), where \(|k|^2 = k_1^2 + k_2^2\); the sum is taken to be \(0\) when the series diverges.

    DefinitionFunctions in \(H^2\)#✓fully proved

    TorusSobolev.IsH2

    A continuous function \(u : (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) with Fourier coefficients \(a_k\) is in \(H^2\) when the series \(\sum_{k \in \mathbb{Z}^2} (1 + |k|^2)^2 \, |a_k|^2\) converges, where \(|k|^2 = k_1^2 + k_2^2\).

    This is the full spectral \(H^2\) condition, not a restriction to trigonometric polynomials.

    def IsH2 (u : C(Torus, ℝ)) : Prop :=
  Summable fun k ↦ (1 + frequencyWeight k) ^ 2 * ‖fourierCoefficient u k‖ ^ 2

    DefinitionMean of a function on the torus#✓fully proved

    TorusSobolev.mean

    For a continuous function \(u : \mathbb{T}^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) on the torus \(\mathbb{T}^2 = (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2\), its mean is \(\int_{(\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})^2} u(2\pi y)\, d\mu(y)\), where \(\mu\) is the normalized Haar measure on the unit torus \((\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})^2\) and \(y \mapsto 2\pi y\) is the inverse of the rescaling to unit period.

    TorusSobolev.UniformRemainderBound

    For a real constant \(L\), the bound with constant \(L\) holds when, for every continuous \(u : \mathbb{T}^2 \to \mathbb{R}\) on the torus \(\mathbb{T}^2 = (\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^2\) that has mean zero and lies in \(H^2\), has positive gradient energy \(E(u)\), and, writing \(\Delta(u)\) for its Laplace energy, has frequency ratio \(\Lambda(u) = \Delta(u)/E(u) \ge 1\), every point \(x\) of the torus satisfies \(u(x)^2 \le \frac{E(u)}{4\pi}\bigl(\log \Lambda(u) + \log(1 + \log \Lambda(u)) + L\bigr)\).
