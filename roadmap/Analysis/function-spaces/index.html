    There is a constant \(C > 0\) such that every real-valued Schwartz function \(f \colon \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) satisfies

    \[\|f\|_{L^\infty} \le C \, \|f\|_{L^2}^{1/2} \, \|f'\|_{L^2}^{1/2},\]

    where \(f'\) is the derivative of \(f\) and the \(L^\infty\) and \(L^2\) norms are taken with respect to Lebesgue measure on \(\mathbb{R}\).

    public theorem_wanted exists_norm_toLp_top_le_mul_sqrt_norm_toLp_two_mul_sqrt_deriv :

    Let \(X\), \(Y\), \(Z\) be real Banach spaces, let \(i \colon X \to Y\) be an injective compact bounded linear operator, and let \(j \colon Y \to Z\) be an injective bounded linear operator.     TheoremSobolev trace bound on the plane \(z = 0\) in \(\mathbb{R}^3\)#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Analysis.FunctionalAnalysis.SobolevTraceR3PlaneBoundedWanted.exists_bound_integral_sq_trace_of_integral_sq_add_fderiv_sq_le_one

    Let \((f_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\) be a sequence of continuously differentiable functions \(\mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\), where \(\mathbb{R}^3\) has coordinates \((x, y, z)\) and the max norm. For \(p \in \mathbb{R}^3\) write \(\|Df_n(p)\|\) for the operator norm of the derivative of \(f_n\) at \(p\), which equals \(|\partial_x f_n(p)| + |\partial_y f_n(p)| + |\partial_z f_n(p)|\), the sum of the absolute values of the partial derivatives. Suppose that for every \(n\) the function \(p \mapsto |f_n(p)|^2 + \|Df_n(p)\|^2\) is Lebesgue integrable on \(\mathbb{R}^3\) with integral at most \(1\). Then there is a constant \(C \ge 0\) such that for every \(n\) the function \((x, y) \mapsto |f_n(x, y, 0)|^2\) is Lebesgue integrable on \(\mathbb{R}^2\) with integral at most \(C\).

    Since \(\|Df_n(p)\|\) is at least the Euclidean length \(|\nabla f_n(p)|\) of the gradient, the hypothesis implies the bound \(\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} (|\nabla f_n|^2 + |f_n|^2) \le 1\) of the source problem; conversely, if \(f_n\) satisfies that bound, then \(f_n / \sqrt{3}\) satisfies the hypothesis.
