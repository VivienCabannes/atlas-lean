    Mirror-prox method

    The mirror-prox method (Nemirovski, 2004) solves variational inequalities with a monotone Lipschitz operator \(F\) on a convex set \(Z\), which include convex-concave saddle-point problems such as matrix games, at the rate \(O(1/N)\). Each step makes two prox steps of mirror descent from the same centre \(z_t\): a leading step \(w_t\) with \(F(z_t)\), then the update \(z_{t+1}\) with \(F(w_t)\). The one-step inequality has no error term once \(\gamma L \le \alpha\), so the average of the leading points satisfies the ergodic bound \(F(u)(\bar w_N - u) \le V_{z_0}(u)/(\gamma N)\), and with Nemirovski's step the efficiency estimate \(\sqrt{2}\Theta L/(\alpha N)\). For a convex-concave \(f\) and \(F = (\partial_x f, -\partial_y f)\) the same bound controls the saddle-point gap of the averaged leading points. In the Euclidean setup it is Korpelevich's extragradient method. As for mirror descent, prox steps are used through their optimality conditions and strong convexity in the Bregman form.

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Convex.MirrorProx.mirror_prox_step_le

    Throughout, \(E\) is a real normed space, \(E^*\) its dual with the dual norm \(\|\cdot\|_*\), \(Z \subseteq E\), \(\omega\) a distance-generating function with derivative selection \(\omega'\) and Bregman divergence \(V\), strongly convex on \(Z\) in the form \(V_x(u) \ge \tfrac{\alpha}{2}\|u - x\|^2\) (\(\alpha > 0\)), and \(F \colon E \to E^*\) an operator. Let \(\gamma \ge 0\) and \(z, w, z^+ \in Z\), where \(w\) and \(z^+\) satisfy the optimality conditions of the two prox steps

    \[ w = \operatorname{argmin}_{u \in Z}\{\gamma F(z)(u) + V_z(u)\}, \qquad z^+ = \operatorname{argmin}_{u \in Z}\{\gamma F(w)(u) + V_z(u)\}, \]

    and let \(\gamma\|F(z) - F(w)\|_* \le \alpha\|z - w\|\) (true when \(F\) is \(L\)-Lipschitz from \(\|\cdot\|\) to \(\|\cdot\|_*\) and \(\gamma L \le \alpha\)). Then for every \(u \in Z\),

    \[ \gamma\, F(w)(w - u) \le V_z(u) - V_{z^+}(u). \]

    The leading point \(w\) is evaluated by the operator, and the update \(z^+\) uses \(F(w)\) from the old centre \(z\); this "look-ahead" is what removes the error term \(\gamma^2\|F\|_*^2\) of plain mirror descent. The proof applies the three-point identity twice and bounds the remainder \(\gamma\|F(z) - F(w)\|_*\|w - z^+\| - \tfrac{\alpha}{2}(\|w - z\|^2 + \|w - z^+\|^2) \le 0\) directly; the source first bounds \(\|w - z^+\| \le \gamma\|F(z) - F(w)\|_*/\alpha\), (3.7.a), which requires the smaller step \(\gamma L \le \alpha/\sqrt{2}\).

    theorem mirror_prox_step_le (ω : E → ℝ) (ω' : E → E →L[ℝ] ℝ) {α γ : ℝ} (hα : 0 < α)
    (hγ : 0 ≤ γ) {Z : Set E} (hsc : ∀ x ∈ Z, ∀ u ∈ Z, α / 2 * ‖u - x‖ ^ 2 ≤ bregman ω ω' x u)
    (F : E → E →L[ℝ] ℝ) {z w zplus : E} (hz : z ∈ Z) (hw : w ∈ Z) (hzplus : zplus ∈ Z)
    (hoptw : ∀ u ∈ Z, 0 ≤ (γ • F z - ω' z + ω' w) (u - w))
    (hoptz : ∀ u ∈ Z, 0 ≤ (γ • F w - ω' z + ω' zplus) (u - zplus))
    (hlip : γ * ‖F z - F w‖ ≤ α * ‖z - w‖) {u : E} (hu : u ∈ Z) :
    γ * F w (w - u) ≤ bregman ω ω' z u - bregman ω ω' zplus u
    noncomputable def bregman (ω : E → ℝ) (ω' : E → E →L[ℝ] ℝ) (x u : E) : ℝ :=
  ω u - ω x - ω' x (u - x)

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Convex.MirrorProx.mirror_prox_average_le

    Throughout, \(E\) is a real normed space, \(E^*\) its dual with the dual norm \(\|\cdot\|_*\), \(Z \subseteq E\), \(\omega\) a distance-generating function with derivative selection \(\omega'\) and Bregman divergence \(V\), strongly convex on \(Z\) in the form \(V_x(u) \ge \tfrac{\alpha}{2}\|u - x\|^2\) (\(\alpha > 0\)), and \(F \colon E \to E^*\) an operator. Let \(\gamma > 0\) with \(\gamma L \le \alpha\), and let \(F\) be monotone on \(Z\), \((F(a) - F(b))(a - b) \ge 0\), and \(L\)-Lipschitz there, \(\|F(a) - F(b)\|_* \le L\|a - b\|\). Let \(z_t, w_t \in Z\) satisfy, for every \(t\), the optimality conditions of the prox steps

    \[ w_t = \operatorname{argmin}_{u \in Z}\{\gamma F(z_t)(u) + V_{z_t}(u)\}, \qquad z_{t+1} = \operatorname{argmin}_{u \in Z}\{\gamma F(w_t)(u) + V_{z_t}(u)\}. \]

    Then for \(N \ge 1\) the average \(\bar w_N = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{t<N} w_t\) satisfies, for every \(u \in Z\),

    \[ F(u)(\bar w_N - u) \le \frac{V_{z_0}(u)}{\gamma N} . \]

    The left-hand side, maximized over \(u \in Z\), is the accuracy \(\epsilon(\bar w_N)\) of \(\bar w_N\) as a (weak) solution of the variational inequality: \(\epsilon(z^\star) \le 0\) characterizes solutions. The proof telescopes the one-step inequality and uses monotonicity, \(F(u)(w_t - u) \le F(w_t)(w_t - u)\).

    theorem mirror_prox_average_le (ω : E → ℝ) (ω' : E → E →L[ℝ] ℝ) {α γ L : ℝ} (hα : 0 < α)
    (hγ : 0 < γ) (hγL : γ * L ≤ α) {Z : Set E}
    (hsc : ∀ x ∈ Z, ∀ u ∈ Z, α / 2 * ‖u - x‖ ^ 2 ≤ bregman ω ω' x u)
    (F : E → E →L[ℝ] ℝ) (hmono : ∀ a ∈ Z, ∀ b ∈ Z, 0 ≤ (F a - F b) (a - b))
    (hlip : ∀ a ∈ Z, ∀ b ∈ Z, ‖F a - F b‖ ≤ L * ‖a - b‖)
    {z w : ℕ → E} (hz : ∀ t, z t ∈ Z) (hw : ∀ t, w t ∈ Z)
    (hoptw : ∀ t, ∀ u ∈ Z, 0 ≤ (γ • F (z t) - ω' (z t) + ω' (w t)) (u - w t))
    (hoptz : ∀ t, ∀ u ∈ Z, 0 ≤ (γ • F (w t) - ω' (z t) + ω' (z (t + 1))) (u - z (t + 1)))
    {N : ℕ} (hN : 0 < N) {u : E} (hu : u ∈ Z) :
    F u ((N : ℝ)⁻¹ • ∑ t ∈ range N, w t - u) ≤ bregman ω ω' (z 0) u / (γ * N)
    noncomputable def bregman (ω : E → ℝ) (ω' : E → E →L[ℝ] ℝ) (x u : E) : ℝ :=
  ω u - ω x - ω' x (u - x)

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Convex.MirrorProx.mirror_prox_average_le_sqrt_two

    In the setting of the ergodic rate with \(L > 0\) and the step \(\gamma = \alpha/(\sqrt{2}L)\), if \(V_{z_0}(u) \le \Theta\) for all \(u \in Z\) (for instance \(\Theta = \max_Z \omega - \min_Z \omega\) when \(z_0\) minimizes \(\omega\) on \(Z\)), then for \(N \ge 1\)

    \[ \max_{u \in Z} F(u)(\bar w_N - u) \le \frac{\sqrt{2}\,\Theta L}{\alpha N} . \]

    Each step costs two evaluations of \(F\) and two prox steps. The \(O(1/N)\) rate beats the \(O(1/\sqrt{N})\) black-box lower bound for nonsmooth problems because the structure is used: a convex function given as \(\max_y \phi(x, y)\) with smooth convex-concave \(\phi\) is minimized through the monotone operator \((\partial_x \phi, -\partial_y \phi)\) of the saddle-point problem. In the Euclidean setup the method is Korpelevich's extragradient method.

    {z w : ℕ → E} (hz : ∀ t, z t ∈ Z) (hw : ∀ t, w t ∈ Z)

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Convex.MirrorProx.mirror_prox_sum_le

    In the setting of the ergodic rate, without assuming monotonicity: if \(0 < \gamma\), \(\gamma L \le \alpha\) and \(F\) is \(L\)-Lipschitz on \(Z\), then the leading points satisfy, for every \(u \in Z\) and every \(N\),

    \[ \gamma \sum_{t<N} F(w_t)(w_t - u) \le V_{z_0}(u) . \]

    It is the sum of the one-step inequality. Monotonicity turns it into the variational-inequality bound; convexity-concavity turns it into the saddle-point bound.

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Convex.MirrorProx.mirror_prox_saddle_le

    Let \(E_x, E_y\) be real normed spaces, \(X \subseteq E_x\), \(Y \subseteq E_y\), and let \(f\) be convex in \(x \in X\) for every \(y \in Y\) and concave in \(y \in Y\) for every \(x \in X\). Let \(F(x, y)(a, b) = F_x(x, y)(a) - F_y(x, y)(b)\) on \(E_x \times E_y\) (with the max norm), where \(F_x\) and \(F_y\) satisfy the first-order inequalities

    \[ f(x', y) \ge f(x, y) + F_x(x, y)(x' - x), \qquad f(x, y') \le f(x, y) + F_y(x, y)(y' - y) \]

    for \((x, y) \in X \times Y\), \(x' \in X\), \(y' \in Y\) (for differentiable \(f\), the partial derivatives, so that \(F = (\partial_x f, -\partial_y f)\)). Let \(F\) be \(L\)-Lipschitz on \(Z = X \times Y\), \(\omega\) \(\alpha\)-strongly convex on \(Z\) in the Bregman form, \(0 < \gamma\) with \(\gamma L \le \alpha\), and let \(z_t, w_t \in Z\) satisfy the optimality conditions of the mirror-prox steps. Then for \(N \ge 1\) the average \((\bar x_N, \bar y_N) = \frac1N\sum_{t<N} w_t\) satisfies

    \[ f(\bar x_N, y) - f(x, \bar y_N) \le \frac{V_{z_0}(x, y)}{\gamma N} \qquad \text{for all } x \in X,\ y \in Y . \]

    The proof bounds \(F(w_t)(w_t - (x, y)) \ge f(x_t, y) - f(x, y_t)\) at each leading point \(w_t = (x_t, y_t)\), sums with the telescoped bound, and applies Jensen's inequality in each variable.

    MathlibExt.Analysis.Convex.MirrorProx.mirror_prox_saddle_le_sqrt_two

    In the setting of the saddle-point bound with \(L > 0\), the step \(\gamma = \alpha / (\sqrt{2}L)\) and \(V_{z_0} \le \Theta\) on \(X \times Y\),

    \[ f(\bar x_N, y) - f(x, \bar y_N) \le \frac{\sqrt{2}\,\Theta L}{\alpha N} \qquad \text{for all } x \in X,\ y \in Y . \]

    Taking the supremum over \(y\) and the infimum over \(x\), the gap \(\sup_{y \in Y} f(\bar x_N, y) - \inf_{x \in X} f(x, \bar y_N)\) is at most \(\sqrt{2}\Theta L/(\alpha N)\). The source's accuracy \(\epsilon_f(\bar z_N)\), the sum of \(\max_y f(\bar x_N, y) - \min_x \max_y f\) and \(\max_y \min_x f - \min_x f(x, \bar y_N)\), is at most this gap, with equality when \(\min_x \max_y f = \max_y \min_x f\), as for convex compact \(X\) and \(Y\). For a matrix game, the bilinear \(x^T A y\) on \(\Delta_p \times \Delta_q\) with the entropy-type setups of the source on both simplices, this gives \(\epsilon_f(\bar z_N) \le O(1) \max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| \sqrt{\ln(p+1)\ln(q+1)}/N\) at the cost of four matrix-vector products per step.
