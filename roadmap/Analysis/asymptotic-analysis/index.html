    Asymptotic analysis

    Asymptotic expansions and methods: Euler–Maclaurin and Watson's lemma, Laplace-type integrals, coefficient asymptotics of power series (Bender's lemma), Stirling-type and inverse-factorial expansions, and asymptotic expansions of power sums, Lambert series and log sums; complex-analytic saddle-point results on entire functions go to Complex analysis.

    TheoremEuler–Maclaurin formula#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.eulerMaclaurinFormula

    Let \(a < b\) be integers, let \(p \ge 1\) be a natural number, and let \(f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) be \(p\) times continuously differentiable on \([a, b]\), its derivatives \(f^{(k)}\) being taken relative to \([a, b]\) (one-sided at the endpoints). Let \(B_j\) be the Bernoulli numbers with \(B_1 = -\tfrac{1}{2}\). Then there are \(1\)-periodic functions \(P_m : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) for \(m \ge 0\) such that \(P_p\) agrees on \([0, 1)\) with the Bernoulli polynomial \(\sum_{j=0}^{p} \binom{p}{j} B_j x^{p-j}\), and \(\sum_{n=a}^{b} f(n) = I + E + R\), where \(I = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx + \frac{f(a) + f(b)}{2}\), \(E = \sum_{k=1}^{\lfloor p/2 \rfloor} \frac{B_{2k}}{(2k)!} (f^{(2k-1)}(b) - f^{(2k-1)}(a))\) and \(R = \frac{(-1)^{p+1}}{p!} \int_a^b P_p(x) f^{(p)}(x)\,dx\).

    MetaMathlibExt.eulerMaclaurinFormula · 18 lines 
    theorem eulerMaclaurinFormula :
    ∀ (a b : ℤ) (p : ℕ) (f : ℝ → ℝ),
      a < b →
      1 ≤ p →
      ContDiffOn ℝ (↑p) f (Set.Icc (↑a) (↑b)) →
      ∃ P : ℕ → ℝ → ℝ,
        (∀ (m : ℕ) (x : ℝ), P m (x + 1) = P m x) ∧
        (∀ x ∈ Set.Ico (0 : ℝ) 1, P p x =
          ∑ j ∈ Finset.range (p + 1), (Nat.choose p j : ℝ) *
            (if j = 1 then (-1 / 2 : ℝ) else (bernoulli j : ℝ)) * x ^ (p - j)) ∧
        ∑ n ∈ Finset.Icc a b, f (↑n) =
          (∫ x in (↑a)..(↑b), f x) + (f (↑a) + f (↑b)) / 2 +
            (∑ k ∈ Finset.Icc 1 (p / 2),
              (bernoulli (2 * k) : ℝ) / (Nat.factorial (2 * k) : ℝ) *
                (iteratedDerivWithin (2 * k - 1) f (Set.Icc (↑a) (↑b)) (↑b) -
                  iteratedDerivWithin (2 * k - 1) f (Set.Icc (↑a) (↑b)) (↑a))) +
            ((-1 : ℝ) ^ (p + 1) / (Nat.factorial p : ℝ) *
              (∫ x in (↑a)..(↑b), P p x * iteratedDerivWithin p f (Set.Icc (↑a) (↑b)) x))

    TheoremWatson's inverse factorial expansion#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.watson_inverse_factorial_series

    Let \(A,B,\rho,\sigma,\alpha,\delta>0\), \(\gamma\ge 0\) and \(p\) be real numbers with \(\alpha+3\delta\lt \pi/2\) and \(1\lt p<1+e^{-\pi\cot\alpha}\). Let \(S\) be the sector \(\{z\in\mathbb{C} : |z|>\gamma,\ |\arg z|\le\pi/2+\alpha+3\delta\}\), where \(\arg\) is the principal argument. Let \(f\) be a complex function that is analytic at every point of the half-plane \(\{z : \mathrm{Re}\,z>0\}\) and at every point of \(S\), let \(a_0,a_1,\dots\) be complex numbers with \(|a_n|\lt A\rho^n n!\) for every \(n\ge 0\), and let \(R_0,R_1,\dots\) be complex functions such that for every \(n\ge 0\) and every \(z\in S\)

    \[f(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{a_k}{z^k}+R_n(z)\qquad\text{and}\qquad|R_n(z)\,z^{n+1}|\lt B\sigma^n n!.\]

    Suppose that \(M_0\) is the largest real \(x>0\) with

    \[e^{-2\cos\alpha/(\rho x)}-2\cos\big(\sin\alpha/(\rho x)\big)e^{-\cos\alpha/(\rho x)}+1-p^2=0.\]

    Then for every real \(M\) with \(0\lt M\le M_0\) and every complex \(w\) with \(\mathrm{Re}\,w\ge 0\) there are complex numbers \(b_0,b_1,\dots\) such that for every \(z\) with \(\mathrm{Re}\,z>0\) the series

    \[\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{b_k}{(Mz+w+1)(Mz+w+2)\cdots(Mz+w+k)}\]

    converges absolutely and its sum is \(f(z)-b_0\).

    TheoremWatson's lemma for Laplace integrals#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.watson_laplace_integral_asymptotic

    Let \(g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) and let \((c_k)_{k \ge 1}\) be real numbers such that for every \(N \ge 0\), \(g(s) - \sum_{k=1}^{N} c_k s^k = O(s^{N+1})\) as \(s \to 0^+\). Suppose there is \(m_0 \in \mathbb{R}\) such that \(s \mapsto g(s) e^{-ms}\) is integrable on \((0, \infty)\) for every \(m \ge m_0\). Then for every \(N \ge 0\), \(\int_0^\infty g(s) e^{-ms} \, ds - \sum_{k=1}^{N} k! \, c_k / m^{k+1} = O(1 / m^{N+2})\) as \(m \to \infty\).

    The case \(N = 0\) of the hypothesis says \(g(s) = O(s)\) as \(s \to 0^+\): the expansion of \(g\) has no constant term.

    theorem watson_laplace_integral_asymptotic
  (g : ℝ → ℝ) (c : ℕ → ℝ)
  (hlocal : ∀ N : ℕ, Asymptotics.IsBigO (nhdsWithin 0 (Set.Ioi 0))
    (fun s : ℝ => g s - (∑ k ∈ Finset.range N, c (k + 1) * s ^ (k + 1)))
    (fun s : ℝ => s ^ (N + 1)))
  (hconv : ∃ m₀ : ℝ, ∀ m ≥ m₀, MeasureTheory.IntegrableOn
    (fun s : ℝ => g s * Real.exp (-m * s)) (Set.Ioi 0)) :
  ∀ N : ℕ, Asymptotics.IsBigO Filter.atTop
    (fun m : ℝ => (∫ s in Set.Ioi 0, g s * Real.exp (-m * s)) -
      (∑ k ∈ Finset.range N,
        ((Nat.factorial (k + 1) : ℕ) : ℝ) * c (k + 1) / m ^ (k + 2)))
    (fun m : ℝ => 1 / m ^ (N + 2))
