    DefinitionRamanujan's product series#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.Ramanujan.Part1Ch3.Entry9Odd.entry9_P

    For complex numbers \(a\), \(b\), \(x\), \(z\), Ramanujan's product series of Entry 9(i) is

    \[P(a, b, x; z) = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-b)^j x^{j+1}}{\prod_{r=0}^{j} (z + a + b(r + 1))}.\]

    The definition is totalized: a quotient with vanishing denominator is taken to be \(0\), and the sum of a non-summable series is taken to be \(0\). Hence at a pole of the source expression, for example \(a = 0\), \(b = 1\), \(x = 1\), \(z = -1\), where every product contains the vanishing factor \(z + a + b = 0\), every term and so \(P\) itself equals \(0\), whereas the source expression has a singularity there. Off the poles, when \(b \neq 0\) and \(w = (z + a)/b\) is nonzero and satisfies \(|\arg w| \le \pi - \varepsilon\) for some \(0 < \varepsilon < \pi\), the closed form of Ramanujan's product series identifies \(P\) with the source's closed form.

    noncomputable def entry9_P (a b x : ℂ) (z : ℂ) : ℂ :=
  ∑' (j : ℕ), ((-b) ^ j * x ^ (j + 1) / ∏ r ∈ Finset.range (j + 1), (z + a + b * ((r : ℂ) + 1)))

    Let \(a_0, a_1, \ldots\) be complex numbers, let \(\psi : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}\), let \(\lambda\) be either a real number or \(-\infty\), and let \(0 < \varepsilon < \pi/2\). Suppose that for every complex \(z\) that is not a negative integer and satisfies \(\operatorname{Re} z > \lambda\), the series

    \[\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^j a_j}{(z + 1)(z + 2) \cdots (z + j + 1)}\]

    converges absolutely with sum \(\psi(z)\). Write \(S(n, k)\) for the Stirling numbers of the second kind and \(c_j = \sum_{k=0}^{j} a_k S(j + 1, k + 1)\). Then for every natural number \(N\) there are real numbers \(C \ge 0\) and \(R > 0\) such that

    \[\Big|\psi(z) - \sum_{j=0}^{N} \frac{(-1)^j c_j}{z^{j+1}}\Big| \le \frac{C}{|z|^{N+2}}\]

    for every complex \(z\) that is not a negative integer, satisfies \(\operatorname{Re} z > \lambda\) and \(|z| \ge R\), and lies in the sector \(|\arg z| \le \pi/2 - \varepsilon\) when \(\lambda = -\infty\), or \(|\arg(z - \lambda)| \le \pi/2 - \varepsilon\) when \(\lambda\) is real.
