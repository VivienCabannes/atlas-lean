    Generalized hypergeometric series

    DefinitionGeneralized hypergeometric coefficient#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.generalizedHypergeometricCoefficient

    Let \(K\) be a field, let \(a\) and \(b\) be finite multisets of elements of \(K\) (the upper and lower parameters), and let \(n\) be a natural number. The \(n\)-th generalized hypergeometric coefficient is

    \[ (n!)^{-1} \prod_{x \in a} (x)_n \Big(\prod_{y \in b} (y)_n\Big)^{-1}, \]

    where \((x)_n = x(x+1)\cdots(x+n-1)\) is the rising factorial, with \((x)_0 = 1\), and the products run over the multisets with multiplicity. It is the coefficient of \(z^n\) in the generalized hypergeometric series \({}_pF_q\) with \(p = |a|\) and \(q = |b|\).

    Inverses are taken in \(K\) with the convention \(0^{-1} = 0\), so the coefficient is \(0\) whenever the lower product or \(n!\) is zero in \(K\); the latter can happen in positive characteristic.

    noncomputable def generalizedHypergeometricCoefficient (a b : Multiset K) (n : ℕ) : K :=
  (n.factorial : K)⁻¹ * (a.map fun x => (ascPochhammer K n).eval x).prod *
    (b.map fun x => (ascPochhammer K n).eval x).prod⁻¹

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.generalizedHypergeometricSeries

    Let \(K\) be a field and \(A\) a topological ring that is a \(K\)-algebra. For finite multisets \(a\) and \(b\) of elements of \(K\), the generalized hypergeometric series is the formal multilinear series on \(A\) whose \(n\)-th term is the \(n\)-linear map \((x_1, \dots, x_n) \mapsto c_n \, x_1 x_2 \cdots x_n\), where \(c_n \in K\) is the \(n\)-th generalized hypergeometric coefficient with upper parameters \(a\) and lower parameters \(b\).

    DefinitionAdmissible lower parameters#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.LowerParametersAdmissible

    A finite multiset \(B\) of complex numbers is admissible as lower parameters if no element of \(B\) is \(0\) or a negative integer, that is, \(-k \notin B\) for every natural number \(k\).

    def LowerParametersAdmissible (bs : Multiset ℂ) : Prop :=
  ∀ k : ℕ, (-(k : ℂ) ∉ bs)

    AbbreviationSummability of the generalized hypergeometric series#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.SummableHypergeometric

    For finite multisets \(a\) and \(b\) of complex numbers and \(z \in \mathbb{C}\), the generalized hypergeometric series with upper parameters \(a\) and lower parameters \(b\) is summable at \(z\) if the family \((c_n z^n)_{n \ge 0}\) is summable in \(\mathbb{C}\), where \(c_n\) is the \(n\)-th generalized hypergeometric coefficient.

    Summability of a family in \(\mathbb{C}\) is unconditional summability, which is equivalent to absolute convergence of \(\sum_n c_n z^n\).

    abbrev SummableHypergeometric (a b : Multiset ℂ) (z : ℂ) : Prop :=
  Summable (fun n : ℕ => generalizedHypergeometricCoefficient a b n * z ^ n)

    DefinitionGeneralized hypergeometric function#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.generalizedHypergeometricValue

    For finite multisets \(a\) and \(b\) of complex numbers and \(z \in \mathbb{C}\), the value \({}_pF_q(a; b; z)\), where \(p = |a|\) and \(q = |b|\), is the sum \(\sum_{n \ge 0} c_n z^n\), where \(c_n\) is the \(n\)-th generalized hypergeometric coefficient with upper parameters \(a\) and lower parameters \(b\).

    The sum is the unconditional sum of the family \((c_n z^n)_{n \ge 0}\), which is \(0\) by convention when that family is not summable; no condition on the parameters or on \(z\) is imposed.

    noncomputable def generalizedHypergeometricValue (a b : Multiset ℂ) (z : ℂ) : ℂ :=
  ∑' n : ℕ, generalizedHypergeometricCoefficient a b n * z ^ n

    TheoremRising factorials at admissible lower parameters are nonzero#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.lowerParametersAdmissible_ascPochhammer_ne_zero

    Let \(B\) be a finite multiset of complex numbers that is admissible as lower parameters. Then for every \(b \in B\) and every natural number \(n\), the rising factorial \((b)_n = b(b+1)\cdots(b+n-1)\), with \((b)_0 = 1\), is nonzero.

    This follows because over \(\mathbb{C}\), \((b)_n = 0\) if and only if \(b = -k\) for some natural number \(k < n\).

    (hadm : LowerParametersAdmissible bs) {b : ℂ} (hb : b ∈ bs) (n : ℕ) :
    (ascPochhammer ℂ n).eval b ≠ 0

    TheoremTerminating generalized hypergeometric series are summable#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.summableHypergeometric_of_neg_coe_nat_mem_upper

    Let \(a\) and \(b\) be finite multisets of complex numbers, and let \(c_n\) be the \(n\)-th generalized hypergeometric coefficient with upper parameters \(a\) and lower parameters \(b\). If \(-N \in a\) for some natural number \(N\), then for every \(z \in \mathbb{C}\) the family \((c_n z^n)_{n \ge 0}\) is summable, that is, the generalized hypergeometric series with these parameters is summable at \(z\).

    Only finitely many terms are nonzero, since \(c_n = 0\) for \(n > N\); no condition on the lower parameters is needed.

    (hmem : (-(N : ℂ) ∈ a)) :
    SummableHypergeometric a b z

    TheoremTerminating generalized hypergeometric series are finite sums#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.GeneralizedHypergeometric.generalizedHypergeometric_tsum_eq_finset_sum

    Let \(a\) and \(b\) be finite multisets of complex numbers, and let \(c_n\) be the \(n\)-th generalized hypergeometric coefficient with upper parameters \(a\) and lower parameters \(b\). If \(-N \in a\) for some natural number \(N\), then for every \(z \in \mathbb{C}\)

    \[ \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n z^n = \sum_{n=0}^{N} c_n z^n. \]

    No condition on the lower parameters is needed: \(c_n = 0\) for \(n > N\) because the rising factorial \((-N)_n = (-N)(-N+1)\cdots(-N+n-1)\) vanishes.

    theorem generalizedHypergeometric_tsum_eq_finset_sum
    {a b : Multiset ℂ} {N : ℕ} {z : ℂ}
    (hmem : (-(N : ℂ) ∈ a)) :
    (∑' n : ℕ, generalizedHypergeometricCoefficient a b n * z ^ n) =
      ∑ n ∈ Finset.range (N + 1), generalizedHypergeometricCoefficient a b n * z ^ n
