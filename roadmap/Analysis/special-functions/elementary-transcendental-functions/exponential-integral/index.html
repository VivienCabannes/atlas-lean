    DefinitionTotalized principal exponential integral#✓fully proved

    Complex.principalExponentialIntegralE1Raw

    For every complex number \(z\), this is \(\operatorname{Ein}(z) - \operatorname{Log} z - \gamma\), where \(\operatorname{Ein}(z) = \sum_{j \ge 1} (-1)^{j+1} z^j / (j \cdot j!)\) is the entire exponential integral, \(\operatorname{Log}\) is the principal branch of the complex logarithm (imaginary part in \((-\pi, \pi]\)) extended by \(\operatorname{Log} 0 = 0\), and \(\gamma\) is the Euler–Mascheroni constant.

    At \(z = 0\) the value is therefore \(-\gamma\). This is a convention, not a value of the classical principal \(E_1\), which has a logarithmic singularity at \(0\).
