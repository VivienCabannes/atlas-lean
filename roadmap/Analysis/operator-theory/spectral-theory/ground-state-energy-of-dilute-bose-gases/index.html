    StructureSmooth functions supported inside the box#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.BoseGasesWanted.BoseGases.CInfty0Box

    For a natural number \(N\) and a real number \(L\), \(C_0^\infty(([0,L]^3)^N) \setminus \{0\}\) consists of the functions \(\Psi : (\mathbb{R}^3)^N \to \mathbb{R}\) that are smooth on the box \(([0,L]^3)^N\), whose closed support is contained in the interior of the box, and that are not identically zero.

    The functions are defined on all of \((\mathbb{R}^3)^N\) rather than on the box only.

    StructureScattering solutions#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.BoseGasesWanted.BoseGases.IsScatteringSolution

    Let \(v, w : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\). The function \(w\) is a scattering solution for the potential \(v\) if \(w\) and \(v\,(1 - w)\) are locally integrable, \(w(x) \to 0\) as \(\|x\| \to \infty\), and \(w\) solves the scattering equation \(-\Delta w = \frac{1}{2} v\,(1 - w)\) (Equation (3.4) of Fournais–Solovej) in the weak sense: for every smooth, compactly supported \(\varphi : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\),

    \[-\int_{\mathbb{R}^3} w\,\Delta\varphi = \frac{1}{2} \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} v\,(1 - w)\,\varphi.\]
    structure IsScatteringSolution (w v : ℝ³ → ℝ) where
  lhs_integrable : LocallyIntegrable w
  rhs_integrable : LocallyIntegrable (v * (1 - w))
  eq : ∀ φ, ContDiff ℝ ∞ φ ∧ HasCompactSupport φ →
    - ∫ x, w x * (Δ φ) x = (1 / 2) * ∫ x, v x * (1 - w x) * φ x
  lim : Tendsto w (cocompact ℝ³) (𝓝 0)

    DefinitionScattering length#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.BoseGasesWanted.BoseGases.a

    Let \(v : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\) be a potential. If there is a radial scattering solution \(w\) to the scattering equation with potential \(v\), the scattering length of \(v\) is \(a(v) = (8\pi)^{-1} \int_{\mathbb{R}^3} v(x)\,(1 - w(x))\,dx\), where \(w\) is one such solution, chosen arbitrarily; if there is no radial scattering solution, \(a(v) = 0\).

    This is Equation (3.5) of Fournais–Solovej. Under the assumptions of Theorem 1.2 the radial scattering solution exists and is unique, so the choice does not matter there.

    TheoremLee–Huang–Yang lower bound for dilute Bose gases#●statement formalized

    MathlibExt.Geometry.BoseGasesWanted.BoseGases.theorem_1_2

    There are a real number \(\eta\) and a function \(\mathcal{C}\) of two real variables such that the following holds. Let \(v : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}\) be a non-negative, radial, integrable potential that is not almost everywhere zero, and let \(R > 0\) be such that the closed support of \(v\) lies in the open ball \(B(0, R)\). Let \(a\) be the scattering length of \(v\), let \(\mathcal{R}(v) = \int v/(8\pi a)\), and let \(e(\rho, v)\) be the ground state energy density at density \(\rho\): the limit, as \(L \to \infty\) and \(N/L^3 \to \rho\), of \(L^{-3}\) times the infimum, over \(\Psi \in C_0^\infty(([0,L]^3)^N) \setminus \{0\}\), of \(\langle \Psi, H_N \Psi \rangle / \|\Psi\|^2\), where the inner product and norm are those of \(L^2(([0,L]^3)^N)\) and, writing points of \((\mathbb{R}^3)^N\) as \((x_1, \dots, x_N)\) and \(\Delta_{x_i}\) for the Laplacian in the variable \(x_i\), \(H_N = \sum_{i=1}^{N} -\Delta_{x_i} + \sum_{1 \le i < j \le N} v(x_i - x_j)\). Then \(\eta > 0\), and for all sufficiently small \(\rho > 0\),

    \[ e(\rho, v) \ge 4\pi\rho^2 a \left(1 + \frac{128}{15\sqrt{\pi}}\sqrt{\rho a^3} - \mathcal{C}(\mathcal{R}(v), R/a)\,(\rho a^3)^{1/2 + \eta}\right). \]

    The source takes the limit \(\rho a^3 \to 0^+\); for fixed \(v\) this is the same as \(\rho \to 0^+\). Although the wording of Theorem 1.2 in the source suggests that \(\eta\) may depend on \(\mathcal{R}(v)\) and \(R/a\), Theorem 6.8 there shows that \(\eta\) is a universal constant.
