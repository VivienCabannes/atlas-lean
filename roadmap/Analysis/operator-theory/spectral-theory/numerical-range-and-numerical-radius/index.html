    DefinitionNumerical radius#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.NumericalRadius.numericalRadius

    The numerical radius of a bounded linear operator \(T\) on a complex Hilbert space \(H\) is the real number \(w(T) = \sup \{ |\langle T x, x \rangle| : x \in H,\ \|x\| = 1 \}\), the supremum of the moduli of the values \(\langle T x, x \rangle\) over the unit vectors of \(H\) (taken to be \(0\) when \(H = 0\)).

    noncomputable def numericalRadius (T : H →L[ℂ] H) : ℝ :=

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.NumericalRadius.quadForm_cartesianImagPart

    Let \(T\) be a bounded operator on a complex Hilbert space \(H\), whose inner product \(\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle\) is conjugate-linear in its first argument, and let \(B = (T - T^*)/(2i)\) be the imaginary Cartesian part of \(T\).
