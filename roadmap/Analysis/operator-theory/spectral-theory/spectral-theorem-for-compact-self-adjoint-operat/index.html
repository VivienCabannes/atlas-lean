    Let \(T\) be a compact self-adjoint operator on a Hilbert space \(H\) over \(\mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\), let \(S\) be a linear subspace of \(H\) that admits an orthogonal projection \(P : H \to H\) onto \(S\) and satisfies \(T(S) \subseteq S\), and let \(T_1 = PTP\) be the compression of \(T\) to \(S\).     MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.CompactSelfAdjointDeflation.exists_next_eigenvector_of_finite_eigenvectors

    Let \(T\) be a compact self-adjoint operator on a Hilbert space \(H\) over \(\mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\), let \(u_1, \dots, u_n \in H\) and scalars \(\mu_1, \dots, \mu_n\) satisfy \(T u_i = \mu_i u_i\) for every \(i\), and let \(S\) be the orthogonal complement of the span of \(u_1, \dots, u_n\). If \(Tz \neq 0\) for some \(z \in S\), then there are a real number \(r\) and a unit vector \(v\) with \(\langle v, u_i \rangle = 0\) for every \(i\), \(Tv = rv\) and \(\langle Tv, v \rangle = r\), such that \(|\langle Tx, x \rangle| \le |r|\) for every unit vector \(x \in S\). In particular \(v \in S\), so \(|r|\) is the maximum of \(|\langle Tx, x \rangle|\) over unit vectors \(x \in S\).

    The vectors \(u_i\) need not be orthonormal, or even nonzero.

    theorem exists_next_eigenvector_of_finite_eigenvectors
    {n : ℕ} {u : Fin n → H} {μ : Fin n → 𝕜} {T : H →L[𝕜] H}
    (hT : IsSelfAdjoint T) (hc : IsCompactOperator (T : H → H))
    (hμ : ∀ i, T (u i) = μ i • u i)
    (hne : ∃ z ∈ (Submodule.span 𝕜 (Set.range u))ᗮ, T z ≠ 0) :
    ∃ r : ℝ, ∃ v : H,
      ‖v‖ = 1 ∧
      (∀ i, ⟪v, u i⟫_𝕜 = 0) ∧
      T v = (r : 𝕜) • v ∧
      (∀ x ∈ (Submodule.span 𝕜 (Set.range u))ᗮ, ‖x‖ = 1 →
        ‖⟪T x, x⟫_𝕜‖ ≤ |r|) ∧
      ⟪T v, v⟫_𝕜 = (r : 𝕜)

    TheoremMaximum principle for compact self-adjoint operators#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.CompactSelfAdjointMaximumPrinciple.exists_nonzero_eigenvalue_enumeration

    Let \(T\) be a compact self-adjoint operator on a Hilbert space \(H\) over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\). Then one of the following two alternatives holds.

    Either there are \(N \in \mathbb{N}\) (possibly \(N = 0\), as for \(T = 0\)), nonzero real numbers \(s_0, \dots, s_{N-1}\) and an orthonormal family \(u_0, \dots, u_{N-1}\) in \(H\) such that \(T u_i = s_i u_i\) for every \(i < N\), \(|s_0| \ge |s_1| \ge \dots \ge |s_{N-1}|\), for every \(i < N\) every unit vector \(x \in H\) orthogonal to \(u_0, \dots, u_{i-1}\) satisfies \(|\langle T x, x \rangle| \le |s_i|\) while \(|\langle T u_i, u_i \rangle| = |s_i|\), and \(T x = 0\) for every \(x \in H\) orthogonal to all of \(u_0, \dots, u_{N-1}\).

    Or there are nonzero real numbers \(s_0, s_1, s_2, \dots\) and an orthonormal sequence \(u_0, u_1, u_2, \dots\) in \(H\) such that \(T u_n = s_n u_n\) for every \(n\), the sequence \(|s_n|\) is nonincreasing and tends to \(0\) as \(n \to \infty\), for every \(n\) every unit vector \(x \in H\) orthogonal to \(u_0, \dots, u_{n-1}\) satisfies \(|\langle T x, x \rangle| \le |s_n|\) while \(|\langle T u_n, u_n \rangle| = |s_n|\), and \(T x = 0\) for every \(x \in H\) orthogonal to every \(u_n\).

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.CompactSelfAdjointMaximumPrinciple.exists_ordered_eigenvalue_sequence

    Let \(T\) be a compact self-adjoint operator on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space \(H\) over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\). Then there are real numbers \(s_0, s_1, s_2, \dots\) and an orthonormal sequence \(u_0, u_1, u_2, \dots\) in \(H\) such that \(T u_n = s_n u_n\) and \(|s_{n+1}| \le |s_n|\) for every \(n\), \(|s_n| \to 0\) as \(n \to \infty\), and for every \(n\) every unit vector \(x \in H\) orthogonal to \(u_0, \dots, u_{n-1}\) satisfies \(|\langle T x, x \rangle| \le |s_n|\), while \(|\langle T u_n, u_n \rangle| = |s_n|\).

      (∀ n, T (u n) = (s n : 𝕜) • u n) ∧

    TheoremSpectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators (range decomposition form)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.CompactSelfAdjointRangeDecomposition.spectral_theorem_range_decomposition

    Spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators (range decomposition form). Let \(H\) be a Hilbert space over \(\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{R}\) or \(\mathbb{C}\) and let \(T : H \to H\) be a compact self-adjoint bounded linear operator. Then: (1) the closure of the subspace spanned by the eigenspaces \(\ker(T - \mu I)\) for all nonzero \(\mu \in \mathbb{K}\) equals the closure of the range of \(T\); (2) the closure of the range of \(T\) and the kernel of \(T\) are complementary subspaces: their intersection is \(\{0\}\) and their sum is \(H\); and (3) the eigenspaces \(\ker(T - \mu I)\), \(\mu \in \mathbb{K}\), are pairwise orthogonal for distinct values of \(\mu\).

    theorem spectral_theorem_range_decomposition {T : H →L[𝕜] H}
    (hT : IsSelfAdjoint T) (hc : IsCompactOperator (T : H → H)) :
    (⨆ (μ : 𝕜) (_ : μ ≠ 0), (eigenspace (T : End 𝕜 H) μ : Submodule 𝕜 H)).topologicalClosure =
      (LinearMap.range T.toLinearMap).topologicalClosure ∧
    IsCompl (LinearMap.range T.toLinearMap).topologicalClosure (LinearMap.ker T.toLinearMap) ∧
    OrthogonalFamily 𝕜 (fun μ : 𝕜 => eigenspace (T : End 𝕜 H) μ)
      (fun μ => (eigenspace (T : End 𝕜 H) μ).subtypeₗᵢ)
