    MathlibExt.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.WoldDecomposition.exists_wold_decomposition

    Finite Wold decomposition: every vector is a finite sum of pushed-forward wandering vectors plus a remainder in the range of V ^ n. The wandering coefficients vanish at and beyond n.
