    Let \(H\) be a real inner product space, let \(T : H \to H\) be a continuous linear operator and let \((u_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\) be a sequence in \(H\). The Hilbert–Schmidt norm of \(T\) in the family \((u_n)\) is the square root, taken in \([0, \infty]\) with \(\sqrt{\infty} = \infty\), of the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm \(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \|T u_n\|^2 \in [0, \infty]\).

    In particular it is \(\infty\), not \(0\), when the series \(\sum_n \|T u_n\|^2\) diverges, for example for the identity operator when \((u_n)\) is an orthonormal basis of \(H\) indexed by \(\mathbb{N}\).
