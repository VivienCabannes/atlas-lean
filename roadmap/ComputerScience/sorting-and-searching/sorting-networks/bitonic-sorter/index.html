    DefinitionBitonic 0-1 sequences#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.SortingNetworks.Bitonic.IsBitonic

    A sequence \(s : \mathbb{N} \to \{0, 1\}\) is bitonic of length \(L\) if either \(s\) or its complement \(1 - s\) is an interval indicator of length \(L\); that is, on the positions \(0, \dots, L - 1\) it has the form \(0^a 1^b 0^c\) or \(1^a 0^b 1^c\). Values of \(s\) at positions \(i \ge L\) are unconstrained.

  IsInterval s L ∨ IsInterval (fun i => !s i) L

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.SortingNetworks.Bitonic.halfClean_bitonic
