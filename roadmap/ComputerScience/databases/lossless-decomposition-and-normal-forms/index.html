    Cslib.Databases.IsBCNF

    A scheme \(S\) is in Boyce–Codd normal form for a set \(F\) of functional dependencies when, for every dependency \(X \to Y\) derivable from \(F\) with \(X, Y \subseteq S\), either \(Y \subseteq X\) or \(F\) derives \(X \to S\) (that is, \(X\) is a superkey of \(S\)).
