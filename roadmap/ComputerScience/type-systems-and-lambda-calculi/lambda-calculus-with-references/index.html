    InductiveOne-step evaluation with references#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Languages.LambdaCalculus.References.Step

    One step of call-by-value evaluation \(t \mid \mu \to t' \mid \mu'\), from a term \(t\) with a store \(\mu\) to a term \(t'\) with a store \(\mu'\), is the least relation closed under the following rules, in which \(v\), \(v_1\) and \(v_2\) are values. If \(t \mid \mu \to t' \mid \mu'\), then \(t\,t_2 \mid \mu \to t'\,t_2 \mid \mu'\), \(v_1\,t \mid \mu \to v_1\,t' \mid \mu'\), \(\mathsf{ref}\,t \mid \mu \to \mathsf{ref}\,t' \mid \mu'\), \(!t \mid \mu \to {!t'} \mid \mu'\), \(t := t_2 \mid \mu \to t' := t_2 \mid \mu'\) and \(v_1 := t \mid \mu \to v_1 := t' \mid \mu'\). The application \((\lambda{:}T.\,s)\,v_2\) with store \(\mu\) steps, with \(\mu\) unchanged, to the result of substituting \(v_2\) for variable \(0\) in \(s\). The allocation \(\mathsf{ref}\,v\) with store \(\mu\) steps to the location \(n\), where \(n\) is the length of \(\mu\), and the store becomes \(\mu\) with \(v\) appended at the end. If \(\mu\) has an entry \(u\) at location \(l\), then \(!l \mid \mu \to u \mid \mu\). If \(l\) is less than the length of \(\mu\), then \(l := v \mid \mu \to \mathsf{unit} \mid \mu'\), where \(\mu'\) is \(\mu\) with its entry at \(l\) replaced by \(v\).
