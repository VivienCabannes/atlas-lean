    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.MarkovDecision.MDP

    A Markov decision process with states in \(S\), actions in \(A\) and values in \(K\) consists of a reward \(r(s, a) \in K\) for taking action \(a\) in state \(s\) and, for all states \(s, s'\) and actions \(a\), a value \(p(s, a, s') \in K\), the probability of moving from \(s\) to \(s'\) under action \(a\).

    No condition is imposed here: finiteness of the states and actions, an order on \(K\) and the stochasticity of \(p\) are hypotheses of the results that use them.

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.MarkovDecision.MDP.Stochastic

    A Markov decision process with finitely many states and transition probabilities \(P(s,a,s')\) in a field \(K\) with a linear order is stochastic when \(0 \le P(s,a,s')\) for all states \(s, s'\) and actions \(a\), and \(\sum_{s'} P(s,a,s') = 1\) for every state \(s\) and action \(a\).

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.MarkovDecision.valueIteration

    Let \(M\) be a Markov decision process with finitely many states, a nonempty finite set of actions, and rewards and transition probabilities in a field \(K\) carrying a linear order, let \(\gamma \in K\), and let \(T\) be the Bellman optimality operator of \(M\) with discount \(\gamma\).     DefinitionTabulated value iteration#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.MarkovDecision.valueIterationVec

    Let \(M\) be a Markov decision process with states \(\{0, \dots, m-1\}\), a nonempty finite set of actions, and rewards and transition probabilities in a field \(K\) carrying a linear order, let \(\gamma \in K\), and let \(T\) be the Bellman optimality operator of \(M\) with discount \(\gamma\). Tabulated value iteration takes a number of steps \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and a vector \(v \in K^m\), read as a function on states: with \(0\) steps it returns \(v\), and with \(k + 1\) steps it computes the vector \(\bigl((Tv)(0), \dots, (Tv)(m-1)\bigr)\) and then runs \(k\) steps from that vector.

  | 0, v => v
