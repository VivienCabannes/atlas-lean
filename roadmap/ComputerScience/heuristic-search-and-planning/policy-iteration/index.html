    Let \(M\) be a Markov decision process with a finite set of states \(S\), a finite nonempty set of actions \(A\), real rewards \(r(s, a)\) and stochastic transition probabilities \(P(s' \mid s, a)\), and let \(0 \le \gamma < 1\).
