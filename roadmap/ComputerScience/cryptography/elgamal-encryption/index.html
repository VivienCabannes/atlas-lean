    Cslib.Crypto.PublicKey.ElGamal.encrypt

    Let \(G\) be a commutative group with a fixed element \(g\) and let \(q\) be a natural number.     def encrypt (h m : G) (y : ZMod q) : G × G := (gpow g y, gpow h y * m)

    Cslib.Crypto.PublicKey.ElGamal.eavGame

    Let \(G\) be a commutative group, \(g \in G\) and \(q \ge 1\). For an eavesdropping adversary \(A\) with coins in a finite nonempty type \(R\), the experiment draws \(x, y \in \mathbb{Z}/q\), coins \(r \in R\) and a bit \(b\) independently and uniformly. It sets the public key \(h = g^x\), lets \(A\) choose messages \((m_0, m_1)\) from \(h\) and \(r\), and gives \(A\) the key \(h\), the ElGamal ciphertext \((g^y, h^y m_b)\) and the coins \(r\), where \(m_b\) is \(m_1\) if \(b\) is true and \(m_0\) otherwise; powers use the value of the exponent in \(\{0, \dots, q - 1\}\). The experiment outputs the distribution of the bit "the guess of \(A\) equals \(b\)".

    noncomputable def eavGame (A : Adversary G R) : PMF Bool :=
  (Cslib.Probability.PMF.uniformOfFintype (ZMod q × ZMod q × R × Bool)).map fun ω =>
    A.guess (publicKey g ω.1)
      (encrypt g (publicKey g ω.1) (pick (A.choose (publicKey g ω.1) ω.2.2.1) ω.2.2.2) ω.2.1)
