    Let a store assign to each identity below \(n\) either nothing or a doubly linked list node whose pointers are identities below \(n\) or NIL, let the head be an identity below \(n\) or NIL, and let \(x\) be an identity whose store entry is a node, where the store entry of the head is also a node when the head is not NIL. The procedure sets the next pointer of \(x\) to the head, then sets the previous pointer of \(x\) to NIL, then tests whether the head is NIL; if it is not, it sets the previous pointer of the node at the head (read after the two writes at \(x\)) to \(x\).     (head : Option (Fin capacity)) (x : Fin capacity)

  TimeM.tick 1
