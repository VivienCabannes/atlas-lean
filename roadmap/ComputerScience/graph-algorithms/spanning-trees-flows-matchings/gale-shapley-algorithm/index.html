    DefinitionA proposal#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.Matching.GaleShapley.propose

    Fix, for each proposer, a ranking of the \(n\) receivers and, for each receiver, a ranking of the \(n\) proposers (rank \(0\) is the first choice). Let \(s\) be a state of deferred acceptance in which proposer \(m\) has made \(k\) proposals. If \(k < n\), then \(m\) proposes to the receiver \(w\) of rank \(k\) in his ranking: his number of proposals becomes \(k + 1\), and \(w\) now holds \(m\) if she held nobody or ranks \(m\) strictly above the proposer she held, and keeps her old holder otherwise; all other counts and holders are unchanged. If \(k \ge n\), the state is unchanged.

    def propose (s : State n) (m : Fin n) : State n :=

    ⟨s.next.set m (s.next[m] + 1),

  else s

    DefinitionDeferred acceptance#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.Matching.GaleShapley.galeShapley

    Let each of \(n\) proposers rank the \(n\) receivers, and each receiver rank the \(n\) proposers, with rank \(0\) the best. Deferred acceptance is the state reached from the initial state, in which no one has proposed and no receiver holds anyone, by repeating the following step at most \(n^2\) times, stopping early once no proposer is both free and has made fewer than \(n\) proposals: the least such proposer, having made \(p\) proposals so far, proposes to the receiver of rank \(p\) in his ranking, and she then holds whichever of him and her current holder she ranks better, or him if she held no one.

    These \(n^2\) steps always suffice: in the resulting state no proposer is both free and has made fewer than \(n\) proposals.

    StructureInvariant of deferred acceptance#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.Matching.GaleShapley.Inv

    Let each proposer \(m\) have a ranking \(\sigma_m\) of the \(n\) receivers and each receiver \(w\) a ranking \(\tau_w\) of the \(n\) proposers, where \(\sigma_m(k)\) is the choice of \(m\) of rank \(k\) (rank \(0\) is the best) and \(\tau_w^{-1}(x)\) is the rank of \(x\) for \(w\). A state \(s\) of deferred acceptance satisfies the invariant when: every proposer has made at most \(n\) proposals; if \(w\) holds \(m\), then \(m\) has made \(k + 1\) proposals for some rank \(k\) with \(\sigma_m(k) = w\); no proposer is held by two different receivers; for every proposer \(m\) and every rank \(k\) less than the number of proposals of \(m\), the receiver \(w = \sigma_m(k)\) holds some \(m'\) with \(\tau_w^{-1}(m') \le \tau_w^{-1}(m)\); and for such \(m\) and \(k\), if \(\sigma_m(k)\) does not hold \(m\), then no perfect matching that is stable for the preferences given by the rankings matches \(m\) with \(\sigma_m(k)\).
