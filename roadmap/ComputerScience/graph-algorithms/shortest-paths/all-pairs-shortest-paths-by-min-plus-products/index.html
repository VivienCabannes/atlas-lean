    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.FloydWarshall.IsEdgeBoundedShortestPathWeight

    Let \(w\) be an \(n \times n\) matrix of weights in \(\mathbb{Z} \cup \{\infty\}\) on the vertices \(\{0, \dots, n-1\}\), let \(r\) be a natural number, let \(i\) and \(j\) be vertices, and let \(d \in \mathbb{Z} \cup \{\infty\}\). A weighted path from \(i\) to \(j\) is a nonempty list \(p\) of vertices that starts at \(i\), ends at \(j\) and has finite weight on each pair of consecutive vertices; its number of edges is its length minus one, and its weight \(w(p)\) is the sum of the weights of its edges. Then \(d\) is the shortest weight of a path from \(i\) to \(j\) with at most \(r\) edges when \(d \le w(p)\) for every weighted path \(p\) from \(i\) to \(j\) with at most \(r\) edges, and either \(d = \infty\) or \(d = w(p)\) for some weighted path \(p\) from \(i\) to \(j\) with at most \(r\) edges.

    Paths may repeat vertices; no absence-of-negative-cycles hypothesis is needed.

    @[expose] public def IsEdgeBoundedShortestPathWeight {n : Nat}
    (weight : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) (WithTop Int))
    (r : Nat) (i j : Fin n) (distance : WithTop Int) : Prop :=
  (∀ path, IsWeightedPath weight i j path → path.length - 1 ≤ r →
    distance ≤ pathWeight weight path) ∧
  (distance = ⊤ ∨ ∃ path, IsWeightedPath weight i j path ∧
    path.length - 1 ≤ r ∧ pathWeight weight path = distance)
