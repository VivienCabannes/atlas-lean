    StructureDining-philosophers protocol#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Concurrency.DiningPhilosophers.Protocol

    A protocol for three philosophers \(i \in \{0, 1, 2\}\) sitting between forks \(0, 1, 2\) consists of two maps \(\mathrm{first}, \mathrm{second}\) from \(\{0, 1, 2\}\) to itself, the fork philosopher \(i\) picks up first and the one it picks up second, such that for every \(i\) these are its two neighbouring forks \(i\) and \(i + 1\) (modulo \(3\)) in some order: either \(\mathrm{first}(i) = i\) and \(\mathrm{second}(i) = i + 1\), or \(\mathrm{first}(i) = i + 1\) and \(\mathrm{second}(i) = i\).

    DefinitionWhen a philosopher can move#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Concurrency.DiningPhilosophers.canMove

    Given a protocol and a state, philosopher \(i\) can move exactly when it is thinking (phase \(0\)) and its first fork is not held by any philosopher, or it holds its first fork (phase \(1\)) and its second fork is not held by any philosopher, or it is eating (phase \(2\)), in which case its move puts both forks down.

    def canMove (P : Protocol) (s : State) (i : Fin 3) : Bool :=

  | 1 => !held P s (P.second i)
  | _ => true
