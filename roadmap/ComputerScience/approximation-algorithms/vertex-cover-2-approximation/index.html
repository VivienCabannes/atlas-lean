    DefinitionVertex cover scan#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.TimeM.vertexCoverScan

    Given a finite set \(C\) of vertices and a list of pairs of vertices, the scan processes the pairs in order, starting from the cover \(C\) and charging one time unit per pair. A pair \((u, v)\) is covered when \(u\) or \(v\) is already in the current cover; a covered pair is skipped, and an uncovered pair adds both \(u\) and \(v\) to the cover. The scan returns the cover reached after the last pair.

    def vertexCoverScan (cover : Finset V) : List (V × V) → TimeM ℕ (Finset V)
  | [] => pure cover
  | (u, v) :: es => do
      ✓
      if u ∈ cover ∨ v ∈ cover then vertexCoverScan cover es
      else vertexCoverScan (insert u (insert v cover)) es

    DefinitionAPPROX-VERTEX-COVER#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.TimeM.approxVertexCover

    Given a list \(L\) of ordered pairs of vertices, APPROX-VERTEX-COVER starts from the empty cover and goes through \(L\) in order: a pair \((u, v)\) with \(u\) or \(v\) already in the cover is skipped, and otherwise both \(u\) and \(v\) are added to the cover. It returns the final cover, a finite set of vertices, at a cost of one unit per pair of \(L\).

    CLRS (third edition, Section 35.1) takes an arbitrary uncovered edge at each step; here it is the first uncovered pair in list order.

    def approxVertexCover (edges : List (V × V)) : TimeM ℕ (Finset V) :=
  vertexCoverScan ∅ edges

    TheoremCounting invariant of the vertex cover scan#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.TimeM.card_vertexCoverScan_le

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph, \(S\) a vertex cover of \(G\), \(C_0\) a finite set of vertices with \(|C_0| \le 2|C_0 \cap S|\), and \(L\) a list of ordered pairs of vertices such that \(u\) and \(v\) are adjacent in \(G\) for every \((u, v)\) in \(L\). Then the cover \(C\) that the vertex cover scan returns on \(L\) starting from \(C_0\) satisfies \(|C| \le 2|C \cap S|\).

    The invariant holds because each taken edge adds two new vertices, at least one of them in \(S\).

    theorem card_vertexCoverScan_le (G : SimpleGraph V) (S : Set V) (hS : G.IsVertexCover S)
    [DecidablePred (· ∈ S)] (cover : Finset V) (edges : List (V × V))
    (hsound : ∀ e ∈ edges, G.Adj e.1 e.2)
    (hcover : cover.card ≤ 2 * (cover.filter (· ∈ S)).card) :
    (vertexCoverScan cover edges).ret.card ≤
      2 * ((vertexCoverScan cover edges).ret.filter (· ∈ S)).card

    TheoremAPPROX-VERTEX-COVER is a 2-approximation#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.TimeM.approxVertexCover_two_approximation

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph and \(L\) a list of ordered pairs of vertices that lists exactly the edges of \(G\), each in at least one orientation: \(u\) and \(v\) are adjacent in \(G\) for every \((u, v)\) in \(L\), and for every edge \(uv\) of \(G\), \((u, v)\) or \((v, u)\) is in \(L\).     Two pairs \((a, b)\) and \((c, d)\) of vertices are vertex-disjoint when they share no endpoint: \(a \ne c\), \(a \ne d\), \(b \ne c\) and \(b \ne d\).

    def VertexDisjoint (e f : V × V) : Prop :=

    TheoremMatching invariant of the vertex cover scan#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.TimeM.exists_matching_vertexCoverScan

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph, \(C_0\) a finite set of vertices, and \(L\) a list of ordered pairs of vertices such that \(u\) and \(v\) are adjacent in \(G\) for every \((u, v)\) in \(L\), and let \(C\) be the cover that the vertex cover scan returns on \(L\) starting from \(C_0\). Then there is a sublist \(A\) of \(L\) such that neither endpoint of any pair in \(A\) lies in \(C_0\), pairs at different positions of \(A\) are vertex-disjoint, a vertex lies in \(C\) exactly when it lies in \(C_0\) or is an endpoint of some pair in \(A\), and \(|C| = |C_0| + 2|A|\), where \(|A|\) is the length of \(A\).

    theorem exists_matching_vertexCoverScan (G : SimpleGraph V) (cover : Finset V)
    (edges : List (V × V)) (hsound : ∀ e ∈ edges, G.Adj e.1 e.2) :
    ∃ A : List (V × V), A.Sublist edges ∧ (∀ e ∈ A, e.1 ∉ cover ∧ e.2 ∉ cover) ∧
      A.Pairwise VertexDisjoint ∧
      (∀ x, x ∈ (vertexCoverScan cover edges).ret ↔ x ∈ cover ∨ ∃ e ∈ A, x = e.1 ∨ x = e.2) ∧
      (vertexCoverScan cover edges).ret.card = cover.card + 2 * A.length

  e.1 ≠ f.1 ∧ e.1 ≠ f.2 ∧ e.2 ≠ f.1 ∧ e.2 ≠ f.2

    TheoremThe edges APPROX-VERTEX-COVER takes form a maximal matching of the listed pairs#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.TimeM.approxVertexCover_matching

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph and \(L\) a list of ordered pairs of vertices such that \(u\) and \(v\) are adjacent in \(G\) for every \((u, v)\) in \(L\), and let \(C\) be the cover that APPROX-VERTEX-COVER returns on \(L\).     theorem approxVertexCover_matching (G : SimpleGraph V) (edges : List (V × V))

      (∀ e ∈ edges, ∃ f ∈ A, e.1 = f.1 ∨ e.1 = f.2 ∨ e.2 = f.1 ∨ e.2 = f.2) ∧
      (∀ x, x ∈ (approxVertexCover edges).ret ↔ ∃ e ∈ A, x = e.1 ∨ x = e.2) ∧
      (approxVertexCover edges).ret.card = 2 * A.length
