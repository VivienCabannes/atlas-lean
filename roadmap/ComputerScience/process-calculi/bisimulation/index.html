    TheoremBisimilarity transfers multistep transitions#✓fully proved

    Cslib.LTS.HomBisimilarity.mtr

    Let \(s\) and \(t\) be bisimilar states of a labelled transition system, and suppose \(s\) reaches a state \(s'\) by a multistep transition along a finite (possibly empty) list of labels \(\mu_1, \dots, \mu_n\). Then there is a state \(t'\) such that \(t\) reaches \(t'\) by a multistep transition along the same list of labels, and \(s'\) and \(t'\) are bisimilar.

    theorem HomBisimilarity.mtr {left right left' : State} {labels : List Label}
    (hbisim : HomBisimilarity lts left right) (htr : lts.MTr left labels left') :
    ∃ right', lts.MTr right labels right' ∧ HomBisimilarity lts left' right'

    TheoremWeak bisimilarity is bisimilarity of the saturated system#✓fully proved

    Cslib.LTS.HomWeakBisimilarity.iff_homBisimilarity_saturate

    In a labelled transition system with a silent label \(\tau\), write \(\Rightarrow\) for the reflexive-transitive closure of the \(\tau\)-transition relation. The weak transitions \(s \stackrel{\mu}{\Longrightarrow} t\) are the trivial ones \(s \stackrel{\tau}{\Longrightarrow} s\) and the composites \(s \Rightarrow s_1 \xrightarrow{\mu} s_2 \Rightarrow t\); the saturated system has the same states and has the weak transitions as its transitions. Two states are weakly bisimilar if some bisimulation of the saturated system relates them. Thus two states are weakly bisimilar if and only if they are bisimilar in the saturated system; this holds by definition.

    theorem HomWeakBisimilarity.iff_homBisimilarity_saturate {left right : State} :
    HomWeakBisimilarity lts left right ↔ HomBisimilarity lts.saturate left right
