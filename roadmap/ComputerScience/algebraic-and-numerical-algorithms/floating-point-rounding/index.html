    For \(m\) in a linearly ordered field \(K\) with a floor function, \(\operatorname{rne}(m)\) is the integer \(\lfloor m \rfloor\) if \(m - \lfloor m \rfloor < 1/2\), the integer \(\lfloor m \rfloor + 1\) if \(m - \lfloor m \rfloor > 1/2\), and, when \(m - \lfloor m \rfloor = 1/2\), whichever of \(\lfloor m \rfloor\) and \(\lfloor m \rfloor + 1\) is even.

    def rne (m : K) : ℤ :=
  if m - ⌊m⌋ < 1 / 2 then ⌊m⌋
  else if 1 / 2 < m - ⌊m⌋ then ⌊m⌋ + 1
  else if Even ⌊m⌋ then ⌊m⌋ else ⌊m⌋ + 1

    DefinitionExponent of the last place#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.FloatingPoint.ulpExp

    For a natural number \(p\) (the significand length) and \(x\) in a linearly ordered field \(K\) with a floor function, the exponent of the last place of \(x\) is the integer \(\lfloor \log_2 |x| \rfloor - (p - 1)\). Here \(\lfloor \log_2 |x| \rfloor\) is the integer logarithm: the largest integer \(n\) with \(2^n \le |x|\) when \(x \ne 0\), and \(0\) when \(x = 0\).

    def ulpExp (x : K) : ℤ := Int.log 2 |x| - ((p : ℤ) - 1)
