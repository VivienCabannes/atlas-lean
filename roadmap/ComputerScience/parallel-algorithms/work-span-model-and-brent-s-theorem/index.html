    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.Parallel.Brent.depthTable

    StructureSchedule of a computation DAG#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.Parallel.Brent.Schedule

    A schedule of a computation DAG on \(n\) tasks on \(p\) processors assigns to each task \(v\) a step \(t(v) \in \mathbb{N}\) in which it runs, such that \(t(u) < t(v)\) whenever \(u\) is a predecessor of \(v\), and for every step \(s\) at most \(p\) tasks \(v\) have \(t(v) = s\).

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.Parallel.Brent.levelTime

    Let \(G\) be a computation DAG and \(p\) a natural number.
