    Cslib.MachineLearning.GBLUP.condDistrib_markerKernel

    If the genetic values Z' β of individuals with genotypes Z' and the phenotypes y = Z β + ε of the training individuals are jointly Gaussian with the covariance of the marker kernel (marker-effect variance s > 0, residual variance σ² > 0), then given y the genetic values are Gaussian with mean Z' Zᵀ (ZZᵀ + (σ²/s) I)⁻¹ y, which is the RR-BLUP prediction Z' β̂ (gpMean_markerKernel_mulVec), and covariance s (Z' Z'ᵀ - Z' Zᵀ (ZZᵀ + (σ²/s) I)⁻¹ Z Z'ᵀ).
