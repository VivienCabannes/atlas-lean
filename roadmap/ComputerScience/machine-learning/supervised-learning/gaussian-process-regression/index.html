    Cslib.MachineLearning.GaussianProcessRegression.condDistrib_test_given_targets

    Let \(k : X \times X \to \mathbb{R}\) be a kernel, let \(x^* = (x^*_i)_{i \in \tau}\) (test inputs) and \(x = (x_j)_{j \in \nu}\) (training inputs) be finite families in \(X\), and write \(K(a, b)\) for the kernel matrix \((k(a_i, b_j))_{i, j}\). Assume that the kernel matrix of the combined family \((x^*, x)\), indexed by \(\tau \sqcup \nu\), is positive semidefinite, and let \(\sigma^2 > 0\). Let \(V\) be a measurable random vector in \(\mathbb{R}^{\tau \sqcup \nu}\) on a probability space whose law is the Gaussian \(N(0, \Sigma)\), where \(\Sigma\) is the joint covariance of \(k\), \(\sigma^2\), \(x^*\) and \(x\), and let \(F\) (the test values) and \(Y\) (the training targets), random vectors in \(\mathbb{R}^{\tau}\) and \(\mathbb{R}^{\nu}\), be the test and training blocks of \(V\). Then for almost every \(y\) with respect to the law of \(Y\), the conditional distribution of \(F\) given \(Y = y\) is the Gaussian with mean \(K(x^*, x) (K(x, x) + \sigma^2 I)^{-1} y\) and covariance \(K(x^*, x^*) - K(x^*, x) (K(x, x) + \sigma^2 I)^{-1} K(x, x^*)\).

    (hlaw : P.map V = multivariateGaussian 0 (jointCov k σ2 xt xs)) :
