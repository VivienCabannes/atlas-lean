    DefinitionFinite Kullback–Leibler formula#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Variational.kl

    Let \(Z\) be a finite set and let \(q, r : Z \to \mathbb{R}\). The Kullback–Leibler formula of \(q\) against \(r\) is \(\mathrm{KL}(q \,\|\, r) = \sum_{z \in Z} q(z) \bigl(\log q(z) - \log r(z)\bigr)\); terms with \(q(z) = 0\) vanish.

    This is the Kullback–Leibler divergence of \(q\) from \(r\) when \(r(z) > 0\) for every \(z\), the only case used here (where \(r\) is the posterior of a positive joint). If \(r\) vanishes where \(q\) does not, the divergence is infinite, but this real-valued formula is finite, since it reads \(\log 0\) as \(0\).

    noncomputable def kl (q r : Z → ℝ) : ℝ := ∑ z, q z * (log (q z) - log (r z))

    DefinitionMean-field (CAVI) update of the first factor#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Variational.cavi

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be finite sets, let \(p : A \times B \to \mathbb{R}\) be a joint and let \(q_2 : B \to \mathbb{R}\). The coordinate-ascent update of the first factor is the function \(q_1 : A \to \mathbb{R}\) given by

    \[q_1(a) = \frac{\exp E(a)}{\sum_{a' \in A} \exp E(a')},\]

    noncomputable def cavi (p : A × B → ℝ) (q₂ : B → ℝ) (a : A) : ℝ :=
  exp (expectedLog p q₂ a) / ∑ a', exp (expectedLog p q₂ a')

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Variational.elbo_prodDist

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be finite sets, let \(p : A \times B \to \mathbb{R}\) be a joint, and let \(q_1\) and \(q_2\) be probability distributions on \(A\) and \(B\), with product \((q_1 q_2)(a, b) = q_1(a) q_2(b)\). Then
