    DefinitionBayes posterior#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Bayesian.bayesPosterior

    Let \(\kappa\) be a kernel from a measurable space \(\Theta\) of hypotheses to a measurable space \(\mathcal X\) of observations (the likelihood) and \(\mu\) a measure on \(\Theta\) (the prior). The Bayes posterior after observing \(x \in \mathcal X\) is the measure \(\pi_x\) on \(\Theta\) with

    \[\pi_x(s) = E(x)^{-1} \int_s \kappa_\theta(\{x\}) \, d\mu(\theta)\]

    for every measurable \(s \subseteq \Theta\), where \(E(x) = (\kappa \circ \mu)(\{x\})\) is the evidence of \(x\): the mass of \(\{x\}\) under the composition \(\kappa \circ \mu\) of \(\mu\) with \(\kappa\), the marginal law of the observation. The inverse is taken in \([0, \infty]\), with \(0^{-1} = \infty\), \(\infty^{-1} = 0\) and \(\infty \cdot 0 = 0\); in particular \(\pi_x\) is the zero measure when the evidence is zero.

    noncomputable def bayesPosterior (κ : Kernel Θ 𝓧) (μ : Measure Θ) (x : 𝓧) : Measure Θ :=
  ((κ ∘ₘ μ) {x})⁻¹ • μ.withDensity fun θ ↦ κ θ {x}

    Let \(\Theta\) be a countable measurable space and \(\mathcal X\) a measurable space, both with measurable singletons, let \(\kappa\) be a kernel from \(\Theta\) to \(\mathcal X\) (the likelihood) and \(\mu\) a measure on \(\Theta\) (the prior).     \[\pi_x(\{\theta\}) = \frac{\mu(\{\theta\}) \, \kappa_\theta(\{x\})} {\sum_{\theta' \in \Theta} \mu(\{\theta'\}) \, \kappa_{\theta'}(\{x\})},\]

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Bayesian.isMAP_iff

    Let \(\Theta\) and \(\mathcal X\) be countable measurable spaces with measurable singletons, \(\kappa\) a Markov kernel from \(\Theta\) to \(\mathcal X\), \(\mu\) a finite measure on \(\Theta\), and \(x \in \mathcal X\) an observation with \((\kappa \circ \mu)(\{x\}) \neq 0\), where \(\kappa \circ \mu\) is the composition of \(\mu\) with \(\kappa\).
