    Let \(Z\) be a finite set, let \(c \colon Z \to \mathbb{R}\) be nonnegative counts with positive total \(C = \sum_{z \in Z} c_z > 0\), and let \(w \colon Z \to \mathbb{R}\) be positive weights with \(\sum_{z \in Z} w_z = 1\).     DefinitionJoint distribution of the Bernoulli mixture#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.LatentVariable.BernoulliMixture.joint

    For valid parameters \(\theta\) of a mixture of \(K\) products of \(d\) Bernoulli distributions, with mixing weights \(\pi_k\) and success probabilities \(p_{kj}\), the joint probability of a binary vector \(x \in \{0, 1\}^d\) and a component \(k\) is

    \[p_\theta(x, k) = \pi_k \prod_{j=1}^{d} p_{kj}^{x_j} (1 - p_{kj})^{1 - x_j},\]

    noncomputable def joint (θ : ValidParams d K) (x : Fin d → Bool) (k : Fin K) : ℝ :=
  θ.1.weight k * bernPMF (θ.1.prob k) x

    Cslib.MachineLearning.LatentVariable.BernoulliMixture.mStep

    TheoremDecomposition of the EM objective for the Bernoulli mixture#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.LatentVariable.BernoulliMixture.emQ_eq

    Let \(x_1, \dots, x_N \in \{0, 1\}^d\) be data and let \(\theta\) and \(\theta'\) be valid parameters of a mixture of \(K\) products of \(d\) Bernoulli distributions (positive mixing weights summing to one and success probabilities in \((0, 1)\)), where \(\theta'\) has mixing weights \(\pi'_k\) and success probabilities \(p'_{kj}\). Write \(p_\theta(x, k)\) for the joint probability of \(x\) and component \(k\) under \(\theta\) (the mixing weight of \(k\) times the product Bernoulli probability of \(x\)), \(r_{nk} = p_\theta(k \mid x_n) = p_\theta(x_n, k) / \sum_l p_\theta(x_n, l)\) for the posterior probability of component \(k\) given \(x_n\), \(N_k = \sum_n r_{nk}\), and \(A_{kj}\) and \(B_{kj}\) for the sums of \(r_{nk}\) over the points \(x_n\) whose \(j\)-th coordinate \(x_{nj}\) is \(1\) and is \(0\) respectively. Then the EM objective \(Q(\theta' \mid \theta) = \sum_n \sum_k r_{nk} \log p_{\theta'}(x_n, k)\) equals

    \[\sum_{k=1}^{K} \Big( N_k \log \pi'_k + \sum_{j=1}^{d} \big( A_{kj} \log p'_{kj} + B_{kj} \log (1 - p'_{kj}) \big) \Big).\]

    The first term depends only on the mixing weights and each remaining term on a single \(p'_{kj}\).

    theorem emQ_eq (θ' θ : ValidParams d K) :
    emQ joint xs θ' θ = ∑ k, (mass xs θ k * log (θ'.1.weight k) +
      ∑ j, (ones xs θ k j * log (θ'.1.prob k j) + zeros xs θ k j * log (1 - θ'.1.prob k j)))
