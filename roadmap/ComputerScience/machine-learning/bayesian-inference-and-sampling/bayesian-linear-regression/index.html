    DefinitionThe linear regression likelihood density#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Bayesian.linRegLikelihoodPDFReal

    For \(\sigma^2 \ge 0\), \(x, y \in \mathbb{R}^n\) and \(w \in \mathbb{R}\), it is the real number \(\prod_i \varphi(y_i; w x_i, \sigma^2)\), where \(\varphi(t; \mu, \tau) = (2\pi\tau)^{-1/2} e^{-(t - \mu)^2 / (2\tau)}\) is the Gaussian density with mean \(\mu\) and variance \(\tau\) at \(t\), read as \(0\) when \(\tau = 0\).     Cslib.MachineLearning.Bayesian.linRegEvidencePDFReal

    For \(m_0 \in \mathbb{R}\), \(s_0, \sigma^2 \ge 0\) and \(x, y \in \mathbb{R}^n\), let \(m\) and \(v\) be the posterior mean and variance of the weight, write \(\varphi(t; \mu, \tau) = (2\pi\tau)^{-1/2} e^{-(t - \mu)^2 / (2\tau)}\) for the Gaussian density with mean \(\mu\) and variance \(\tau\) at \(t\) (read as \(0\) when \(\tau = 0\)), and let \(L(y \mid w) = \prod_i \varphi(y_i; w x_i, \sigma^2)\) be the likelihood density. The evidence density at \(y\) is

    \[Z(y) = \frac{\varphi(m; m_0, s_0) \, L(y \mid m)}{\varphi(m; m, v)},\]

    prior density times likelihood divided by the posterior density, all at the posterior mean (a quotient by \(0\) is read as \(0\)).

    For \(s_0 \ne 0\) and \(\sigma^2 \ne 0\) the ratio does not depend on the point where it is evaluated, and \(Z\) is the Lebesgue density of the marginal law of the data. It is not a density of the evidence in the degenerate cases.

  gaussianPDFReal m₀ s₀ (linRegPostMean m₀ s₀ σ2 x y) *
      linRegLikelihoodPDFReal σ2 x (linRegPostMean m₀ s₀ σ2 x y) y /
    gaussianPDFReal (linRegPostMean m₀ s₀ σ2 x y) (linRegPostVar s₀ σ2 x)

    DefinitionThe likelihood of Bayesian linear regression#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Bayesian.linRegLikelihood

    The likelihood of Bayesian linear regression with one feature and known noise variance \(\sigma^2 \ge 0\), for fixed inputs \(x \in \mathbb{R}^n\): the kernel from the weight \(w \in \mathbb{R}\) to the responses in \(\mathbb{R}^n\) under which, given \(w\), the responses are independent with \(y_i \sim \mathcal{N}(w x_i, \sigma^2)\). For \(\sigma^2 \ne 0\) it is given by the density \(\prod_i \varphi(y_i; w x_i, \sigma^2)\) with respect to Lebesgue measure, where \(\varphi(t; \mu, \tau)\) is the Gaussian density with mean \(\mu\) and variance \(\tau\) at \(t\); for \(\sigma^2 = 0\) it sends \(w\) to the Dirac measure at \((w x_i)_i\).

    noncomputable def linRegLikelihood (σ2 : ℝ≥0) (x : Fin n → ℝ) : Kernel ℝ (Fin n → ℝ) :=
  if σ2 = 0 then Kernel.deterministic (fun w i ↦ w * x i) (by fun_prop)
  else (Kernel.const ℝ volume).withDensity (linRegLikelihoodPDF σ2 x)
