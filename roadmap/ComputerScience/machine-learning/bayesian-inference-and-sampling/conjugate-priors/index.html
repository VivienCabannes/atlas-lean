    Cslib.MachineLearning.Bayesian.categoricalKernel

    For \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), the categorical likelihood is the kernel from parameters \(\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}\) to labels \(\{0, \dots, n\}\) that sends a probability vector \(\theta\) (all \(\theta_i \ge 0\) and \(\sum_i \theta_i = 1\)) to the law of one label drawn with probability \(\theta_i\) for label \(i\), and sends every other \(\theta\) to the point mass at label \(0\).
