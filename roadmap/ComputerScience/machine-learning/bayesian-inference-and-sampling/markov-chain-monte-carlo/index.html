    Cslib.MachineLearning.Sampling.mhStep

    For a probability distribution \(\pi\) on \(\alpha\) (the target) and a proposal \(Q\) assigning to each \(x \in \alpha\) a probability distribution \(Q(x, \cdot)\) on \(\alpha\), one Metropolis–Hastings step from \(x\) is the distribution of the following move: draw \(y\) from \(Q(x, \cdot)\), then move to \(y\) with the Metropolis–Hastings acceptance probability \(a(x, y)\), and otherwise stay at \(x\).

    DefinitionConditional law of a coordinate#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.Sampling.condLaw

    Let \(\pi\) be a probability distribution on the configurations \(x : \iota \to S\), with \(\iota\) and \(S\) finite, such that \(\pi(x) \neq 0\) for every \(x\). For a coordinate \(i \in \iota\) and a configuration \(x\), the conditional law of coordinate \(i\) given the others is the probability distribution on \(S\) that gives \(s \in S\) the mass \(\pi(x[i \mapsto s]) / Z_i(x)\), where \(x[i \mapsto s]\) is \(x\) with coordinate \(i\) set to \(s\) and \(Z_i(x) = \sum_{t \in S} \pi(x[i \mapsto t])\). In the usual notation this is \(\pi(x_i = s \mid x_{-i})\), where \(x_i\) is coordinate \(i\) of \(x\) and \(x_{-i}\) is the family of its other coordinates.

    noncomputable def condLaw (π : PMF (ι → S)) (hπ : ∀ x, π x ≠ 0) (i : ι) (x : ι → S) : PMF S :=
  PMF.ofFintype (fun s ↦ π (update x i s) / condNorm π i x) (by
    simp_rw [div_eq_mul_inv]
    rw [← Finset.sum_mul]
    exact ENNReal.mul_inv_cancel (ne_bot_of_le_ne_bot (hπ x) (le_condNorm π i x))
      (condNorm_ne_top π i x))
