    DefinitionAdaBoost's combined score#✓fully proved

    Cslib.MachineLearning.AdaBoost.score

    After \(T\) rounds, the combined score of training point \(i\) is

    \[F_T(i) = \sum_{t < T} \alpha(\varepsilon_t)\, h_t(i),\]

    where \(\varepsilon_t\) is the weighted error and \(h_t\) the weak hypothesis of round \(t\), \(\alpha(\varepsilon) = \frac{1}{2} \log \frac{1 - \varepsilon}{\varepsilon}\) is the hypothesis weight, and the prediction \(h_t(i)\) is read as \(\pm 1\). The final classifier is the sign of \(F_T\).

    noncomputable def score (T : ℕ) (i : Fin m) : ℝ :=
  ∑ t ∈ Finset.range T, alpha (eps y wl t) * sign (hyp y wl t i)
    noncomputable def alpha (ε : ℝ) : ℝ := log ((1 - ε) / ε) / 2
