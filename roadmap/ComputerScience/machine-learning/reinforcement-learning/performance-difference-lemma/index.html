    TheoremPerformance difference lemma#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.MarkovDecision.performance_difference

    Let \(M\) be a Markov decision process with a finite set of states \(S\), a finite set of actions \(A\), real rewards \(r(s,a)\) and transition probabilities \(P(s,a,s')\) that are nonnegative and sum to \(1\) over \(s'\), and let \(0 \le \gamma < 1\). For all stochastic policies \(\pi, \pi'\) and every state \(s_0\),

    \[V^{\pi'}(s_0) - V^\pi(s_0) = \frac{1}{1-\gamma}\, \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d^{\pi'}_{s_0}}\, \mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi'(\cdot \mid s)} \bigl[A^\pi(s,a)\bigr].\]

    The value gap between two policies is the advantage of one over the other, averaged along the state distribution of the first. It underlies policy iteration, conservative policy iteration and the analysis of policy gradient methods. The policy improvement theorem for deterministic policies is a special case of its consequence policy improvement for stochastic policies.

    theorem performance_difference (π π' : StochasticPolicy S A) (s₀ : S) :
    stochValue M γ hM hγ0 hγ1 π' s₀ - stochValue M γ hM hγ0 hγ1 π s₀ =
      (1 - γ)⁻¹ * ∑ s, occupancy hM hγ0 hγ1 π' s₀ s *
        ∑ a, π'.prob s a * advantage hM hγ0 hγ1 π s a
    abbrev StochasticPolicy (S A : Type*) := S → StdSimplex ℝ A
