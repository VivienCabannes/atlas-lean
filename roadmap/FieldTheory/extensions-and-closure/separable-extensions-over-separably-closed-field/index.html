    Algebra.TensorProduct.algHom_linearIndependent

    Let \(K\), \(L\) and \(\Omega\) be fields such that \(L\) and \(\Omega\) are \(K\)-algebras. Then the \(K\)-algebra homomorphisms \(L \to \Omega\), viewed as functions \(L \to \Omega\), are linearly independent over \(\Omega\).

    This is an instance of Dedekind's theorem on the linear independence of characters.

    theorem algHom_linearIndependent :
    LinearIndependent Ω (fun (σ : L →ₐ[K] Ω) (l : L) => σ l)

    Algebra.TensorProduct.column_functions_linearIndependent

    Let \(L/K\) be a finite separable field extension of degree \(n\), let \(\Omega\) be a separably closed field that is a \(K\)-algebra, and write \(\mathrm{Hom}_K(L, \Omega)\) for the set of \(K\)-algebra homomorphisms \(L \to \Omega\).
