    DefinitionDescent of the general polynomial extension#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.FieldTheory.EssentialDimensionSymmetric.IsDescent

    For a natural number \(n\), let \(E = \mathbb{C}(x_1, \dots, x_n)\) be the field of rational functions in \(n\) indeterminates and let \(F = \mathbb{C}(a_1, \dots, a_n) \subseteq E\) be the coefficient field of the general polynomial, where \(a_k\) is the \(k\)-th elementary symmetric function of \(x_1, \dots, x_n\).
