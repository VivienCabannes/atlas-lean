    TheoremBertrand's postulate for Gaussian integers#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.gaussianInt_exists_prime_norm_between

    Let \(N(a + bi) = a^2 + b^2\) be the norm on the Gaussian integers \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\). For every prime element \(\pi\) of \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\) with \(N(\pi) \ne 2\) (that is, \(\pi\) is not an associate of \(1 + i\)) there is a prime element \(\rho\) of \(\mathbb{Z}[i]\) with \(N(\pi) < N(\rho) \le 2N(\pi)\).

    public theorem_wanted gaussianInt_exists_prime_norm_between {π : GaussianInt} (hπ : Prime π)
    (hπ₂ : π.norm ≠ 2) :
    ∃ ρ : GaussianInt, Prime ρ ∧ π.norm < ρ.norm ∧ ρ.norm ≤ 2 * π.norm

    TheoremSchatunowsky's theorem#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.nat_schatunowsky

    The natural numbers \(a \ge 2\) such that every natural number \(b\) with \(1 < b < a\) and \(\gcd(a, b) = 1\) is prime are exactly \(2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 18, 24\) and \(30\).

    The numbers \(a = 0\) and \(a = 1\), for which no such \(b\) exists, are excluded.

    public theorem_wanted nat_schatunowsky :
    {a : ℕ | 2 ≤ a ∧ ∀ b : ℕ, 1 < b → b < a → Nat.Coprime a b → b.Prime} =
      {2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 18, 24, 30}

    DefinitionNorm of the \(n\)-th prime#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.quadPrimeNorm

    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\). For \(n \ge 1\), \(N(\pi_n)\) is the \(n\)-th smallest natural number that is the absolute norm of some nonzero prime ideal of \(\mathcal{O}_K\), each such norm counted once however many prime ideals have it; and \(N(\pi_0) = 1\).

    For an imaginary quadratic field \(K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})\) of class number one the prime ideals above distinct rational primes \(p\) have distinct norms \(p\) or \(p^2\), so \(N(\pi_n)\) is the norm of the \(n\)-th prime \(\pi_n\) of the paper's norm-ordered set \(P_m^+\) (Definition 1), and \(N(\pi_0) = 1\) matches its convention \(\pi_0 = 1\).

    public noncomputable def quadPrimeNorm (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  if n = 0 then 1
  else Nat.nth (fun x => ∃ P : Ideal (𝓞 K), P.IsPrime ∧ P ≠ ⊥ ∧ Ideal.absNorm P = x) (n - 1)

    MetaMathlibExt.quadPrimorialNorm

    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\), and let \(n\) be a natural number. The primorial norm \(N(\pi_n\#)\) is the product of the absolute norms of the nonzero prime ideals of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) whose norm is at most \(N(\pi_n)\), where \(N(\pi_n)\) is the \(n\)-th smallest norm of a nonzero prime ideal of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) for \(n \ge 1\) and \(N(\pi_0) = 1\).

  ∏ᶠ P ∈ {P : Ideal (𝓞 K) | P.IsPrime ∧ P ≠ ⊥ ∧ Ideal.absNorm P ≤ quadPrimeNorm K n},
    Ideal.absNorm P

    DefinitionSchatunowsky set of a number field#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.quadSchatunowskySet

    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\) and let \(\ell\) be a natural number. For \(\alpha \in \mathcal{O}_K\) write \(N(\alpha)\) for the absolute norm of the principal ideal \((\alpha)\) and \(\Omega(\alpha)\) for the number of prime ideal factors of \((\alpha)\) counted with multiplicity. The Schatunowsky set \(Q_\ell(K)\) is the set of \(\alpha \in \mathcal{O}_K\) with \(N(\alpha) > 1\) such that every \(\beta \in \mathcal{O}_K\) coprime to \(\alpha\) (that is, \((\alpha) + (\beta) = \mathcal{O}_K\)) with \(1 < N(\beta) < N(\alpha)\) satisfies \(\Omega(\beta) \le \ell\).

    The set is closed under multiplication by units. For \(K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{m})\) imaginary quadratic of class number one it is the paper's set \(Q_{m,\ell}\) (Equation (4)).

    public def quadSchatunowskySet (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] (ℓ : ℕ) : Set (𝓞 K) :=
  {α | 1 < Ideal.absNorm (Ideal.span {α}) ∧ ∀ β : 𝓞 K,
      1 < Ideal.absNorm (Ideal.span {β}) →
      Ideal.absNorm (Ideal.span {β}) < Ideal.absNorm (Ideal.span {α}) →
    IsCoprime α β → (UniqueFactorizationMonoid.normalizedFactors (Ideal.span {β})).card ≤ ℓ}

    TheoremRatios of consecutive prime ideal norms in an imaginary quadratic field tend to \(1\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.tendsto_quadPrimeNorm_succ_div

    Let \(K\) be an imaginary quadratic field, that is, a totally complex number field of degree \(2\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\), with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\). Let \(N(\pi_m)\) be the \(m\)-th smallest element of the set of absolute norms of nonzero prime ideals of \(\mathcal{O}_K\).
