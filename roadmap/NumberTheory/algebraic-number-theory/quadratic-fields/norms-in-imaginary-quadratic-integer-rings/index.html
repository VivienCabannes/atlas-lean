    Let \(d < 0\) be an integer such that the ring \(\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt d]\) of elements \(a + b\sqrt d\) (\(a, b \in \mathbb{Z}\)) is a unique factorization domain with exactly \(k\) units, and let \(N(a + b\sqrt d) = a^2 - db^2\) be the norm. Assume that for each \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) the set of \(\alpha \in \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt d]\) with \(|N(\alpha)| = n\) is finite, and let \(r(n)\) be its size. Let \(D : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}\) satisfy \(D(1) = 1\) and \(D(mn) = D(m)\,D(n)\) for all coprime \(m, n\). If \(r(p^e) = k\,D(p^e)\) for every prime \(p\) and every \(e \ge 1\), then \(r(n) = k\,D(n)\) for every \(n \ne 0\).
