    Let \(p > 3\) be a prime and let \(K\) be a number field of degree \(2\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) that contains a square root of \(-p\), with class number \(h\). Let \(B_n\) be the Bernoulli numbers, \(t/(e^t - 1) = \sum_n B_n t^n/n!\), and \(E_n\) the Euler numbers, \(2/(e^t + e^{-t}) = \sum_n E_n t^n/n!\) (so \(E_2 = -1\)). If \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), then \(p\) divides the numerator, in lowest terms, of the rational number \(h + 2B_{(p+1)/2}\). If \(p \equiv 1 \pmod 4\), then \(p\) divides the integer \(2h - E_{(p-1)/2}\).

    Since the denominator of \(B_{(p+1)/2}\) is prime to \(p\) for \(p > 3\) (von Staudt–Clausen) and \(p\) is odd, these are the congruences \(h \equiv -2B_{(p+1)/2} \pmod p\) and \(h \equiv E_{(p-1)/2}/2 \pmod p\).
