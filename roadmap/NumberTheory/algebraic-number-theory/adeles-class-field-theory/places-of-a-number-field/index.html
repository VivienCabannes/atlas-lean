    NumberField.FinitePlace.normalizedAbsoluteValue

    Let \(K\) be a number field of degree \(d = [K : \mathbb{Q}]\) and let \(v\) be a finite place of \(K\), with absolute value \(|\cdot|_v\). The degree-normalized absolute value at \(v\) is the absolute value \(x \mapsto |x|_v^{1/d}\) on \(K\).

    The finite-place absolute value \(|\cdot|_v\) already contains the local-degree factor, so the remaining normalization exponent is \(1/d\).

    noncomputable def FinitePlace.normalizedAbsoluteValue (v : FinitePlace K) :
    AbsoluteValue K ℝ := by
  refine v.val.rpow (1 / (Module.finrank ℚ K : ℝ)) ?_ ?_
  · have hfinrank : 0 < (Module.finrank ℚ K : ℝ) := by
      exact_mod_cast Module.finrank_pos (R := ℚ) (M := K)
    positivity
  · have hfinrank : 0 < (Module.finrank ℚ K : ℝ) := by
      exact_mod_cast Module.finrank_pos (R := ℚ) (M := K)
    rw [div_le_one hfinrank]
    exact_mod_cast Module.finrank_pos (R := ℚ) (M := K)

    NumberField.FinitePlace.normalizedAbsoluteValue_apply_eq_adicAbv

    Let \(K\) be a number field of degree \(d = [K : \mathbb{Q}]\), let \(v\) be a finite place of \(K\), and let \(\mathfrak{p}\) be the nonzero prime ideal of the ring of integers of \(K\) that defines \(v\). For every \(x \in K\), the degree-normalized absolute value of \(x\) at \(v\) equals \(|x|_{\mathfrak{p}}^{1/d}\), where \(|x|_{\mathfrak{p}} = N(\mathfrak{p})^{-v_{\mathfrak{p}}(x)}\) for \(x \neq 0\) and \(|0|_{\mathfrak{p}} = 0\), with \(N(\mathfrak{p})\) the absolute norm of \(\mathfrak{p}\) and \(v_{\mathfrak{p}}\) the \(\mathfrak{p}\)-adic valuation.

    theorem FinitePlace.normalizedAbsoluteValue_apply_eq_adicAbv (v : FinitePlace K) (x : K) :
    v.normalizedAbsoluteValue x =
      (HeightOneSpectrum.adicAbv K v.maximalIdeal x) ^
        (1 / (Module.finrank ℚ K : ℝ))

    DefinitionPlaces of a number field#✓fully proved

    NumberField.Place

    For a number field \(K\), the places of \(K\) form the disjoint union of the infinite places of \(K\) (the absolute values coming from complex embeddings of \(K\)) and the finite places of \(K\) (the absolute values coming from nonzero prime ideals of the ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\)).

    def Place (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] : Type _ :=
  InfinitePlace K ⊕ FinitePlace K

    NumberField.FinitePlace.residueNorm

    Let \(v\) be a finite place of a number field \(K\) and \(\mathfrak{p}_v\) the corresponding nonzero prime ideal of the ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\). The residue norm \(N(v)\) is the absolute norm \(|\mathcal{O}_K / \mathfrak{p}_v|\) of \(\mathfrak{p}_v\), the cardinality of the residue field at \(v\), regarded as a real number.

    noncomputable def residueNorm {K : Type*} [Field K] [NumberField K]
    (v : FinitePlace K) : ℝ :=
  (Ideal.absNorm v.maximalIdeal.asIdeal : ℝ)
