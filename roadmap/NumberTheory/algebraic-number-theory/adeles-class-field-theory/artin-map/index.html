    NumberField.Modulus.CoprimePrimes

    Let \(\mathfrak m\) be a modulus of a number field \(K\). The primes coprime to \(\mathfrak m\) are the nonzero prime ideals \(\mathfrak p\) of the ring of integers \(\mathcal O_K\) that do not contain the finite part of \(\mathfrak m\), that is, that do not divide it. They index the generators of a free group that maps to the group of fractional ideals coprime to the finite part of \(\mathfrak m\).

    abbrev CoprimePrimes :=

    Let \(\mathfrak m\) be a modulus of a number field \(K\), let \(I_K^{\mathfrak m}\) be the group of invertible fractional ideals of \(K\) coprime to the finite part of \(\mathfrak m\), and let \(P\) be the set of nonzero prime ideals of the ring of integers \(\mathcal O_K\) that do not divide the finite part of \(\mathfrak m\).     NumberField.Modulus.frobeniusAtCoprimePrime

    Let \(m\) be a modulus of a number field \(K\), let \(L / K\) be a Galois extension of number fields that is unramified outside \(m\), and let \(\mathfrak p\) be a finite prime of \(K\) not dividing the finite part of \(m\). The chosen Frobenius element at \(\mathfrak p\) is a fixed element \(\sigma \in \mathrm{Gal}(L/K)\) that is an arithmetic Frobenius at \(\mathfrak P\), that is, \(\sigma(x) \equiv x^q \pmod{\mathfrak P}\) for all \(x \in \mathcal O_L\), where \(q\) is the number of elements of \(\mathcal O_K / \mathfrak p\).
