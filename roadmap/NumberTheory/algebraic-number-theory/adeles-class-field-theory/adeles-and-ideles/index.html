    DefinitionTopological idele group#✓fully proved

    NumberField.TopologicalIdeleGroup

    Let \(R\) be a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(K\). The topological idele group is the product \(\mathbb{I}_{K,\infty} \times \mathbb{I}_{K,f}\) of the infinite idele group \(\mathbb{I}_{K,\infty} = \prod_w K_w^\times\), the product of the unit groups of the completions of \(K\) at its infinite places \(w\), and the finite idele group \(\mathbb{I}_{K,f}\), the restricted product of the unit groups \(K_v^\times\) of the completions at the height-one primes \(v\) of \(R\) with respect to the integral unit groups \(\mathcal{O}_v^\times\). It carries the componentwise group structure and the product of the product topology on \(\mathbb{I}_{K,\infty}\) and the restricted-product topology on \(\mathbb{I}_{K,f}\).

    As a group it is isomorphic to the unit group of the adele ring of \(K\), but its topology differs from the subspace topology on those units.

  (InfiniteIdeleGroup K × FiniteIdeleGroup R K)

    NumberField.TopologicalIdeleGroup.unitsEquiv

    Let \(R\) be a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(K\), and let \(\mathbb{A}_K\) be the adele ring of \(K\) built from the infinite places of \(K\) and the height-one primes of \(R\). For an infinite place \(w\) of \(K\) write \(K_w\) for the completion of \(K\) at \(w\), and for a height-one prime \(v\) of \(R\) write \(K_v\) for the \(v\)-adic completion of \(K\).     DefinitionAdelic norm#✓fully proved

    NumberField.TopologicalIdeleGroup.adelicNorm

    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\). For each infinite place \(w\) of \(K\) let \(K_w\) be the completion of \(K\) at \(w\), with the absolute value \(\|\cdot\|_w\) extending \(w\), and let \(m_w\) be \(1\) if \(w\) is real and \(2\) if \(w\) is complex. For each height-one prime \(v\) of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) let \(K_v\) be the \(v\)-adic completion of \(K\), with the normalized absolute value \(\|\cdot\|_v\). The adelic norm is the monoid homomorphism from the topological idele group of \(K\) over the Dedekind domain \(\mathcal{O}_K\) to the real numbers under multiplication that sends an idele \(a\), with components \(a_w \in K_w^\times\) and \(a_v \in K_v^\times\), to \(\bigl(\prod_w \|a_w\|_w^{m_w}\bigr) \cdot \prod_v \|a_v\|_v\), where the product over \(v\) is the product of its finitely many factors different from \(1\).
