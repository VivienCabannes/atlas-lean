    NumberField.ArakelovDivisor.sizeHom_coe

    Let \(D = (I, (r_w)_w)\) be an Arakelov divisor of a number field \(K\), where \(I\) is an invertible fractional ideal of the ring of integers of \(K\) and \(r_w > 0\) for each infinite place \(w\) of \(K\).     NumberField.ArakelovDivisor.sectionSet

    Let \(D\) be an Arakelov divisor of a number field \(K\), with invertible fractional ideal \(I\) and positive real number \(r_w\) at each infinite place \(w\) of \(K\). The section set of \(D\) is the set of \(x \in I\) such that \(w(x) \le r_w\) for every infinite place \(w\) of \(K\).

    NumberField.size_principal

    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\). For every nonzero \(x \in K\), the principal Arakelov divisor of \(x\), formed by the principal fractional ideal \(x\mathcal{O}_K\) and the values \(w(x)\) at the infinite places \(w\) of \(K\), has size \(1\).
