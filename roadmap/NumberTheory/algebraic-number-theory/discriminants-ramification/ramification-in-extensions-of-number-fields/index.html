    TheoremLocalizing the ring of integers at an unramified prime gives an étale algebra#✓fully proved

    NumberField.IsUnramifiedIn.etale_localization

    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\), and let \(p\) be a prime ideal of \(\mathbb{Z}\) that is unramified in \(\mathcal{O}_K\), that is, every prime ideal of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) lying over \(p\) is unramified over \(\mathbb{Z}\). Let \(S\) be the localization of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) at the image of the multiplicative set \(\mathbb{Z} \setminus p\). Then \(S\) is an étale algebra over the local ring \(\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}\).

    The ring \(S\) is semilocal: it keeps every prime of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) above \(p\) at once, rather than the localization \((\mathcal{O}_K)_Q\) at a single such prime \(Q\).

    theorem IsUnramifiedIn.etale_localization {K : Type*} [Field K] [NumberField K]
    (p : Ideal ℤ) [p.IsPrime] (h : Algebra.IsUnramifiedIn (𝓞 K) p) :
    Algebra.Etale (Localization.AtPrime p)
      (Localization (Algebra.algebraMapSubmonoid (𝓞 K) p.primeCompl))

    NumberField.IsUnramifiedIn.tensorProductLocalizationEquivIntegralClosure

    Let \(E\) and \(F\) be number fields and let \(\mathfrak p\) be a prime ideal of \(\mathbb Z\) that is unramified in the ring of integers \(\mathcal O_F\). Write \(R = \mathbb Z_{\mathfrak p}\) for the localization of \(\mathbb Z\) at \(\mathfrak p\), and for \(K = E, F\) write \(S_K\) for the localization of \(\mathcal O_K\) at the image of \(\mathbb Z \setminus \mathfrak p\). Let \(K_E\) be the fraction field of \(\mathcal O_E\) and let \(\iota : S_E \to K_E\) be the canonical map. This is the isomorphism of \(S_F\)-algebras from \(S_F \otimes_R S_E\) onto the integral closure of \(S_F\) in \(S_F \otimes_R K_E\) that, for \(s \in S_F\) and \(t \in S_E\), sends \(s \otimes t\) to \(s \otimes \iota(t)\).

    It is obtained from the identification of \(S_E\) with the integral closure of \(R\) in \(K_E\), using that \(S_F\) is étale over \(R\).

    Localization.semilocalizationToAtPrime

    Let \(R \to S \to T\) be homomorphisms of commutative rings whose algebra structures form a scalar tower, let \(\mathfrak p\) be a prime ideal of \(R\), and let \(\mathfrak Q\) be a prime ideal of \(T\) lying over \(\mathfrak p\).     Let \(E\) and \(F\) be intermediate fields of a number field \(L\) over \(\mathbb Q\), let \(\mathfrak p\) be a prime ideal of \(\mathbb Z\) that is unramified in \(\mathcal O_F\), and let \(\mathfrak Q\) be a prime ideal of the ring of integers \(\mathcal O_{EF}\) of the compositum \(EF\) of \(E\) and \(F\) in \(L\) lying over \(\mathfrak p\). Write \(R = \mathbb Z_{\mathfrak p}\) for the localization of \(\mathbb Z\) at \(\mathfrak p\); for \(K = E, F\) write \(S_K\) for the localization of \(\mathcal O_K\) at the image of \(\mathbb Z \setminus \mathfrak p\) and \(\varphi_K : S_K \to (\mathcal O_{EF})_{\mathfrak Q}\) for the map induced by the inclusion \(\mathcal O_K \subseteq \mathcal O_{EF}\); and let \(C\) be the integral closure of \(S_F\) in \(S_F \otimes_R \operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O_E)\). This is the \(R\)-algebra homomorphism \(C \to (\mathcal O_{EF})_{\mathfrak Q}\) obtained by composing the inverse of the canonical isomorphism \(S_F \otimes_R S_E \cong C\) with the map \(S_F \otimes_R S_E \to (\mathcal O_{EF})_{\mathfrak Q}\) that, for \(s \in S_F\) and \(t \in S_E\), sends \(s \otimes t\) to \(\varphi_F(s) \varphi_E(t)\).

    No claim is made that this map induces an isomorphism from a localization of \(C\) onto \((\mathcal O_{EF})_{\mathfrak Q}\).
