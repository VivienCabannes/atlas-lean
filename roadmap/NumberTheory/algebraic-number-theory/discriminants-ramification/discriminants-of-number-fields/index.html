    Let \(K\) be a number field with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\) and discriminant \(d_K\). The absolute norm of the discriminant ideal of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) over \(\mathbb{Z}\) equals \(|d_K|\).

    NumberField.pow_div_factorial_sq_mul_pi_div_four_pow_gt

    Let \(n\) and \(s\) be natural numbers with \(n \ne 0\) and \(2s \le n\), and let \(e\) be Euler's number. Then

    \[\frac{1}{e^2 n} \left(\frac{\pi e^2}{4}\right)^n < \left(\frac{n^n}{n!}\right)^2 \left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^{2s}.\]
    theorem pow_div_factorial_sq_mul_pi_div_four_pow_gt (n s : ℕ) (hn : n ≠ 0)
    (hs : 2 * s ≤ n) :
    (1 / (Real.exp 1 ^ 2 * n)) * ((Real.pi * Real.exp 1 ^ 2) / 4) ^ n
      < ((n : ℝ) ^ n / (Nat.factorial n : ℝ)) ^ 2 *
          (Real.pi / 4) ^ (2 * s)

    NumberField.middle_term_le_abs_discr

    Let \(K\) be a number field of degree \(n = [K : \mathbb{Q}]\) with \(s\) complex places and discriminant \(d_K\). Then

    \[|d_K| \ge \left(\frac{n^n}{n!}\right)^2 \left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^{2s}.\]
    theorem middle_term_le_abs_discr (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] :
    (((Module.finrank ℚ K : ℝ) ^ (Module.finrank ℚ K) /
      (Nat.factorial (Module.finrank ℚ K) : ℝ)) ^ 2 *
      (Real.pi / 4) ^ (2 * nrComplexPlaces K)) ≤ (↑|discr K| : ℝ)

    NumberField.abs_discr_gt_exp_term

    Let \(K\) be a number field of degree \(n = [K : \mathbb{Q}]\) and discriminant \(d_K\), and let \(e\) be Euler's number. Then

    \[|d_K| > \frac{1}{e^2 n} \left(\frac{\pi e^2}{4}\right)^n,\]

    so the absolute discriminant grows at least exponentially in the degree.

    theorem abs_discr_gt_exp_term (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] :
    (1 / (Real.exp 1 ^ 2 * Module.finrank ℚ K)) *
      ((Real.pi * Real.exp 1 ^ 2) / 4) ^ (Module.finrank ℚ K) <
        (↑|discr K| : ℝ)

    latticeDiscriminantSet

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(L/K\) a finite extension of fields, with \(A\)-algebra structures on \(K\) and \(L\) forming a scalar tower \(A \to K \to L\), and let \(n = [L : K]\). For an \(A\)-submodule \(M\) of \(L\), this is the set of all discriminants \(\operatorname{disc}_{L/K}(b_1, \dots, b_n) = \det(\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(b_i b_j))\) in \(K\) of \(n\)-tuples \((b_1, \dots, b_n)\) of elements of \(M\).

    def latticeDiscriminantSet [FiniteDimensional K L] [_hAKL : IsScalarTower A K L]
    (M : Submodule A L) : Set K :=
  {d | ∃ b : Fin (Module.finrank K L) → L, (∀ i, b i ∈ M) ∧ d = Algebra.discr K b}

    latticeDiscriminant

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(L/K\) a finite extension of fields, with \(A\)-algebra structures on \(K\) and \(L\) forming a scalar tower \(A \to K \to L\), and let \(n = [L : K]\).     def latticeDiscriminant [_hAKL : IsScalarTower A K L]

    latticeDiscriminant_le_span_singleton

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(L/K\) a finite extension of fields, with \(A\)-algebra structures on \(K\) and \(L\) forming a scalar tower \(A \to K \to L\), and let \(n = [L : K]\). For an \(A\)-submodule \(M\) of \(L\), let \(D(M)\) be its discriminant, the \(A\)-submodule of \(K\) generated by the discriminants over \(K\) of all \(n\)-tuples of elements of \(M\). If \(b_1, \dots, b_n\) is a \(K\)-basis of \(L\) and \(M\) is contained in the \(A\)-span of \(b_1, \dots, b_n\), then \(D(M) \subseteq A \cdot \operatorname{disc}_{L/K}(b_1, \dots, b_n)\).

    theorem latticeDiscriminant_le_span_singleton [_hAKL : IsScalarTower A K L]
    (bK : Module.Basis (Fin (Module.finrank K L)) K L)
    (M : Submodule A L)
    (hM : M ≤ Submodule.span A (Set.range (bK : Fin _ → L))) :
    latticeDiscriminant A K M ≤
      Submodule.span A {Algebra.discr K (bK : Fin _ → L)}

    latticeDiscriminant_isFractional

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(L/K\) a finite extension of fields, with \(A\)-algebra structures on \(K\) and \(L\) forming a scalar tower \(A \to K \to L\), and let \(n = [L : K]\). For an \(A\)-submodule \(M\) of \(L\), let \(D(M)\) be its discriminant, the \(A\)-submodule of \(K\) generated by the discriminants over \(K\) of all \(n\)-tuples of elements of \(M\). Assume \(K\) is the fraction field of \(A\). If \(M\) is contained in the \(A\)-span of a \(K\)-basis \(b_1, \dots, b_n\) of \(L\), then \(D(M)\) is a fractional ideal: there is a non-zero-divisor \(a \in A\) such that \(a x\) lies in the image of \(A\) in \(K\) for every \(x \in D(M)\).

    theorem latticeDiscriminant_isFractional [IsFractionRing A K]
    [_hAKL : IsScalarTower A K L]
    (M : Submodule A L)
    (bN : Module.Basis (Fin (Module.finrank K L)) K L)
    (hMN : M ≤ Submodule.span A (Set.range (bN : Fin _ → L))) :
    IsFractional (nonZeroDivisors A) (latticeDiscriminant A K M)
    def latticeDiscriminant [_hAKL : IsScalarTower A K L]

    latticeDiscriminant_eq_imp_eq

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(L/K\) a finite extension of fields, with \(A\)-algebra structures on \(K\) and \(L\) forming a scalar tower \(A \to K \to L\), and let \(n = [L : K]\).
