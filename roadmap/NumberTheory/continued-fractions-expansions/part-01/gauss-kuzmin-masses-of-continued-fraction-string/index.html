    DefinitionConvergent matrix#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.convergentMatrix

    For a finite string \(a = (a_1, \ldots, a_n)\) of natural numbers, the convergent matrix \(C(a)\) is the \(2 \times 2\) integer matrix given by the ordered product \(C(a) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & a_1 \end{pmatrix} \cdots \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & a_n \end{pmatrix}\) (the identity matrix when \(n = 0\)). Its entries are written \(C(a) = \begin{pmatrix} p' & p \\ q' & q \end{pmatrix}\).

    When \(n \ge 1\) and all \(a_i\) are positive, \(p/q = [0; a_1, \ldots, a_n]\) and \(p'/q'\) is the preceding convergent.

    def convergentMatrix (a : List ℕ) : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℤ :=
  (a.map fun t => !![0, 1; 1, (t : ℤ)]).prod

    DefinitionFundamental interval of a string#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.cfCylinder

    For a finite string \(a = (a_1, \ldots, a_n)\) of natural numbers, the fundamental interval \(I(a)\) is the set of real numbers \(x \in [0, 1)\) whose continued fraction expansion \(x = [0; a_1(x), a_2(x), \ldots]\) has at least \(n\) digits, with \(a_i(x) = a_i\) for \(1 \le i \le n\).

    The digits are those produced by the continued fraction algorithm, so a rational \(x\) uses the finite expansion whose last digit is at least \(2\) (the resolution in the paper's footnote 2).

    def cfCylinder (a : List ℕ) : Set ℝ :=
  {x | x ∈ Set.Ico 0 1 ∧
    ∀ i (hi : i < a.length), (GenContFract.of x).partDens.get? i = some (a[i] : ℝ)}

    DefinitionGauss–Kuzmin mass of a string#✓fully proved

    For a finite string \(a\) of natural numbers with fundamental interval \(I(a)\), the Gauss–Kuzmin mass of \(a\) is \(P_{GK}(a) = \frac{1}{\log 2} \int_{I(a)} \frac{dx}{1 + x}\), the measure of \(I(a)\) under the Gauss–Kuzmin measure with density \(\frac{1}{(1 + x) \log 2}\).

    MetaMathlibExt.HasNontrivialSymmetry
