    DefinitionThe variable \(x\) of \(K((x^{-1}))\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.laurentInvVar

    Let \(K\) be a field. Formal Laurent series in \(x^{-1}\) over \(K\) are realized as Laurent series \(\sum_n c_n X^n\) in \(X = x^{-1}\), and the variable \(x\) is the series \(X^{-1}\), with coefficient \(1\) at \(X^{-1}\) and \(0\) elsewhere.

    A series \(\alpha = \sum_n c_n x^{-n} \ne 0\) has absolute value \(|\alpha| = |x|^{-v(\alpha)}\), where its order \(v(\alpha)\) is the least \(n\) with \(c_n \ne 0\).

    def laurentInvVar (K : Type*) [Field K] : LaurentSeries K :=
  HahnSeries.single (-1 : ℤ) (1 : K)
