    Polynomial continued fractions and Farey paths

    DefinitionFinite continued fraction with polynomial partial quotients#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.polyContFract

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots\) be polynomials in \(F[X]\) and let \(n \in \mathbb{N}\). The continued fraction \([a_0; a_1, \ldots, a_n]\) is the generalized continued fraction over the field \(F(X)\) of rational functions with integral part \(a_0\) and, for \(1 \le i \le n\), an \(i\)th term with partial numerator \(1\) and partial denominator \(a_i\); it has no further terms. For \(i \ge 0\) write \(A_i\) and \(B_i\) for the numerator and denominator of its \(i\)th convergent \(A_i / B_i\). Thus, with \(A_{-1} = 1\) and \(B_{-1} = 0\), we have \(A_0 = a_0\), \(B_0 = 1\), and \(A_{i+1} = a_{i+1} A_i + A_{i-1}\) and \(B_{i+1} = a_{i+1} B_i + B_{i-1}\) for \(0 \le i < n\).

    public noncomputable def polyContFract (a : ℕ → F[X]) (n : ℕ) :
    GenContFract (RatFunc F) :=
  ⟨algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (a 0),
    Stream'.Seq.ofList
      ((List.range n).map fun i => ⟨1, algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (a (i + 1))⟩)⟩

    TheoremConvergents of a polynomial continued fraction form a Farey path#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.polyContFract_convergents_farey_path

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(n \in \mathbb N\), and let \(a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n \in F[X]\) with \(a_i\) nonconstant for \(1 \le i \le n\). Write \(A_i\) and \(B_i\) for the numerator and the denominator of the \(i\)th convergent \(A_i / B_i\) of the continued fraction \([a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n]\) over \(F(X)\). Then for every \(i \le n\), \(A_i\) and \(B_i\) are polynomials in \(F[X]\), \(B_i \ne 0\), and \(A_i\) and \(B_i\) are coprime in \(F[X]\); and for \(1 \le i \le n\), the polynomial \(A_i B_{i-1} - A_{i-1} B_i\) is a unit of \(F[X]\), that is, a nonzero constant.

    In the language of the paper, each convergent \(A_i / B_i\) is a vertex of the Farey graph over \(F\) and consecutive convergents are adjacent in it.

    public theorem_wanted polyContFract_convergents_farey_path (a : ℕ → F[X]) (n : ℕ)
    (ha : ∀ i, 1 ≤ i → i ≤ n → 0 < (a i).natDegree) :
    ∃ A B : ℕ → F[X], ∀ i ≤ n,
      (polyContFract a n).nums i = algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (A i) ∧
      (polyContFract a n).dens i = algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (B i) ∧
      B i ≠ 0 ∧ IsCoprime (A i) (B i) ∧
      (1 ≤ i → IsUnit (A i * B (i - 1) - A (i - 1) * B i))

    TheoremExtending a Farey path extends the continued fraction#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.polyContFract_extend_farey_path

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(n \ge 1\), and let \(a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n \in F[X]\) with \(a_i\) nonconstant for \(1 \le i \le n\). Let \(A_0, \dots, A_n, B_0, \dots, B_n \in F[X]\) be such that, for every \(i \le n\), \(A_i\) and \(B_i\) are the numerator and the denominator of the \(i\)th convergent of the continued fraction \([a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n]\) over \(F(X)\). Let \(u, v \in F[X]\) be such that \(u B_n - v A_n\) is a unit of \(F[X]\) (a nonzero constant) and \(\deg B_n < \deg v\). Then there is a nonconstant polynomial \(b \in F[X]\) such that, in \(F(X)\),

    \[b = \frac{v A_{n-1} - u B_{n-1}}{u B_n - v A_n},\]

    and the \((n+1)\)st convergent of the continued fraction \([a_0, a_1, \dots, a_n, b]\) equals \(u/v\).

    Compared with the paper: the case \(n = 0\), which needs \(A_{-1} = 1\) and \(B_{-1} = 0\), is excluded; the paper's degree condition on the reduced denominator of \(A_n / B_n\) is stated with \(B_n\) itself (the two agree, since \(A_n / B_n\) is already in lowest terms); and the hypothesis that \(u\) and \(v\) are coprime is omitted, since adjacency implies it.

    public theorem_wanted polyContFract_extend_farey_path (a : ℕ → F[X]) {n : ℕ}
    (hn : 1 ≤ n) (ha : ∀ i, 1 ≤ i → i ≤ n → 0 < (a i).natDegree)
    (A B : ℕ → F[X])
    (hA : ∀ i ≤ n, (polyContFract a n).nums i = algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (A i))
    (hB : ∀ i ≤ n, (polyContFract a n).dens i = algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (B i))
    (u v : F[X]) (hadj : IsUnit (u * B n - v * A n))
    (hdeg : (B n).degree < v.degree) :
    ∃ b : F[X], 0 < b.natDegree ∧
      algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) b =
        algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (v * A (n - 1) - u * B (n - 1)) /
          algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) (u * B n - v * A n) ∧
      (polyContFract (Function.update a (n + 1) b) (n + 1)).convs (n + 1) =
        algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) u / algebraMap F[X] (RatFunc F) v

    TheoremContinued fraction along a Farey path of polynomials#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.polyContFract_of_farey_path

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(n \in \mathbb N\), let \(u_i, v_i \in F[X]\) for \(i \ge 0\), and let \(c_i \in F\) for \(i \ge 1\). Suppose that \(v_0 = 1\) and that for \(1 \le i \le n\) we have \(c_i \ne 0\), \(\deg v_{i-1} < \deg v_i\) and \(u_i v_{i-1} - u_{i-1} v_i = c_i\). Let \(a_0, a_1, \ldots \in F[X]\) satisfy \(a_0 = u_0\), \(a_1 = c_1^{-1} v_1\), \(a_2 = -c_2^{-1} c_1 (u_2 v_0 - u_0 v_2)\) and, for \(3 \le i \le n\),

    \[a_i = (-1)^{i+1}c_i^{-1}c_{i-1}(u_i v_{i-2} - u_{i-2} v_i)\prod_{j=1}^{i-2} c_j^{2(-1)^{i-j+1}}.\]

    Then \(a_i\) is nonconstant for \(1 \le i \le n\). Moreover, for every \(i \le n\) let \(t_i = \varepsilon_i \prod_{j=1}^{i} c_j^{(-1)^{i-j+1}}\), where \(\varepsilon_i = 1\) if \(i \equiv 0, 1 \pmod 4\) and \(\varepsilon_i = -1\) if \(i \equiv 2, 3 \pmod 4\). Then the numerator \(A_i\) and the denominator \(B_i\) of the \(i\)th convergent of the continued fraction \([a_0; a_1, \ldots, a_n]\) over \(F(X)\) are \(A_i = t_i u_i\) and \(B_i = t_i v_i\), and this convergent equals \(u_i / v_i\).

    The paper's coprimality hypothesis \((u_i, v_i) = 1\) is omitted: it follows from the determinant condition.

    MetaMathlibExt.polyFareySeries

    Let \(F\) be a field and \(n\) a natural number. The Farey series \(\mathcal{F}_n\) of order \(n\) over \(F\) is the set of pairs \((u, v)\) of polynomials in \(F[x]\), read as the fraction \(u / v\), such that \(\deg u < \deg v \le n\), \(u\) and \(v\) are coprime, and \(v\) is monic. Here \(\deg 0 = -\infty\), so \(u = 0\) is allowed.

    Each fraction is thus represented by exactly one such pair.

    public def polyFareySeries (n : ℕ) : Set (F[X] × F[X]) :=
  {w | w.1.degree < w.2.degree ∧ w.2.natDegree ≤ n ∧ IsCoprime w.1 w.2 ∧ w.2.Monic}
