    MetaMathlibExt.watsonCosSum

    For natural numbers \(n\) and \(\nu\), \(C_n(\nu)\) is the real number

    \[ C_n(\nu) = \sum_{l=1}^{n-1} \frac{\cos(2\pi\nu l/n)}{\sin(\pi l/n)}. \]

    For \(n \le 1\) the sum is empty and \(C_n(\nu) = 0\).

    public noncomputable def watsonCosSum (n ν : ℕ) : ℝ :=

      (1 / Real.sin (Real.pi * (l : ℝ) / (n : ℝ)))

    DefinitionOdd cotangent derivatives#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.watsonOddCotangentDeriv

    For natural numbers \(r\), \(n\) and \(\nu\), \(\mathcal{H}_{2r-1}(\nu/n)\) is the real number \(\cot^{(2r-1)}(\pi\nu/n)\), the \((2r-1)\)st derivative of the cotangent function evaluated at \(\pi\nu/n\). Here \(2r - 1\) is computed in the natural numbers, so for \(r = 0\) this is \(\cot(\pi\nu/n)\) itself.

    The derivative is taken of the cotangent as a function on all of \(\mathbb{R}\); the value is the classical one whenever \(\pi\nu/n\) is not a multiple of \(\pi\).

    public noncomputable def watsonOddCotangentDeriv (r n ν : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  iteratedDeriv (2 * r - 1) Real.cot (Real.pi * (ν : ℝ) / (n : ℝ))

    DefinitionWatson expansion terms#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.watsonExpansionTerm

    For natural numbers \(r\), \(n\) and \(\nu\), let \(B_{2r}\) be the \(2r\)th Bernoulli number and let \(\mathcal{H}_{2r-1}(\nu/n) = \cot^{(2r-1)}(\pi\nu/n)\) be the \((2r-1)\)st derivative of the cotangent evaluated at \(\pi\nu/n\). The \(r\)th Watson expansion term is the real number

    \[ \Bigl(1 - \frac{1}{2^{2r-1}}\Bigr) \frac{\pi^{2r-1} B_{2r} \, \mathcal{H}_{2r-1}(\nu/n)}{(2r)! \, n^{2r-1}}. \]

    Here \(2r - 1\) is computed in the natural numbers, so for \(r = 0\) the factor \(1 - 1/2^{2r-1}\) is \(0\) and the term vanishes.

    public noncomputable def watsonExpansionTerm (r n ν : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  (1 - 1 / (2 : ℝ) ^ (2 * r - 1)) * Real.pi ^ (2 * r - 1) *
      (((bernoulli (2 * r) : ℚ) : ℝ)) * watsonOddCotangentDeriv r n ν /
    ((Nat.factorial (2 * r) : ℝ) * (n : ℝ) ^ (2 * r - 1))

    Let \(n \ge 2\), \(N \ge 2\) and \(\nu\) be natural numbers with \(1 \le \nu \le n - 1\), and let

    \[ C_n(\nu) = \sum_{l=1}^{n-1} \frac{\cos(2 \pi \nu l / n)}{\sin(\pi l / n)}. \]

    For \(r \ge 1\) write

    \[ T_r(n, \nu) = \frac{(1 - 2^{1-2r}) \, \pi^{2r-1} B_{2r} \, \cot^{(2r-1)}(\pi \nu / n)} {(2r)! \, n^{2r-1}}, \]

    where \(B_{2r}\) is the \(2r\)-th Bernoulli number and \(\cot^{(2r-1)}\) is the \((2r-1)\)-st derivative of the cotangent. Then there is a real number \(\lambda\) with \(0 < \lambda < 1\) such that

    TheoremComplete asymptotic expansion of Watson's alternating cosecant sum#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.watson_alternating_complete_asymptotic

    For natural numbers \(N\) and \(n\) let \(C_n = \sum_{l=1}^{n-1} (-1)^{l+1} / \sin(\pi l / n)\) and

    \[ M_N(n) = \frac{2 n \log 2}{\pi} + 2 \sum_{r=1}^{N-1} \frac{(-1)^{r+1} (2^{2r-1} - 1)(2^{2r} - 1) \pi^{2r-1} B_{2r}^2}{r \, (2r)! \, n^{2r-1}}, \]

    where \(B_{2r}\) is the \(2r\)-th Bernoulli number. For every \(N \ge 2\) there is a real constant \(K \ge 0\) such that for every even \(n \ge 2\),

    \[ |C_n - M_N(n)| \le \frac{K}{n^{2N-1}}. \]

    In other words, \(C_n = M_N(n) + O(n^{1-2N})\) as \(n \to \infty\) through even values.
