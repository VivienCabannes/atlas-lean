    Mean values of products of modular inverses

    DefinitionThe modular product sum \(S_k(n, m)\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.modularProductSum

    For natural numbers \(k\), \(n\) and \(m\), the modular product sum \(S_k(n, m)\) is the natural number

    \[S_k(n, m) = \sum a_1 a_2 \cdots a_k,\]

    the sum running over all \(k\)-tuples \((a_1, \dots, a_k) \in \{1, \dots, n\}^k\) such that every \(a_j\) is coprime to \(n\) and \(a_1 a_2 \cdots a_k \equiv m \pmod n\). For \(k = 2\) and \(m = 1\) this is the sum of the products \(a \bar{a}\), where \(a\) runs over the numbers in \(\{1, \dots, n\}\) coprime to \(n\) and \(\bar{a} \in \{1, \dots, n\}\) is the inverse of \(a\) modulo \(n\).

    public def modularProductSum (k n m : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ∑ a ∈ Fintype.piFinset (fun _ : Fin k => Icc 1 n) with
      (∀ j, Nat.Coprime (a j) n) ∧ (∏ j, a j) ≡ m [MOD n],
    ∏ j, a j

    DefinitionXu–Zhang constant#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.xuZhangConstant

    For a natural number \(q\), the Xu–Zhang constant \(C_q\) is the product over the prime factors \(p\) of \(q\), with \(p^\alpha\) the exact power of \(p\) dividing \(q\),

    \[C_q = \prod_{p^\alpha \parallel q} \left(1 + \frac{2p^3 + p^2 - 1}{(p^2 + 1)(p^2 + p + 1)} - \frac{1}{p^{3(\alpha - 1)} (p^2 + p + 1)}\right).\]

    For \(q = 1\), and by convention for \(q = 0\), there are no prime factors, so \(C_0 = C_1 = 1\).

    public noncomputable def xuZhangConstant (q : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  ∏ p ∈ q.primeFactors,
    (1 + (2 * (p : ℝ) ^ 3 + (p : ℝ) ^ 2 - 1) / (((p : ℝ) ^ 2 + 1) * ((p : ℝ) ^ 2 + p + 1)) -
      1 / ((p : ℝ) ^ (3 * (q.factorization p - 1)) * ((p : ℝ) ^ 2 + p + 1)))
