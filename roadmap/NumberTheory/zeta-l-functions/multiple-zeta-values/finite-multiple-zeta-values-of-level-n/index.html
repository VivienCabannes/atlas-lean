    DefinitionColored finite multiple harmonic sums#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.coloredFiniteMZV

    Let \(p\) and \(N\) be natural numbers, let \(k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) be a finite sequence of natural numbers and let \(\alpha = (\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_r) \in (\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^r\). The colored finite multiple harmonic sum \(\zeta^{\alpha}_{p,N}(k) \in \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\) is the sum of \(m_1^{-k_1} \cdots m_r^{-k_r}\) over all integers \(0 < m_1 < \dots < m_r < p\) with \(m_i \equiv \alpha_i \pmod N\) for every \(i\).

    Here \(m^{-1}\) is the inverse of \(m\) in \(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\); when \(p\) is prime every \(0 < m < p\) is invertible, and for other \(p\) non-units get a conventional value.

    noncomputable def coloredFiniteMZV (p N : ℕ) {r : ℕ} (k : Fin r → ℕ) (α : Fin r → ZMod N) :
    ZMod p :=
  ∑ m ∈ (Finset.univ : Finset (Fin r → Fin p)).filter
      (fun m : Fin r → Fin p => StrictMono m ∧
        ∀ i, 0 < (m i : ℕ) ∧ ((m i : ℕ) : ZMod N) = α i),
    ∏ i, (((m i : ℕ) : ZMod p)⁻¹) ^ k i

    MetaMathlibExt.finiteMZVColored

    MetaMathlibExt.coloredFiniteMZV_one_eq_pow_mul_sum_prod

    Let \(N \ge 1\) and let \(k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) be a finite sequence of natural numbers (entries equal to \(0\) are allowed) of weight \(w = k_1 + \dots + k_r\).     \[ \zeta_p(k) = N^w \sum_{0 \le i_1 \le \dots \le i_{N-1} \le r} \prod_{j=0}^{N-1} \zeta^{[j]}_{p,N}(k_{i_j + 1}, \dots, k_{i_{j+1}}), \]

    where \(i_0 = 0\), \(i_N = r\), and \((k_{i_j + 1}, \dots, k_{i_{j+1}})\) is empty when \(i_j = i_{j+1}\).
