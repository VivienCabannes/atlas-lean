    Identify an index \(k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) of natural numbers with the word \(p^{k_1} y \cdots p^{k_r} y\) in the letters \(p\) and \(y\), and let \(\tilde\tau\) be the anti-automorphism of words with \(\tilde\tau(p) = y\) and \(\tilde\tau(y) = p\) (reverse the word and swap the letters).     DefinitionBradley–Zhao dual index#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.bzDualIndex

    For an index \(k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) of natural numbers, form the word \(x^{k_1 - 1} y \cdots x^{k_r - 1} y\) in two letters \(x\) and \(y\) (each \(k_i - 1\) truncated at \(0\)); when all \(k_i \ge 1\) this is \(z_{k_1} \cdots z_{k_r}\) with \(z_k = x^{k-1} y\). Apply the anti-automorphism \(\tau\) of words with \(\tau(x) = y\) and \(\tau(y) = x\), which reverses a word and swaps its letters, and write the result as \(x^{n_1} y \cdots x^{n_s} y \, x^e\). The BZ dual index of \(k\) is \((n_1 + 1, \dots, n_s + 1)\); the trailing \(x^e\) is dropped. For an admissible index (\(k_1 \ge 2\) and all \(k_i \ge 1\), or empty) \(e = 0\), so the dual index is the index of \(\tau(z_{k_1} \cdots z_{k_r})\).

    Equivalently, it is the SZ dual index of \((k_1 - 1, \dots, k_r - 1)\) with every entry increased by one.
