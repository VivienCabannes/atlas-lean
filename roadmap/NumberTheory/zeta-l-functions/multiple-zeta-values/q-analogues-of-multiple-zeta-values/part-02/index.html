    DefinitionGeneral q-multiple zeta values#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.qMZV

    For natural numbers \(k_1, \dots, k_r\) and polynomials \(Q_1, \dots, Q_r \in \mathbb{Q}[X]\), the general qMZV \(\zeta_q(k_1, \dots, k_r; Q_1, \dots, Q_r)\) is the formal power series in \(q\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) whose coefficient of \(q^N\), for every \(N\), is the coefficient of \(q^N\) in the finite sum \(\sum_{N \ge m_1 > m_2 > \dots > m_r > 0} \prod_{j=1}^{r} Q_j(q^{m_j}) / (1 - q^{m_j})^{k_j}\) over integers \(m_1, \dots, m_r\), and \(\zeta_q(\varnothing; \varnothing) = 1\).

    When \(Q_1(0) = 0\) (the paper's condition \(Q_1 \in X \mathbb{Q}[X]\)), the summands with \(m_1 > N\) have \(q\)-adic order greater than \(N\), so this is the series \(\sum_{m_1 > \dots > m_r > 0} \prod_{j=1}^{r} Q_j(q^{m_j}) / (1 - q^{m_j})^{k_j}\); for other data the value is a junk value.

    public noncomputable def qMZV : List (ℕ × Polynomial ℚ) → ℚ⟦X⟧

    DefinitionBradley–Zhao q-multiple zeta values#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.bzQMZV

    For an index \(k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) of natural numbers, the Bradley–Zhao q-multiple zeta value is the general qMZV \(\zeta_q^{BZ}(k) = \zeta_q(k_1, \dots, k_r; X^{k_1 - 1}, \dots, X^{k_r - 1})\), a formal power series in \(q\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\), where \(k_j - 1\) is truncated at \(0\). For an admissible index (\(k_1 \ge 2\) and all \(k_i \ge 1\)) it equals

    \[\zeta_q^{BZ}(k) = \sum_{m_1 > \dots > m_r > 0} \prod_{j=1}^{r} \frac{q^{m_j (k_j - 1)}}{(1 - q^{m_j})^{k_j}};\]

    for other indices the value has no intended meaning.

    public noncomputable def bzQMZV (k : List ℕ) : ℚ⟦X⟧ :=
  qMZV (k.map fun k => (k, Polynomial.X ^ (k - 1)))

    DefinitionBlock index of a list of pairs#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.bzBlockIndex

    The block index of a list \([(a_1, b_1), \dots, (a_r, b_r)]\) of pairs of natural numbers is the index \((a_1 + 1, \{1\}^{b_1 - 1}, \dots, a_r + 1, \{1\}^{b_r - 1})\), where \(\{1\}^n\) stands for \(n\) consecutive entries equal to \(1\) and \(b_j - 1\) is truncated at \(0\).

    When all \(a_j, b_j \ge 1\) this is an admissible index; it corresponds to the word \(x^{a_1} y^{b_1} \cdots x^{a_r} y^{b_r}\) of the space \(\mathfrak{h}^0\) of admissible words in the letters \(x, y\), and the duality \(\tau\), the anti-automorphism of \(\mathbb{Q}\langle x, y \rangle\) exchanging \(x\) and \(y\), maps this word to the word of the block index of \([(b_r, a_r), \dots, (b_1, a_1)]\).

    public def bzBlockIndex (ab : List (ℕ × ℕ)) : List ℕ :=
  (ab.map fun p => (p.1 + 1) :: List.replicate (p.2 - 1) 1).flatten
