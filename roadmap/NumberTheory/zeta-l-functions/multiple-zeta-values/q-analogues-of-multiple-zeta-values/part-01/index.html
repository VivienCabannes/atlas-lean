    MetaMathlibExt.szQMZVPartial

    For an index \((k_1, \dots, k_r)\) of natural numbers and a natural number \(M\), the partial sum is the formal power series in \(q\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\)

    \[\sum_{M > m_1 > \dots > m_r > 0} \prod_{j=1}^{r} \frac{q^{m_j k_j}}{(1 - q^{m_j})^{k_j}},\]

    where the sum runs over integers \(m_1, \dots, m_r\) and \(1/(1 - q^m)\) is the inverse in \(\mathbb{Q}[[q]]\). For the empty index it equals \(1\) for every \(M\).

    public noncomputable def szQMZVPartial : List ℕ → ℕ → ℚ⟦X⟧
  | [], _ => 1
  | k :: ks, M => ∑ m ∈ Finset.Ioo 0 M, (X ^ m * (1 - X ^ m)⁻¹) ^ k * szQMZVPartial ks m

    DefinitionSchlesinger–Zudilin qMZVs#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.szQMZV

    For an index \(k = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) of natural numbers, the Schlesinger–Zudilin qMZV \(\zeta_q^{SZ}(k)\) is the formal power series in \(q\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) whose coefficient of \(q^N\), for each \(N \ge 0\), is the coefficient of \(q^N\) in the partial sum

    \[\sum_{N \ge m_1 > \dots > m_r > 0} \prod_{j=1}^{r} \frac{q^{m_j k_j}}{(1 - q^{m_j})^{k_j}}\]

    over integers \(m_1, \dots, m_r\). In particular \(\zeta_q^{SZ}(\emptyset) = 1\).

    When the index is empty or \(k_1 \ge 1\), the summands with \(m_1 > N\) have no term of degree at most \(N\), so \(\zeta_q^{SZ}(k)\) is the full series \(\sum_{m_1 > \dots > m_r > 0} \prod_{j} q^{m_j k_j} / (1 - q^{m_j})^{k_j}\). The paper does not define the value when \(k_1 = 0\).

    public noncomputable def szQMZV (k : List ℕ) : ℚ⟦X⟧ :=
  PowerSeries.mk fun N => coeff N (szQMZVPartial k (N + 1))
