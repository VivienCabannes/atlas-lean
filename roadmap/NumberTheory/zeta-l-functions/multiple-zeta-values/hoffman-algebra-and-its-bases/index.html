    MetaMathlibExt.MultipleZeta.zMonomial

    For an index \(\mathbf{k} = (k_1, \dots, k_r)\) of positive integers, this is the monomial \(z_{k_1} z_{k_2} \cdots z_{k_r}\) with coefficient \(1\) in the free associative algebra \(\mathbb{Q}\langle x, y \rangle\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) on two noncommuting letters \(x\) and \(y\), where \(z_k = x^{k-1} y\).     DefinitionThe subalgebra \(\mathfrak{H}^1\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.MultipleZeta.hOne

    Let \(\mathbb{Q}\langle x, y\rangle\) be the free associative \(\mathbb{Q}\)-algebra on two letters \(x\) and \(y\), whose monomials are the words in \(x\) and \(y\). The subalgebra \(\mathfrak{H}^1\) of \(\mathbb{Q}\langle x, y\rangle\) is the \(\mathbb{Q}\)-linear span of the words that are empty or end in \(y\); equivalently, it consists of the polynomials in which every word with nonzero coefficient is empty or ends in \(y\).

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.MultipleZeta.WordAlgebraWanted.hZero_basis

    Let \(\mathbb{Q}\langle x, y\rangle\) be the free associative \(\mathbb{Q}\)-algebra on two letters \(x\) and \(y\), and let \(\mathfrak{H}^0\) be its subalgebra consisting of the \(\mathbb{Q}\)-linear combinations of the empty word and of words that begin with \(x\) and end with \(y\).
