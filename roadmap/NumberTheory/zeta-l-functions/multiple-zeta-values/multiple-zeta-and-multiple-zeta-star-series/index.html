    DefinitionMultiple zeta value#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.MultipleZeta.strictValue

    Let \(\mathbf{k} = (k_0, \dots, k_{r-1})\) be an index of depth \(r\) that is admissible (\(r \ge 1\) and \(k_0 \ge 2\)) or empty. Its multiple zeta value is the real number

    \[\zeta(\mathbf{k}) = \sum_{m_0 > m_1 > \dots > m_{r-1} \ge 1} \prod_{i=0}^{r-1} m_i^{-k_i}\]

    where the sum runs over all strictly decreasing \(r\)-tuples \((m_0, \dots, m_{r-1})\) of positive integers. The sum is unconditional, with value \(0\) by convention when the family is not summable.
