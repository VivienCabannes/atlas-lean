    DefinitionCoefficients of the Dedekind zeta function#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.dedekindZetaCoeff

    For a number field \(K\) with ring of integers \(\mathcal{O}_K\) and a natural number \(n\), \(a_K(n)\) is the number of ideals of \(\mathcal{O}_K\) of absolute norm \(n\). These are the coefficients of \(\zeta_K(s) = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_K(n)\, n^{-s}\).

    noncomputable def dedekindZetaCoeff (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  Nat.card {I : Ideal (𝓞 K) // Ideal.absNorm I = n}

    DefinitionSix-squares moments of Dedekind zeta coefficients#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.sixSquaresDedekindMoment

    For a number field \(K\), a natural number \(\ell\) and a real number \(x\), the moment is the real number \(S_\ell(x) = \sum_{1 \le n \le x} r_6(n)\, a_K(n)^\ell\), where \(n\) runs over the integers in \([1, x]\), \(a_K(n)\) is the number of ideals of the ring of integers of \(K\) of absolute norm \(n\), and \(r_6(n)\) is the coefficient of \(q^n\) in \(\theta(q)^6\), with \(\theta(q) = \sum_{m \in \mathbb{Z}} q^{m^2}\); that is, \(r_6(n)\) is the number of representations of \(n\) as a sum of six squares of integers.

    noncomputable def sixSquaresDedekindMoment (K : Type*) [Field K] [NumberField K] (ℓ : ℕ)
    (x : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  ∑ n ∈ Finset.Icc 1 ⌊x⌋₊, (sumOfSquaresRepCount 6 n : ℝ) * (dedekindZetaCoeff K n : ℝ) ^ ℓ

    DefinitionThe exponent \(a_{0,\ell}\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.dedekindSixSquaresA0

    For a natural number \(\ell\), define

    \[a_{0,\ell} = 1 + \sum_{i=1}^{\lfloor \ell/2 \rfloor} \binom{\ell}{2i} C_i,\]

    where \(C_i = (2i)!/(i!\,(i+1)!)\) is the \(i\)-th Catalan number.

    def dedekindSixSquaresA0 (ℓ : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  1 + ∑ i ∈ Finset.Icc 1 (ℓ / 2), ℓ.choose (2 * i) * catalan i

    DefinitionThe exponent \(a_{3,\ell}\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.dedekindSixSquaresA3

    For a natural number \(\ell\), \(a_{3,\ell}\) is the sum over the integers \(1 \le i \le \lfloor (\ell - 1)/2 \rfloor\) of \(\binom{\ell}{2i+1} q_i\), where \(q_i\) is the integer part of \(4\,(2i+1)!/((i-1)!\,(i+3)!)\).

    Each quotient \(4\,(2i+1)!/((i-1)!\,(i+3)!)\) is in fact an integer, and the upper limit \(\lfloor (\ell - 1)/2 \rfloor\) equals the source's \(\lceil \ell/2 - 1 \rceil\).

    def dedekindSixSquaresA3 (ℓ : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ∑ i ∈ Finset.Icc 1 ((ℓ - 1) / 2), ℓ.choose (2 * i + 1) *
    (4 * (2 * i + 1).factorial / ((i - 1).factorial * (i + 3).factorial))
