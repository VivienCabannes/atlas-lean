    StructureArtin local datum#✓fully proved

    Representation.ArtinLocalDatum

    For a finite group \(G\), an Artin local datum consists of a subgroup \(D \le G\) (the decomposition group), a normal subgroup \(I\) of \(D\) (the inertia group), and an element \(\sigma\) of the quotient group \(D/I\) (the Frobenius coset).

    In the arithmetic application, \(L/\mathbb{Q}\) is a finite Galois extension with \(G = \mathrm{Gal}(L/\mathbb{Q})\), \(D\) is the decomposition group at a place \(w\) of \(L\) above a prime \(p\), \(I\) is its inertia group and \(\sigma\) is the Frobenius. A complex representation of \(G\) restricted to \(D\) then gives an action of \(D/I\) on the \(I\)-invariant vectors, on which \(\sigma\) acts as the local operator. The definition does not require the data to arise this way.

    structure ArtinLocalDatum (G : Type u) [Group G] [Finite G] where
  decomposition : Subgroup G
  inertia : Subgroup decomposition
  [isNormal : inertia.Normal]
  frobenius : decomposition ⧸ inertia
      decomposition : Subgroup G

    DefinitionArtin local denominator#✓fully proved

    Representation.artinLocalDenominator

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), let \(p\) be a prime, let \((D, I, \sigma)\) be an Artin local datum for \(G\), and let \(s \in \mathbb{C}\). Let \(V^{I}\) be the subspace of vectors fixed by \(\rho(I)\), on which \(D/I\) acts, and let \(\rho(\sigma)\) be the action of \(\sigma\) on \(V^{I}\). The Artin local denominator is

    \[\det\left(\mathrm{id} - p^{-s}\,\rho(\sigma) \mid V^{I}\right).\]
    noncomputable def artinLocalDenominator (ρ : Representation ℂ G V)
    [Module.Free ℂ V] [Module.Finite ℂ V] (p : Nat.Primes)
    (d : ArtinLocalDatum G) (s : ℂ) : ℂ :=
  LinearMap.det
    (LinearMap.id - ((p.1 : ℂ) ^ (-s)) •
      ((Representation.quotientToInvariants

    Representation.ArtinLocalFactorRegular

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), let \(p\) be a prime, let \(d\) be an Artin local datum for \(G\), and let \(s \in \mathbb{C}\).     DefinitionArtin local factor#✓fully proved

    Representation.artinLocalFactorRaw

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), let \(p\) be a prime, let \((D, I, \sigma)\) be an Artin local datum for \(G\), and let \(s \in \mathbb{C}\). Let \(V^{I}\) be the subspace of vectors fixed by \(\rho(I)\) and \(\rho(\sigma)\) the induced action of \(\sigma \in D/I\) on it. The Artin local factor is the inverse of the Artin local denominator,

    \[\det\left(\mathrm{id} - p^{-s}\,\rho(\sigma) \mid V^{I}\right)^{-1},\]

    with the value \(0\) when the denominator vanishes.

    Where the denominator vanishes the true factor has a pole; the regularity condition picks out the \(s\) at which this value is a genuine finite Euler factor.

    noncomputable def artinLocalFactorRaw (ρ : Representation ℂ G V)
    [Module.Free ℂ V] [Module.Finite ℂ V] (p : Nat.Primes)

    DefinitionEuler product of Artin local factors#✓fully proved

    Representation.artinEulerProductOfLocalData

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), and suppose that for every prime \(p\) an Artin local datum \(d_p\) for \(G\) is given. For \(s \in \mathbb{C}\), this is the product over all primes \(p\) of the Artin local factors of \(\rho\) at \(p\), \(d_p\) and \(s\), taken as an unconditional infinite product (the limit of the products over finite sets of primes); when this product does not converge, the value is \(1\) by convention.

    The data need not come from the places of a number field, and no independence of the choice of place above \(p\) is asserted.

    noncomputable def artinEulerProductOfLocalData (ρ : Representation ℂ G V)
    [Module.Free ℂ V] [Module.Finite ℂ V]
    (localData : Nat.Primes → ArtinLocalDatum G) (s : ℂ) : ℂ :=
  ∏' p, artinLocalFactorRaw ρ p (localData p) s

    DefinitionConvergent Artin Euler product#✓fully proved

    Representation.HasArtinEulerProduct

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), let \(d_p\) be an Artin local datum for \(G\) for every prime \(p\), and let \(s, z \in \mathbb{C}\). The Artin Euler product of \(\rho\) and \((d_p)\) has value \(z\) at \(s\) when the products of the Artin local factors of \(\rho\) at \(p\), \(d_p\) and \(s\) over finite sets of primes \(p\) converge to \(z\) as the finite set grows to all primes (unconditional convergence). The value \(z = 0\) is allowed.

    DefinitionNonzero convergent Artin Euler product#✓fully proved

    Representation.HasNonzeroArtinEulerProduct

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), let \(d_p\) be an Artin local datum for \(G\) for every prime \(p\), and let \(s, z \in \mathbb{C}\). The Artin Euler product of \(\rho\) and \((d_p)\) has nonzero value \(z\) at \(s\) when it has value \(z\) at \(s\) and \(z \ne 0\).

    In particular every local factor is then regular at \(s\): a factor whose denominator vanishes has value \(0\) and would force the product to be \(0\), so this describes a genuine finite Euler-product value rather than a pole.

    Representation.artinLocalFactorRaw_of_action_eq_id

    Let \(\rho\) be a representation of a finite group \(G\) on a finite-dimensional complex vector space \(V\), let \(p\) be a prime, let \((D, I, \sigma)\) be an Artin local datum for \(G\), and let \(s \in \mathbb{C}\). Let \(V^{I}\) be the subspace of vectors fixed by \(\rho(I)\). If \(\sigma\) acts as the identity on \(V^{I}\) and \(V^{I}\) is one-dimensional, then the Artin local factor of \(\rho\) at \(p\), \((D, I, \sigma)\) and \(s\) equals \((1 - p^{-s})^{-1}\), where the inverse of \(0\) is read as \(0\).
