    For a natural number \(N\), let \(F_N(0) < F_N(1) < \dots < F_N(L_N - 1)\) be the terms of the Farey sequence of order \(N\), and for \(k < L_N\) let \(\delta_{N,k} = F_N(k) - k/(L_N - 1)\).     TheoremLagarias' elementary criterion for the Riemann hypothesis#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.LagariasWanted.NumberTheory.riemann_hypothesis_iff_lagarias_elementary_criterion

    The Riemann hypothesis holds (every zero \(s\) of the Riemann zeta function with \(s \ne 1\) that is not one of the trivial zeros \(-2, -4, -6, \dots\) has real part \(1/2\)) if and only if, for every positive integer \(n\),

    \[\sigma(n) \le H_n + e^{H_n} \log H_n,\]

    where \(\sigma(n)\) is the sum of the positive divisors of \(n\) and \(H_n = \sum_{k=1}^{n} 1/k\) is the \(n\)-th harmonic number.
