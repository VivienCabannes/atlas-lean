    Let \(p\) be a prime and \(A\) a \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-module. If two elements \(\varphi, \psi\) of the algebraic \(p\)-adic Pontryagin dual of \(A\) satisfy \(\varphi(a) = \psi(a)\) for every \(a \in A\), then \(\varphi = \psi\).

    MetaMathlibExt.contragredientSMul

    Let \(p\) be a prime, \(G\) a group and \(A\) a \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-module on which \(G\) acts by additive maps commuting with the \(\mathbb{Z}_p\)-action. For \(g \in G\) and \(\varphi\) in the algebraic \(p\)-adic Pontryagin dual of \(A\), \(g \cdot \varphi\) is the element \(a \mapsto \varphi(g^{-1} a)\) of the dual.

    noncomputable def contragredientSMul (p : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime] (G A : Type*) [Group G]
    [AddCommGroup A] [Module ℤ_[p] A] [DistribMulAction G A] [SMulCommClass G ℤ_[p] A]
    (g : G) (φ : PadicPontryaginDual p A) : PadicPontryaginDual p A where
  toFun a := φ (g⁻¹ • a)
  map_add' a b := by
    simp [smul_add, map_add]
  map_smul' r a := by
    change φ (g⁻¹ • (r • a)) = (RingHom.id ℤ_[p] r) • φ (g⁻¹ • a)
    rw [smul_comm g⁻¹ r a]
    simpa only [RingHom.id_apply] using φ.map_smul r (g⁻¹ • a)
