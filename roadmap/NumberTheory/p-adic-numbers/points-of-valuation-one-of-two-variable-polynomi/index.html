    Let \(p\) be a prime, let \(f \in \mathbb{Z}[x, y]\) have partial derivatives \(f_x\) and \(f_y\), and let \((r_1, s_1)\) and \((r_2, s_2)\) be pairs of integers in \(\{0, \dots, p - 1\}\) with \(p \mid f(r_i, s_i)\) for \(i = 1, 2\). Without this they fail for \(f = -x^2 + y^2 - x - 1\), \(p = 3\), \((r_1, s_1) = (0, 1)\), \((r_2, s_2) = (1, 0)\): the gradient vanishes at \((1, 0)\), the rank is \(2\), and \((n_1, n_2, n_3) = (6, 3, 0)\) rather than \((3, 3, 0)\).
