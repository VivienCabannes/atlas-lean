    Distinct multiples in an interval

    DefinitionDistinct multiples of \(1, \dots, n\) in an interval#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.HasDistinctMultiplesInInterval

    For natural numbers \(n\), \(m\) and \(L\), the interval \((m, m + L]\) contains distinct multiples of \(1, \dots, n\) when there are pairwise distinct natural numbers \(a_1, \dots, a_n\) with \(m < a_i \le m + L\) and \(i \mid a_i\) for every \(1 \le i \le n\).

    def HasDistinctMultiplesInInterval (n m L : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ a : Fin n → ℕ, Function.Injective a ∧
    ∀ i : Fin n, m < a i ∧ a i ≤ m + L ∧ (i.val + 1) ∣ a i
