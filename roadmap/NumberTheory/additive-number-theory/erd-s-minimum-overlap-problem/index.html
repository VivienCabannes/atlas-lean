    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.ErdosMinimumOverlapConstantWanted.ContinuousAdmissible

    A pair of functions \(f, g : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) is admissible when \(f\) and \(g\) are almost everywhere measurable with respect to Lebesgue measure, take values in \([0, 1]\) at every point, vanish at every point outside the support interval \([-1, 1]\), satisfy \(f(t) + g(t) = 1\) for every \(t \in [-1, 1]\), and satisfy \(\int_{\mathbb{R}} f(t)\,dt = 1\).
