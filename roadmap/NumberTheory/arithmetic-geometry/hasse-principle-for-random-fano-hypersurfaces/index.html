    DefinitionHeight of a rational polynomial#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.HassePrincipleRandomFanoWanted.HassePrincipleRandomFano.height

    Let \(f\) be a multivariate polynomial with rational coefficients. Multiply the coefficients of \(f\) by the least common multiple of their denominators and divide the resulting integers by their greatest common divisor; this gives the primitive integer coefficient vector \((a_m)_m\) of \(f\), indexed by the monomials \(m\) in the support of \(f\). The height of \(f\) is the Euclidean norm \(\sqrt{\sum_m a_m^2}\) of this vector. The zero polynomial has height \(0\).

    noncomputable def height {σ : Type*} (f : MvPolynomial σ ℚ) : ℝ :=
  let D : ℕ := f.support.lcm fun m ↦ (f.coeff m).den
  let intCoeff m := (D * f.coeff m).num
  let a m := intCoeff m / f.support.gcd intCoeff
  √ (f.support.sum fun m ↦ (a m) ^ 2)

    DefinitionUnimodular solutions of a polynomial#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.HassePrincipleRandomFanoWanted.HassePrincipleRandomFano.HasSolutionsIn

    Let \(R\) and \(S\) be commutative semirings with \(S\) an \(R\)-algebra, and let \(f\) be a multivariate polynomial over \(R\) in variables indexed by a type \(\sigma\). Then \(f\) has a solution in \(S\) if there is a vector \(x \in S^\sigma\) whose components generate the unit ideal of \(S\) and such that \(f(x) = 0\), where \(f\) is evaluated at \(x\) through the algebra map \(R \to S\).

    def HasSolutionsIn {σ : Type*} {R : Type*} [CommSemiring R] (f : MvPolynomial σ R) (S : Type*)
    [CommSemiring S] [Algebra R S] : Prop :=
  ∃ x : σ → S, Ideal.span (Set.range x) = ⊤ ∧ aeval x f = 0

    AbbreviationParameter space of hypersurfaces#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.HassePrincipleRandomFanoWanted.HassePrincipleRandomFano.𝕍

    \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}\) is the projectivization of the \(\mathbb{Q}\)-vector space of homogeneous polynomials of degree \(d\) in \(n + 1\) variables with rational coefficients; its points are the degree-\(d\) hypersurfaces in \(\mathbb{P}^n\) defined over \(\mathbb{Q}\). Since this space has dimension \(N_{d,n} = \binom{n+d}{d}\), the number of degree-\(d\) monomials in \(n + 1\) variables, \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}\) is a copy of \(\mathbb{P}^{N_{d,n} - 1}(\mathbb{Q})\).

    abbrev 𝕍 : Type := ℙ ℚ (homogeneousSubmodule (Fin (n + 1)) ℚ d)

    DefinitionLocal density of everywhere locally soluble hypersurfaces#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.HassePrincipleRandomFanoWanted.HassePrincipleRandomFano.ρLoc

    For natural numbers \(d\) and \(n\) and a real number \(A\), let \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\) be the set of degree-\(d\) hypersurfaces in \(\mathbb{P}^n\) of height at most \(A\), and let \(\mathbb{V}^{\mathrm{loc}}_{d,n}\) be the set of everywhere locally soluble ones. The local density \(\rho^{\mathrm{loc}}_{d,n}\) is the limit, as \(A \to \infty\), of the number of elements of \(\mathbb{V}^{\mathrm{loc}}_{d,n} \cap \mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\) divided by the number of elements of \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\), a quotient taken to be \(0\) when \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\) is empty; when this limit does not exist, \(\rho^{\mathrm{loc}}_{d,n}\) is an unspecified real number.

    By a theorem of Poonen–Voloch [17, Th. 3.6], this limit exists (and is positive) when \(d \ge 2\), \(n \ge 2\) and \((d, n) \ne (2, 2)\), and equals a product of local densities over all places of \(\mathbb{Q}\).

    noncomputable def ρLoc : ℝ :=
  atTop.limUnder fun A ↦ (𝕍Loc d n ∩ heightLE d n A).ncard / (heightLE d n A).ncard

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.HassePrincipleRandomFanoWanted.HassePrincipleRandomFano.ρ

    For a real number \(A\), \(\rho_{d,n}(A)\) is the proportion of hypersurfaces in \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\), the degree-\(d\) hypersurfaces in \(\mathbb{P}^n\) of height at most \(A\), that have a rational point: it is the number of \(V \in \mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\) with \(V(\mathbb{Q}) \ne \emptyset\) divided by the number of elements of \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\), taken to be \(0\) when \(\mathbb{V}_{d,n}(A)\) is empty.
