    TheoremKellner–Sondow factorization of the denominators of Bernoulli polynomials#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.kellner_sondow_bernoulli_denom

    Let \(B_k\) denote the \(k\)-th Bernoulli number, with \(B_1 = -1/2\), let \(B_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} B_k x^{n-k}\) be the \(n\)-th Bernoulli polynomial, and let \(D_n\) be the least common multiple of the denominators of its nonzero coefficients. For a prime \(p\), let \(s_p(N)\) denote the sum of the base-\(p\) digits of a natural number \(N\). Then for every \(n \ge 1\), \(D_n\) is the product of three factors: the product of the primes \(p \le n + 1\) with \(p \nmid n + 1\) and \(s_p(n+1) \ge p\); the product of the primes \(p \le n + 1\) with \(p \mid n + 1\) and \(s_p(n+1) \ge p\); and the product of the primes \(p \le n + 1\) with \(p \mid n + 1\) and \(s_p(n+1) < p\).

    theorem kellner_sondow_bernoulli_denom : ∀ (n : ℕ), 1 ≤ n →
  (Polynomial.bernoulli n).support.lcm (fun i => ((Polynomial.bernoulli n).coeff i).den) =
    Finset.prod
      ((Finset.range (n + 2)).filter
        (fun p => Nat.Prime p ∧ ¬ p ∣ (n + 1) ∧ p ≤ (Nat.digits p (n + 1)).sum))
      (fun p => p) *
    Finset.prod
      ((Finset.range (n + 2)).filter
        (fun p => Nat.Prime p ∧ p ∣ (n + 1) ∧ p ≤ (Nat.digits p (n + 1)).sum))
      (fun p => p) *
    Finset.prod
      ((Finset.range (n + 2)).filter
        (fun p => Nat.Prime p ∧ p ∣ (n + 1) ∧ (Nat.digits p (n + 1)).sum < p))
      (fun p => p)

    DefinitionDenominator of \(B_n(x) - B_n\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.bernoulliDiffDenominator

    For a natural number \(n\) and a prime \(p\), let \(s_p(n)\) be the sum of the base-\(p\) digits of \(n\). Define \(\mathbb{D}_n = \prod_{p \text{ prime},\ s_p(n) \ge p} p\), the product of all primes \(p\) with \(s_p(n) \ge p\); every such prime satisfies \(p \le n\), so the product is finite.

    By a theorem of Kellner (2017, Section 5), for \(n \ge 1\) the number \(\mathbb{D}_n\) is the denominator of \(B_n(x) - B_n\), where \(B_n(x)\) is the \(n\)-th Bernoulli polynomial and \(B_n\) the \(n\)-th Bernoulli number.

    def bernoulliDiffDenominator (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ∏ p ∈ (Finset.range (n + 1)).filter (fun p => p.Prime ∧ p ≤ (Nat.digits p n).sum), p
