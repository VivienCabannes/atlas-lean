    MetaMathlibExt.IsBinomidIndex

    An infinite binomid index is a sequence \((e_1, e_2, \dots)\) of nonnegative integers such that \(\sum_{i=n-k+1}^{n} e_i \ge \sum_{i=1}^{k} e_i\) for all \(1 \le k \le n\).

  ∀ k n : ℕ, k ≤ n → ∑ i ∈ Finset.range k, η i ≤ ∑ i ∈ Finset.Ico (n - k) n, η i
