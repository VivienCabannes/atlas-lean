    SumSquares.divChiSum

    For a natural number \(n\), this is the integer \(\sum_{d \mid n} \chi_4(d)\), the sum over the positive divisors \(d\) of \(n\) of the nonprincipal Dirichlet character \(\chi_4\) modulo 4, which takes the value \(0\) at even \(d\), \(1\) at \(d \equiv 1 \pmod 4\) and \(-1\) at \(d \equiv 3 \pmod 4\). For \(n = 0\) there are no divisors and the sum is \(0\).

    public noncomputable def divChiSum (n : ℕ) : ℤ := ∑ d ∈ Nat.divisors n, ZMod.χ₄ d

    SumSquares.pairEquivGaussianNormFiber

    For a natural number \(j\), the map \((a, b) \mapsto a + bi\) is a bijection from the set of integer pairs \((a, b)\) with \(a^2 + b^2 = j\) to the set of Gaussian integers \(z\) with norm \(N(z) = j\); its inverse sends \(z\) to the pair of its real and imaginary parts.

    SumSquares.representationNumber_two_jacobi_count

    Let \(\chi_4\) be the nonprincipal Dirichlet character modulo \(4\), so \(\chi_4(d)\) is \(0\) for even \(d\), \(1\) for \(d \equiv 1 \pmod 4\) and \(-1\) for \(d \equiv 3 \pmod 4\). For every natural number \(j \ge 1\), the representation number \(r_2(j)\), the number of integer pairs \((x_1, x_2)\) with \(x_1^2 + x_2^2 = j\) counted with order and signs, satisfies \(r_2(j) = 4 \sum_{d \mid j} \chi_4(d)\), where \(d\) runs over the positive divisors of \(j\).

    public theorem representationNumber_two_jacobi_count (j : ℕ) (hj : 1 ≤ j) :
    (SumSquares.representationNumber 2 j : ℤ) =
      4 * ∑ d ∈ j.divisors, ZMod.χ₄ d
