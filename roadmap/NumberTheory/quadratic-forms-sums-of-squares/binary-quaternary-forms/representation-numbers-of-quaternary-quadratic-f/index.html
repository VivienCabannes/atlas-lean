    TheoremRepresentation numbers of the quaternary forms \((1,1,10,10)\) and \((2,2,5,5)\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.quaternaryRep_one_one_ten_ten_and_two_two_five_five

    For a complex number \(q\) write \(f_k = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{km})\). For integers \(a, b, c, d\) and \(n\), let \(N(a,b,c,d;n)\) be the number of \((x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4) \in \mathbb{Z}^4\) with \(a x_1^2 + b x_2^2 + c x_3^2 + d x_4^2 = n\), and let \(\sigma(n/k)\) be the sum of the positive divisors of \(n/k\) if \(k \mid n\) and \(0\) otherwise. Let \(b_1, b_2, b_3\) be sequences of complex numbers indexed by \(n \ge 0\) such that for every complex \(q\) with \(|q| < 1\) the series \(\sum_{n \ge 0} b_i(n) q^n\) converge, with sums \(q f_2^2 f_{10}^2\), \(q^2 f_4^2 f_{20}^2\) and \(q^3 f_4^5 f_{10} f_{40}^2 / (f_2 f_8^2 f_{20})\) for \(i = 1, 2, 3\) respectively. For \(n \ge 1\) put

    \[ T(n) = \tfrac{2}{3}\sigma(n) - \tfrac{2}{3}\sigma(n/2) + \tfrac{4}{3}\sigma(n/4) + \tfrac{10}{3}\sigma(n/5) - \tfrac{16}{3}\sigma(n/8) - \tfrac{10}{3}\sigma(n/10) + \tfrac{20}{3}\sigma(n/20) - \tfrac{80}{3}\sigma(n/40). \]

    Then for every \(n \ge 1\),

    \[ N(1,1,10,10;n) = T(n) + \tfrac{10}{3} b_1(n) + \tfrac{8}{3} b_2(n) + 4b_3(n) \]

    and

    \[ N(2,2,5,5;n) = T(n) - \tfrac{2}{3} b_1(n) + \tfrac{8}{3} b_2(n) - 4b_3(n). \]

    With \(\eta_k = q^{k/24} f_k\), the three generating functions are the eta quotients \(\eta_2^2\eta_{10}^2\), \(\eta_4^2\eta_{20}^2\) and \(\eta_4^5\eta_{10}\eta_{40}^2/(\eta_2\eta_8^2\eta_{20})\). These formulas are due to Alaca and Altiary; Vasuki and Nagendra reprove them elementarily.

Let \(a_1, \dots, a_5\) be sequences of complex numbers indexed by \(n \ge 0\) such that for every complex \(q\) with \(|q| < 1\) the series \(\sum_{n \ge 0} a_i(n) q^n\) converge, with sums \(q f_1 f_3 f_5 f_{15}\), \(q^2 f_2 f_6 f_{10} f_{30}\), \(q^4 f_4 f_{12} f_{20} f_{60}\), \(q f_3 f_5 f_6 f_{10}\) and \(q^2 f_6 f_{10} f_{12} f_{20}\) for \(i = 1, \dots, 5\) respectively.     \[ S(n) = \tfrac{2}{3}\sigma(n) - \tfrac{4}{3}\sigma(n/2) + \tfrac{8}{3}\sigma(n/4) - 2\sigma(n/3) + 4\sigma(n/6) - 8\sigma(n/12) + \tfrac{10}{3}\sigma(n/5) - \tfrac{20}{3}\sigma(n/10) + \tfrac{40}{3}\sigma(n/20) - 10\sigma(n/15) + 20\sigma(n/30) - 40\sigma(n/60). \]

    \[ N(1,1,15,15;n) = S(n) + \tfrac{2}{3}(a_1(n) - 2a_2(n) + 4a_3(n) + 4a_4(n) + 8a_5(n)), \]

    With \(\eta_k = q^{k/24} f_k\), the five generating functions are the eta quotients \(\eta_1\eta_3\eta_5\eta_{15}\), \(\eta_2\eta_6\eta_{10}\eta_{30}\), \(\eta_4\eta_{12}\eta_{20}\eta_{60}\), \(\eta_3\eta_5\eta_6\eta_{10}\) and \(\eta_6\eta_{10}\eta_{12}\eta_{20}\). These formulas are due to Alaca; Vasuki and Nagendra reprove them elementarily.
