    StructureNondegenerate Lucas parameters#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.LucasParameters

    For real numbers \(s\) and \(t\), let \(\{n\}_{s,t}\) be the sequence of real numbers with \(\{0\}_{s,t} = 0\), \(\{1\}_{s,t} = 1\) and \(\{n+2\}_{s,t} = s\{n+1\}_{s,t} + t\{n\}_{s,t}\) for every natural number \(n\). Nondegenerate Lucas parameters are a pair of real numbers \((s, t)\) with \(s \ne 0\), \(t \ne 0\) and \(\{n\}_{s,t} \ne 0\) for every natural number \(n \ne 0\).

    The last condition ensures that the \(s,t\)-factorials are nonzero, so quotients of them never divide by zero.

    DefinitionLucasnomial coefficients#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lucasnomial

    Let \((s, t)\) be nondegenerate Lucas parameters and write \(\{m\}_{s,t}!\) for the \(s,t\)-factorial of a natural number \(m\). For natural numbers \(n\) and \(k\), the Lucasnomial coefficient is the real number

    \[\binom{n}{k}_{s,t} = \frac{\{n\}_{s,t}!}{\{k\}_{s,t}! \{n-k\}_{s,t}!}\]

    when \(k \le n\), and \(\binom{n}{k}_{s,t} = 0\) when \(k > n\).

    noncomputable def lucasnomial (P : LucasParameters) (n k : ℕ) : ℝ :=

    TheoremSum-of-cubes identities for Fibonacci, Pell, Jacobsthal and Mersenne numbers#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.sum_cubes_specializations

    Let \(F_n\) be the Fibonacci numbers, \(P_n\) the Pell numbers, and \(J_n\) the Jacobsthal numbers, that is, the Lucas sequence \(U(1, -2)\), with \(J_0 = 0\), \(J_1 = 1\) and \(J_{n+2} = J_{n+1} + 2 J_n\). For every natural number \(n\),

    \[\sum_{k=1}^{n} (F_{k+1} + F_{k-1}) F_k^3 = F_n^2 F_{n+1}^2,\]
    \[\sum_{k=1}^{n} (P_{k+1} + P_{k-1}) P_k^3 = \tfrac{1}{2} P_n^2 P_{n+1}^2,\]
    \[\sum_{k=1}^{n} 4^{n-k} (J_{k+1} + 2 J_{k-1}) J_k^3 = J_n^2 J_{n+1}^2,\]
    \[\sum_{k=1}^{n} 4^{n-k} (2^k + 1) (2^k - 1)^3 = \tfrac{1}{3} (2^n - 1)^2 (2^{n+1} - 1)^2,\]

    and, for every real number \(q\) with \(q \ne 1\) and \(q \ne -1\) (Warnaar's identity),

    \[\sum_{k=1}^{n} q^{2(n-k)} \frac{1 - q^{2k}}{1 - q^2} \Big(\frac{1 - q^k}{1 - q}\Big)^2 = \Big(\frac{(1 - q^{n+1}) (1 - q^n)}{(1 - q) (1 - q^2)}\Big)^2.\]

    The numbers \(2^k - 1\) are the Mersenne numbers, and the right side of the last identity is the square of the Gaussian binomial coefficient \(\binom{n+1}{2}_q\).
