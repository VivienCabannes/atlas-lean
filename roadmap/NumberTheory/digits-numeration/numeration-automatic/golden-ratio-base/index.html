    DefinitionValue of a base phi expansion#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.basePhiValue

    For a finite set \(S\) of integers, the base phi value of \(S\) is the real number \(\sum_{i \in S} \varphi^i\), where \(\varphi = (1 + \sqrt 5)/2\) is the golden ratio. It is the number represented by the base phi expansion with digit \(1\) at each position in \(S\) and digit \(0\) at every other position.

    noncomputable def basePhiValue (S : Finset ℤ) : ℝ :=
  ∑ i ∈ S, φ ^ i

    Encode a finite base phi expansion with digits \(0\) and \(1\) by the finite set \(S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}\) of positions of its digits \(1\). The expansion is a Bergman expansion when no two digits \(1\) are adjacent, that is, \(i + 1 \notin S\) for every \(i \in S\).

    DefinitionGolden mean flips#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsGoldenMeanFlip

    Encode a finite base phi expansion with digits \(0\) and \(1\) by the finite set of positions of its digits \(1\). An expansion \(T\) arises from an expansion \(S\) by a golden mean flip \(100 \to 011\) when there is a position \(i \in S\) with \(i - 1 \notin S\) and \(i - 2 \notin S\) such that \(T = (S \setminus \{i\}) \cup \{i - 1, i - 2\}\): the digit \(1\) at position \(i\) is replaced by digits \(1\) at positions \(i - 1\) and \(i - 2\), which were \(0\).

    def IsGoldenMeanFlip (S T : Finset ℤ) : Prop :=
  ∃ i ∈ S, i - 1 ∉ S ∧ i - 2 ∉ S ∧ T = insert (i - 1) (insert (i - 2) (S.erase i))

    DefinitionKnott's truncation rule#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.SatisfiesKnottCondition

    Let \(\varphi\) be the golden ratio. Encode a finite base \(\varphi\) expansion with digits \(0\) and \(1\) by the finite set \(S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}\) of the positions of its digits \(1\), and write \(a_i\) for its digit at position \(i\). The expansion satisfies Knott's condition when it does not end in the digits \(0, 1, 1\): if \(S\) is nonempty with least element \(R\), then \(a_{R+2} a_{R+1} a_R \ne 011\), that is, it is not the case that both \(R + 1 \in S\) and \(R + 2 \notin S\). The empty expansion satisfies the condition.

    def SatisfiesKnottCondition (S : Finset ℤ) : Prop :=
  ∀ m ∈ S, (∀ j ∈ S, m ≤ j) → ¬ (m + 1 ∈ S ∧ m + 2 ∉ S)

    DefinitionNumber of Fibonacci representations#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.fibonacciRepresentationCount

    For a natural number \(m\), \(\mathrm{Tot}_{\mathrm{FIB}}(m)\) is the number of finite sets \(T\) of indices \(i \ge 2\) with \(\sum_{i \in T} F_i = m\), where \(F_i\) are the Fibonacci numbers (\(F_0 = 0\), \(F_1 = 1\)); that is, the number of ways to write \(m\) as a sum of Fibonacci numbers with distinct indices at least \(2\) (OEIS A000119). Indices above \(m + 1\) never occur because \(F_{m+2} > m\).

    def fibonacciRepresentationCount (m : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((Finset.Icc 2 (m + 1)).powerset.filter fun T => ∑ i ∈ T, Nat.fib i = m).card
