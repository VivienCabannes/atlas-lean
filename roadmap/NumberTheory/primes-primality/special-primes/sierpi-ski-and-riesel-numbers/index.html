    DefinitionRepstrings#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.repstring

    For natural numbers \(b\), \(k\), \(z\) and \(t\), let \(\ell\) be the number of base-\(b\) digits of \(k\). The \(b\)-repstring is

    \[k_b^{(z;t)} = \left\lfloor \frac{k\,(b^{(z+\ell)t} - 1)}{b^{z+\ell} - 1} \right\rfloor,\]

    where subtraction is truncated at \(0\) and the integer division gives \(0\) when the denominator is \(0\). For \(z = 0\) it is the \(b\)-repinteger \(k_b^{(t)}\).

    For \(b \ge 2\) and \(k \ge 1\) the division is exact, and \(k_b^{(z;t)}\) is the number whose base-\(b\) expansion is \(k\) written \(t\) times with \(z\) zeros between consecutive copies.

    public def repstring (b k z t : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  k * (b ^ ((z + (Nat.digits b k).length) * t) - 1) / (b ^ (z + (Nat.digits b k).length) - 1)

    DefinitionSierpiński covering data#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsSierpinskiCoveringData

    Let \(k\) be a natural number and \(S\) a finite set of triples \((r, m, p)\) of natural numbers. Then \(S\) is Sierpiński covering data for \(k\) if every integer is congruent to \(r\) modulo \(m\) for some \((r, m, p) \in S\), distinct triples in \(S\) have distinct third entries, and every \((r, m, p) \in S\) has \(m \ge 1\), \(p\) prime, \(p \mid 2^m - 1\) and \(p \mid k \cdot 2^r + 1\).

    This is the input of the covering system method for Sierpiński numbers; the last condition says that \(k \equiv -2^{-r} \pmod{p}\).

    public def IsSierpinskiCoveringData (k : ℕ) (S : Finset (ℕ × ℕ × ℕ)) : Prop :=
  Int.IsCoveringSystem (S.image fun s => ((s.1 : ℤ), s.2.1)) ∧
    Set.InjOn (fun s : ℕ × ℕ × ℕ => s.2.2) (S : Set (ℕ × ℕ × ℕ)) ∧
    ∀ s ∈ S, 1 ≤ s.2.1 ∧ s.2.2.Prime ∧ s.2.2 ∣ 2 ^ s.2.1 - 1 ∧ s.2.2 ∣ k * 2 ^ s.1 + 1
    public def IsCoveringSystem (classes : Finset (ℤ × ℕ)) : Prop :=
  ∀ z : ℤ, ∃ c ∈ classes, Int.ModEq (c.2 : ℤ) z c.1

    DefinitionRiesel covering data#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsRieselCoveringData

    Let \(k\) be a natural number and let \(S\) be a finite set of triples \((r, m, p)\) of natural numbers. Then \(S\) is Riesel covering data for \(k\) (data producing \(k\) as a Riesel number by the covering system method) when every integer is congruent to \(r\) modulo \(m\) for some \((r, m, p) \in S\), distinct triples in \(S\) have distinct third entries \(p\), and every \((r, m, p) \in S\) has \(m \ge 1\), \(p\) prime, \(p \mid 2^m - 1\) and \(p \mid k \cdot 2^r - 1\) (for \(k \ge 1\) the last condition says \(k \equiv 2^{-r} \pmod p\)).

    Subtraction is in the natural numbers, so for \(k = 0\) the condition \(p \mid k \cdot 2^r - 1\) reads \(p \mid 0\) and holds trivially.

    public def IsRieselCoveringData (k : ℕ) (S : Finset (ℕ × ℕ × ℕ)) : Prop :=
  Int.IsCoveringSystem (S.image fun s => ((s.1 : ℤ), s.2.1)) ∧
    Set.InjOn (fun s : ℕ × ℕ × ℕ => s.2.2) (S : Set (ℕ × ℕ × ℕ)) ∧
    ∀ s ∈ S, 1 ≤ s.2.1 ∧ s.2.2.Prime ∧ s.2.2 ∣ 2 ^ s.2.1 - 1 ∧ s.2.2 ∣ k * 2 ^ s.1 - 1
