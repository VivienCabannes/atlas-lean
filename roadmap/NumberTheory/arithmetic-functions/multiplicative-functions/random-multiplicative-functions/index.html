    MetaMathlibExt.RandomMultiplicativeFunction.rademacherFunction

    Let \((X_p)_p\) be a family of maps \(X_p : \Omega \to \{-1, 1\}\) indexed by the primes, and let \(\omega \in \Omega\). The Rademacher random multiplicative function at \(\omega\) is the arithmetic function \(f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{Z}\) with \(f(0) = 0\) and

    TheoremNo iterated-logarithm constant for Rademacher multiplicative functions#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.RademacherLILConstantWanted.conjecture_refuted

    The Rademacher LIL constant conjecture is false: there is no real constant \(c > 0\) such that, for every probability space \((\Omega, \mu)\) and every family \((X_p)_p\) of independent, measurable random signs indexed by the primes, each uniform on \(\{-1, 1\}\), for \(\mu\)-almost every \(\omega\) the limit superior as \(N \to \infty\) of \(S_N(\omega) / \sqrt{N \log \log N}\) equals \(c\). Here \(S_N(\omega) = \sum_{m=1}^{N} f_\omega(m)\), where \(f_\omega\) is the Rademacher random multiplicative function: \(f_\omega(m) = \prod_{p \mid m} X_p(\omega)\) for squarefree \(m\) and \(f_\omega(m) = 0\) otherwise.

    Durkan and Pearce-Crump, and independently Verreault, proved that for every \(\varepsilon > 0\), almost surely \(|\sum_{n \le x} f_\omega(n)| \ll \sqrt{x} (\log \log x)^{1/4 + \varepsilon}\) as \(x \to \infty\), so the normalized limit superior is almost surely \(0\).
