    DefinitionIndicator of \(B_\kappa\)-free integers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.bFreeIndicator

    Let \(\kappa\) be a function from the natural numbers to the natural numbers (only its values \(\kappa(p)\) at primes \(p\) matter) and let \(B_\kappa\) be the set of prime powers \(p^{\kappa(p)}\). A natural number \(n\) is \(B_\kappa\)-free if \(n \ge 1\) and no \(p^{\kappa(p)}\) with \(p\) prime divides \(n\). The indicator \(q_{B_\kappa}\) is the integer-valued arithmetic function with \(q_{B_\kappa}(n) = 1\) if \(n\) is \(B_\kappa\)-free and \(q_{B_\kappa}(n) = 0\) otherwise.

    The paper indexes the exponents by the position of the prime (\(b_i = p_i^{\kappa_i}\)); here \(\kappa\) is indexed by the prime itself. For constant \(\kappa = k\) this is the indicator \(q_k\) of \(k\)-free integers.

    public noncomputable def bFreeIndicator (κ : ℕ → ℕ) : ArithmeticFunction ℤ :=
  ⟨fun n => if n ≠ 0 ∧ ∀ p : ℕ, p.Prime → ¬ p ^ κ p ∣ n then 1 else 0, by simp⟩

    Let \(R\) be a commutative ring, let \(a, b \in R\) and let \(\tau, \xi\) be natural numbers. Then

    MetaMathlibExt.dirichletConvolution_mul_eq_sum_gcd

    Let \(u\) and \(v\) be complex-valued completely multiplicative arithmetic functions, meaning \(u(mn) = u(m) u(n)\) and \(v(mn) = v(m) v(n)\) for all natural numbers \(m\) and \(n\). Let \(u * v\) be their Dirichlet convolution and \(\mu\) the Möbius function. Then for all natural numbers \(r\) and \(s\),

    \[(u * v)(rs) = \sum_{m \mid \gcd(r, s)} \mu(m) u(m) v(m) (u * v)(r/m) (u * v)(s/m),\]

    the sum running over the positive divisors \(m\) of \(\gcd(r, s)\) (it is empty when \(r = s = 0\)).

    The paper takes \(r, s\) positive; the identity also holds (both sides vanish) when \(r\) or \(s\) is \(0\), so no positivity hypothesis is imposed.

    public theorem_wanted dirichletConvolution_mul_eq_sum_gcd {u v : ArithmeticFunction ℂ}
    (hu : u.IsCompletelyMultiplicative) (hv : v.IsCompletelyMultiplicative) (r s : ℕ) :
    (u * v) (r * s) = ∑ m ∈ (Nat.gcd r s).divisors,
      (μ m : ℂ) * u m * v m * (u * v) (r / m) * (u * v) (s / m)
