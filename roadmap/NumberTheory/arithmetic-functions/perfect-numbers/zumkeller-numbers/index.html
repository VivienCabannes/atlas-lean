    Zumkeller numbers

    Definitions-Zumkeller numbers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsSZumkeller

    A natural number \(n\) is s-Zumkeller if its proper divisors can be split into two disjoint nonempty sets whose elements have equal sums of squares. Each of the two sums is then \((\sigma_2(n) - n^2)/2\), where \(\sigma_2(n)\) is the sum of the squares of the positive divisors of \(n\).

    def IsSZumkeller (n : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ A ⊆ n.properDivisors, A.Nonempty ∧ (n.properDivisors \ A).Nonempty ∧
    ∑ d ∈ A, d ^ 2 = ∑ d ∈ n.properDivisors \ A, d ^ 2

    DefinitionUnitary s-Zumkeller numbers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsUnitarySZumkeller

    A natural number \(n\) is a unitary s-Zumkeller number when its proper unitary divisors (the unitary divisors of \(n\) other than \(n\)) split into two nonempty disjoint sets \(A\) and \(B\) with equal sums of squares, \(\sum_{d \in A} d^2 = \sum_{d \in B} d^2\).

    def IsUnitarySZumkeller (n : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ A ⊆ (Nat.unitaryDivisors n).erase n, A.Nonempty ∧
    ((Nat.unitaryDivisors n).erase n \ A).Nonempty ∧
    ∑ d ∈ A, d ^ 2 = ∑ d ∈ (Nat.unitaryDivisors n).erase n \ A, d ^ 2
    def unitaryDivisors (n : ℕ) : Finset ℕ :=
  n.divisors.filter fun d => d.Coprime (n / d)

    TheoremProduct formulas for the sum of divisors#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.sigma_one_prod_formulas

    Let \(n \ne 0\) be a natural number, let \(\sigma(n)\) be the sum of the divisors of \(n\), and for each prime \(p\) dividing \(n\) let \(k_p\) be the exponent of \(p\) in \(n\). Then, with products over the primes \(p\) dividing \(n\),

    \[\sigma(n) = \prod_{p \mid n} \frac{p^{k_p + 1} - 1}{p - 1}\]

    and

    \[\frac{\sigma(n)}{n} = \prod_{p \mid n} \frac{p^{k_p + 1} - 1}{p^{k_p} (p - 1)}.\]

    If moreover \(n > 1\), then \(\sigma(n)/n < \prod_{p \mid n} p/(p - 1)\).

    public theorem_wanted sigma_one_prod_formulas (n : ℕ) (hn : n ≠ 0) :
    (ArithmeticFunction.sigma 1 n : ℚ) =
        ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors, ((p : ℚ) ^ (n.factorization p + 1) - 1) / (p - 1) ∧
      (ArithmeticFunction.sigma 1 n : ℚ) / n =
        ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors,
          ((p : ℚ) ^ (n.factorization p + 1) - 1) / ((p : ℚ) ^ n.factorization p * (p - 1)) ∧
      (1 < n → (ArithmeticFunction.sigma 1 n : ℚ) / n < ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors, (p : ℚ) / (p - 1))

    TheoremProduct formulas for the sum of squared divisors#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.sigma_two_prod_formulas

    Let \(n \ne 0\) be a natural number, let \(\sigma_2(n)\) be the sum of the squares of the divisors of \(n\), and for each prime \(p\) dividing \(n\) let \(k_p\) be the exponent of \(p\) in \(n\). Then, with products over the primes \(p\) dividing \(n\),

    \[\sigma_2(n) = \prod_{p \mid n} \frac{p^{2(k_p + 1)} - 1}{p^2 - 1}\]

    and

    \[\frac{\sigma_2(n)}{n^2} = \prod_{p \mid n} \frac{p^{2(k_p + 1)} - 1}{p^{2k_p} (p^2 - 1)}.\]

    If moreover \(n > 1\), then \(\sigma_2(n)/n^2 < \prod_{p \mid n} p^2/(p^2 - 1)\).

    public theorem_wanted sigma_two_prod_formulas (n : ℕ) (hn : n ≠ 0) :
    (ArithmeticFunction.sigma 2 n : ℚ) =
        ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors, ((p : ℚ) ^ (2 * (n.factorization p + 1)) - 1) / (p ^ 2 - 1) ∧
      (ArithmeticFunction.sigma 2 n : ℚ) / n ^ 2 =
        ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors,
          ((p : ℚ) ^ (2 * (n.factorization p + 1)) - 1) /
            ((p : ℚ) ^ (2 * n.factorization p) * (p ^ 2 - 1)) ∧
      (1 < n → (ArithmeticFunction.sigma 2 n : ℚ) / n ^ 2 <
        ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors, (p : ℚ) ^ 2 / (p ^ 2 - 1))

    TheoremSum of squares of unitary divisors#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.sum_sq_unitaryDivisors

    For every positive integer \(n\), the sum of the squares of the unitary divisors of \(n\) equals \(\prod_{p} \left(1 + p^{2k_p}\right)\), where \(p\) ranges over the distinct prime factors of \(n\) and \(k_p\) is the exponent of \(p\) in \(n\).

    public theorem_wanted sum_sq_unitaryDivisors (n : ℕ) (hn : n ≠ 0) :
    ∑ d ∈ Nat.unitaryDivisors n, d ^ 2 =
      ∏ p ∈ n.primeFactors, (1 + p ^ (2 * n.factorization p))
    def unitaryDivisors (n : ℕ) : Finset ℕ :=
  n.divisors.filter fun d => d.Coprime (n / d)

    MetaMathlibExt.isSZumkeller_iff_of_even

    Let \(n\) be an even positive integer and let \(\sigma_2(n)\) be the sum of the squares of the positive divisors of \(n\).
