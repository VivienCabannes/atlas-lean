    TheoremEuler's theorem on odd perfect numbers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.euler_odd_perfect_form

    Every odd perfect number \(n\) can be written as \(n = q^s m^2\) with natural numbers \(q\), \(s\) and \(m\), where \(q\) is prime, \(q \equiv 1 \pmod 4\), \(s \equiv 1 \pmod 4\), and \(q\) and \(m\) are coprime.

    The statement is conditional: whether an odd perfect number exists remains open.

    public theorem euler_odd_perfect_form (n : ℕ) (hodd : Odd n) (hperf : n.Perfect) :
    ∃ q s m : ℕ,
      q.Prime ∧ n = q ^ s * m ^ 2 ∧ Nat.ModEq 4 q 1 ∧
        Nat.ModEq 4 s 1 ∧ q.Coprime m
