    DefinitionSpoof totient#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.spoofTotient

    Let \(F\) be a finite multiset of pairs \((x_i, a_i)\) of natural numbers, \(i = 1, \dots, r\), viewed as the formal product \(x_1^{a_1} \cdots x_r^{a_r}\). The spoof totient of \(F\) is the integer \(\tilde\varphi(F) = \prod_{i=1}^{r} (x_i^{a_i} - x_i^{a_i - 1})\), where the exponent \(a_i - 1\) is truncated at \(0\), so a pair with \(a_i = 0\) contributes the factor \(0\).

    def spoofTotient (F : Multiset (ℕ × ℕ)) : ℤ :=
  (F.map fun p => (p.1 : ℤ) ^ p.2 - (p.1 : ℤ) ^ (p.2 - 1)).prod

    DefinitionSpoof Lehmer factorizations#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsSpoofLehmer

    Let \(k\) be an integer and \(F\) a finite multiset of pairs \((x, a)\) of natural numbers. Write \(\tilde e(F) = \prod x^a\) for the spoof evaluation and \(\tilde\varphi(F) = \prod (x^a - x^{a-1})\) for the spoof totient of \(F\), the products running over the pairs of \(F\) with multiplicity.     def IsSpoofLehmer (k : ℤ) (F : Multiset (ℕ × ℕ)) : Prop :=
  IsSpoofFactorization F ∧ k * spoofTotient F = spoofEval F - 1 ∧
    (2 ≤ Multiset.card F ∨ ∃ p ∈ F, 1 < p.2)
