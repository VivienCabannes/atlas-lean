    MetaMathlibExt.primitivePrimeDivisor_pow_sub_one_mod_eq_one

    Let \(a \ge 2\) and \(n \ge 2\) be natural numbers, and let \(p\) be a primitive prime divisor of the integer sequence \(m \mapsto a^m - 1\) at index \(n\), that is, a prime that divides \(a^n - 1\) and divides no \(a^m - 1\) with \(1 \le m < n\). Then \(p \equiv 1 \pmod n\).
