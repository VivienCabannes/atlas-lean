    Let \(s\) be a finite subset of a type \(\alpha\), let \(f : \alpha \to \alpha\) satisfy \(f(a) \in s\), \(f(f(a)) = a\) and \(f(a) \neq a\) for every \(a \in s\), and let \(g : \alpha \to \mathbb{Z}\) satisfy \(g(f(a)) = g(a)\) for every \(a \in s\).     Finset.sum_div_two_mod_two_eq_card_filter

    Let \(s\) be a finite set of natural numbers and let \(f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) be a function such that \(f(k)\) is even for every \(k \in s\). Then the natural number \(\frac{1}{2} \sum_{k \in s} f(k)\) is congruent modulo \(2\) to the number of elements \(k \in s\) with \(f(k) \equiv 2 \pmod 4\).

    theorem sum_div_two_mod_two_eq_card_filter (s : Finset ℕ) (f : ℕ → ℕ)
    (heven : ∀ k ∈ s, Even (f k)) :
    ((∑ k ∈ s, f k) / 2) % 2
      = (Finset.filter (fun k => f k % 4 = 2) s).card % 2
