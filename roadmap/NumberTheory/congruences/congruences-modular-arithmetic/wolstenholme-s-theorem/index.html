    TheoremCarlitz's block harmonic congruence#✓fully proved

    ZMod.carlitz_sum_inv_block_Ico_eq_zero_of_five_le

    Let \(p \ge 5\) be a prime and let \(k \in \mathbb{N}\). Then in \(\mathbb{Z}/p^2\mathbb{Z}\)

    \[\sum_{r=1}^{p-1} (kp + r)^{-1} = 0.\]

    Each \(kp + r\) with \(1 \le r \le p - 1\) is prime to \(p\), so these inverses are genuine inverses modulo \(p^2\).

    Cited original: L. Carlitz, "A note on Wolstenholme's theorem", American Mathematical Monthly 61 (1954), 174–176.

    theorem ZMod.carlitz_sum_inv_block_Ico_eq_zero_of_five_le
    (p k : ℕ) (hp : p.Prime) (hp5 : 5 ≤ p) :
    (Finset.Ico 1 p).sum
      (fun r => (((k * p + r : ℕ) : ZMod (p ^ 2))⁻¹)) = 0

    TheoremWolstenholme's theorem#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.Wolstenholme.wolstenholme

    If \(p \ge 5\) is prime, then \(\sum_{k=1}^{p-1} k^{-1} = 0\) in \(\mathbb{Z}/p^2\mathbb{Z}\), where \(k^{-1}\) is the inverse of \(k\) modulo \(p^2\).

    theorem wolstenholme (p : ℕ) (hp : p.Prime) (h5 : 5 ≤ p) :
    ∑ k ∈ Finset.Ico 1 p, ((k : ZMod (p ^ 2))⁻¹) = 0

    TheoremWolstenholme's congruence for odd powers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.wolstenholme_harmonic_power_sum

    Let \(\nu \ge 1\) be odd and let \(p\) be a prime with \(p \ge \nu + 4\). Then in \(\mathbb{Z}/p^2\mathbb{Z}\)

    \[ \sum_{t=1}^{p-1} (t^{\nu})^{-1} = 0, \]

    where each \(t^{\nu}\) is invertible modulo \(p^2\) because \(1 \le t < p\).

    For \(\nu = 1\) this is Wolstenholme's congruence of 1862; the general statement is Theorem 131 of Hardy and Wright and was first studied by Leudesdorf.

    theorem wolstenholme_harmonic_power_sum
    (ν p : ℕ) (hν : 1 ≤ ν) (hνodd : Odd ν)
    (hp : p.Prime) (hpν : ν + 4 ≤ p) :
    ∑ t ∈ Finset.Icc 1 (p - 1), (((t : ZMod (p ^ 2)) ^ ν)⁻¹) = 0

    Let \(p \ge 11\) be a prime and \(t\) a natural number.     \[\binom{tp}{p} \equiv t + t^2 (t-1) \, p \, H_{p-1}^{(1)} \pmod{p^5}.\]
