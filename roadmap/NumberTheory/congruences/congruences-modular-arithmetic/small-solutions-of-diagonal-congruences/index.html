    MetaMathlibExt.product_add_add_eq_univ_of_card_mul_gt

    Let \(p\) be a prime and let \(A_1, A_2, A_3, D\) be subsets of \(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}\) with \(|A_1| |A_2| |A_3| |D| > p^3\).     TheoremDiagonal congruences in \(k\) variables are solvable#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.diagonal_congruence_solvable_of_card_eq_exponent

    Let \(p\) be a prime and \(k \ge 1\). For all integers \(a_1, \dots, a_k\) not divisible by \(p\) and every integer \(c\), there are integers \(x_1, \dots, x_k\) with \(a_1 x_1^k + \dots + a_k x_k^k \equiv c \pmod p\).

    Equivalently, \(\Gamma(k, p) \le k\), where \(\Gamma(k, p)\) is the least \(n\) such that every such congruence in \(n\) variables is solvable; extra variables can be set to \(0\).

    public theorem_wanted diagonal_congruence_solvable_of_card_eq_exponent (p : ℕ) [Fact p.Prime]

    ∃ x : Fin k → ℤ, ∑ i, a i * x i ^ k ≡ c [ZMOD p]
