    POND and PEND partitions

    DefinitionThe POND condition#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsPondParts

    A multiset \(s\) of natural numbers (the parts of a partition) satisfies the POND condition when no odd number occurs in \(s\) exactly once; equivalently, every odd part that occurs in \(s\) has multiplicity at least \(2\), so the odd parts are not distinct.

    def IsPondParts (s : Multiset ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∀ i ∈ s, Odd i → s.count i ≠ 1

    DefinitionNumber of POND partitions#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.pondCount

    For a natural number \(n\), \(\mathrm{pond}(n)\) is the number of partitions of \(n\) that are POND, that is, in which every odd part that occurs has multiplicity at least \(2\) (OEIS A265256).

    def pondCount (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((Finset.univ : Finset n.Partition).filter fun p => IsPondParts p.parts).card
    def IsPondParts (s : Multiset ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∀ i ∈ s, Odd i → s.count i ≠ 1

    TheoremExamples of POND and PEND parts#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.isPondParts_isPendParts_examples

    The multisets of parts \(\{1, 1\}\) and \(\{2\}\) satisfy the POND condition, while \(\{1\}\) and \(\{2, 1\}\) do not; the multisets \(\{1\}\) and \(\{2, 2\}\) satisfy the PEND condition, while \(\{2\}\) and \(\{2, 4\}\) do not.

    theorem isPondParts_isPendParts_examples :
    IsPondParts {1, 1} ∧ ¬ IsPondParts {1} ∧ ¬ IsPondParts {2, 1} ∧ IsPondParts {2} ∧
      IsPendParts {1} ∧ IsPendParts {2, 2} ∧ ¬ IsPendParts {2} ∧ ¬ IsPendParts {2, 4}
    def IsPondParts (s : Multiset ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∀ i ∈ s, Odd i → s.count i ≠ 1
