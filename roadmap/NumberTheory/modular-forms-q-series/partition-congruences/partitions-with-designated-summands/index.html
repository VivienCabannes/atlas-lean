    DefinitionGenerating function of odd designated-summand partitions#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.oddDesignatedSummandsSeries

    For \(k \ge 1\) let \(f_k = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{km})\) be the Euler product. This is the integer power series \(f_4 f_6^2 / (f_1 f_3 f_{12})\) in \(q\), where the denominator \(f_1 f_3 f_{12}\) is invertible because its constant coefficient is \(1\). It is the generating function \(\sum_{n \ge 0} \mathrm{PD}_2(n) q^n\) of the numbers \(\mathrm{PD}_2(n)\) of partitions of \(n\) with designated summands into odd parts.

    noncomputable def oddDesignatedSummandsSeries : ℤ⟦X⟧ :=
  𝔣 4 * 𝔣 6 ^ 2 * invOfUnit (𝔣 1 * 𝔣 3 * 𝔣 12) 1
