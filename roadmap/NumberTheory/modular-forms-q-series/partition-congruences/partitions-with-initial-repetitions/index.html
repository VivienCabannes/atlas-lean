    TheoremResidues that are squares modulo \(2^{2k-1}\) but not modulo \(2^{2k}\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.isSquare_mod_two_pow_odd_not_even_iff

    Let \(k \ge 0\) and let \(x\) be an integer with \(0 \le x < 2^{2k}\), where the exponents \(2k - 1\) and \(2k - 2\) are truncated at \(0\). Then \(x\) is a square modulo \(2^{2k-1}\) but not a square modulo \(2^{2k}\) if and only if \(x = 2^{2k-1}\) or \(x = 2^{2k-1} + 2^{2k-2}\).

    For \(k \ge 1\) these are exactly two residues; for \(k = 0\) both sides of the equivalence are false.

    public theorem_wanted isSquare_mod_two_pow_odd_not_even_iff (k : ℕ) (x : ℕ)
    (hx : x < 2 ^ (2 * k)) :
    (IsSquare (x : ZMod (2 ^ (2 * k - 1))) ∧ ¬IsSquare (x : ZMod (2 ^ (2 * k)))) ↔
      x = 2 ^ (2 * k - 1) ∨ x = 2 ^ (2 * k - 1) + 2 ^ (2 * k - 2)

    TheoremResidues \(r\) with \(16r + 1\) and \(8r + 1\) both non-squares modulo \(p\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.card_not_isSquare_sixteen_mul_add_one_eight_mul_add_one

    For every prime \(p\), the number of integers \(r\) with \(1 \le r \le p - 1\) such that neither \(16r + 1\) nor \(8r + 1\) is a square modulo \(p\) equals \(\lfloor p/4 \rfloor\).

    Here \(0\) counts as a square modulo \(p\), so "not a square" means "quadratic nonresidue". The paper assumes \(p \ge 5\); for \(p = 2\) and \(p = 3\) both sides are \(0\). If \(0\) were counted as a non-square, the count at \(p = 5\) would be \(2\) instead of \(1\).

    public theorem_wanted card_not_isSquare_sixteen_mul_add_one_eight_mul_add_one (p : ℕ)
    (hp : p.Prime) :
    ((Finset.Icc 1 (p - 1)).filter fun r =>
        ¬IsSquare ((16 * r + 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p) ∧ ¬IsSquare ((8 * r + 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p)).card =
      p / 4

    TheoremResidues \(r\) with \(12r + 1\) and \(8r + 1\) both non-squares modulo \(p\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.card_not_isSquare_twelve_mul_add_one_eight_mul_add_one

        ¬IsSquare ((12 * r + 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p) ∧ ¬IsSquare ((8 * r + 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p)).card =

    TheoremCounting \(r\) with \(mr + 1\) and \(nr + 1\) both quadratic nonresidues#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.four_mul_card_legendreSym_pair_eq_neg_one

    Let \(p\) be an odd prime, write \(\left(\frac{a}{p}\right)\) for the Legendre symbol of an integer \(a\), and let \(m\) and \(n\) be integers with \(p \nmid mn(n - m)\).     TheoremAndrews' theorem on partitions with initial repetitions#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.card_initialRepetitions_eq_card_not_dvd_eq_card_count_le

    Let \(k \ge 1\) and \(n \ge 0\) be integers. Say that a partition has initial \(k\)-repetitions if, whenever a part \(j\) appears at least \(k\) times, every integer \(i\) with \(1 \le i < j\) appears at least \(k\) times. Then the number of partitions of \(n\) with initial \(k\)-repetitions equals the number of partitions of \(n\) into parts not divisible by \(2k\), and this number equals the number of partitions of \(n\) in which no part appears more than \(2k - 1\) times.

    public theorem_wanted card_initialRepetitions_eq_card_not_dvd_eq_card_count_le (n k : ℕ)
    (hk : 1 ≤ k) :
    ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
        ∀ j ∈ p.parts, k ≤ p.parts.count j → ∀ i ∈ Finset.Ico 1 j, k ≤ p.parts.count i).card =
      ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
        ∀ a ∈ p.parts, ¬ 2 * k ∣ a).card ∧
    ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
        ∀ a ∈ p.parts, ¬ 2 * k ∣ a).card =
      ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
        ∀ a ∈ p.parts, p.parts.count a ≤ 2 * k - 1).card

    DefinitionThe partition function \(c_2\) of Nyirenda and Mugwangwavari#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.initialRepetitionsC2

    For an integer \(n \ge 0\), \(c_2(n)\) is the number of partitions of \(n\) for which there is an integer \(j\) with \(1 \le j \le n\) such that \(j\) appears exactly \(j\) times, every part other than \(j\) is at least \(2j + 1\), and every even part that is at least \(2j + 2\) appears only once.

    Odd parts that are at least \(2j + 1\) may appear any number of times.

    public def initialRepetitionsC2 (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
    ∃ j ∈ Finset.Icc 1 n, p.parts.count j = j ∧ (∀ a ∈ p.parts, a = j ∨ 2 * j + 1 ≤ a) ∧
      ∀ a ∈ p.parts, Even a → 2 * j + 2 ≤ a → p.parts.count a = 1).card

    DefinitionThe partition function \(c_8\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.initialRepetitionsC8

    For \(n \ge 0\), \(c_8(n)\) is the number of partitions of \(n\) such that either (a) all parts are even and distinct, or (b) for some positive integer \(j\), the largest odd part is \(2j - 1\), every positive odd integer \(i \le j\) appears once or twice, every odd integer \(i\) with \(j < i \le 2j - 1\) appears exactly once, all even parts are distinct, and every even part greater than \(j\) is at least \(2j + 2\).

    For \(j = 1\) the paper's clauses "the largest odd part \(2j - 1\) appears once" and "odd integers \(\le j\) appear once or twice" conflict; the second governs, as in the generating function \((q^2;q^2)_\infty \sum_n q^{n^2}/(q;q)_n\) the paper uses (the other reading gives \(c_8(2) = 1\), contradicting Theorem 11 of the paper since \(31\) is not a square).

    public def initialRepetitionsC8 (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
    (∀ a ∈ p.parts, Even a ∧ p.parts.count a = 1) ∨
      ∃ j ∈ Finset.Icc 1 n, 2 * j - 1 ∈ p.parts ∧ (∀ a ∈ p.parts, Odd a → a ≤ 2 * j - 1) ∧
        (∀ i ∈ Finset.Icc 1 j, Odd i → p.parts.count i = 1 ∨ p.parts.count i = 2) ∧
        (∀ i ∈ Finset.Ioc j (2 * j - 1), Odd i → p.parts.count i = 1) ∧
        (∀ a ∈ p.parts, Even a → p.parts.count a = 1) ∧
        ∀ a ∈ p.parts, Even a → j < a → 2 * j + 2 ≤ a).card

    DefinitionThe initial-repetitions partition function \(c_8'\)#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.initialRepetitionsC8Prime

    For \(n \ge 0\), \(c_8'(n)\) is the number of partitions of \(n\) into distinct even parts plus the sum, over \(m = 1, \dots, n\), of the number of partitions of \(n\) in which every even number \(2i\) with \(1 \le i \le m\) is a part, every even part at most \(m\) appears at most twice, every odd part at most \(m\) appears exactly once, and every part greater than \(m\) is even and appears exactly once.

    Equivalently, \(c_8'(n)\) counts partitions of \(n\) with a possibly distinguished part: either all parts are even and distinct and no part is distinguished, or a part \(2m\) is distinguished and the conditions above hold for \(m\).

    public def initialRepetitionsC8Prime (n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
      ∀ a ∈ p.parts, Even a ∧ p.parts.count a = 1).card +
    ∑ m ∈ Finset.Icc 1 n, ((Finset.univ : Finset (Nat.Partition n)).filter fun p =>
      (∀ i ∈ Finset.Icc 1 m, 2 * i ∈ p.parts) ∧
        (∀ a ∈ p.parts, Even a → a ≤ m → p.parts.count a ≤ 2) ∧
        (∀ a ∈ p.parts, Odd a → a ≤ m → p.parts.count a = 1) ∧
        ∀ a ∈ p.parts, m < a → Even a ∧ p.parts.count a = 1).card
