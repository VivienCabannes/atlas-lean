    DefinitionGeneralized tau function#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.generalizedTau

    For an integer \(k\) and a natural number \(n\), the generalized tau function \(\tau_k(n)\) is the coefficient of \(q^n\) in the integer power series \(q \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^m)^k\). For negative \(k\) the power is taken in the group of units of \(\mathbb{Z}[[q]]\), where \(\prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^m)\) is invertible because its constant term is \(1\).

    The paper requires \(k \ne 0\); \(k = 0\) gives \(\tau_0(1) = 1\) and \(\tau_0(n) = 0\) for \(n \ne 1\). \(\tau_{24}\) is Ramanujan's tau function, and \(\tau_1(n + 1)\) is Euler's \(\omega(n)\), the coefficient of \(q^n\) in \(\prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^m)\).

    public noncomputable def generalizedTau (k : ℤ) (n : ℕ) : ℤ :=
  coeff n (X * ((isUnit_pentagonalSeries_int.unit ^ k : ℤ⟦X⟧ˣ) : ℤ⟦X⟧))

    MetaMathlibExt.generalizedTau_succ_eq_partition_sum

    Let \(k \ge 1\) and \(n \ge 0\) be natural numbers. For a partition \(\lambda\) of \(n\) and a part \(a\) of \(\lambda\), write \(f_a\) for the number of times \(a\) occurs in \(\lambda\). Then

    \[\tau_k(n + 1) = \sum_{\lambda} \prod_{a} (-1)^{f_a} \binom{k}{f_a},\]

    where \(\lambda\) runs over the partitions of \(n\) with \(f_a \le k\) for every part \(a\) and the product runs over the distinct parts \(a\) of \(\lambda\), and

    \[\tau_{-k}(n + 1) = \sum_{\lambda} \prod_{a} \binom{f_a + k - 1}{k - 1},\]

    where \(\lambda\) runs over all partitions of \(n\) and the product again runs over the distinct parts \(a\) of \(\lambda\).
