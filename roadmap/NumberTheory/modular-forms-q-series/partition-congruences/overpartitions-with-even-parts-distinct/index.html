    MetaMathlibExt.overpartitionPairEvenDistinctGF

    For natural numbers \(j\) and \(k\), let \(f_a = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{am})\) be the Euler products. This generating function is the integer power series \(f_4^2 \bigl(\prod_{i \ge 0} (1 + q^{j+ki})\bigr)^2 \, g\), where \(g\) is the inverse of \(f_1^2\) (whose constant coefficient is \(1\)) and the infinite products are taken in the coefficientwise topology. In \(q\)-Pochhammer notation it is \((q^4; q^4)_\infty^2 (-q^j; q^k)_\infty^2 / (q; q)_\infty^2\). It is used for \(k \ge j \ge 1\).

    public noncomputable def overpartitionPairEvenDistinctGF (j k : ℕ) : ℤ⟦X⟧ :=
  qEulerProduct 4 ^ 2 * (∏' i : ℕ, (1 + X ^ (j + k * i))) ^ 2 *
    PowerSeries.invOfUnit (qEulerProduct 1 ^ 2) 1
