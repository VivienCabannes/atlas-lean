    Overcubic partition triples

    DefinitionThe overcubic partition triple series#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.overcubicTripleSeries

    The generating function of overcubic partition triples is the integer power series \(f_4^3 / (f_1^6 f_2^3)\) in \(q\), where \(f_k = \prod_{j \ge 1} (1 - q^{kj})\); the inverse of \(f_1^6 f_2^3\) exists because its constant coefficient is \(1\).

    noncomputable def overcubicTripleSeries : ℤ⟦X⟧ :=
  𝔣 4 ^ 3 * invOfUnit (𝔣 1 ^ 6 * 𝔣 2 ^ 3) 1

    TheoremGenerating function for overcubic partition triples#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.overcubicTripleCount_generatingFunction

    For \(k \ge 1\) let \(f_k = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{km})\), and let \(\mathrm{bt}(n)\), the number of overcubic partition triples of \(n\), be the coefficient of \(q^n\) in \(f_4^3/(f_1^6 f_2^3)\). Then, as integer power series in \(q\),

    TheoremThe 2-dissection of \(f_1^2\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.f1_sq_two_dissection

    For \(k \ge 1\) let \(f_k = \prod_{m \ge 1} (1 - q^{km})\), an integer power series in \(q\). Then

    \[f_1^2 f_4^2 f_8 f_{16}^2 = f_2 f_8^6 - 2 q f_2 f_4^2 f_{16}^4.\]

    Dividing by \(f_4^2 f_8 f_{16}^2\) gives \(f_1^2 = \frac{f_2 f_8^5}{f_4^2 f_{16}^2} - 2q \frac{f_2 f_{16}^2}{f_8}\).

    public theorem_wanted f1_sq_two_dissection :
    𝔣 1 ^ 2 * 𝔣 4 ^ 2 * 𝔣 8 * 𝔣 16 ^ 2 =
      𝔣 2 * 𝔣 8 ^ 6 - 2 * X * 𝔣 2 * 𝔣 4 ^ 2 * 𝔣 16 ^ 4
