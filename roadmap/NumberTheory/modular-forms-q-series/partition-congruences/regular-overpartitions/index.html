    DefinitionNumber of \(\ell\)-regular overpartitions#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.regularOverpartitionCount

    For natural numbers \(\ell\) and \(n\), \(A_\ell(n)\) is the number of overpartitions of \(n\) in which no part is divisible by \(\ell\).

    No positivity restriction is placed on \(\ell\); for \(\ell = 0\) every overpartition of \(n\) is counted, since parts are positive.

    public noncomputable def regularOverpartitionCount (ℓ n : ℕ) : ℕ :=
  Nat.card {O : Nat.Overpartition n // ∀ a ∈ O.toPartition.parts, ¬ ℓ ∣ a}

    TheoremTruncated generating function for \(\ell\)-regular overpartitions#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.regularOverpartitionCount_eq_coeff_truncatedProduct

    Let \(\ell \ge 1\), \(N\) and \(n \le N\) be natural numbers, and let \(A_\ell(n)\) be the number of overpartitions of \(n\) with no part divisible by \(\ell\). Then the coefficient of \(X^n\) in the polynomial

    \[\prod_{1 \le k \le N,\ \ell \nmid k} \Big((1 + X^k) \sum_{i=0}^{N} X^{ki}\Big) \in \mathbb{N}[X]\]

    equals \(A_\ell(n)\).

    This is a finite-polynomial form of the generating function \(\sum_{n \ge 0} A_\ell(n) q^n = \prod_{k \ge 1,\ \ell \nmid k} (1 + q^k)/(1 - q^k)\): each geometric series is cut off at \(i = N\), and only coefficients up to degree \(N\) are compared.

    public theorem_wanted regularOverpartitionCount_eq_coeff_truncatedProduct
    (ℓ N n : ℕ) (hℓ : 1 ≤ ℓ) (hn : n ≤ N) :
    (∏ k ∈ (Finset.Icc 1 N).filter (fun k => ¬ ℓ ∣ k),
      (((1 : Polynomial ℕ) + Polynomial.X ^ k) *
        ∑ i ∈ Finset.range (N + 1), Polynomial.X ^ (k * i))).coeff n =
      regularOverpartitionCount ℓ n

    TheoremDouble coset representatives for \(\Gamma_0(N)\)#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.exists_gamma0_lowerUnipotent_T_zpow_decomposition

    Let \(N\) be a natural number such that either \(N\) is squarefree, or \(N\) is even and \(N/2\) is squarefree.
