    Let \(F\) be a field and let \(f \in F[T]\) be a nonzero polynomial. Then the cardinality of the quotient ring \(F[T]/(f)\) is \(|F|^{\deg f}\), where cardinalities are natural numbers and an infinite set has cardinality \(0\).

    theorem cardQuot_span_eq_pow_natDegree {f : F[X]} (hf : f ≠ 0) :
    (Ideal.span {f}).cardQuot = Nat.card F ^ f.natDegree

    DefinitionThe norm on \(F(T)\)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.ChowlaAndTwinPrimeOverFqTWanted.ChowlaAndTwinPrimeOverFqT.ringNorm

    Let \(F\) be a finite field. The ring norm on the polynomial ring \(F[T]\) sends \(f\) to the number of elements of the quotient ring \(F[T]/(f)\), written \(|f| = |F[T]/(f)|\); for \(f = 0\) the quotient is infinite and \(|0| = 0\). It is a ring norm: \(|f| = 0\) only for \(f = 0\), \(|-f| = |f|\), \(|f + g| \le |f| + |g|\) and \(|fg| \le |f|\,|g|\).

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.ChowlaAndTwinPrimeOverFqTWanted.ChowlaAndTwinPrimeOverFqT.𝔖_q

    Let \(F\) be a finite field with \(q\) elements and let \(h \in F[T]\). The Hardy–Littlewood constant of the prime pair \(\{f, f + h\}\) is the infinite product over all primes \(P\) of \(F[T]\) (the monic irreducible polynomials)

    \[\mathfrak{S}_q(h) = \prod_P \frac{1 - |P|^{-1} - |P|^{-1}\,\mathbf{1}_{P \nmid h}}{(1 - |P|^{-1})^2},\]

    where \(|P|\) is the norm of \(P\) and \(\mathbf{1}_{P \nmid h}\) is \(1\) if \(P\) does not divide \(h\) and \(0\) otherwise.

    The product is an unconditional infinite product of real numbers; when it does not converge its value is taken to be \(1\).

    noncomputable def 𝔖_q (h : F[X]) : ℝ :=
  ∏' P : primes F, (1 - ‖P‖⁻¹ - ‖P‖⁻¹ * indicator (¬ (P : F[X]) ∣ h)) / (1 - ‖P‖⁻¹) ^ 2

    Let \(F\) be a finite field and let \(X\) be a real number. This is the set of monic polynomials \(f \in F[T]\) whose norm \(|f| = |F[T]/(f)|\), the number of elements of the quotient ring \(F[T]/(f)\), satisfies \(|f| \le X\).

    Let \(p\) be an odd prime, let \(k \ge 1\) be a natural number, and let \(q\) be a power of \(p\) with \(q > p^2 k^2 e^2\), where \(e\) is Euler's number; write \(\mathbb{F}_q\) for the finite field with \(q\) elements.     \[\sum_{f \text{ monic},\ |f| \le X} \mu(f + h_1)\,\mu(f + h_2) \cdots \mu(f + h_k) = o(X),\]
