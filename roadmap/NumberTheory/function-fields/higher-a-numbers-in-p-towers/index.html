    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero, let \(f : \mathbb{N} \to K\) and let \(L \ge 1\) be a natural number such that \(f(i + L) = f(i)\) for all \(i \ge 1\). Let \(\bar f = \frac{1}{L} \sum_{k=1}^{L} f(k)\). Then for every natural number \(N\),

    \[\sum_{i=1}^{N} f(i) = N \bar f + \sum_{k=1}^{N \bmod L} \left(f(k) - \bar f\right).\]

    DefinitionPrime-to-\(p\) part of the denominator#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.primeToPDen

    For a natural number \(p\) and a rational number \(x\) whose denominator in lowest terms is \(b \ge 1\), \(x_D\) is the natural number \(b / p^k\), where \(p^k\) is the largest power of \(p\) dividing \(b\) (with \(k = 0\) when \(p = 1\)).

    def primeToPDen (p : ℕ) (x : ℚ) : ℕ := x.den / p ^ padicValNat p x.den
