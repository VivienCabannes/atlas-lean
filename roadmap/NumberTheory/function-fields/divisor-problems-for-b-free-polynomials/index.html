    DefinitionDirichlet convolution over monic divisors#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.monicDirichletConv

    Let \(F\) be a field. The Dirichlet convolution of functions \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) from \(F[T]\) to \(\mathbb{C}\) is the function \(\alpha * \beta\) given at \(f \in F[T]\) by

    \[(\alpha * \beta)(f) = \sum_{g \text{ monic},\ g \mid f} \alpha(g)\, \beta(f/g),\]

    the sum running over the monic divisors \(g\) of \(f\).

    When \(f = 0\) every monic polynomial divides \(f\); the sum is then the sum of its nonzero terms if there are finitely many, and \(0\) otherwise.

    public noncomputable def monicDirichletConv {F : Type*} [Field F] (α β : F[X] → ℂ)
    (f : F[X]) : ℂ :=
  ∑ᶠ g ∈ {g : F[X] | g.Monic ∧ g ∣ f}, α g * β (f /ₘ g)

    MetaMathlibExt.monic_sum_moebius_div_norm

    Let \(F\) be a finite field with \(q\) elements and let \(\mu\) be the Möbius function of \(F[T]\). For every monic \(f \in F[T]\),

    \[\sum_{g \text{ monic},\ g \mid f} \frac{\mu(g)}{q^{\deg g}} = \prod_{P \text{ monic irreducible},\ P \mid f} \left(1 - \frac{1}{q^{\deg P}}\right).\]

      ∏ᶠ P ∈ {P : F[X] | P.Monic ∧ Irreducible P ∧ P ∣ f},
        (1 - 1 / (Fintype.card F : ℝ) ^ P.natDegree)
