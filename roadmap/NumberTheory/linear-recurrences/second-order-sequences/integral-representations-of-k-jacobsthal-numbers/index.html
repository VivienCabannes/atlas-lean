  | 1 => b
  | n + 2 => k * companionKJacobsthal k a b (n + 1) + 2 * companionKJacobsthal k a b n

    MetaMathlibExt.kJacobsthal_binet_identities

    Let \(k\) and \(n\) be natural numbers, let \(J_{k,n} = U_n(k, -2)\) and \(j_{k,n} = V_n(k, -2)\) be the \(k\)-Jacobsthal and \(k\)-Jacobsthal–Lucas numbers, and let \(\Delta_k = \sqrt{k^2 + 8}\) and \(\sigma_k = (k + \Delta_k)/2\).
