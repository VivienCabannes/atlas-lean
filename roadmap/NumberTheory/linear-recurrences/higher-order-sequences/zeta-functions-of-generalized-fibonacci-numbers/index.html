    Zeta functions of generalized Fibonacci numbers

    Definitionℓ-generalized Fibonacci sequence#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.IsLGeneralizedFibonacci

    Let \(\ell\) be a natural number. A function \(F : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}\) is an \(\ell\)-generalized Fibonacci sequence when \(F(n) = 0\) for every integer \(n\) with \(2 - \ell \le n \le 0\), \(F(1) = 1\), and \(F(n) = \sum_{i=0}^{\ell-1} F(n-1-i)\) for every integer \(n > 1\).

    The values of \(F\) below \(2 - \ell\) are unconstrained, since the sequence is defined only from that index onward.

    public def IsLGeneralizedFibonacci (ℓ : ℕ) (F : ℤ → ℤ) : Prop :=
  (∀ n : ℤ, 2 - (ℓ : ℤ) ≤ n → n < 1 → F n = 0) ∧
    F 1 = 1 ∧
    ∀ n : ℤ, 1 < n →
      F n = ∑ i ∈ Finset.range ℓ, F (n - 1 - (i : ℤ))

    DefinitionCharacteristic polynomial of the ℓ-generalized Fibonacci recurrence#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lGeneralizedFibonacciCharacteristic

    For a natural number \(\ell\), the characteristic polynomial of the \(\ell\)-generalized Fibonacci recurrence is the polynomial \(\varphi_\ell(X) = X^\ell - \sum_{i=0}^{\ell-1} X^i\) in \(\mathbb{C}[X]\); for \(\ell \ge 2\) this is \(X^\ell - X^{\ell-1} - \cdots - X - 1\).

    public noncomputable def lGeneralizedFibonacciCharacteristic (ℓ : ℕ) : Polynomial ℂ :=
  Polynomial.X ^ ℓ - ∑ i ∈ Finset.range ℓ, Polynomial.X ^ i

    DefinitionDresden–Du coefficient#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lGeneralizedFibonacciCoefficient

    For a natural number \(\ell\) and a complex number \(\beta\), the Dresden–Du coefficient is the complex number \(c(\beta) = \frac{\beta - 1}{2 + (\ell + 1)(\beta - 2)}\), taken to be \(0\) when the denominator vanishes.

    public noncomputable def lGeneralizedFibonacciCoefficient (ℓ : ℕ) (β : ℂ) : ℂ :=
  (β - 1) / (2 + ((ℓ : ℂ) + 1) * (β - 2))

    DefinitionPossible pole of the ℓ-generalized Fibonacci zeta function at a given index#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lGeneralizedFibonacciZetaPole

    Let \(\ell\) be a natural number, let \(\alpha\) be a real number, and let \(R\) be the set of complex roots of \(X^\ell - \sum_{i\lt \ell} X^i\) other than \(\alpha\). For a function \(\kappa\) from \(\mathbb{C}\) to the natural numbers and an integer \(n\), the possible pole indexed by \((\kappa, n)\) is

    \[ s_{\kappa,n} = -\sum_{\beta \in R} \kappa(\beta) + \frac{2 n \pi i + \sum_{\beta \in R} \kappa(\beta) \log \beta}{\log \alpha}, \]

    where \(\log\) is the principal branch of the complex logarithm.

    Only the values of \(\kappa\) on \(R\) enter; their sum is the number called \(k\) in the paper.

    public noncomputable def lGeneralizedFibonacciZetaPole

  -((∑ β ∈ lGeneralizedFibonacciOtherRoots ℓ α, κ β : ℕ) : ℂ) +
    (2 * (n : ℂ) * (Real.pi : ℂ) * Complex.I +
        ∑ β ∈ lGeneralizedFibonacciOtherRoots ℓ α, (κ β : ℂ) * Complex.log β) /
      Complex.log (α : ℂ)

    DefinitionResidue expression of the ℓ-generalized Fibonacci zeta function#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lGeneralizedFibonacciZetaResidue

    Let \(\ell\) be a natural number, let \(\alpha\) be a real number, let \(R\) be the set of complex roots of \(X^\ell - \sum_{i\lt \ell} X^i\) other than \(\alpha\), let \(c(\beta) = (\beta - 1)/(2 + (\ell + 1)(\beta - 2))\) be the Dresden–Du coefficient, and put \(d = c(\alpha)/\alpha\). For a function \(\kappa\) from \(\mathbb{C}\) to the natural numbers and an integer \(n\), let \(k = \sum_{\beta \in R} \kappa(\beta)\) and let \(s_0\) be the possible pole indexed by \((\kappa, n)\). The residue expression indexed by \((\kappa, n)\) is

    \[ \rho_{\kappa,n} = d^{-s_0} \binom{-s_0}{k} d^{-k} \cdot \frac{k!}{\prod_{\beta \in R} \kappa(\beta)!} \cdot \prod_{\beta \in R} \left(\frac{c(\beta)}{\beta}\right)^{\kappa(\beta)} \cdot \frac{1}{\log \alpha}, \]

    where \(\binom{-s_0}{k} = (-s_0)(-s_0 - 1)\cdots(-s_0 - k + 1)/k!\) is the generalized binomial coefficient, \(d^{-s_0}\) and \(d^{-k}\) are principal complex powers, and \(\log\) is the principal branch of the complex logarithm.

    TheoremResidues of the ℓ-generalized Fibonacci zeta function#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.lGeneralizedFibonacciZeta_residue

    Let \(\ell \ge 2\), let \(F\) be an \(\ell\)-generalized Fibonacci sequence, and let \(\alpha > 1\) be a real number with \(\alpha^\ell = \sum_{i\lt \ell} \alpha^i\). Let \(R\) be the set of complex roots of \(X^\ell - \sum_{i\lt \ell} X^i\) other than \(\alpha\), let \(s_{\kappa,n}\) be the possible pole and \(\rho_{\kappa,n}\) the residue expression indexed by a function \(\kappa\) from \(\mathbb{C}\) to the natural numbers and an integer \(n\), and let \(S\) be the set of all possible poles \(s_{\kappa,n}\). Fix \(\kappa\) and \(n\) such that whenever \(s_{\kappa',n'} = s_{\kappa,n}\), we have \(n' = n\) and \(\kappa'(\beta) = \kappa(\beta)\) for every \(\beta \in R\). Let \(f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}\) satisfy \(f(s) = \sum_{m \ge 1} F_m^{-s}\) (principal complex powers) for every \(s\) with \(\operatorname{Re} s > 0\), and let \(f\) be complex differentiable at every point outside \(S\). Then \((s - s_{\kappa,n}) f(s)\) tends to \(\rho_{\kappa,n}\) as \(s\) tends to \(s_{\kappa,n}\) with \(s \ne s_{\kappa,n}\).

    The hypothesis that \((\kappa, n)\) is the only index of its pole is not in the paper: when different indices give the same pole, their residue contributions add up, a case the printed formula does not address.
