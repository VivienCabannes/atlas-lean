    DefinitionN-step Fibonacci numbers#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.nStepFibonacci

    For a natural number \(N\), the \(N\)-step Fibonacci numbers \(F^{(N)}_n\) (\(n \ge 0\)), in the convention of Noe and Post, are defined by \(F^{(N)}_0 = 0\), \(F^{(N)}_1 = 1\) and, for \(n \ge 2\), \(F^{(N)}_n = \sum_{i=1}^{N} F^{(N)}_{n-i}\), where a term whose index \(n - i\) is negative counts as \(0\). In particular \(F^{(0)}_n = 0\) for \(n \ge 2\).

    def nStepFibonacci (N : ℕ) : ℕ → ℕ
  | 0 => 0
  | 1 => 1
  | n + 2 => ∑ i : Fin N, nStepFibonacci N (n + 1 - i)

    MetaMathlibExt.binet_general

    Let \(r \ge 2\), let \(A_1, \dots, A_r\) and \(c_1, \dots, c_r\) be nonnegative real numbers with \(A_r > 0\) and \(c_j > 0\) for some \(j\), and suppose that the indices \(j\) with \(A_j > 0\) have greatest common divisor \(1\).
