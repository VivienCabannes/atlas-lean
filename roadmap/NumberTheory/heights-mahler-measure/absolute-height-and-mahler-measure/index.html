    NumberField.primitiveIntMinpoly

    Let \(K\) be a field that is an algebra over \(\mathbb{Q}\), let \(x \in K\), and let \(m_x \in \mathbb{Q}[X]\) be the minimal polynomial of \(x\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\), with \(m_x = 0\) when \(x\) is not algebraic over \(\mathbb{Q}\). Choose a nonzero integer \(b\) such that \(b\,m_x\) has integer coefficients. The primitive integral minimal polynomial \(P_x \in \mathbb{Z}[X]\) of \(x\) is the primitive part of \(b\,m_x\): when \(b\,m_x \neq 0\) it is \(b\,m_x\) divided by the nonnegative greatest common divisor of its coefficients, and when \(b\,m_x = 0\) it is \(1\).

    Thus for algebraic \(x\), \(P_x\) is a primitive polynomial with integer coefficients that is a nonzero rational multiple of \(m_x\), determined up to sign, and for nonalgebraic \(x\), \(P_x = 1\). The sign is immaterial for applications to Mahler measure.

    noncomputable def primitiveIntMinpoly (x : K) : ℤ[X] :=
  (IsLocalization.integerNormalization (nonZeroDivisors ℤ) (minpoly ℚ x)).primPart

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.Height.AbsoluteWanted.absLogHeight₁_eq_log_leadingCoeff_add_sum_log_roots_of_isIntegral

    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero and let \(x \in K\) be integral over \(\mathbb{Q}\). Let \(d\) be the degree of the minimal polynomial of \(x\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\), and let \(P_x \in \mathbb{Z}[X]\) be the primitive integral minimal polynomial of \(x\), with leading coefficient \(a\). Then the absolute logarithmic height of \(x\) is

    \[h(x) = \frac{1}{d}\Big(\log |a| + \sum_{z} \log^+ |z|\Big),\]

    where the sum runs over the complex roots \(z\) of \(P_x\), counted with multiplicity, and \(\log^+ t = \max(0, \log t)\).

    public theorem absLogHeight₁_eq_log_leadingCoeff_add_sum_log_roots_of_isIntegral
    {K : Type*} [Field K] [CharZero K] [Algebra ℚ K] (x : K) (hx : IsIntegral ℚ x) :
    NumberField.absLogHeight₁ x =
      (Real.log
          ‖((NumberField.primitiveIntMinpoly x).map (Int.castRingHom ℂ)).leadingCoeff‖ +
        (((NumberField.primitiveIntMinpoly x).map (Int.castRingHom ℂ)).roots.map
          (fun z => Real.posLog ‖z‖)).sum) /
        ((minpoly ℚ x).natDegree : ℝ)
    noncomputable def primitiveIntMinpoly (x : K) : ℤ[X] :=
  (IsLocalization.integerNormalization (nonZeroDivisors ℤ) (minpoly ℚ x)).primPart

    NumberField.positivePrimitiveIntMinpoly

    Let \(K\) be a field that is a \(\mathbb{Q}\)-algebra, let \(x \in K\), and let \(p\) be the canonical primitive integral minimal polynomial of \(x\). The positive primitive integral minimal polynomial of \(x\) is \(-p\) if the leading coefficient of \(p\) is negative, and \(p\) otherwise.

    Since \(p\) is nonzero, the result has positive leading coefficient. When \(x\) is not algebraic over \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(p\) is the constant polynomial \(1\), and so is the result.

    noncomputable def positivePrimitiveIntMinpoly (x : K) : Polynomial ℤ :=
  let p := primitiveIntMinpoly x
  if p.leadingCoeff < 0 then -p else p
