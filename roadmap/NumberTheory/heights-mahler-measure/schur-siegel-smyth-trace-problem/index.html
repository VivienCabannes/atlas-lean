    DefinitionAbsolute trace#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.SchurSiegelSmyth.absoluteTrace

    The absolute trace of a complex number \(\alpha\) is the real number obtained by dividing the real part of the trace of \(\alpha\) by the degree of \(\alpha\). If \(\alpha\) is transcendental, its trace and its degree are both \(0\), and its absolute trace is taken to be \(0\).

    For algebraic \(\alpha\) the trace is real, and for a totally positive algebraic integer \(\alpha\) the absolute trace is the mean of the conjugates of \(\alpha\).

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.SchurSiegelSmyth.schurSiegelSmythConstant

    The Schur–Siegel–Smyth trace constant \(C_{86}\) is the supremum, taken in the extended reals, of the admissible real bounds: the real numbers \(\rho \ge 0\) such that only finitely many complex numbers that are totally positive algebraic integers have absolute trace less than \(\rho\).

    DefinitionSum of the conjugates of a complex number#✓fully proved

    DefinitionThe Schur–Siegel–Smyth constant \(\lambda_{\mathrm{SSS}}\)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.NumberTheory.AlgebraicIntegersWanted.AlgebraicIntegers.lambda_SSS

    Let \(\lambda_{\mathrm{SSS}}\) be the supremum in \(\mathbb{R}\) of the set of real numbers \(\lambda\) such that, for every \(\varepsilon > 0\), only finitely many complex numbers \(\alpha\) are totally positive algebraic integers with \(\operatorname{tr}(\alpha) < (\lambda - \varepsilon) \deg(\alpha)\), where \(\operatorname{tr}(\alpha)\) and \(\deg(\alpha)\) are the trace and the degree of \(\alpha\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\) (the trace of a totally positive \(\alpha\) is real). By the convention for suprema in \(\mathbb{R}\), \(\lambda_{\mathrm{SSS}} = 0\) if this set is empty or unbounded above.

    The set is closed downwards and contains its supremum when it is nonempty and bounded above, so \(\lambda_{\mathrm{SSS}}\) is then the greatest such \(\lambda\). The source says "least real number", but this is a typo, as the set has no lower bound. The greatest real number agrees with Orloski, Sardari and Smith, "New lower bounds for the Schur-Siegel-Smyth trace problem" (arXiv:2401.03252), where \(\lambda_{\mathrm{SSS}}\) is the limit inferior of \(\operatorname{tr}(\alpha)/\deg(\alpha)\) over all totally positive algebraic integers \(\alpha\).

    noncomputable def lambda_SSS : ℝ := sSup { lam : ℝ | ∀ ε > (0 : ℝ),
  { α : ℂ | IsIntegral ℤ α ∧ IsTotallyPositive α ∧ trace α < (lam - ε) * degree α }.Finite }
