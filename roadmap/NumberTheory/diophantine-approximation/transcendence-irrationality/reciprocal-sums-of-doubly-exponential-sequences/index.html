    DefinitionPseudo-greedy remainder sequence#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.pseudoGreedyRemainder

    For a real number \(r\), the remainder sequence \((x_n)_{n \ge 0}\) of the pseudo-greedy expansion of \(r\) is defined by \(x_0 = r\) and \(x_{n+1} = x_n - 1/a_n\), where \(a_n = \lfloor x_n^{-1} + 1 + 1/2 \rfloor\) is the integer nearest to \(x_n^{-1} + 1\), with halves rounded up.

    Here \(0^{-1}\) and \(1/0\) are read as \(0\).

    noncomputable def pseudoGreedyRemainder (r : ℝ) : ℕ → ℝ
  | 0 => r
  | n + 1 => pseudoGreedyRemainder r n -
      1 / (⌊(pseudoGreedyRemainder r n)⁻¹ + 1 + 1 / 2⌋ : ℝ)
