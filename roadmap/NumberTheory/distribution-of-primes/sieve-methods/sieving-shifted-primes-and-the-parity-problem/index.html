    MetaMathlibExt.calzado_sum_inv_totient_fifth_to_half_tendsto

    As the real number \(x\) tends to infinity, the sum of \(1/\varphi(p)\) over the primes \(p\) with \(x^{1/5} < p \le x^{1/2}\) tends to \(\log(5/2)\), where \(\varphi\) is Euler's totient function.

    The paper prints the limit as \(0.916\ldots\); the exact value \(\log(5/2) = 0.91629\ldots\) is forced by Mertens' theorem.
