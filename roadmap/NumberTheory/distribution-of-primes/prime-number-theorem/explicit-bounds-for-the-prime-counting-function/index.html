    Let \(\pi(x)\) be the number of primes at most \(x\), and let \(\operatorname{Li}(x) = \int_2^x dt / \log t\). Then \(\pi(\pi(x)) \sim x / (\log x)^2\) as the real number \(x\) tends to infinity, and there is a constant \(A > 0\) such that, as \(x \to \infty\),

    \[ \pi(\pi(x)) - \operatorname{Li}(\operatorname{Li}(x)) = O\left(x \exp\left(-A (\log x)^{3/5} (\log \log x)^{-1/5}\right)\right). \]
