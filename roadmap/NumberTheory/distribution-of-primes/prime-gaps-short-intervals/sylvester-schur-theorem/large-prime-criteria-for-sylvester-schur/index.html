    Nat.SylvesterSchur.exists_large_prime_dvd_choose_of_factorial_mul_prod_erdos_layers_lt_lower_pow

    Let \(N\) and \(r\) be natural numbers with \(0 < N\) and \(r \le N\), write \(m\#\) for the product of the primes at most \(m\), and let \(L = \lfloor \log_2 N \rfloor\). Put \(P_1 = r\#\) and \(P_k = \lfloor N^{1/k} \rfloor\#\) for \(k \ge 2\). If

    \[r! \cdot \prod_{k=1}^{L} P_k < (N + 1 - r)^r,\]

    then \(\binom{N}{r}\) has a prime divisor \(p\) with \(p > r\).
