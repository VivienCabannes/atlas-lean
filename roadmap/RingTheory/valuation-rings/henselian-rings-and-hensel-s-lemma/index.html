    TheoremHensel lifting of monic coprime factorizations#✓fully proved

    Polynomial.exists_monic_coprime_factorization_of_isAdicComplete

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring and \(I\) an ideal of \(A\) such that \(A\) is \(I\)-adically complete, and let \(f \in A[X]\) be monic. Suppose the reduction of \(f\) modulo \(I\) equals \(\bar g \bar h\), where \(\bar g, \bar h \in (A/I)[X]\) are monic and coprime (that is, \(a \bar g + b \bar h = 1\) for some \(a, b \in (A/I)[X]\)). Then there exist monic \(g, h \in A[X]\) with \(f = gh\) whose reductions modulo \(I\) are \(\bar g\) and \(\bar h\) respectively.

    The proof packages the residue factorization via the universal coprime factorization ring, lifts its structure map through \(I\)-adic completeness, and evaluates the universal factors over \(A\).

    theorem exists_monic_coprime_factorization_of_isAdicComplete
    (I : Ideal A) [IsAdicComplete I A]
    (f : A[X]) (hf : f.Monic)
    (g_bar h_bar : (A ⧸ I)[X])
    (hg : g_bar.Monic) (hh : h_bar.Monic)
    (hfac : f.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) = g_bar * h_bar)
    (hcop : IsCoprime g_bar h_bar) :
    ∃ g h : A[X], g.Monic ∧ h.Monic ∧ f = g * h ∧
      g.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) = g_bar ∧
      h.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk I) = h_bar

    TheoremElements of the maximal ideal have positive valuation#✓fully proved

    IsDiscreteValuationRing.addVal_lt_addVal_add_of_mem_maximalIdeal

    Let \(A\) be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak{m}\) and normalized additive valuation \(v \colon A \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\). If \(d \in A\) is nonzero and \(q \in \mathfrak{m}\), then \(v(d) < v(d) + v(q)\).

    Since \(v(dq) = v(d) + v(q)\), multiplying a nonzero element by an element of the maximal ideal strictly increases its valuation.

    theorem addVal_lt_addVal_add_of_mem_maximalIdeal {d q : A}
    (hd : d ≠ 0) (hq : q ∈ maximalIdeal A) :
    addVal A d < addVal A d + addVal A q

    Polynomial.exists_isRoot_of_two_mul_addVal_derivative_lt_addVal

    Let \(A\) be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak{m}\) that is complete and Hausdorff for the \(\mathfrak{m}\)-adic topology, and let \(v \colon A \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\) be its normalized additive valuation. Let \(f \in A[X]\) have derivative \(f'\), and let \(a_0 \in A\) satisfy \(2 v(f'(a_0)) < v(f(a_0))\). Then \(f\) has a root \(a \in A\) such that \(a - a_0 \in \mathfrak{m}\), \(v(f'(a_0)) < v(a - a_0)\), \(v(f(a_0)) \le v(a - a_0) + 2 v(f'(a_0))\) and \(v(f'(a)) = v(f'(a_0))\). Moreover, every root \(a' \in A\) of \(f\) with \(v(f'(a_0)) < v(a' - a_0)\) equals \(a\).

    theorem Polynomial.exists_isRoot_of_two_mul_addVal_derivative_lt_addVal
    (f : A[X]) (a₀ : A)
    (hval : 2 * addVal A (f.derivative.eval a₀) < addVal A (f.eval a₀)) :
    ∃ a : A,
    f.IsRoot a ∧
    a - a₀ ∈ maximalIdeal A ∧
    addVal A (f.derivative.eval a₀) < addVal A (a - a₀) ∧
    addVal A (f.eval a₀) ≤
      addVal A (a - a₀) + 2 * addVal A (f.derivative.eval a₀) ∧
    (∀ a' : A, f.IsRoot a' →
      addVal A (f.derivative.eval a₀) < addVal A (a' - a₀) → a' = a) ∧
    addVal A (f.derivative.eval a) = addVal A (f.derivative.eval a₀)
