    DefinitionThe ring \(\mathrm{LIP}(U)\) of locally integer polynomial functions#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.lipSubring

    For \(U \subseteq \mathbb{Z}\), \(\mathrm{LIP}(U)\) is the subring of the ring of all functions \(U \to \mathbb{Z}\) (with pointwise operations) consisting of the functions \(f\) such that for every finite subset \(X \subseteq U\) there is a polynomial \(p \in \mathbb{Z}[x]\) with \(p(x) = f(x)\) for all \(x \in X\).
