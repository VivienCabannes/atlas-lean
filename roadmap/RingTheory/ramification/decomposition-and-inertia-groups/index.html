    Subgroup.le_of_card_subgroupOf_eq

    Let \(H\) and \(K\) be subgroups of a group \(G\) with \(H\) finite. If \(H \cap K\) has as many elements as \(H\), then \(H \le K\).

    theorem le_of_card_subgroupOf_eq {G : Type*} [Group G] {H K : Subgroup G}
    [Finite H]
    (h : Nat.card (H.subgroupOf K) = Nat.card H) :
    H ≤ K

    Ideal.card_inertia_tower_formula

    Let \(L/K\) be a finite Galois extension with Galois group \(G\). Let \(A\) and \(B\) be Dedekind domains with \(B\) a finite torsion-free \(A\)-algebra, and let \(G\) act on \(B\) by ring automorphisms as a Galois group of \(B\) over \(A\): faithfully, fixing the image of \(A\), and with every element of \(B\) fixed by \(G\) in the image of \(A\). Let \(E\) be an intermediate field of \(L/K\) and \(H \le G\) the subgroup fixing \(E\) pointwise. Let \(C\) be a Dedekind domain and a torsion-free \(A\)-algebra, and give \(E\) an \(A\)-algebra structure and an injective ring map \(C \to E\) whose image is the set of elements of \(E\) integral over \(A\) (the composite \(A \to C \to E\) need not be the given map \(A \to E\)). Suppose \(B\) is also a finite torsion-free \(C\)-algebra, with \(A \to C \to B\) equal to \(A \to B\), and that \(H\), acting on \(B\) by restriction, is a Galois group of \(B\) over \(C\). Let \(\mathfrak p\), \(\mathfrak P_E\) and \(\mathfrak P\) be maximal ideals of \(A\), \(C\) and \(B\) such that \(\mathfrak P\) lies over \(\mathfrak p\) and over \(\mathfrak P_E\) and \(\mathfrak P_E\) lies over \(\mathfrak p\), and suppose that every prime of \(B\) over \(\mathfrak p\), and every prime of \(B\) over \(\mathfrak P_E\), has a separable residue field extension. For a subgroup \(S \le G\) write \(I_S(\mathfrak P)\) for the inertia group of \(\mathfrak P\) in \(S\), the set of \(\sigma \in S\) with \(\sigma(x) - x \in \mathfrak P\) for all \(x \in B\). Then \(e(\mathfrak P_E \mid \mathfrak p) \cdot |I_H(\mathfrak P)| = |I_G(\mathfrak P)|\), where \(e(\mathfrak P_E \mid \mathfrak p)\) is the ramification index of \(\mathfrak P_E\) over \(\mathfrak p\).
