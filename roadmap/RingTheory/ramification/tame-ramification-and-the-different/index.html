    IsDiscreteValuationRing.MaximalIdealGradedPiece

    Let \(R\) be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak{m}\). For \(i \in \mathbb{N}\), the \(i\)-th graded piece is the \(R\)-module \(\mathfrak{m}^i/\mathfrak{m}^{i+1}\), realized as the quotient of the ideal \(\mathfrak{m}^i\), viewed as an \(R\)-module, by its submodule \(\mathfrak{m} \cdot \mathfrak{m}^i\).

    residue_intTrace_eq_nsmul_trace_residue_of_map_maximalIdeal_eq_pow

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be discrete valuation rings with maximal ideals \(\mathfrak m_A\), \(\mathfrak m_B\) and residue fields \(\kappa_A\), \(\kappa_B\), and let \(B\) be an \(A\)-algebra that is finitely generated and torsion-free as an \(A\)-module. Let \(e\) be a natural number such that the ideal \(\mathfrak m_A B\) generated by the image of \(\mathfrak m_A\) equals \(\mathfrak m_B^e\). Then for every \(b \in B\), writing \(\bar b\) for the residue of \(b\) in \(\kappa_B\), the residue in \(\kappa_A\) of the integral trace \(\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(b)\) (the trace of the extension of fraction fields, restricted to \(B\)) equals \(e \cdot \operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa_B/\kappa_A}(\bar b)\).

    This determines the integral trace only modulo \(\mathfrak m_A\).

    intTrace_surjective_of_isUnit_natCast_of_map_maximalIdeal_eq_pow

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be discrete valuation rings with maximal ideals \(\mathfrak m_A\), \(\mathfrak m_B\) and residue fields \(\kappa_A\), \(\kappa_B\), and let \(B\) be an \(A\)-algebra that is finitely generated and torsion-free as an \(A\)-module, with \(\kappa_B / \kappa_A\) separable. Let \(e\) be a natural number that is a unit in \(B\) and such that the ideal \(\mathfrak m_A B\) generated by the image of \(\mathfrak m_A\) equals \(\mathfrak m_B^e\). Then the integral trace \(\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A} \colon B \to A\) (the trace of the extension of fraction fields, restricted to \(B\)) is surjective.

    Since \(A \to B\) is local, \(e\) is a unit in \(B\) if and only if it is a unit in \(A\).

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be discrete valuation rings with maximal ideals \(\mathfrak{m}_A\) and \(\mathfrak{m}_B\), and let \(B\) be an \(A\)-algebra that is finite and torsion-free as an \(A\)-module, such that the extension of fraction fields and the extension of residue fields are both separable.
