    Ideal.le_differentIdeal_iff_mul_dual_le_one

    Let \(A\) be an integrally closed domain with fraction field \(K\), let \(L/K\) be a finite separable extension, and let \(B\) be the integral closure of \(A\) in \(L\); assume that \(B\) is a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(L\) and is torsion-free as an \(A\)-module. Write \(B^\vee = \{x \in L : \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xB) \subseteq A\}\) for the trace dual of \(B\) and \(\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}\) for the different ideal of \(B\) over \(A\). Then an ideal \(I\) of \(B\) satisfies \(I \subseteq \mathfrak{D}_{B/A}\) if and only if \(I B^\vee \subseteq B\), where the product is taken in the fractional ideals of \(B\) in \(L\).

    theorem le_differentIdeal_iff_mul_dual_le_one (I : Ideal B) :
    I ≤ differentIdeal A B ↔
      (I : FractionalIdeal B⁰ L) * FractionalIdeal.dual A K
          (1 : FractionalIdeal B⁰ L) ≤ 1

    TheoremThe trace dual commutes with localization#✓fully proved

    FractionalIdeal.extendedHom_dual_one_eq_dual_one_ofField

    Let \(A\) be a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(K\), let \(L/K\) be a finite separable extension, and let \(B\) be the integral closure of \(A\) in \(L\), a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(L\). Let \(S \subseteq A\) be a multiplicative set of nonzerodivisors, let \(A_S \subseteq K\) be the localization of \(A\) at \(S\) inside \(K\), and let \(B_S\) be a localization of \(B\) at the image of \(S\) that is also an \(A_S\)-algebra, with a map \(B_S \to L\) such that all composite maps from \(A\), \(A_S\), \(B\), \(B_S\) and \(K\) into \(L\) agree. Assume that \(B_S\) is a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(L\), torsion-free over \(B\), and the integral closure of \(A_S\) in \(L\). Write \(B^\vee = \{x \in L : \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xB) \subseteq A\}\) and \(B_S^\vee = \{x \in L : \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xB_S) \subseteq A_S\}\) for the trace duals. Then the fractional ideal of \(B_S\) generated by \(B^\vee\) is \(B_S^\vee\).

    theorem extendedHom_dual_one_eq_dual_one_ofField :
    extendedHom L SB (dual A K (1 : FractionalIdeal (nonZeroDivisors B) L)) =
      dual Aₛ K (1 : FractionalIdeal (nonZeroDivisors SB) L)

    DefinitionDiscriminant ideal of a Dedekind extension#✓fully proved

    Ideal.discriminantIdeal

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be Dedekind domains, with \(B\) an \(A\)-algebra that is finite and torsion-free as an \(A\)-module. The discriminant ideal \(\mathfrak{d}_{B/A}\) is the ideal of \(A\) generated by the norms \(N_{B/A}(x)\) of the elements \(x\) of the different ideal \(\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}\) of \(B\) over \(A\).

    noncomputable def discriminantIdeal (A B : Type*) [CommRing A]
    [IsDedekindDomain A] [CommRing B] [IsDedekindDomain B] [Algebra A B]
    [Module.Finite A B] [Module.IsTorsionFree A B] : Ideal A :=
  Ideal.spanNorm A (differentIdeal A B)

    TheoremTower formula for discriminant ideals#✓fully proved

    Ideal.discriminantIdeal_tower

    Let \(A\), \(B\), \(C\) be Dedekind domains with algebra maps \(A \to B \to C\) whose composite is the algebra map \(A \to C\), such that \(B\) is finite and torsion-free over \(A\), \(C\) is finite and torsion-free over \(A\) and over \(B\), and the fraction field of \(C\) is separable over the fraction field of \(A\). Write \(\mathfrak{d}_{B/A}\), \(\mathfrak{d}_{C/A}\) and \(\mathfrak{d}_{C/B}\) for the discriminant ideals of \(B\) over \(A\), of \(C\) over \(A\) and of \(C\) over \(B\), let \(n = [\operatorname{Frac} C : \operatorname{Frac} B]\), and for an ideal \(I\) of \(B\) let \(N_{B/A}(I)\) be the ideal of \(A\) generated by the norms of the elements of \(I\). Then \(\mathfrak{d}_{C/A} = \mathfrak{d}_{B/A}^{\,n} \, N_{B/A}(\mathfrak{d}_{C/B})\).

    theorem discriminantIdeal_tower (A B C : Type*) [CommRing A]
    [IsDedekindDomain A] [CommRing B] [IsDedekindDomain B] [CommRing C]
    [IsDedekindDomain C] [Algebra A B] [Algebra B C] [Algebra A C]
    [IsScalarTower A B C] [Module.Finite A B] [Module.Finite A C]
    [Module.Finite B C] [Module.IsTorsionFree A B]
    [Module.IsTorsionFree A C] [Module.IsTorsionFree B C]
    [Algebra.IsSeparable (FractionRing A) (FractionRing C)] :
    discriminantIdeal A C =
      (discriminantIdeal A B) ^
          Module.finrank (FractionRing B) (FractionRing C) *
        Ideal.spanNorm A (discriminantIdeal B C)
    noncomputable def discriminantIdeal (A B : Type*) [CommRing A]
    [IsDedekindDomain A] [CommRing B] [IsDedekindDomain B] [Algebra A B]
    [Module.Finite A B] [Module.IsTorsionFree A B] : Ideal A :=
  Ideal.spanNorm A (differentIdeal A B)

    DefinitionElement different#✓fully proved

    elementDifferent

    Let \(A\) be a commutative ring, \(B\) a commutative \(A\)-algebra, \(L\) a field that is a \(B\)-algebra, and \(K\) a field with \(L\) a \(K\)-algebra. For \(\alpha \in B\), let \(f \in A[X]\) be the minimal polynomial of \(\alpha\) over \(A\) (taken to be \(0\) when \(\alpha\) is not integral over \(A\)). The element different \(\delta_{B/A}(\alpha) \in B\) is \(f'(\alpha)\) when the image of \(\alpha\) in \(L\) generates \(L\) as a \(K\)-algebra, and \(0\) otherwise.

    noncomputable def elementDifferent (α : B) : B :=
  if Algebra.adjoin K {algebraMap B L α} = ⊤ then
    Polynomial.aeval α (Polynomial.derivative (minpoly A α))
  else 0
