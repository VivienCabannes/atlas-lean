    TheoremBounds on the ramification index#✓fully proved

    Ideal.ramificationIdx_mem_Icc

    Let \(R\) and \(S\) be Dedekind domains, with \(S\) an \(R\)-algebra that is finitely generated and torsion-free as an \(R\)-module, and let \(n\) be the rank of \(S\) as an \(R\)-module. If \(\mathfrak{p}\) is a maximal ideal of \(R\) and \(\mathfrak{q}\) is a prime ideal of \(S\) lying over \(\mathfrak{p}\), then the ramification index \(e_{\mathfrak{q}}\) of \(\mathfrak{q}\) over \(\mathfrak{p}\) satisfies \(1 \le e_{\mathfrak{q}} \le n\).

    theorem ramificationIdx_mem_Icc (p : Ideal R) [p.IsMaximal]
    (q : Ideal S) [q.IsPrime] [q.LiesOver p] :
    q.ramificationIdx R ∈ Set.Icc 1 (Module.finrank R S)

    Let \(R\) be a commutative local ring and let \(p\) be the characteristic of its residue field. For every natural number \(n\), the image of \(n\) in \(R\) is a unit if and only if \(p\) does not divide \(n\).
