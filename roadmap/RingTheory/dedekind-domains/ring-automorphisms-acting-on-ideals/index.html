Sending \(\sigma\) to the map \(I \mapsto \tilde\sigma(I)\) on fractional ideals of \(R\) in \(K\) defines a group homomorphism from the group of ring automorphisms of \(R\) to the group of ring automorphisms of the semiring of fractional ideals of \(R\) in \(K\).

    FractionalIdeal.unitsMulDistribMulAction

    Let a group \(G\) act on a commutative domain \(R\) by ring automorphisms, let \(K\) be a field of fractions of \(R\), and let \(G\) act on fractional ideals of \(R\) in \(K\) by \(g \cdot I = \tilde\sigma_g(I)\), where \(\tilde\sigma_g\) is the automorphism of \(K\) extending \(r \mapsto g \cdot r\). This induces an action of \(G\) on the group of invertible fractional ideals: \(g\) sends an invertible fractional ideal \(I\) to \(g \cdot I\), whose inverse is \(g \cdot I^{-1}\). This action respects products and fixes the unit fractional ideal \(R\).
