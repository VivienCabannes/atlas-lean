    DefinitionPrime factorization isomorphism for fractional ideals#✓fully proved

    FractionalIdeal.factorizationMulEquiv

    Let \(R\) be a Dedekind domain with fraction field \(K\), and for a unit fractional ideal \(I\) of \(R\) in \(K\) and a height-one prime \(v\) of \(R\) write \(\operatorname{val}_v(I)\) for the \(v\)-adic valuation of \(I\). The factorization isomorphism is the group isomorphism from the unit group of the fractional ideals of \(R\) in \(K\) to the additive group of finitely supported functions from the height-one primes of \(R\) to \(\mathbb{Z}\) (written multiplicatively) that sends \(I\) to the function \(v \mapsto \operatorname{val}_v(I)\). Its inverse sends \(e\) to \(\prod_v v^{e(v)}\), the product over the primes \(v\) with \(e(v) \neq 0\).

    TheoremIdeal multiplicity is unchanged by localization at a maximal ideal#✓fully proved

    IsLocalization.AtPrime.emultiplicity_maximalIdeal_map_eq

    Let \(R\) be a Dedekind domain, \(\mathfrak p\) a maximal ideal of \(R\), and \(R_{\mathfrak p}\) a local ring that is a localization of \(R\) at \(\mathfrak p\), with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak m\). Then for every ideal \(I\) of \(R\), the multiplicity of \(\mathfrak m\) in the extended ideal \(I R_{\mathfrak p}\) equals the multiplicity of \(\mathfrak p\) in \(I\).
