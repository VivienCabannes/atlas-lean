    Subalgebra.conductorIdeal

    Let \(R\) be a commutative semiring, \(S\) a commutative \(R\)-algebra and \(A\) an \(R\)-subalgebra of \(S\). The conductor ideal of \(A\) is the ideal of \(S\) consisting of all \(s \in S\) such that \(st \in A\) for every \(t \in S\); it is the largest ideal of \(S\) contained in \(A\).

    def conductorIdeal (A : Subalgebra R S) : Ideal S where

  smul_mem' c a ha t := by
    simpa only [smul_eq_mul, mul_left_comm, mul_assoc] using ha (c * t)

    Ideal.quotient_coprime_residue_equiv

    Let \(B\) be an algebra over \(O\), where \(O\) and \(B\) are commutative rings, with structure map \(\varphi : O \to B\). Let \(\mathfrak q\) be a maximal ideal of \(B\) and let \(\mathfrak c\) be an ideal of \(B\) with \(\mathfrak c \not\subseteq \mathfrak q\) such that for every \(x \in \mathfrak c\) and every \(b \in B\) there is \(o \in O\) with \(\varphi(o) = xb\).
