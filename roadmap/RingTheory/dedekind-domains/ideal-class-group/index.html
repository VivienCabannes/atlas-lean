    Ideal.exists_isCoprime_mul_isPrincipal

    Let \(A\) be a Dedekind domain and let \(I\) and \(\mathfrak{a}\) be nonzero ideals of \(A\). Then there is an ideal \(J\) of \(A\) with \(J + \mathfrak{a} = A\) such that the product \(IJ\) is a principal ideal.

    theorem Ideal.exists_isCoprime_mul_isPrincipal (I a : Ideal A) (hI : I ≠ ⊥)
    (ha : a ≠ ⊥) :
    ∃ J : Ideal A, IsCoprime J a ∧ Submodule.IsPrincipal (I * J)

    ClassGroup.exists_mk0_eq_and_isCoprime

    Let \(A\) be a Dedekind domain and let \(\mathfrak{a}\) be a nonzero ideal of \(A\). Then every class \(c\) in the ideal class group of \(A\) is the class of some nonzero ideal \(K\) of \(A\) with \(K + \mathfrak{a} = A\).

    theorem ClassGroup.exists_mk0_eq_and_isCoprime (c : ClassGroup A) (a : Ideal A)
    (ha : a ≠ ⊥) :
    ∃ (K : Ideal A) (hK : K ≠ ⊥),
      ClassGroup.mk0 ⟨K, mem_nonZeroDivisors_iff_ne_zero.mpr hK⟩ = c ∧
        IsCoprime K a

    IdealFactorization.IdealsSimilar

    Let \(R\) be a Dedekind domain. Two nonzero ideals \(I\) and \(J\) of \(R\) are similar when their classes in the ideal class group of \(R\) are equal.

    abbrev IdealsSimilar (I J : (Ideal R)⁰) : Prop := ClassGroup.mk0 I = ClassGroup.mk0 J
