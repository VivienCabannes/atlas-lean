    Submodule.mul_dualSubmoduleToDual_surjective

    Let \(A\) be an integral domain with fraction field \(K\), identify \(A\) with its image in \(K\), and let \(M\) be a nonzero \(A\)-submodule of \(K\).     Submodule.dualEquivMulDualSubmodule

    Let \(A\) be an integral domain with fraction field \(K\), identify \(A\) with its image in \(K\), and let \(M\) be a nonzero \(A\)-submodule of \(K\). Let \(M^\vee = \{x \in K : xy \in A \text{ for all } y \in M\}\) be the dual submodule of \(M\) for the multiplication form \((x, y) \mapsto xy\) on \(K\). This is the \(A\)-linear isomorphism \(\operatorname{Hom}_A(M, A) \to M^\vee\) inverse to the map sending \(x \in M^\vee\) to the functional \(y \mapsto xy\) on \(M\); it sends \(f\) to the unique \(x \in M^\vee\) with \(f(y) = xy\) for all \(y \in M\).

    FractionalIdeal.coe_inv_eq_mul_dualSubmodule

    Let \(A\) be an integral domain with fraction field \(K\), and let \(I\) be a nonzero fractional ideal of \(A\) in \(K\). Then \(I^{-1}\), viewed as an \(A\)-submodule of \(K\), equals the dual submodule of \(I\) with respect to the multiplication form \((x, y) \mapsto xy\) on \(K\), namely the set \(\{x \in K : xy \in A \text{ for all } y \in I\}\), where \(A\) is identified with its image in \(K\).

    theorem FractionalIdeal.coe_inv_eq_mul_dualSubmodule

    ((I⁻¹ : FractionalIdeal A⁰ K) : Submodule A K) =
      LinearMap.BilinForm.dualSubmodule
        (LinearMap.mul K K : LinearMap.BilinForm K K)
        (I : Submodule A K)

    Submodule.localized'_div_of_fg

    Let \(R\), \(S\) and \(K\) be commutative rings with algebra maps \(R \to S \to K\) forming a scalar tower, let \(p\) be a submonoid of \(R\) such that \(S\) is the localization of \(R\) at \(p\), and suppose the identity map of \(K\) is a localization of the \(R\)-module \(K\) at \(p\). For an \(R\)-submodule \(M\) of \(K\) write \(M_p = \{x/s : x \in M,\ s \in p\}\) for its localization, an \(S\)-submodule of \(K\), where \(x/s\) is the element \(y \in K\) with \(s y = x\). For submodules write \(M/N = \{x \in K : xN \subseteq M\}\). Then for \(R\)-submodules \(I\) and \(J\) of \(K\) with \(J\) finitely generated, \((I/J)_p = I_p/J_p\).

    lemma localized'_div_of_fg (I J : Submodule R K) (hJ : J.FG) :
    (I / J).localized' S p (.id : K →ₗ[R] K) =
      I.localized' S p (.id : K →ₗ[R] K) /
        J.localized' S p (.id : K →ₗ[R] K)

    FractionalIdeal.coe_extendedHom_eq_localized'

    Let \(A\) and \(B\) be integral domains with \(B\) a torsion-free \(A\)-algebra, and let \(K\) be a field that is a fraction field of both \(A\) and \(B\), compatibly with \(A \to B \to K\).
