    DefinitionBinomial (Hurwitz) product of power series#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.binomialProduct

    Let \(K\) be a commutative ring. The binomial product of formal power series \(A = \sum_n a_n X^n\) and \(B = \sum_n b_n X^n\) in \(K[[X]]\) is the power series \(\sum_n c_n X^n\) with \(c_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \binom{n}{k} a_k b_{n-k}\).

    public noncomputable def binomialProduct {K : Type*} [CommRing K] (A B : PowerSeries K) :
    PowerSeries K :=
  PowerSeries.mk fun n => ∑ k ∈ Finset.range (n + 1),
    (n.choose k : K) * PowerSeries.coeff k A * PowerSeries.coeff (n - k) B

    MetaMathlibExt.prod_one_sub_add_eq_resultant

    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic \(0\), let \(m, n \ge 0\) be integers, and let \(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_m\) and \(\beta_1, \dots, \beta_n\) be elements of \(K\).     \[ \prod_{i=1}^{m} \prod_{j=1}^{n} \bigl(1 - (\alpha_i + \beta_j)x\bigr) = (-1)^{mn} \operatorname{Res}_{m,n}(f, g). \]

    With \(U(x) = \prod_{i=1}^{m} (1 - \alpha_i x)\) and \(V(x) = \prod_{j=1}^{n} (1 - \beta_j x)\), these are \(f(y) = (1 - y)^m U(x / (1 - y))\) and \(g(y) = y^n V(x / y)\), the form used in the source.

    public theorem_wanted prod_one_sub_add_eq_resultant {K : Type*} [Field K] [CharZero K] (m n : ℕ)

      (-1) ^ (m * n) * Polynomial.resultant
        (∏ i, (1 - Polynomial.X - Polynomial.C (Polynomial.C (α i) * Polynomial.X)))

    TheoremHadamard products of rational series#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.hadamardProduct_rational

    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic \(0\), let \(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_m, \beta_1, \dots, \beta_n \in K\), and let \(A = \sum_k a_k x^k\) and \(B = \sum_k b_k x^k\) be power series over \(K\) such that \(A(x) \prod_{i=1}^m (1 - \alpha_i x)\) and \(B(x) \prod_{j=1}^n (1 - \beta_j x)\) are polynomials. Then their Hadamard product \(A * B = \sum_k a_k b_k x^k\) has the property that \((A * B)(x) \prod_{i=1}^m \prod_{j=1}^n (1 - \alpha_i \beta_j x)\) is a polynomial. Moreover, call a power series \(f\) over \(K\) proper if \(f Q = P\) for some polynomials \(P\), \(Q\) over \(K\) with \(Q(0) \ne 0\) and \(\deg P < \deg Q\). If \(A\) and \(B\) are proper, then so is \(A * B\).

    Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic \(0\), let \(m, n \ge 0\) be integers, and let \(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_m\) and \(\beta_1, \dots, \beta_n\) be elements of \(K\). Regard \(f(y) = \prod_{i=1}^{m} (1 - \alpha_i y)\) and \(g(y) = \prod_{j=1}^{n} (y - \beta_j x)\) as polynomials in \(y\) with coefficients in \(K[x]\), and let \(\operatorname{Res}_{m,n}(f, g)\) be the determinant of their Sylvester matrix formed with the formal degrees \(m\) and \(n\). Then in \(K[x]\)

    \[ \prod_{i=1}^{m} \prod_{j=1}^{n} (1 - \alpha_i \beta_j x) = (-1)^{mn} \operatorname{Res}_{m,n}(f, g). \]

    With \(U(x) = \prod_{i=1}^{m} (1 - \alpha_i x)\) and \(V(x) = \prod_{j=1}^{n} (1 - \beta_j x)\), these are \(f(y) = U(y)\) and \(g(y) = y^n V(x / y)\), the form used in the source.
