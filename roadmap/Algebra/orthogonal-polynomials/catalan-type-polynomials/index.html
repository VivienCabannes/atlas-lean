    DefinitionNegative real power of a trinomial as a power series#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.trinomialNegPowSeries

    For real numbers \(\lambda\), \(c\), \(\alpha\), \(\beta\) and a natural number \(m\), this is the formal power series in \(t\) with real coefficients

    \[c^{-\lambda} \sum_{n \ge 0} \binom{-\lambda}{n} u^n, \qquad u = \frac{-\alpha t + \beta t^m}{c},\]

    that is, the real power \(c^{-\lambda}\) times the binomial series \((1 + t)^{-\lambda} = \sum_{n \ge 0} \binom{-\lambda}{n} t^n\) with \(u\) substituted for \(t\) (for \(c = 0\) the division is read as \(0\), so \(u = 0\)). The substitution is meaningful when \(u\) has zero constant term, which holds for \(m \ge 1\); for \(c > 0\) and \(m \ge 1\) the series is the expansion of \((c - \alpha t + \beta t^m)^{-\lambda}\) in powers of \(t\).

    For \(c > 0\) and \(m \ge 1\) this is the denominator factor of every generating function in Pathan and Usman's paper; Section 2 takes \(c = 1\), \(\alpha = m\) and \(\beta = x\).

    noncomputable def trinomialNegPowSeries (lam c α β : ℝ) (m : ℕ) : PowerSeries ℝ :=
  C (c ^ (-lam)) *
    (binomialSeries ℝ (-lam)).subst (C (-α / c) * X + C (β / c) * X ^ m : PowerSeries ℝ)

    DefinitionKampé de Fériet polynomials#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.kampeDeFerietHermite

    For natural numbers \(m, n\) and elements \(x, y\) of a commutative \(\mathbb{R}\)-algebra, the Kampé de Fériet (two-variable Hermite) polynomial is

    \[H^m_n(x, y) = \sum_{r=0}^{q} \frac{n!}{r!\,(n - m r)!}\, y^r x^{n - m r},\]

    where \(q = \lfloor n / m \rfloor\), read as \(q = 0\) when \(m = 0\). The paper writes \(H_n(x, y)\) for \(H^2_n(x, y)\); taking values in an algebra lets \(H_k(x + 2yt, y)\) be formed as a formal power series in \(t\).

    noncomputable def kampeDeFerietHermite {A : Type*} [CommRing A] [Algebra ℝ A] (m n : ℕ)
    (x y : A) : A :=
  ∑ r ∈ Finset.range (n / m + 1),
    ((n.factorial : ℝ) / ((r.factorial : ℝ) * ((n - m * r).factorial : ℝ))) •
      (y ^ r * x ^ (n - m * r))

    MetaMathlibExt.coeff_nietoHermiteSeries_mul_trinomialNegPowSeries

    Let \(j, k, m, r\) be natural numbers with \(k \le r\) and let \(\lambda, x, y\) be real numbers.
