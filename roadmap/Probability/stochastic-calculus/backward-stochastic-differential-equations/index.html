    Backward stochastic differential equations

    A backward stochastic differential equation Y_t = ξ + ∫_t^T g(s, Y_s, Z_s) ds - ∫_t^T Z_s dB_s prescribes the terminal value ξ and asks for a pair (Y, Z) of adapted processes; the martingale representation theorem supplies Z. Pardoux and Peng (1990) proved existence and uniqueness for Lipschitz generators by a fixed point. In dimension one the comparison theorem plays the role of a maximum principle, and it makes ξ ↦ Y_0 a monotone, time-consistent nonlinear expectation, Peng's g-expectation, when g(·, y, 0) ≡ 0. For Markovian data Y_t = u(t, X_t), where u solves a quasilinear parabolic PDE: the nonlinear Feynman–Kac formula.

    DefinitionSolution of a one-dimensional BSDE on [0, T]#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.BSDE.IsBSDESolution

    Solution of a one-dimensional BSDE on [0, T] (S. Peng, Proc. ICM 2010, Vol. I, §1.2, equation (1.7) and Theorem 1.1, with m = d = 1). The pair (Y, Z) solves the BSDE with generator g and terminal value ξ if Y and Z are F-progressive with E ∫_0^T Y_t² dt < ∞ and E ∫_0^T Z_t² dt < ∞ (the space M²(0, T)), and for every t ≤ T, almost surely, Y_t = ξ + ∫_t^T g(s, Y_s, Z_s) ds - (∫_0^T Z dB - ∫_0^t Z dB), where ∫ Z dB is the repository's L² Itô integral ProbabilityTheory.itoIntegral.

    def IsBSDESolution (P : Measure Ω) (F : Filtration ℝ≥0 mΩ) (B : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ) (T : ℝ≥0)
    (g : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ → ℝ → ℝ) (ξ : Ω → ℝ) (Y Z : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ) : Prop :=
  IsProgressive F Y ∧ IsProgressive F Z ∧
    ∫⁻ ω, (∫⁻ u in Set.Ioc (0 : ℝ) T, ‖Y u.toNNReal ω‖ₑ ^ 2) ∂P ≠ ∞ ∧
    ∫⁻ ω, (∫⁻ u in Set.Ioc (0 : ℝ) T, ‖Z u.toNNReal ω‖ₑ ^ 2) ∂P ≠ ∞ ∧
    ∀ t ≤ T, ∀ᵐ ω ∂P, Y t ω = ξ ω +
      (∫ u in (t : ℝ)..T, g u.toNNReal ω (Y u.toNNReal ω) (Z u.toNNReal ω)) -
        (itoIntegral P F B Z T ω - itoIntegral P F B Z t ω)
    noncomputable def itoIntegral (P : Measure Ω) (F : Filtration ℝ≥0 mΩ) (B H : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ)

    MathlibExt.Probability.BSDEWanted.bsde_existsUnique

    Existence and uniqueness for BSDEs with Lipschitz generators (Pardoux–Peng 1990). Let B be a real Brownian motion with continuous paths and F its augmented filtration. Let the generator g(t, ω, y, z) be progressive in (t, ω) with E ∫_0^T g(t, y, z)² dt < ∞ for each (y, z), and Lipschitz in (y, z): |g(t, y, z) - g(t, y', z')| ≤ μ (|y - y'| + |z - z'|). Then for every F_T-measurable square-integrable ξ the BSDE Y_t = ξ + ∫_t^T g(s, Y_s, Z_s) ds - ∫_t^T Z_s dB_s has a solution (Y, Z) in M²(0, T) whose Y has almost surely continuous paths on [0, T], and two solutions agree in M²(0, T). This is the case d = m = 1 of S. Peng, Proc. ICM 2010, Vol. I, Theorem 1.1 (E. Pardoux and S. Peng, Systems Control Lett. 14 (1990)).

    Background

    MathlibExt.Probability.BSDEWanted.bsde_feynmanKac_of_classical

    Nonlinear Feynman–Kac formula, classical case (Peng 1991; Pardoux–Peng 1992). Let b, σ, φ : ℝ → ℝ be Lipschitz, f(x, y, z) Lipschitz on ℝ³, and let X solve the SDE X_t = x + ∫_0^t σ(X_s) dB_s + ∫_0^t b(X_s) ds (in the solution class of MathlibExt.Probability.LipschitzSDEWanted.sde_lipschitz_existsUnique). Suppose that u(t, x) is C² with bounded ∂ₓu and linear growth, and solves the quasilinear parabolic PDE ∂ₜu + b ∂ₓu + ½ σ² ∂ₓ²u + f(x, u, σ ∂ₓu) = 0 on [0, T] × ℝ with u(T, ·) = φ. Then every solution (Y, Z) of the Markovian BSDE with generator f(X_t, y, z) and terminal value φ(X_T) satisfies Y_t = u(t, X_t) almost surely for each t ∈ [0, T]; in particular Y_0 = u(0, x). S. Peng, Proc. ICM 2010, Vol. I, Theorem 1.3 (stated there for the viscosity solution) and the paragraph after it, which records Peng's (1991) proof when the PDE (1.11) has a classical solution.
