    Let \(J\) be a finite set with \(n\) elements, let \(\mu, x \in \mathbb{R}^J\), let \(S\) be a positive definite real \(J \times J\) matrix, and write \(\mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu, S)\) for the multivariate Gaussian density at \(x\) with mean \(\mu\) and covariance \(S\). Then \(\log \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu, S) = -\frac{n}{2} \log (2\pi) - \frac{1}{2} \log \det S - \frac{1}{2} (x - \mu)^{\mathsf T} S^{-1} (x - \mu)\).

      log S.det / 2 - ofLp (x - μ) ⬝ᵥ S⁻¹ *ᵥ ofLp (x - μ) / 2
