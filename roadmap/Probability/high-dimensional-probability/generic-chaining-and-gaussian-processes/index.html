    Generic chaining and Gaussian processes

    How large is \(\mathbb{E}\sup_{t \in T} X_t\) for a centred process whose increments are sub-Gaussian for a distance \(d\) on \(T\)? Talagrand's generic chaining answers with the geometry of \((T, d)\) alone. Approximate each \(t\) by points \(\pi_n(t)\) taken from sets of at most \(N_n = 2^{2^n}\) points, write \(X_t - X_{t_0}\) as the telescoping sum of the increments \(X_{\pi_n(t)} - X_{\pi_{n-1}(t)}\), and control all increments at level \(n\) at once by a union bound over at most \(N_{n+1}\) pairs. The outcome is \(\mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t \le L\,\gamma_2(T, d)\), where \(\gamma_2\) is the infimum, over admissible sequences of partitions, of \(\sup_t \sum_{n \ge 0} 2^{n/2} \Delta(A_n(t))\). For Gaussian processes with their canonical distance the bound is sharp: the majorizing measure theorem of Fernique and Talagrand gives \(\gamma_2(T, d)/L \le \mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t\), and its proof starts from Sudakov's minoration.

    The index set is finite throughout, as in the source: for an infinite \(T\) the supremum is defined through finite subsets. The generic chaining bound is proved with the explicit constant \(L = 10\), and Dudley's entropy bound \(\mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t \le 17\sum_n 2^{n/2} e_n(T)\), the classical form in which the sets of the chain are optimal coverings, with \(L = 17\). For a centred Gaussian process whose canonical distance is the metric, the increments satisfy the sub-Gaussian condition, so the upper half of the majorizing measure theorem is proved; its lower half and Sudakov's minoration are stated, not yet proved.

    Source: M. Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes: Decomposition Theorems, 2nd ed., Springer 2021, Chapter 2 (theorem numbers on these pages refer to this edition).

    StructureAdmissible sequence of partitions#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.AdmissibleSequence

    An admissible sequence is an increasing sequence \((\mathcal{A}_n)_{n \ge 0}\) of partitions of \(T\) with \(\operatorname{card}\mathcal{A}_n \le N_n\), that is \(\operatorname{card}\mathcal{A}_0 = 1\) and \(\operatorname{card}\mathcal{A}_n \le 2^{2^n}\) for \(n \ge 1\); increasing means that every set of \(\mathcal{A}_{n+1}\) is contained in a set of \(\mathcal{A}_n\) (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, Definition 2.7.1). The partition \(\mathcal{A}_n\) is recorded by its cell map \(t \mapsto A_n(t)\), the unique element of \(\mathcal{A}_n\) containing \(t\): each point lies in its cell, two points of a cell have the same cell, the cell of \(t\) at level \(n + 1\) is contained in its cell at level \(n\), and the cells at level \(n\) number at most \(N_n\).

    The double exponential makes \(\log \operatorname{card}\mathcal{A}_n\) double at each step, so a partition can be refined by splitting each cell into at most \(N_n\) pieces (\(N_n^2 \le N_{n+1}\)).

    structure AdmissibleSequence (T : Type*) where
  /-- `cell n t` is `A_n(t)`, the cell of the `n`-th partition containing `t`. -/
  cell : ℕ → T → Set T
  mem_cell : ∀ n t, t ∈ cell n t
  cell_eq_of_mem : ∀ n s t, s ∈ cell n t → cell n s = cell n t
  cell_succ_subset : ∀ n t, cell (n + 1) t ⊆ cell n t
  encard_range_le : ∀ n, (Set.range (cell n)).encard ≤ chainingCard n

    DefinitionSub-Gaussian increment condition#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.SatisfiesIncrementCondition

    A process \((X_t)_{t \in T}\) on a metric space \((T, d)\) satisfies the increment condition when $\(\mathbb{P}(|X_s - X_t| \ge u) \le 2\exp\Big(-\frac{u^2}{2 d(s, t)^2}\Big)\)$ for all \(s, t \in T\) and \(u > 0\) (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, (2.4)). A centred Gaussian process satisfies it for its canonical distance \(d(s, t) = (\mathbb{E}(X_s - X_t)^2)^{1/2}\).

    Because \(d\) is a metric, \(d(s, t) = 0\) only when \(s = t\), where the condition is empty; the generic chaining bound is stated for metric spaces for this reason.

    def SatisfiesIncrementCondition [PseudoMetricSpace T] (P : Measure Ω) (X : T → Ω → ℝ) : Prop :=
  ∀ s t : T, ∀ u : ℝ, 0 < u →
    P.real {ω | u ≤ |X s ω - X t ω|} ≤ 2 * exp (-u ^ 2 / (2 * dist s t ^ 2))

    DefinitionCardinality bounds of generic chaining#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.chainingCard

    \(N_0 = 1\) and \(N_n = 2^{2^n}\) for \(n \ge 1\) (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, (2.29)). They satisfy \(N_n^2 \le N_{n+1}\), and \(\sqrt{\log N_n}\) is of order \(2^{n/2}\), which is where the weights \(2^{n/2}\) of \(\gamma_2\) come from: a union bound over \(N_n\) sub-Gaussian variables costs a factor \(\sqrt{\log N_n}\).

    DefinitionTalagrand's \(\gamma_\alpha\) functional#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.gammaFunctional

    For \(\alpha > 0\) and a distance \(d\) on \(T\), $\(\gamma_\alpha(T, d) = \inf \sup_{t \in T} \sum_{n \ge 0} 2^{n/\alpha}\,\Delta(A_n(t)),\)$ the infimum over all admissible sequences of partitions, where \(\Delta\) is the diameter for \(d\) (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, Definition 2.7.3). The cases \(\alpha = 2\) and \(\alpha = 1\) matter most; \(\gamma_2\) governs sub-Gaussian processes. The source requires \(\alpha > 0\); the Lean definition accepts any real \(\alpha\), and only \(\alpha = 2\) (and \(\alpha > 0\) in the general lemmas) is used.

    The value lies in \([0, \infty]\), so the definition makes sense for every admissible sequence, including those along which the series diverges. The distance is any function \(d : T \times T \to [0, \infty]\), which covers both a metric and the canonical pseudo-distance of a Gaussian process.

    noncomputable def gammaFunctional (α : ℝ) (d : T → T → ℝ≥0∞) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  ⨅ A : AdmissibleSequence T, ⨆ t, ∑' n : ℕ, (2 : ℝ≥0∞) ^ ((n : ℝ) / α) * diamWith d (A.cell n t)

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.gammaFunctional_lt_top

    TheoremGeneric chaining bound#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.integral_iSup_le_gammaTwo

    Let \(T\) be a finite nonempty metric space and \((X_t)_{t \in T}\) a process of integrable, centred random variables (\(\mathbb{E}X_t = 0\)) that satisfies the sub-Gaussian increment condition. Then $\(\mathbb{E}\sup_{t \in T} X_t \le L\,\gamma_2(T, d)\)$ with \(L = 10\) (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, Theorem 2.7.11, where \(L\) is an unspecified universal constant). The bound is stated with the expectation in \([0, \infty]\); the real form is the next page.

    The proof follows Talagrand's: for an admissible sequence, choose one point in each cell, chain every \(t\) through these points (Theorem 2.7.2), and bound the chain by the union-bound argument of Section 2.4. Since \(T\) is finite, the chain may end at \(t\) itself after \(\operatorname{card} T + 1\) steps. The constant comes out as \(7\sqrt 2 < 10\).

    theorem integral_iSup_le_gammaTwo [MetricSpace T] [Finite T] [Nonempty T] (P : Measure Ω)
    [IsProbabilityMeasure P] (X : T → Ω → ℝ) (hX : ∀ t, Measurable (X t))
    (hint : ∀ t, Integrable (X t) P) (hcen : ∀ t, ∫ ω, X t ω ∂P = 0)
    (hinc : SatisfiesIncrementCondition P X) :
    ENNReal.ofReal (∫ ω, ⨆ t, X t ω ∂P) ≤ 10 * gammaFunctional 2 (edist : T → T → ℝ≥0∞)
    def SatisfiesIncrementCondition [PseudoMetricSpace T] (P : Measure Ω) (X : T → Ω → ℝ) : Prop :=
  ∀ s t : T, ∀ u : ℝ, 0 < u →
    P.real {ω | u ≤ |X s ω - X t ω|} ≤ 2 * exp (-u ^ 2 / (2 * dist s t ^ 2))

    TheoremThe chaining argument along a finite chain#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.lintegral_iSup_sub_le_of_chain

    Let \(T\) be a finite metric space, \((X_t)\) a process with the sub-Gaussian increment condition, and \(\pi_0, \dots, \pi_M : T \to T\) approximation maps with \(\pi_0 \equiv t_0\), \(\pi_M = \mathrm{id}\) and, for \(1 \le n \le M\), at most \(2^{2^{n+1}}\) distinct pairs \((\pi_n(t), \pi_{n-1}(t))\), as when \(\operatorname{card}\pi_n(T) \le N_n\). Then $\(\mathbb{E}\sup_{t}(X_t - X_{t_0}) \le 7 \sup_t \sum_{n=1}^{M} 2^{n/2} d(\pi_n(t), \pi_{n-1}(t))\)$ (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, Section 2.4, the argument proving (2.34)). In Lean the maps are named φ, and the expectation is a lower Lebesgue integral.

    Write \(X_t - X_{t_0}\) as the telescoping sum of the increments along the chain. Normalised by \(2^{n/2} d(\pi_n(t), \pi_{n-1}(t))\), the increments at level \(n\) take at most \(2^{2^{n+1}}\) values, so the union bound gives \(\mathbb{P}(Z \ge v) \le \sum_n 2^{2^{n+1}} \cdot 2 e^{-v^2 2^{n}/2}\) for their maximum \(Z\) over all levels. At \(v = 3 + j\) this is at most \(2 \cdot 2^{-j}\), so \(\mathbb{E}Z \le 3 + 4\), and \(\sup_t (X_t - X_{t_0})\) is at most \(Z\) times the right-hand side sum.

    theorem lintegral_iSup_sub_le_of_chain [MetricSpace T] [Fintype T] [DecidableEq T] (P : Measure Ω)
    [IsProbabilityMeasure P] (X : T → Ω → ℝ) (hX : ∀ t, Measurable (X t))
    (hinc : SatisfiesIncrementCondition P X) (M : ℕ) (hM : 0 < M) (φ : ℕ → T → T) (t₀ : T)
    (h0 : ∀ t, φ 0 t = t₀) (hMid : ∀ t, φ M t = t)
    (hcard : ∀ i < M,
      (Finset.univ.image fun t => (φ (i + 1) t, φ i t)).card ≤ 2 ^ 2 ^ (i + 2)) :
    ∫⁻ ω, ENNReal.ofReal (⨆ t, (X t ω - X t₀ ω)) ∂P ≤
      7 * ENNReal.ofReal (⨆ t, ∑ i ∈ range M, √2 ^ (i + 1) * dist (φ (i + 1) t) (φ i t))

    TheoremThe chaining bound for a centred process#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.GenericChaining.ofReal_integral_iSup_le_of_chain

    If, in the chaining argument along a finite chain, every \(X_t\) is moreover integrable and centred, then \(\mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t = \mathbb{E}\sup_t (X_t - X_{t_0})\) (Talagrand's (2.5)) and $\(\mathbb{E}\sup_t X_t \le 7\sup_t\sum_{n=1}^M 2^{n/2} d(\pi_n(t), \pi_{n-1}(t))\)$ (Talagrand, Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes, 2nd ed., 2021, Section 2.4). This is the common step behind the generic chaining bound and Dudley's bound, which differ only in the choice of the approximation maps.
