    MathlibExt.Analysis.WignerSemicircleWanted.Analysis.WignerSemicircleProblem.empiricalSpectralMeasureHerm

    Let \(W\) be a Hermitian \(n \times n\) complex matrix with real eigenvalues \(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n\), listed with multiplicity. The empirical spectral measure of \(W\) is the measure \(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \delta_{\lambda_j}\) on \(\mathbb{R}\), where \(\delta_\lambda\) is the Dirac measure at \(\lambda\); for \(n = 0\) it is the zero measure.

    noncomputable def empiricalSpectralMeasureHerm {n : ℕ}
    {W : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ} (hW : W.IsHermitian) : Measure ℝ :=
  (n : ℝ≥0∞)⁻¹ • ∑ j : Fin n, Measure.dirac (hW.eigenvalues j)

    DefinitionSemicircle distribution#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.WignerSemicircleWanted.Analysis.WignerSemicircleProblem.semicircleLaw

    The semicircle distribution is the measure \(\sigma\) on \(\mathbb{R}\) given by \(\sigma(A) = \int_{A \cap [-2, 2]} \frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{2\pi} \, dx\) for every measurable set \(A \subseteq \mathbb{R}\).

    Since \(\int_{-2}^{2} \sqrt{4 - x^2} \, dx = 2\pi\), it is a probability measure.

    noncomputable def semicircleLaw : Measure ℝ :=
  (volume.restrict (Set.Icc (-2 : ℝ) 2)).withDensity
    (fun x => ENNReal.ofReal (Real.sqrt (4 - x ^ 2) / (2 * Real.pi)))

    DefinitionWigner matrix#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.WignerSemicircleWanted.Analysis.WignerSemicircleProblem.wignerMatrix

    Let \(X_{ij} : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}\), for \(i, j \in \mathbb{N}\), be real functions on a set \(\Omega\). For \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(\omega \in \Omega\), the Wigner matrix \(W_n(\omega)\) is the \(n \times n\) complex matrix, with rows and columns indexed by \(\{0, \dots, n - 1\}\), whose entry at \((i, j)\) is the real number \(X_{\min(i, j)\, \max(i, j)}(\omega)\). Only the \(X_{ij}\) with \(i \le j\) are used, and the matrix is real symmetric.

    noncomputable def wignerMatrix {Ω : Type*} (X : ℕ → ℕ → Ω → ℝ) (n : ℕ)
    (ω : Ω) : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℂ :=
  fun i j => ((X (min (i : ℕ) j) (max (i : ℕ) j) ω : ℝ) : ℂ)

    LemmaWigner matrices are Hermitian#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Analysis.WignerSemicircleWanted.Analysis.WignerSemicircleProblem.wignerMatrix_isHermitian

    For every family of real functions \(X_{ij} : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}\), \(i, j \in \mathbb{N}\), on a set \(\Omega\), every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and every \(\omega \in \Omega\), the Wigner matrix \(W_n(\omega)\) is Hermitian.

    The matrix is in fact real symmetric: its entry at \((i, j)\) is the real number \(X_{\min(i, j)\, \max(i, j)}(\omega)\), and \(\min\) and \(\max\) are symmetric in their arguments.

    lemma wignerMatrix_isHermitian {Ω : Type*} (X : ℕ → ℕ → Ω → ℝ) (n : ℕ)
    (ω : Ω) : (wignerMatrix X n ω).IsHermitian

    TheoremWigner's semicircle law#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Analysis.WignerSemicircleWanted.Analysis.WignerSemicircleProblem.wigner_semicircle

    Let \((\Omega, \mu)\) be a probability space and let \(X_{ij} : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}\), for \(i, j \in \mathbb{N}\), be measurable functions. Suppose that the family \((X_{ij})_{i \le j}\) is mutually independent, that all \(X_{ij}\) with \(i \le j\) have the same distribution, and that for \(i \le j\) both \(X_{ij}\) and \(X_{ij}^2\) are integrable with \(\mathbb{E}[X_{ij}] = 0\) and \(\mathbb{E}[X_{ij}^2] = 1\). For \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(\omega \in \Omega\), let \(W_n(\omega)\) be the Wigner matrix of \(X\) and let \(L_n(\omega)\) be the image of its empirical spectral measure under \(x \mapsto x / \sqrt{n}\) (for \(n \ge 1\), the empirical spectral measure of \(W_n(\omega) / \sqrt{n}\)). Then for \(\mu\)-almost every \(\omega\), every bounded continuous function \(f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) satisfies

    \[\int f \, dL_n(\omega) \to \int f \, d\sigma \quad (n \to \infty),\]

    where \(\sigma\) is the semicircle distribution.
