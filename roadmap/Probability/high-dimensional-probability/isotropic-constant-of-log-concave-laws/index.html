    StructureLog-concave law#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.IsotropicConstant.LogConcaveLaw

    For a positive integer \(n\), a log-concave law on \(\mathbb{R}^n\) is a probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb{R}^n\) together with a Lebesgue density \(f\) of \(\mu\) that is log-concave: \(f\) is measurable and nonnegative, and \(f((1-t)x + ty) \ge f(x)^{1-t} f(y)^t\) for all \(x, y \in \mathbb{R}^n\) and \(0 < t < 1\). Moreover every coordinate \(x_i\) and every product \(x_i x_j\) is \(\mu\)-integrable, and \(f \log f\) is Lebesgue integrable.
