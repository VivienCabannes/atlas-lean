    MetaMathlibExt.noiseKernel

    Let \(E\) be an additive commutative group with a measurable structure for which addition \(E \times E \to E\) is measurable, let \(F\) be a measurable space, let \(f \colon F \to E\) be a measurable map and let \(\nu\) be a measure on \(E\). If \(\nu\) is s-finite, the additive-noise kernel from \(F\) to \(E\) sends \(y \in F\) to the image of the product measure \(\delta_{f(y)} \otimes \nu\) (where \(\delta_{f(y)}\) is the Dirac measure at \(f(y)\)) under addition \((a, z) \mapsto a + z\), that is, to the image of \(\nu\) under \(z \mapsto f(y) + z\). If \(\nu\) is not s-finite, it is the zero kernel.

    When \(\nu\) is a probability measure, the kernel sends \(y\) to the law of \(f(y) + Z\) for a random variable \(Z\) with law \(\nu\).

    noncomputable def noiseKernel (f : F → E) (hf : Measurable f) (ν : Measure E) : Kernel F E :=
  (Kernel.deterministic f hf ×ₖ Kernel.const F ν).map fun p => p.1 + p.2

    TheoremGaussian conditioning#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.condDistrib_of_hasGaussianLaw

    Let \(E\) and \(F\) be finite-dimensional real inner product spaces, and let \(X\) and \(Y\) be measurable random vectors in \(E\) and \(F\) on a probability space such that \((X, Y)\) is jointly Gaussian. Let \(A \colon F \to E\) be a linear map whose residual \(X - A Y\) is uncorrelated with \(Y\) (the normal equations): for all \(u \in E\) and \(w \in F\), \(\operatorname{Cov}(\langle u, X - A Y \rangle, \langle w, Y \rangle) = 0\). Then \(X - A Y\) is Gaussian and independent of \(Y\), and for almost every \(y\) with respect to the law of \(Y\), the conditional distribution of \(X\) given \(Y = y\) is the law of \(A y + (X - A Y)\).

    theorem condDistrib_of_hasGaussianLaw {X : Ω → E} {Y : Ω → F} (hX : Measurable X)
    (hY : Measurable Y) (hXY : HasGaussianLaw (fun ω => (X ω, Y ω)) P) (A : F →L[ℝ] E)
    (hA : ∀ x y, cov[fun ω => ⟪x, X ω - A (Y ω)⟫, fun ω => ⟪y, Y ω⟫; P] = 0) :
    HasGaussianLaw (fun ω => X ω - A (Y ω)) P ∧
      IndepFun (fun ω => X ω - A (Y ω)) Y P ∧
      condDistrib X Y P =ᵐ[P.map Y]
        noiseKernel A A.continuous.measurable (P.map fun ω => X ω - A (Y ω))

    MetaMathlibExt.condDistrib_linear_add_of_indepFun

    Let \(n\) and \(p\) be finite index sets, and let \(x\) and \(v\) be independent measurable random vectors in \(\mathbb{R}^n\) and \(\mathbb{R}^p\) on a probability space, with \(x \sim N(m, S)\) and \(v \sim N(0, R)\) for positive semidefinite matrices \(S\) and \(R\).
