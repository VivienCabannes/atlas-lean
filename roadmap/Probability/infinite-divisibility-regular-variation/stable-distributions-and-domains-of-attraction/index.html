    TheoremConvolution powers of a Gaussian are Gaussian#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.StableDistribution.convPow_gaussianReal

    The convolution power of a Gaussian is Gaussian with the mean and the variance multiplied by n: the law of the sum of n i.i.d. N(μ, v) variables is N(nμ, nv).

    theorem convPow_gaussianReal (μ : ℝ) (v : ℝ≥0) (n : ℕ) :
    Measure.convPow (gaussianReal μ v) n =
      gaussianReal ((n : ℝ) * μ) ((n : ℝ≥0) * v)

    Domains of attraction

    DefinitionDomain of attraction#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.StableDistribution.InDomainOfAttraction

    The probability measure μ is in the domain of attraction of G if there exist sequences of normalizing constants a n > 0 and centering constants b n such that the law of (S_n - b n) / a n (where S_n has law μ^{*n}) converges weakly to G, expressed here as integration against arbitrary bounded continuous functions.

    def InDomainOfAttraction (μ G : Measure ℝ) : Prop :=
  IsProbabilityMeasure μ ∧ IsProbabilityMeasure G ∧
  ∃ (a : ℕ → ℝ) (b : ℕ → ℝ),
    (∀ n, 0 < a n) ∧
    ∀ (f : ℝ →ᵇ ℝ),
      Tendsto
        (fun n => ∫ x, f x ∂(Measure.map (fun y => (y - b n) / a n)
          (Measure.convPow μ n)))
        atTop (𝓝 (∫ x, f x ∂G))
