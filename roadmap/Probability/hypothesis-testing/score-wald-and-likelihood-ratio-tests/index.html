    Let θ̂ₙ be √n-consistent (√n (θ̂ₙ - θ₀) converges in distribution) and solve the likelihood equation with probability tending to one. If on the ball of radius δ > 0 about θ₀ the log-likelihood is twice differentiable in θ with |∂³_θ log p θ x| ≤ M x for an integrable M, and ∂²_θ log p θ₀ is integrable with mean -I(θ₀), then 2 (ℓₙ(θ̂ₙ) - ℓₙ(θ₀)) - n (θ̂ₙ - θ₀)² I(θ₀) → 0 in probability, where ℓₙ θ = ∑_{i<n} log p θ (X i).

    The positivity of p is not used: the expansion holds for any criterion log p θ x with these derivatives, and the theorems below assume 0 < p θ x.
