    Backwards martingales and Lévy's downward theorem

    TheoremLévy's downward theorem, almost sure convergence#✓fully proved

    MeasureTheory.levy_downward_ae

    Let \((\Omega, \mathcal F, \mu)\) be a probability space, let \(\mathcal F_0 \supseteq \mathcal F_1 \supseteq \cdots\) be a decreasing sequence of sub-σ-algebras of \(\mathcal F\), and let \(\mathcal F_\infty = \bigcap_n \mathcal F_n\). For every function \(g : \Omega \to \mathbb R\), the conditional expectations \(\mathbb E[g \mid \mathcal F_n]\) converge to \(\mathbb E[g \mid \mathcal F_\infty]\) as \(n \to \infty\), \(\mu\)-almost everywhere.

    No integrability hypothesis is needed: for a non-integrable \(g\) every conditional expectation is \(0\) by convention, and the statement is trivial.

    theorem levy_downward_ae {g : Ω → ℝ} (m : ℕ → MeasurableSpace Ω)
    (hm : Antitone m) (hm0 : ∀ n, m n ≤ m0) :
    ∀ᵐ ω ∂μ, Tendsto (fun n => (μ[g | m n]) ω) atTop (𝓝 ((μ[g | ⨅ n, m n]) ω))

    DefinitionBackwards martingale#✓fully proved

    MeasureTheory.IsBackwardsMartingale

    Let \(\mu\) be a measure on a measurable space \((\Omega, \mathcal F)\) and let \((\mathcal F_n)_{n \ge 0}\) be a sequence of σ-algebras on \(\Omega\). A sequence of functions \(M_n : \Omega \to \mathbb R\) is a backwards martingale with respect to \((\mathcal F_n)\) and \(\mu\) if \(M_0\) is \(\mu\)-integrable and, for every \(n\), \(M_n = \mathbb E[M_0 \mid \mathcal F_n]\) \(\mu\)-almost everywhere.

    Here \(\mathbb E[M_0 \mid \mathcal F_n]\) is the conditional expectation with respect to \(\mu\) when \(\mathcal F_n \subseteq \mathcal F\) and the restriction of \(\mu\) to \(\mathcal F_n\) is σ-finite, and is \(0\) by convention otherwise. The definition is used when \((\mathcal F_n)\) is a decreasing sequence of sub-σ-algebras, but it does not require this.

    def IsBackwardsMartingale (M : ℕ → Ω → ℝ) (m : ℕ → MeasurableSpace Ω) (μ : Measure Ω) : Prop :=
  Integrable (M 0) μ ∧ ∀ n, M n =ᵐ[μ] μ[M 0 | m n]

    TheoremAlmost-everywhere convergent conditional expectations converge in L¹#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.CondExpAntitoneLimit.tendsto_eLpNorm_condExp_sub_of_ae_tendsto

    Let \(\mu\) be a finite measure on a measurable space \((\Omega, \mathcal{F})\), let \((\mathcal{G}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\) be a sequence of sub-σ-algebras of \(\mathcal{F}\), let \(g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}\) be integrable, and write \(\mu[g \mid \mathcal{G}_n]\) for the conditional expectation of \(g\) given \(\mathcal{G}_n\) with respect to \(\mu\). If \(h : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}\) is integrable and \(\mu[g \mid \mathcal{G}_n] \to h\) \(\mu\)-almost everywhere as \(n \to \infty\), then \(\int_\Omega |\mu[g \mid \mathcal{G}_n] - h| \, d\mu \to 0\) as \(n \to \infty\).

    This is Vitali's theorem applied to the uniformly integrable family of conditional expectations of a single integrable function.
