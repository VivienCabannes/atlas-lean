    StructureStick-breaking process#✓fully proved

    ProbabilityTheory.StickBreakingProcess

    A stick-breaking process on the probability space (Ω, μ): independent proportions βₖ ∈ [0, 1] (almost surely, with arbitrary laws), i.i.d. atoms atom k with law H, and the proportion sequence independent of the atom sequence. Ishwaran–James (2001) take βₖ ∼ Beta(aₖ, bₖ); Sethuraman's Dirichlet process is IsDirichlet.
