    StatMech.bernoulliProductMeasure.real_singleton_logModular

    The configuration weights of a Bernoulli product measure are log-modular: w(a) w(b) = w(a ⊔ b) w(a ⊓ b). In particular they satisfy the FKG lattice condition.

    theorem real_singleton_logModular [Fintype E] (a b : ConfigSpace E) :
    (bernoulliProductMeasure (E := E) p hp).real {a}
        * (bernoulliProductMeasure (E := E) p hp).real {b}
      = (bernoulliProductMeasure (E := E) p hp).real {a ⊔ b}
        * (bernoulliProductMeasure (E := E) p hp).real {a ⊓ b}
