    Let \(S\) be a nonempty standard Borel space, let \(\kappa\) be a Markov kernel from \(S\) to \(S\), let \(\mu\) be a probability measure on \(S\), and let \(P_\mu\) be the canonical Markov chain measure on \(S^{\mathbb{N}}\) with initial distribution \(\mu\) and transition kernel \(\kappa\).     TheoremMarkov chain construction theorem#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.MarkovChainConstruction.markov_chain_construction

    Let \(S\) be a nonempty standard Borel space, let \(\kappa\) be a Markov kernel from \(S\) to \(S\) and let \(\mu\) be a probability measure on \(S\). Then there is a probability measure \(P\) on the path space \(S^{\mathbb{N}}\) such that, writing \(X_n(\omega) = \omega_n\) for the coordinates, the law of \(X_0\) under \(P\) is \(\mu\), and for every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) the regular conditional distribution of \(X_{n+1}\) given \((X_0, \dots, X_n)\) under \(P\) agrees with the kernel \((x_0, \dots, x_n) \mapsto \kappa(x_n, \cdot)\) for almost every \((x_0, \dots, x_n)\) with respect to the law of \((X_0, \dots, X_n)\) under \(P\).

    That is, the coordinate process \((X_0, X_1, \dots)\) under \(P\) is a Markov chain with initial distribution \(\mu\) and transition kernel \(\kappa\).

    theorem markov_chain_construction
    (κ : Kernel S S) [IsMarkovKernel κ]
    (μ : Measure S) [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    [StandardBorelSpace S] [Nonempty S] :
    ∃ (P : Measure (ℕ → S)) (_ : IsProbabilityMeasure P),
      P.map (fun (ω : ℕ → S) => ω 0) = μ ∧
      ∀ n : ℕ,
        condDistrib (fun (x : ℕ → S) => x (n + 1)) (Preorder.frestrictLe n) P
          =ᵐ[Measure.map (Preorder.frestrictLe n) P] markovLiftKernel κ n
