    MathlibExt.Probability.MarkovPropertyChain.markov_setIntegral_shift

    Let \(S\) be a nonempty standard Borel space, \(\mu\) a finite measure on \(S\), \(\kappa\) a Markov kernel on \(S\), and \(P_\mu\) the path measure on \(S^{\mathbb{N}}\) of the Markov chain with initial measure \(\mu\) and transition kernel \(\kappa\). Let \(Y : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}\) be bounded and measurable for the product \(\sigma\)-algebra, let \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), let \(\theta_n\) be the shift \((\theta_n \omega)_k = \omega_{n+k}\), and let \(\mathcal{F}_n\) be the \(\sigma\)-algebra generated by the coordinates \(\omega_0, \dots, \omega_n\). For \(x \in S\), let \(E_x[Y]\) be the integral of \(Y\) against the path measure of the chain started at \(x\). Then for every set \(A \in \mathcal{F}_n\),

    \[\int_A Y(\theta_n \omega) \, dP_\mu(\omega) = \int_A E_{\omega_n}[Y] \, dP_\mu(\omega).\]
    theorem markov_setIntegral_shift

    MathlibExt.Probability.MarkovPropertyChain.markov_property_full

    Let \(S\) be a nonempty standard Borel space, \(\mu\) a finite measure on \(S\), \(\kappa\) a Markov kernel on \(S\), and \(P_\mu\) the path measure on \(S^{\mathbb{N}}\) of the Markov chain with initial measure \(\mu\) and transition kernel \(\kappa\). Let \(Y : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}\) be bounded and measurable for the product \(\sigma\)-algebra, let \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), let \(\theta_n\) be the shift \((\theta_n \omega)_k = \omega_{n+k}\), and let \(\mathcal{F}_n\) be the \(\sigma\)-algebra generated by the coordinates \(\omega_0, \dots, \omega_n\). For \(x \in S\), let \(E_x[Y]\) be the integral of \(Y\) against the path measure of the chain started at \(x\). Then for \(P_\mu\)-almost every \(\omega\),

    \[E_{P_\mu}[Y \circ \theta_n \mid \mathcal{F}_n](\omega) = E_{\omega_n}[Y].\]

    In other words, conditioning on the trajectory up to time \(n\) depends only on the state at time \(n\).

    theorem markov_property_full

    (Y : (ℕ → S) → ℝ)
    (hY_meas : @Measurable _ _ MeasurableSpace.pi _ Y)
    (hY_bdd : ∃ C : ℝ, ∀ ω, |Y ω| ≤ C)
    (n : ℕ) :
    let P_μ := MarkovChainPathMeasure S μ κ
    P_μ[Y ∘ shiftPath n | pathFiltration S n]
    =ᵐ[P_μ]

    MathlibExt.Probability.StrongMarkovChain.strong_markov_chain_ae_finite

    Let \(S\) be a nonempty standard Borel space, \(\mu\) a finite measure on \(S\), \(\kappa\) a Markov kernel on \(S\), and \(P_\mu\) the law on the path space \(S^{\mathbb{N}}\) of the Markov chain with initial distribution \(\mu\) and transition kernel \(\kappa\). For \(x \in S\) write \(E_x\) for the expectation under the chain started from \(x\), and for a path \(\omega\) and \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) write \(\theta_n \omega\) for the shifted path \(k \mapsto \omega(n + k)\). Let \(T : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\) be a stopping time for the path filtration, with \(\sigma\)-algebra \(\mathcal{F}_T\), such that \(T \ne \infty\) \(P_\mu\)-almost surely, and let \(Y_n : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}\), for \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), be measurable functions with \(|Y_n(\omega)| \le C\) for a single constant \(C\) and all \(n\) and \(\omega\). Then the conditional expectation under \(P_\mu\) given \(\mathcal{F}_T\) of \(\omega \mapsto Y_{T(\omega)}(\theta_{T(\omega)} \omega)\) equals \(\omega \mapsto E_{\omega(T(\omega))}[Y_{T(\omega)}]\) \(P_\mu\)-almost surely.

    On the null event \(\{T = \infty\}\) both sides are evaluated at the same unspecified finite time, which does not affect the almost-sure equality.

    theorem strong_markov_chain_ae_finite
    (S : Type*) [MeasurableSpace S] [StandardBorelSpace S] [Nonempty S]
    (μ : Measure S) [IsFiniteMeasure μ]
    (κ : Kernel S S) [IsMarkovKernel κ]
    (T : (ℕ → S) → WithTop ℕ)
    (hT : @IsStoppingTime (ℕ → S) ℕ MeasurableSpace.pi _ (pathFiltration S) T)
    (hT_finite : ∀ᵐ ω ∂MarkovChainPathMeasure S μ κ, T ω ≠ ⊤)
    (Y : ℕ → (ℕ → S) → ℝ)
    (hY_meas : ∀ n, @Measurable _ _ MeasurableSpace.pi _ (Y n))
    (hY_bdd : ∃ C : ℝ, ∀ n ω, |Y n ω| ≤ C) :

    MathlibExt.Probability.StrongMarkovChain.strong_markov_chain

    Let \(S\) be a nonempty standard Borel space, \(\mu\) a finite measure on \(S\), \(\kappa\) a Markov kernel on \(S\), and \(P_\mu\) the law on the path space \(S^{\mathbb{N}}\) of the Markov chain with initial distribution \(\mu\) and transition kernel \(\kappa\); for \(x \in S\) write \(E_x\) for the expectation under the chain started from \(x\). Let \(T : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{N}\) be a stopping time for the path filtration, with \(\sigma\)-algebra \(\mathcal{F}_T\), and let \(Y_n : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}\), for \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), be measurable functions with \(|Y_n(\omega)| \le C\) for a single constant \(C\) and all \(n\) and \(\omega\). Write \(\theta_T \omega\) for the path \(\omega\) shifted by \(T\), namely \(k \mapsto \omega(T(\omega) + k)\). Then the conditional expectation under \(P_\mu\) given \(\mathcal{F}_T\) of \(\omega \mapsto Y_{T(\omega)}(\theta_T \omega)\) equals \(\omega \mapsto E_{\omega(T(\omega))}[Y_{T(\omega)}]\) \(P_\mu\)-almost surely.

    This is the special case of the strong Markov property for a.s.-finite stopping times in which the time is finite everywhere.
