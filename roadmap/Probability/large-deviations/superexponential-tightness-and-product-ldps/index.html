    Superexponential tightness and product LDPs

    On a Polish space, a large deviation principle with a good rate function forces its measures to concentrate on compact sets up to exponentially small mass: for every \(\ell\) there is a compact \(K\) with \(\mu_n(K^c) \le e^{-n\ell}\) for all \(n\), the large deviation analogue of Prokhorov tightness. This is what reduces large deviation estimates to compact sets, and it gives the product principle: rate functions of independent components add.

    MathlibExt.Probability.LDPProduct.exists_isCompact_measure_compl_le

    Let \((\mu_n)\) be finite measures on a Polish space satisfying the large deviation principle with a good rate function \(I\). Then for every \(\ell \in \mathbb{R}\) there is a compact set \(K_\ell\) with

    \[\mu_n(K_\ell^{c}) \le e^{-n \ell} \quad \text{for every } n.\]

    This is the large deviation analogue of Prokhorov's tightness condition, and the reason a good rate function lets one reduce upper bounds to compact sets. Varadhan's proof covers the compact sub-level set \(\{I \le \ell + 2\}\), for each \(k\), by finitely many balls of radius \(1/k\); the upper bound on the complement of their union gives mass at most \(e^{-k} e^{-n\ell}\) for large \(n\), the finitely many remaining measures are tight individually, and the intersection over \(k\) is totally bounded, hence has compact closure in the complete space.

    theorem exists_isCompact_measure_compl_le {X : Type*} [TopologicalSpace X] [PolishSpace X]
    [MeasurableSpace X] [OpensMeasurableSpace X] {μ : ℕ → Measure X}
    [∀ n, IsFiniteMeasure (μ n)] {I : X → ℝ≥0∞} (hμ : SatisfiesLDP μ I)
    (hI : IsGoodRateFunction I) (ℓ : ℝ) :
    ∃ K : Set X, IsCompact K ∧ ∀ n : ℕ, μ n Kᶜ ≤ ENNReal.ofReal (Real.exp (-(n * ℓ)))

    TheoremProduct large deviation principle#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.LDPProduct.satisfiesLDP_prod

    Let probability measures \((\mu_n)\) on a Polish space \(X\) and \((\nu_n)\) on a Polish space \(Y\) satisfy large deviation principles with good rate functions \(I\) and \(J\). Then the product measures \(\mu_n \otimes \nu_n\) satisfy the large deviation principle on \(X \times Y\) with the good rate function

    \[K(x, y) = I(x) + J(y).\]

    The lower bound is local: every neighbourhood of \((x, y)\) contains a product \(U \times V\) of neighbourhoods. The upper bound is first proved near each point, then on compact sets by a finite subcover, and finally on closed sets by superexponential tightness of both factors.

    theorem satisfiesLDP_prod [PolishSpace X] [PolishSpace Y] (hμ : SatisfiesLDP μ I)
    (hI : IsGoodRateFunction I) (hν : SatisfiesLDP ν J) (hJ : IsGoodRateFunction J) :
    SatisfiesLDP (fun n => (μ n).prod (ν n)) (fun z : X × Y => I z.1 + J z.2)
