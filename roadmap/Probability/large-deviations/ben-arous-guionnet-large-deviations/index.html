    Ben Arous–Guionnet large deviations

    For \(\beta > 0\), the Gaussian \(\beta\)-ensemble is the law on \(\mathbb{R}^N\) with density proportional to \(\prod_{i<j} |\lambda_i - \lambda_j|^\beta \exp(-\frac{\beta N}{4} \sum_i \lambda_i^2)\); for \(\beta = 1, 2, 4\) it is the law of the eigenvalues of the GOE, GUE and GSE. Ben Arous and Guionnet proved that the empirical spectral measure \(L_N = \frac{1}{N} \sum_i \delta_{\lambda_i}\) satisfies a large deviation principle at speed \(N^2\) with the good rate function $\(I_\beta(\mu) = \frac{\beta}{4} \int x^2 \, d\mu(x) - \frac{\beta}{2} \iint \log |x - y| \, d\mu(x)\,d\mu(y) - \frac{3\beta}{8},\)$ whose unique zero is the semicircle law. The double integral is Voiculescu's free entropy, and the speed \(N^2\) reflects that the eigenvalues form a Coulomb gas with \(N^2\) pairwise interactions. Since the rate function has a unique zero, the principle implies the almost sure convergence of \(L_N\) to the semicircle law for these ensembles.

    The definitions are given here and the two main statements are stated, not proved.

    DefinitionThe Gaussian β-ensemble#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.BenArousGuionnetWanted.betaEnsemble

    For \(\beta > 0\) and \(N \ge 1\), the probability law \(Q^N_\beta\) on \(\mathbb{R}^N\) with density \(w_{\beta,N} / Z^N_\beta\) with respect to Lebesgue measure, where \(w_{\beta,N}\) is the density and \(Z^N_\beta = \int w_{\beta,N}\). For \(\beta = 1, 2, 4\) it is the joint law of the (unordered) eigenvalues of a GOE, GUE or GSE matrix whose entries have variance of order \(1/N\), normalised so that the spectrum fills \([-2, 2]\): the change of variables to eigenvalues and eigenvectors produces the Vandermonde factor \(|\Delta(\lambda)|^\beta\), and the eigenvectors are Haar distributed and independent of the eigenvalues.

    noncomputable def betaEnsemble (β : ℝ) (N : ℕ) : Measure (Fin N → ℝ) :=
  (∫⁻ x, betaEnsembleWeight β N x)⁻¹ • volume.withDensity (betaEnsembleWeight β N)
    noncomputable def betaEnsembleWeight (β : ℝ) (N : ℕ) (x : Fin N → ℝ) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  ENNReal.ofReal ((∏ i, ∏ j ∈ Finset.univ.filter (fun j => i < j), |x i - x j| ^ β) *
    Real.exp (-(β * N / 4) * ∑ i, x i ^ 2))

    DefinitionEmpirical spectral measure#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.BenArousGuionnetWanted.spectralEmpiricalMeasure

    For \(\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^N\) with \(N \ge 1\), the probability measure \(L_N = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_{\lambda_i}\) on \(\mathbb{R}\), built with the empirical measure of a sample used in Sanov's theorem.     DefinitionLogarithmic-energy kernel#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.BenArousGuionnetWanted.logEnergyKernel

    The kernel $\(g(x, y) = \log \frac{1}{|x - y|} + \frac{x^2 + y^2}{4} - \Bigl(\frac{1}{2} - \log 2\Bigr) \quad (x \ne y), \qquad g(x, x) = +\infty,\)$ with values in \([0, \infty]\). It is nonnegative: \(\frac{x^2 + y^2}{4} \ge \frac{(x - y)^2}{8}\), and \(d \mapsto -\log d + d^2/8\) has minimum \(\frac{1}{2} - \log 2\) at \(d = 2\). It is the kernel \(f(x,y) = \log|x-y|^{-1} + \frac{x^2+y^2}{4}\) of Guionnet's proof, shifted to be nonnegative so that its double integral is a well-defined element of \([0, \infty]\) for every probability measure.

    noncomputable def logEnergyKernel (x y : ℝ) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  if x = y then ⊤ else
    ENNReal.ofReal (Real.log |x - y|⁻¹ + (x ^ 2 + y ^ 2) / 4 - (1 / 2 - Real.log 2))

    DefinitionThe Ben Arous–Guionnet rate function#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Probability.BenArousGuionnetWanted.benArousGuionnetRate

    For \(\beta > 0\) and a probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb{R}\), $\(I_\beta(\mu) = \frac{\beta}{4} \int x^2 \, d\mu(x) - \frac{\beta}{2} \iint \log |x - y| \, d\mu(x)\,d\mu(y) - \frac{3\beta}{8}.\)$ In Lean it is written with the nonnegative logarithmic-energy kernel \(g\) as $\(I_\beta(\mu) = \frac{\beta}{2} \iint g \, d\mu\, d\mu - \Bigl(\frac{\beta}{8} + \frac{\beta}{2}\log 2\Bigr),\)$ which equals the formula above because \(\frac{\beta}{2}\iint f\,d\mu\,d\mu = \frac{\beta}{4}\int x^2\,d\mu - \frac{\beta}{2}\iint \log|x-y|\,d\mu\,d\mu\) and \(\frac{\beta}{2}(\frac{1}{2} - \log 2) - \frac{3\beta}{8} = -\frac{\beta}{8} - \frac{\beta}{2}\log 2\). The subtraction is truncated at \(0\) in \([0, \infty]\), which loses nothing since \(I_\beta \ge 0\). \(I_\beta(\mu) = \infty\) when \(\mu\) has an atom (the diagonal, where \(g = \infty\), then has positive mass) or an infinite second moment. At the semicircle law, \(\int x^2\,d\sigma = 1\) and \(\iint \log|x-y|\,d\sigma\,d\sigma = -\frac{1}{4}\), so \(I_\beta(\sigma) = \frac{\beta}{4} + \frac{\beta}{8} - \frac{3\beta}{8} = 0\).

    noncomputable def benArousGuionnetRate (β : ℝ) (μ : ProbabilityMeasure ℝ) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  ENNReal.ofReal (β / 2) * ∫⁻ x, ∫⁻ y, logEnergyKernel x y ∂(μ : Measure ℝ) ∂(μ : Measure ℝ) -
    ENNReal.ofReal (β / 8 + β / 2 * Real.log 2)
    noncomputable def logEnergyKernel (x y : ℝ) : ℝ≥0∞ :=

    ENNReal.ofReal (Real.log |x - y|⁻¹ + (x ^ 2 + y ^ 2) / 4 - (1 / 2 - Real.log 2))

    TheoremBen Arous–Guionnet large deviation principle#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Probability.BenArousGuionnetWanted.benArousGuionnet_ldp

    For \(\beta > 0\), the laws of the empirical spectral measure \(L_N\) under the Gaussian β-ensemble satisfy, in the space of probability measures on \(\mathbb{R}\) with the weak topology, a large deviation principle at speed \(N^2\) with the rate function \(I_\beta\), and \(I_\beta\) is a good rate function (its sublevel sets are compact). Stated here, not proved.

    Proof in the sources: write the density of \(Q^N_\beta\) as \(\exp\bigl(-\frac{\beta}{2} N^2 \iint_{x \ne y} f \, dL_N\, dL_N\bigr) \prod_i e^{-\beta\lambda_i^2/4}\); the upper bound truncates the singular kernel \(f\) and uses lower semicontinuity of the energy, the lower bound moves the eigenvalues to a discretisation of the target measure, and the normalising constant is identified from the two bounds. As for Sanov's theorem, the statement assumes that the open sets of the weak topology are measurable for Mathlib's σ-algebra on probability measures; this holds on \(\mathbb{R}\) but is not yet an instance in Mathlib.

    theorem_wanted benArousGuionnet_ldp {β : ℝ} (hβ : 0 < β)
    [OpensMeasurableSpace (ProbabilityMeasure ℝ)] :
    SatisfiesLDPAtSpeed (fun N => (N : ℝ) ^ 2)
        (fun N => (betaEnsemble β N).map (spectralEmpiricalMeasure N)) (benArousGuionnetRate β) ∧
      IsGoodRateFunction (benArousGuionnetRate β)

    Background
    Background
    • The semicircle law is the unique zero of the rate function: with it, the principle gives the almost sure convergence of \(L_N\) to the semicircle law.
    • Sanov's theorem: the large deviation principle at speed \(N\) for empirical measures of independent samples, whose rate is relative entropy instead of free entropy.

    TheoremThe semicircle law is the unique zero of the rate function#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Probability.BenArousGuionnetWanted.benArousGuionnetRate_eq_zero_iff

    For \(\beta > 0\) and a probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb{R}\), \(I_\beta(\mu) = 0\) if and only if \(\mu\) is the semicircle law \(\sigma(dx) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{4 - x^2}\,\mathbf{1}_{|x| \le 2}\,dx\).
