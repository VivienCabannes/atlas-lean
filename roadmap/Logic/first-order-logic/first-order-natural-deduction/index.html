    First-order natural deduction

    InductiveNatural deduction#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Logic.FOL.Derivable

    Fix a first-order language \(L\) and a type \(\alpha\) of variables; formulas and terms below are those of \(L\) with free variables in \(\alpha\). For a formula \(\varphi\) with one additional bound variable and a term \(t\), write \(\varphi[t]\) for \(\varphi\) with \(t\) substituted for that bound variable and \(\forall \varphi\) for its universal quantification. Write \(\neg \psi\) for \(\psi \to \bot\), and \(\psi, \Gamma\) for the list \(\Gamma\) with \(\psi\) added in front. Derivability \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi\) of a formula \(\varphi\) (the conclusion) from a finite list \(\Gamma\) of formulas (the open hypotheses) is the least relation closed under the following rules.

    • Assumption: \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi\) whenever \(\varphi\) is a member of \(\Gamma\).
    • Implication introduction: from \(\varphi, \Gamma \vdash \psi\) infer \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi \to \psi\).
    • Implication elimination: from \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi \to \psi\) and \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi\) infer \(\Gamma \vdash \psi\).
    • Reductio ad absurdum: from \(\neg \varphi, \Gamma \vdash \bot\) infer \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi\).
    • Universal introduction: if a variable \(x\) occurs free in no member of \(\Gamma\) and does not occur free in \(\varphi\), then from \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi[x]\) infer \(\Gamma \vdash \forall \varphi\).
    • Universal elimination: from \(\Gamma \vdash \forall \varphi\) infer \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi[t]\), for every term \(t\).
    • Reflexivity: \(\Gamma \vdash t = t\) for every term \(t\).
    • Substitution: from \(\Gamma \vdash s = t\) and \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi[s]\) infer \(\Gamma \vdash \varphi[t]\).

    TheoremInjective renaming preserves derivability#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Logic.FOL.Derivable.rename

    Let \(f \colon \alpha \to \beta\) be an injective map between types of variables. For a formula \(\psi\) of a first-order language \(L\) with free variables in \(\alpha\), write \(f(\psi)\) for the renaming of the free variables of \(\psi\) by \(f\), and for a finite list \(\Gamma\) of such formulas write \(f(\Gamma)\) for the list obtained by renaming each member of \(\Gamma\).
