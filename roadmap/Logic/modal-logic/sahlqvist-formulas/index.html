    DefinitionPositive modal formulas#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Modal.IsPositive

    Positive and negative modal formulas are defined by simultaneous recursion: every atom is positive, \(\bot\) is positive, \(\varphi \to \psi\) is positive if \(\varphi\) is negative and \(\psi\) is positive, and \(\Box\varphi\) is positive if \(\varphi\) is positive.

    Positive formulas are closed under \(\wedge\), \(\vee\), \(\Box\) and \(\Diamond\), which are encoded through \(\to\), \(\bot\) and \(\Box\).

    def IsPositive : Formula Atom → Prop
  | .atom _ => True
  | .bot => True
  | .imp φ ψ => IsNegative φ ∧ IsPositive ψ
  | .box φ => IsPositive φ
    inductive Formula (α : Type u) : Type u where
  | atom : α → Formula α
  | bot : Formula α
  | imp : Formula α → Formula α → Formula α
  | box : Formula α → Formula α
  deriving DecidableEq, Repr

    Cslib.Modal.boxPow

    For a natural number \(k\) and a modal formula \(\varphi\), the iterated box \(\Box^k \varphi\) is defined by \(\Box^0 \varphi = \varphi\) and \(\Box^{k+1} \varphi = \Box\,\Box^k \varphi\).

    InductiveSahlqvist antecedents#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Modal.IsSahlqvistAntecedent

    The Sahlqvist antecedents form the smallest class of modal formulas that contains every boxed atom \(\Box^k p\) (an atom \(p\) under \(k \ge 0\) boxes) and every negative formula, and is closed under \(\wedge\), \(\vee\) and \(\Diamond\).

    inductive IsSahlqvistAntecedent : Formula Atom → Prop
  /-- A boxed atom is a Sahlqvist antecedent. -/
  | boxedAtom (a : Atom) (k : Nat) :
      IsSahlqvistAntecedent (boxPow k (.atom a))
  /-- A negative formula is a Sahlqvist antecedent. -/
  | ofNegative (φ : Formula Atom) : IsNegative φ → IsSahlqvistAntecedent φ
  /-- Sahlqvist antecedents are closed under conjunction. -/
  | and (φ ψ : Formula Atom) :
      IsSahlqvistAntecedent φ → IsSahlqvistAntecedent ψ →
        IsSahlqvistAntecedent (φ.and ψ)
  /-- Sahlqvist antecedents are closed under disjunction. -/
  | or (φ ψ : Formula Atom) :
      IsSahlqvistAntecedent φ → IsSahlqvistAntecedent ψ →
        IsSahlqvistAntecedent (φ.or ψ)

      IsSahlqvistAntecedent φ → IsSahlqvistAntecedent φ.dia

    Cslib.Modal.IsSahlqvistImplication

    InductiveSahlqvist formulas#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Modal.IsSahlqvist

    The Sahlqvist formulas form the smallest class of modal formulas that contains every Sahlqvist implication, is closed under \(\wedge\) and \(\Box\), and contains \(\varphi \vee \psi\) whenever \(\varphi\) and \(\psi\) are Sahlqvist formulas with no atom in common.

    The side condition on disjunction is essential: arbitrary disjunction leaves the correspondence class. \(\Diamond\) is not part of this outer closure; it belongs to the grammar of Sahlqvist antecedents. For example, the boxed Sahlqvist implication \(\Box(p \to \Box\Diamond p)\) is a Sahlqvist formula.

    inductive IsSahlqvist : Formula Atom → Prop
  /-- A Sahlqvist implication is Sahlqvist. -/
  | impl {φ : Formula Atom} : IsSahlqvistImplication φ → IsSahlqvist φ
  /-- Sahlqvist formulas are closed under conjunction. -/
  | and {φ ψ : Formula Atom} :
      IsSahlqvist φ → IsSahlqvist ψ → IsSahlqvist (φ.and ψ)
  /-- Sahlqvist formulas are closed under disjunction with disjoint atoms. -/
  | or {φ ψ : Formula Atom} :
      IsSahlqvist φ → IsSahlqvist ψ → Disjoint φ.atoms ψ.atoms →
        IsSahlqvist (φ.or ψ)
  /-- Sahlqvist formulas are closed under boxing. -/
  | box {φ : Formula Atom} : IsSahlqvist φ → IsSahlqvist (.box φ)
    inductive Formula (α : Type u) : Type u where
  | atom : α → Formula α
  | bot : Formula α
  | imp : Formula α → Formula α → Formula α
  | box : Formula α → Formula α
  deriving DecidableEq, Repr
