    Cslib.Modal.Formula.ofProposition

    CSLib's unimodal propositions are translated into modal formulas in the \(\bot\), \(\to\), \(\Box\) basis by recursion, writing \(\varphi'\) for the translation of \(\varphi\): an atom \(p\) goes to \(p\), falsehood to \(\bot\), \(\neg\varphi\) to \(\varphi' \to \bot\), \(\varphi \lor \psi\) to \((\varphi' \to \bot) \to \psi'\), and the diamond (the unimodal triangle) applied to \(\varphi\) goes to \(\Diamond\varphi'\), that is, to \(\Box(\varphi' \to \bot) \to \bot\).

    DefinitionTranslation of modal formulas into CSLib propositions#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Modal.Formula.toProposition

    Each modal formula built from atoms, \(\bot\), \(\to\) and \(\Box\) is translated into a CSLib unimodal proposition by recursion: an atom \(p\) goes to the atom \(p\), \(\bot\) goes to false, \(\varphi \to \psi\) goes to \(\neg\varphi' \lor \psi'\), and \(\Box\varphi\) goes to \(\neg\Diamond\neg\varphi'\), where \(\varphi'\) and \(\psi'\) are the translations of \(\varphi\) and \(\psi\) and \(\Diamond\) is the possibility operator of CSLib's unimodal language.

    def Formula.toProposition : Formula Atom → Logic.Modal.Unimodal.Proposition Atom
  | .atom p => .atom p
  | .bot => .false
  | .imp phi psi => .or (.not (toProposition phi)) (toProposition psi)
  | .box phi => .not (.triangle default (fun _ => .not (toProposition phi)))
    inductive Formula (α : Type u) : Type u where
  | atom : α → Formula α
  | bot : Formula α
  | imp : Formula α → Formula α → Formula α
  | box : Formula α → Formula α
  deriving DecidableEq, Repr
