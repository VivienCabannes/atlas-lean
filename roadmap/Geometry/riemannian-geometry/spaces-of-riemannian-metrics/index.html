    Manifold.metricInCoordinates

    Let \(g\) be a smooth Riemannian metric on the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold \(M\) modeled through a model with corners \(I : H \to E\), and let \(e\) be an open partial homeomorphism from \(M\) to \(H\). Write \(\psi\) for the inverse of the extended chart \(I \circ e\), \(T\) for the target of \(I \circ e\), and \(D\psi(x)\) for the derivative of \(\psi\) at \(x\) within \(T\). The coordinate expression of \(g\) in \(e\) is the field of continuous bilinear forms on \(E\) that assigns to each \(x \in E\) the pullback form \((v, w) \mapsto g_{\psi(x)}(D\psi(x)\,v, D\psi(x)\,w)\).

    noncomputable def metricInCoordinates
    (g : ContMDiffRiemannianMetric I ∞ E (fun x : M ↦ TangentSpace I x))
    (e : OpenPartialHomeomorph M H) : E → E →L[ℝ] E →L[ℝ] ℝ :=
  fun x =>
    (show E →L[ℝ] E →L[ℝ] ℝ from g.inner ((e.extend I).symm x)).bilinearComp
      (show E →L[ℝ] E from
        mfderivWithin 𝓘(ℝ, E) I (e.extend I).symm (e.extend I).target x)
      (show E →L[ℝ] E from
        mfderivWithin 𝓘(ℝ, E) I (e.extend I).symm (e.extend I).target x)

    Manifold.metricInCoordinates_apply

    Let \(g\) be a smooth Riemannian metric on the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold \(M\) modeled through a model with corners \(I : H \to E\), and let \(e\) be an open partial homeomorphism from \(M\) to \(H\).     DefinitionThe \(C^k\) topology on smooth Riemannian metrics#✓fully proved

    Manifold.metricCkTopology

    Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold modelled on a real normed space \(E\) through a model with corners \(I\), and let \(k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\). For a chart \(\varphi\) of the maximal smooth atlas of \(M\), write \(T_\varphi \subseteq E\) for the target of the extended chart \(I \circ \varphi\). The (weak, compact-open) \(C^k\) topology on the space of smooth Riemannian metrics on \(M\) is the coarsest topology for which, for every chart \(\varphi\) of the maximal atlas and every natural number \(i \le k\), the map sending a metric \(g\) to the \(i\)-th iterated derivative within \(T_\varphi\) of the coordinate expression of \(g\) in \(\varphi\) is continuous, where these derivatives carry the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets of \(T_\varphi\).

    For \(k = \infty\) this is the \(C^\infty\) topology. Every element of the space is a smooth metric, whatever \(k\) is; \(k\) only indexes the topology.

    @[instance_reducible]
def metricCkTopology (I : ModelWithCorners ℝ E H) (M : Type*) [TopologicalSpace M]
    [ChartedSpace H M] [IsManifold I ∞ M] (k : ℕ∞) :
    TopologicalSpace (Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric I ∞ E
      (fun x : M ↦ TangentSpace I x)) :=
  ⨅ (e : ↥(IsManifold.maximalAtlas I ∞ M)) (i : ℕ) (_hh : (i : ℕ∞) ≤ k),
    TopologicalSpace.induced
      (fun g =>
        UniformOnFun.ofFun {K : Set E | IsCompact K ∧ K ⊆ (e.val.extend I).target}
          (iteratedFDerivWithin ℝ i (metricInCoordinates g e.val)
            (e.val.extend I).target))
      inferInstance

    (g : ContMDiffRiemannianMetric I ∞ E (fun x : M ↦ TangentSpace I x))

    TheoremConvergence in the \(C^k\) topology on smooth metrics#✓fully proved

    Manifold.tendsto_metricCkTopology_iff

    Let \(M\) be a smooth manifold modelled on a real normed space \(E\) through a model with corners \(I\), let \(k \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\), let \(l\) be a filter on a type \(\alpha\), and let \(g_a\) (\(a \in \alpha\)) and \(g_0\) be smooth Riemannian metrics on \(M\). For a chart \(\varphi\) of the maximal smooth atlas of \(M\), write \(T_\varphi \subseteq E\) for the target of the extended chart \(I \circ \varphi\). Then \(g_a\) tends to \(g_0\) along \(l\) in the \(C^k\) topology if and only if, for every chart \(\varphi\) of the maximal atlas, every natural number \(i \le k\) and every compact set \(K \subseteq T_\varphi\), the \(i\)-th iterated derivatives within \(T_\varphi\) of the coordinate expressions of \(g_a\) in \(\varphi\) converge uniformly on \(K\) along \(l\) to the \(i\)-th iterated derivative within \(T_\varphi\) of the coordinate expression of \(g_0\) in \(\varphi\).

    theorem tendsto_metricCkTopology_iff {I : ModelWithCorners ℝ E H} {M : Type*}
    [TopologicalSpace M] [ChartedSpace H M] [IsManifold I ∞ M] {k : ℕ∞}
    {α : Type*} {l : Filter α}
    {g : α → Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric I ∞ E (fun x : M ↦ TangentSpace I x)}
    {g₀ : Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric I ∞ E (fun x : M ↦ TangentSpace I x)} :
    Tendsto g l (@nhds _ (metricCkTopology I M k) g₀) ↔
      ∀ e ∈ IsManifold.maximalAtlas I ∞ M, ∀ i : ℕ, (i : ℕ∞) ≤ k →
        ∀ K : Set E, IsCompact K → K ⊆ (e.extend I).target →
          TendstoUniformlyOn
            (fun a => iteratedFDerivWithin ℝ i (metricInCoordinates (g a) e)
              (e.extend I).target)
            (iteratedFDerivWithin ℝ i (metricInCoordinates g₀ e) (e.extend I).target)
            l K
