    AbbreviationCenter of mass#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.UlamWanted.Ulam.centerOfMass

    The center of mass of a set \(K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d\) is the average \(\frac{1}{\operatorname{vol}(K)} \int_K x \, dx\), where \(dx\) is Lebesgue measure on \(\mathbb{R}^d\). By convention it is \(0\) when \(\operatorname{vol}(K)\) is \(0\) or infinite, or when \(x \mapsto x\) is not integrable on \(K\). For a compact convex body this is the usual center of mass.

    noncomputable abbrev centerOfMass (K : Set ℝᵈ) : ℝᵈ := ⨍ x in K, x

    MathlibExt.Geometry.UlamWanted.Ulam.CuttingHyperplaneAtLevel

    Let \(K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d\), let \(\xi \in \mathbb{R}^d\) be a unit vector and let \(\delta \in \mathbb{R}\). A cutting hyperplane of \(K\) in direction \(\xi\) at level \(\delta\) is a real number \(t\), standing for the hyperplane \(\{p \in \mathbb{R}^d : \xi \cdot p = t\}\), such that the volume of \(K \cap \{p \in \mathbb{R}^d : \xi \cdot p \le t\}\) equals \(\delta\). Here a set of infinite volume is assigned volume \(0\).

    structure CuttingHyperplaneAtLevel (K : Set ℝᵈ) (ξ : S d) (δ : ℝ) where
  /-- The translation of the hyperplane. -/
  t : ℝ
  vol : volume.real (K ∩ H_minus ξ t) = δ

    DefinitionFloating in equilibrium in a direction#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.UlamWanted.Ulam.FloatsInEquilibriumInDirectionAtLevel

    Let \(K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d\), let \(\xi \in \mathbb{R}^d\) be a unit vector and let \(\delta \in \mathbb{R}\). We say that \(K\) floats in equilibrium in direction \(\xi\) at level \(\delta\) if, for every cutting hyperplane \(\{p : \xi \cdot p = t\}\) of \(K\) in direction \(\xi\) at level \(\delta\), the center of mass of \(K\) minus the center of mass of \(K \cap \{p : \xi \cdot p \le t\}\) is a real multiple of \(\xi\) (possibly zero); that is, the line through the two centers of mass is parallel to \(\xi\) when they differ.

    def FloatsInEquilibriumInDirectionAtLevel (K : Set ℝᵈ) (ξ : S d) (δ : ℝ) : Prop :=
  ∀ H : CuttingHyperplaneAtLevel K ξ δ, H.lineThroughCentersOfMass ∈ ℝ ∙ ξ.val

    MathlibExt.Geometry.UlamWanted.Ulam.BodyOfRevolution

    A body of revolution in \(\mathbb{R}^d\) is given by real numbers \(R_1, R_2\) and a function \(f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) that vanishes outside the interval \([-R_1, R_2]\), is infinitely differentiable on the open interval \((-R_1, R_2)\) and is concave on \([-R_1, R_2]\). The dimension \(d\) is only a parameter; these data do not depend on it.

    structure BodyOfRevolution (d : ℕ) where
  /-- The left half-length of the interval; `f` is supported on `[-R₁, R₂]`. -/
  R₁ : ℝ
  /-- The right half-length of the interval; `f` is supported on `[-R₁, R₂]`. -/
  R₂ : ℝ
  /-- The smooth concave function `f : ℝ → ℝ` defining the body of revolution. -/
  f : ℝ → ℝ
  support : f.support ⊆ Set.Icc (-R₁) R₂
  smooth : ContDiffOn ℝ ∞ f (Set.Ioo (-R₁) R₂)

    DefinitionSolid of revolution#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.UlamWanted.Ulam.BodyOfRevolution.body

    Let \(d \ge 1\) and let a body of revolution in \(\mathbb{R}^d\) be given by \(R_1\), \(R_2\) and \(f\). Writing points of \(\mathbb{R}^d\) as \(x = (x_0, \dots, x_{d-1})\), its solid is the set of all \(x\) with \(x_0 \in [-R_1, R_2]\) and \(x_1^2 + \dots + x_{d-1}^2 \le f(x_0)^2\).

    def BodyOfRevolution.body [NeZero d] (K : BodyOfRevolution d) : Set ℝᵈ :=
  { x | x 0 ∈ Set.Icc (-K.R₁) K.R₂ ∧ ∑ i > 0, (x i) ^ 2 ≤ K.f (x 0) ^ 2 }

    DefinitionStrictly convex body#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.UlamWanted.Ulam.IsStrictlyConvexBody

    A set \(K \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d\) is a strictly convex body if it is strictly convex (for distinct \(x, y \in K\), every point of the open segment between \(x\) and \(y\) lies in the interior of \(K\)), compact, and has nonempty interior.

    def IsStrictlyConvexBody (K : Set ℝᵈ) : Prop :=
  StrictConvex ℝ K ∧ IsCompact K ∧ (interior K).Nonempty
