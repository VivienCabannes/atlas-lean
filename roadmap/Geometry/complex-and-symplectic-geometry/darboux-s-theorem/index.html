    DefinitionDarboux normal form#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.DarbouxWanted.Geometry.Darboux.IsDarbouxNormal

    Let \(n\) be a natural number and let \(e_0, \dots, e_{2n-1}\) be the standard basis of \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\). A continuous alternating 2-form \(\omega\) on \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\) is in Darboux normal form if for all \(i, j \in \{0, \dots, n - 1\}\) we have \(\omega(e_i, e_{n+j}) = 1\) when \(i = j\) and \(0\) otherwise, \(\omega(e_i, e_j) = 0\), and \(\omega(e_{n+i}, e_{n+j}) = 0\).

    By antisymmetry these conditions determine the form uniquely: it is the standard symplectic form \(\omega_0 = \sum_i dx_i \wedge dx_{n+i}\).

    def IsDarbouxNormal {n : ℕ} (α : E n [⋀^Fin 2]→L[ℝ] ℝ) : Prop :=
  (∀ i j : Fin n,
      α ![EuclideanSpace.single (idxP i) (1 : ℝ),
          EuclideanSpace.single (idxQ j) (1 : ℝ)]
        = if i = j then (1 : ℝ) else 0) ∧
  (∀ i j : Fin n,
      α ![EuclideanSpace.single (idxP i) (1 : ℝ),
          EuclideanSpace.single (idxP j) (1 : ℝ)] = 0) ∧
  (∀ i j : Fin n,
      α ![EuclideanSpace.single (idxQ i) (1 : ℝ),
          EuclideanSpace.single (idxQ j) (1 : ℝ)] = 0)
    abbrev E (n : ℕ) := EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (2 * n))

    DefinitionSymplectic 2-form fields#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.DarbouxWanted.Geometry.Darboux.IsSymplecticOn

    Let \(n\) be a natural number and \(U\) a subset of \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\). A field \(\alpha\) of continuous alternating 2-forms on \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\) is symplectic on \(U\) if \(\alpha\) is \(C^\infty\) on \(U\), its exterior derivative \(d\alpha\) vanishes at every point of \(U\), and \(\alpha\) is non-degenerate at every point of \(U\): for every \(x \in U\) and every nonzero \(v \in \mathbb{R}^{2n}\) there is \(w \in \mathbb{R}^{2n}\) with \(\alpha(x)(v, w) \neq 0\).

    def IsSymplecticOn {n : ℕ}
    (α : E n → E n [⋀^Fin 2]→L[ℝ] ℝ) (U : Set (E n)) : Prop :=
  ContDiffOn ℝ ∞ α U ∧
  (∀ x ∈ U, extDeriv α x = 0) ∧
  (∀ x ∈ U, ∀ v : E n, v ≠ 0 → ∃ w : E n, α x ![v, w] ≠ 0)
    abbrev E (n : ℕ) := EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (2 * n))

    TheoremDarboux's theorem#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.Geometry.DarbouxWanted.Geometry.Darboux.darboux

    Let \(n\) be a natural number, let \(U\) be an open subset of the model space \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\), and let \(\alpha\) be a field of continuous alternating 2-forms on \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\) that is symplectic on \(U\). Then for every \(x \in U\) there is an open partial homeomorphism \(\varphi\) of \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\) whose source contains \(x\) and is contained in \(U\), such that \(\varphi\) is \(C^\infty\) on its source, \(\varphi^{-1}\) is \(C^\infty\) on its target, and for every \(z\) in the target the pullback of \(\alpha(\varphi^{-1}(z))\) by the derivative \(D\varphi^{-1}(z)\), namely \((v, w) \mapsto \alpha(\varphi^{-1}(z))(D\varphi^{-1}(z)\, v, D\varphi^{-1}(z)\, w)\), is in Darboux normal form.

    public theorem_wanted darboux {n : ℕ} {U : Set (E n)} (_hU : IsOpen U)
    (α : E n → E n [⋀^Fin 2]→L[ℝ] ℝ) (_hα : IsSymplecticOn α U)
    {x : E n} (_hx : x ∈ U) :
    ∃ φ : OpenPartialHomeomorph (E n) (E n),
      x ∈ φ.source ∧ φ.source ⊆ U ∧
      ContDiffOn ℝ ∞ (φ : E n → E n) φ.source ∧
      ContDiffOn ℝ ∞ (φ.symm : E n → E n) φ.target ∧
      ∀ z ∈ φ.target,
        IsDarbouxNormal
          ((α (φ.symm z)).compContinuousLinearMap
            (fderiv ℝ (φ.symm : E n → E n) z))
    abbrev E (n : ℕ) := EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin (2 * n))
