    DefinitionDensity of a packing in a ball#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.SpherePackingDensityFourWanted.finiteRadiusDensity

    For a packing \(P\) of congruent balls in \(\mathbb{R}^4\), a point \(p \in \mathbb{R}^4\) and a radius \(R > 0\), the density of \(P\) in the open ball \(B(p, R)\) of radius \(R\) about \(p\) is \(\operatorname{vol}(U \cap B(p, R)) / \operatorname{vol}(B(p, R))\), where \(U\) is the union of the closed balls of \(P\) and \(\operatorname{vol}\) is Lebesgue measure; the quotient is taken in \([0, \infty]\).

    DefinitionUpper density of a packing#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.SpherePackingDensityFourWanted.upperDensity

    For a packing \(P\) of congruent balls in \(\mathbb{R}^4\), let \(U_P\) be the union of the closed balls of the packing, and for \(p \in \mathbb{R}^4\) and \(R > 0\) let \(B(p, R)\) be the open ball of radius \(R\) about \(p\). The upper density of \(P\) is the extended nonnegative real number

    \[\limsup_{R \to \infty} \sup_{p \in \mathbb{R}^4} \frac{\operatorname{vol}(U_P \cap B(p, R))}{\operatorname{vol}(B(p, R))},\]

    where \(\operatorname{vol}\) is Lebesgue measure and the limit superior is taken as the positive radius \(R\) tends to infinity.

    def upperDensity (P : Packing) : ENNReal :=
  Filter.limsup (centeredRadiusDensity P) Filter.atTop

    DefinitionSphere-packing density of \(\mathbb{R}^4\)#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.SpherePackingDensityFourWanted.spherePackingDensity4

    The sphere-packing density \(\Delta_4 \in [0, \infty]\) is the supremum, over all packings \(P\) of congruent balls in \(\mathbb{R}^4\), of the upper density of \(P\). Here the upper density of \(P\) is the limit superior, as the real radius \(R > 0\) tends to infinity, of the supremum over all points \(p \in \mathbb{R}^4\) of the ratio of the volume of the part of the open ball \(B(p, R)\) covered by \(P\) to the volume of \(B(p, R)\).

    def spherePackingDensity4 : ENNReal := ⨆ P : Packing, upperDensity P

    DefinitionPeriodic packings#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.SpherePackingDensityFourWanted.Packing.IsPeriodic

    A packing \(P\) of congruent balls in \(\mathbb{R}^4\) is periodic if there are a basis \(b_1, b_2, b_3, b_4\) of \(\mathbb{R}^4\) and a finite set \(F \subseteq \mathbb{R}^4\) such that the set of centers of \(P\) equals \(\{c + w : c \in F, w \in \Lambda\}\), where \(\Lambda\) is the set of integer linear combinations of \(b_1, b_2, b_3, b_4\).

    def Packing.IsPeriodic (P : Packing) : Prop :=
  ∃ (b : Module.Basis (Fin 4) ℝ Space) (offsets : Finset Space),
    P.centers = ⋃ c ∈ offsets,
      (fun w : Space => c + w) '' (Submodule.span ℤ (Set.range b) : Set Space)

    For a packing \(Q\) of congruent balls in \(\mathbb{R}^4\), let its upper density \(\overline{\delta}(Q)\) be the limit superior, as \(R \to \infty\), of the supremum over all points \(p\) of the fraction of the volume of the open ball \(B(p, R)\) covered by the closed balls of \(Q\), and let the sphere packing density \(\Delta_4\) of \(\mathbb{R}^4\) be the supremum of \(\overline{\delta}(Q)\) over all such packings \(Q\).
