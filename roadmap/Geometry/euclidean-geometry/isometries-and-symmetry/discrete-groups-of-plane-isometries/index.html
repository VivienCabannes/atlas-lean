    DefinitionTranslation homomorphism#✓fully proved

    DiscreteGroups.translationHom

    The group homomorphism from the additive group of the Euclidean plane \(\mathbb{R}^2\) (written multiplicatively) to the group of affine isometries of the plane that sends a vector \(v\) to translation by \(v\), the isometry \(x \mapsto v + x\).

    noncomputable def translationHom : Multiplicative E2 →* M₂ where
  toFun v := AffineIsometryEquiv.constVAdd ℝ E2 (Multiplicative.toAdd v)
  map_one' := by
    apply AffineIsometryEquiv.ext
    intro x
    change (0 : E2) + x = x
    exact zero_add x
  map_mul' v w := by
    apply AffineIsometryEquiv.ext
    intro x
    change (Multiplicative.toAdd v + Multiplicative.toAdd w) + x =
      Multiplicative.toAdd v + (Multiplicative.toAdd w + x)
    exact add_assoc _ _ _
    abbrev E2 := EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 2)

    DefinitionSeparation criterion for groups of plane isometries#✓fully proved

    DiscreteGroups.IsDiscrete

    A group \(G\) of affine isometries of the Euclidean plane satisfies the separation criterion if there is \(\varepsilon > 0\) such that every element \(f \ne \mathrm{id}\) of \(G\) whose linear part is the identity satisfies \(\|f(0)\| \ge \varepsilon\), and every element \(f\) of \(G\) whose linear part \(A\) is not the identity satisfies \(\|A - I\| \ge \varepsilon\), where \(I\) is the identity map and the norm is the operator norm.

    Its linear-part condition measures operator-norm separation from the identity, rather than the numerical rotation angle.

    def IsDiscrete (G : Subgroup M₂) : Prop :=
  ∃ ε > 0,
    (∀ f ∈ G, linearPart f = LinearIsometryEquiv.refl ℝ E2 → f ≠ 1 → ε ≤ ‖f 0‖) ∧
    (∀ f ∈ G, linearPart f ≠ LinearIsometryEquiv.refl ℝ E2 →
      ε ≤ ‖(linearPart f).toLinearIsometry.toContinuousLinearMap -
        ContinuousLinearMap.id ℝ E2‖)
    abbrev M₂ := E2 ≃ᵃⁱ[ℝ] E2

    DiscreteGroups.discrete_subgroup_R2_classification

    Every discrete additive subgroup G of ℝ² is trivial, cyclic (generated by a single nonzero vector), or a rank-two lattice (generated by two ℝ-linearly independent vectors).

    theorem discrete_subgroup_R2_classification
    (G : AddSubgroup E2) [DiscreteTopology G] :
    G = ⊥ ∨
    (∃ α : E2, α ≠ 0 ∧ G = AddSubgroup.zmultiples α) ∨
    (∃ a b : E2, LinearIndependent ℝ ![a, b] ∧
      G = AddSubgroup.zmultiples a ⊔ AddSubgroup.zmultiples b)

    DiscreteGroups.translation_lattice_classification

    Let \(G\) be a group of affine isometries of the Euclidean plane \(\mathbb{R}^2\), and suppose there is \(\varepsilon > 0\) such that every non-identity translation in \(G\) moves the origin by at least \(\varepsilon\), and every element of \(G\) whose linear part \(A\) is not the identity satisfies \(\|A - I\| \ge \varepsilon\) in operator norm.
