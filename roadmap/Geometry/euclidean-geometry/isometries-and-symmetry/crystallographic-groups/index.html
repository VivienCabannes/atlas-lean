    DefinitionOrientation-preserving affine equivalence#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.SpaceGroupsWanted.Geometry.SpaceGroupsProblem.AffOPEquivalent

    Two subgroups \(G_1\) and \(G_2\) of the group of affine isometries of the Euclidean space \(\mathbb{R}^d\) are orientation-preserving affinely equivalent if there is an affine bijection \(\varphi\) of \(\mathbb{R}^d\) whose linear part has positive determinant such that \(\{\varphi g \varphi^{-1} : g \in G_1\} = G_2\) as sets of affine maps of \(\mathbb{R}^d\).

    def AffOPEquivalent {d : ℕ} (G₁ G₂ : Subgroup (EuclideanIsom d)) : Prop :=
  ∃ φ : AffineGroup d, 0 < (φ.linear.det : ℝ) ∧
    {h : AffineGroup d | ∃ g ∈ G₁, h = φ * g.toAffineEquiv * φ⁻¹} =
    {h : AffineGroup d | ∃ g ∈ G₂, h = g.toAffineEquiv}

    MathlibExt.Geometry.SpaceGroupsWanted.Geometry.SpaceGroupsProblem.crystallographicCountOP

    For a natural number \(d\), this is the number, in \(\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\), of classes of \(d\)-dimensional crystallographic groups under orientation-preserving affine equivalence. Here a crystallographic group is a subgroup \(G\) of the group of affine isometries of \(\mathbb{R}^d\) that is discrete (for every point \(x\) and every \(\varepsilon > 0\) only finitely many \(g \in G\) satisfy \(\operatorname{dist}(g x, x) \le \varepsilon\)) and contains translations by \(d\) linearly independent vectors.

    MathlibExt.Geometry.WallpaperGroupsWanted.Geometry.WallpaperGroupsProblem.there_are_17_wallpaper_groups

    Call a group \(G\) of isometries of the Euclidean plane \(\mathbb{R}^2\) crystallographic if it is discrete, meaning that for every point \(x\) and every \(\varepsilon > 0\) only finitely many \(g \in G\) satisfy \(\operatorname{dist}(g x, x) \le \varepsilon\), and it contains translations by two linearly independent vectors. Call two such groups \(G_1\) and \(G_2\) affinely equivalent if some invertible affine map \(\varphi\) of \(\mathbb{R}^2\) satisfies \(\{\varphi g \varphi^{-1} : g \in G_1\} = G_2\).
