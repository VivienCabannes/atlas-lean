    Let \(u, v \in \mathbb{Z}^2\) with \(u_0 v_1 - u_1 v_0 \neq 0\), and let \(T \subset \mathbb{R}^2\) be the convex hull of \(0\), \(u\) and \(v\). Let \(I\) be the number of points of \(\mathbb{Z}^2\) in the interior of \(T\) and \(B\) the number of points of \(\mathbb{Z}^2\) on the boundary of \(T\). Then the area (two-dimensional Lebesgue measure) of \(T\) equals \(I + B/2 - 1\).

    MetaMathlibExt.pick_convex_lattice_polygon

    Let \(V \subset \mathbb{Z}^2\) be a finite set of lattice points, let \(P \subset \mathbb{R}^2\) be the convex hull of \(V\), and suppose that \(P\) has nonempty interior. Let \(I\) be the number of points of \(\mathbb{Z}^2\) in the interior of \(P\) and \(B\) the number of points of \(\mathbb{Z}^2\) on the boundary of \(P\). Then the area (two-dimensional Lebesgue measure) of \(P\) equals \(I + B/2 - 1\).

    Proof: The triangle case counts lattice points in a half-open fundamental parallelogram. A crossing diagonal then cuts larger polygons into two smaller full-dimensional lattice polygons, allowing strong induction on the vertex set.

    DefinitionSimple polygon#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Geometry.PicksTheoremWanted.Geometry.PicksTheorem.IsSimple

    A polygon in \(\mathbb{R}^2\) with vertices \(p_0, \dots, p_{n-1}\) (indices taken mod \(n\)) and edges the segments \(e_i = [p_i, p_{i+1}]\) is simple when consecutive vertices are distinct (\(p_i \ne p_{i+1}\) for all \(i\)), any two edges \(e_i\) and \(e_j\) with \(i \ne j\), \(j \not\equiv i + 1\) and \(i \not\equiv j + 1 \pmod n\) are disjoint, and every two consecutive edges meet only in their common vertex: \(e_i \cap e_{i+1} = \{p_{i+1}\}\) for all \(i\).

    def IsSimple {n : ℕ} (poly : Polygon (ℝ × ℝ) n) : Prop :=
  poly.HasNondegenerateEdges ∧
  (∀ i j : Fin n, i ≠ j → ¬ Adjacent i j →
      Disjoint (poly.edgeSet ℝ i) (poly.edgeSet ℝ j)) ∧
  (∀ i : Fin n, poly.edgeSet ℝ i ∩ poly.edgeSet ℝ (finRotate n i)
      = {poly (finRotate n i)})
    def Adjacent {n : ℕ} (i j : Fin n) : Prop :=

    Let \(n \ge 3\) and let \(v_0, \dots, v_{n-1} \in \mathbb{Z}^2\) be integer points such that the lattice polygon \(P\) in \(\mathbb{R}^2\) with vertices \(v_0, \dots, v_{n-1}\) is simple.
