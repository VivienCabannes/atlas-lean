    Cslib.CodingTheory.CRC.encode

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(g\) be a polynomial over \(F\) (the generator), and let \(r\) be the degree of \(g\) (with \(r = 0\) when \(g = 0\)). The CRC encoding of a polynomial \(m\) over \(F\) (the message) is \(m X^r - (m X^r \bmod g)\): the message shifted up by \(r\) positions, minus the remainder of the shifted message modulo \(g\).

    noncomputable def encode (g m : F[X]) : F[X] := m * X ^ g.natDegree - m * X ^ g.natDegree % g

    TheoremCRC encoding in characteristic 2#✓fully proved

    Cslib.CodingTheory.CRC.encode_eq_add

    Let \(F\) be a field of characteristic 2 (for example \(\mathbb{F}_2\)), let \(g\) and \(m\) be polynomials over \(F\), and let \(r\) be the degree of \(g\) (with \(r = 0\) when \(g = 0\)). Then the CRC encoding of \(m\) with generator \(g\) equals \(m X^r + (m X^r \bmod g)\): the encoder appends the remainder to the shifted message.

    theorem encode_eq_add [CharP F 2] (g m : F[X]) :
    encode g m = m * X ^ g.natDegree + m * X ^ g.natDegree % g
    noncomputable def encode (g m : F[X]) : F[X] := m * X ^ g.natDegree - m * X ^ g.natDegree % g

    DefinitionBurst errors#✓fully proved

    Cslib.CodingTheory.CRC.IsBurstLE

    Let \(F\) be a field and \(L\) a natural number. A polynomial \(e\) over \(F\) is a burst of length at most \(L\) when \(e = X^i b\) for some natural number \(i\) and some polynomial \(b\) over \(F\) with \(b(0) \ne 0\) and \(\deg b < L\). Then the nonzero coefficients of \(e\) lie in positions \(i, \dots, i + L - 1\), the first one nonzero.

    def IsBurstLE (e : F[X]) (L : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ i : ℕ, ∃ b : F[X], e = X ^ i * b ∧ b.coeff 0 ≠ 0 ∧ b.natDegree < L

    Cslib.CodingTheory.CRC.not_dvd_of_isBurstLE

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(g\) be a polynomial over \(F\) that is not divisible by \(X\), and let \(L\) be a natural number with \(L \le \deg g\). If a polynomial \(e\) over \(F\) is a burst of length at most \(L\), then \(g\) does not divide \(e\).

    Write \(e = X^i b\) with \(b(0) \ne 0\) and \(\deg b < L\). Since \(X \nmid g\), \(g\) is coprime to \(X^i\), so \(g \mid X^i b\) would force \(g \mid b\), impossible for a nonzero \(b\) of smaller degree.

    TheoremCRC error-detection guarantee#✓fully proved

    Cslib.CodingTheory.CRC.detects

    Let \(F\) be a field, let \(g\) be a polynomial over \(F\) with at least two nonzero coefficients that is not divisible by \(X\), let \(m\) be a polynomial over \(F\), and let \(c\) be the CRC encoding of \(m\) with generator \(g\). Then \(g\) does not divide \(c + aX^i\) for any nonzero \(a \in F\) and any natural number \(i\), and \(g\) does not divide \(c + e\) for any polynomial \(e\) over \(F\) that is a burst of length at most \(\deg g\). That is, the check detects every single-symbol error and every burst of length at most \(\deg g\) in every codeword.

    Given \(X \nmid g\), having at least two terms is the same as \(\deg g \ge 1\), and a single-symbol error is a burst of length 1.

    theorem detects {g : F[X]} (hg : 2 ≤ g.support.card) (hX : ¬X ∣ g) (m : F[X]) :
    (∀ a : F, a ≠ 0 → ∀ i, ¬g ∣ encode g m + C a * X ^ i) ∧
      ∀ e, IsBurstLE e g.natDegree → ¬g ∣ encode g m + e
    noncomputable def encode (g m : F[X]) : F[X] := m * X ^ g.natDegree - m * X ^ g.natDegree % g
