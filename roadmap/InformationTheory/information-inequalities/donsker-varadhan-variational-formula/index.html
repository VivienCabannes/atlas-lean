    Donsker–Varadhan variational formula

    Relative entropy is the Legendre transform of the logarithmic moment generating functional: for probability measures \(\mu\) and \(\nu\), \(\mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) = \sup_f \left(\int f\,d\mu - \log \int e^f\,d\nu\right)\) over bounded measurable \(f\). Dually, the Gibbs variational principle \(\log \int e^f\,d\nu = \max_\mu \left(\int f\,d\mu - \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu)\right)\) is attained at the tilted measure \(e^f\nu / \int e^f\,d\nu\). The inequality half is the change-of-measure step behind PAC-Bayes bounds; the formula is the starting point of Sanov's theorem.

    TheoremGibbs variational principle#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.DonskerVaradhan.isGreatest_integral_sub_klDiv

    For a probability measure \(\nu\) and a bounded measurable function \(f\),

    \[\log \int e^{f} \, d\nu = \max \Bigl\{ \int f \, d\mu - \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) \;:\; \mu \text{ a probability measure}, \ \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) < \infty \Bigr\},\]

    and the maximum is attained at the tilted (Gibbs) measure \(d\nu_f = e^{f} \, d\nu / \int e^{f} d\nu\), for which \(\mathrm{KL}(\nu_f \,\|\, \nu) = \int f \, d\nu_f - \log \int e^{f} d\nu\).

    This is the dual of the Donsker–Varadhan formula: logarithmic moment generating functionals and relative entropy are Legendre transforms of each other. In statistical mechanics it says that the free energy is the minimum over states of energy minus entropy, attained at the Gibbs state.

    theorem isGreatest_integral_sub_klDiv [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] (hf : Measurable f) {C : ℝ}
    (hC : ∀ x, |f x| ≤ C) :
    IsGreatest {r : ℝ | ∃ μ : Measure α, IsProbabilityMeasure μ ∧ klDiv μ ν ≠ ⊤ ∧
        r = ∫ x, f x ∂μ - (klDiv μ ν).toReal}
      (log (∫ x, exp (f x) ∂ν))

    TheoremDonsker–Varadhan variational formula#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.DonskerVaradhan.klDiv_eq_iSup

    For probability measures \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) on a measurable space,

    \[\mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) = \sup_f \Bigl( \int f \, d\mu - \log \int e^{f} \, d\nu \Bigr)^{+},\]

    the supremum being over bounded measurable \(f\), with \(\mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu) = \infty\) unless \(\mu \ll \nu\) with integrable log-likelihood ratio. (The positive part is harmless: \(f = 0\) gives the value \(0\).)

    The inequality \(\ge\) is the Donsker–Varadhan inequality. For \(\le\): if \(\mu \not\ll \nu\), the functions \(t \mathbf{1}_A\) with \(\nu(A) = 0 < \mu(A)\) make the supremum infinite; if \(\mu \ll \nu\) with density \(\rho\), the clamped log-densities \(f_k = \max(-k, \min(\log \rho, k))\) satisfy, by \(\log z \le z - 1\), \(\int f_k \, d\mu - \log \int e^{f_k} d\nu \ge \int (\rho f_k - e^{f_k} + 1) \, d\nu\), and Fatou's lemma gives \(\liminf_k\) of the right side \(\ge \int (\rho \log \rho - \rho + 1) \, d\nu = \mathrm{KL}(\mu \,\|\, \nu)\).

    Relative entropy is thus the Legendre transform of the functional \(f \mapsto \log \int e^f d\nu\); this is the form in which it enters the upper bound of Sanov's theorem, and its inequality half is the change-of-measure step of PAC-Bayes bounds.

    theorem klDiv_eq_iSup {α : Type*} {mα : MeasurableSpace α} (μ ν : Measure α)
    [IsProbabilityMeasure μ] [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] :
    klDiv μ ν = ⨆ (f : α → ℝ) (_ : Measurable f ∧ ∃ C, ∀ x, |f x| ≤ C),
      ENNReal.ofReal (∫ x, f x ∂μ - log (∫ x, exp (f x) ∂ν))
