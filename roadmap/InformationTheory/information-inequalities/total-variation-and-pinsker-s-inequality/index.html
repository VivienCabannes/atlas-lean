    Total variation and Pinsker's inequality

    For probability measures on an arbitrary measurable space, the total variation distance \(\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu) = \sup_B |\mu(B) - \nu(B)|\) is attained at a Hahn set, equals half of the total variation norm \(\|\mu - \nu\|\), and determines the least total error \(1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)\) of a test between \(\mu\) and \(\nu\). Pinsker's inequality \(2\,\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)^2 \le D(\mu \,\|\, \nu)\) holds with no positivity or absolute-continuity hypothesis; its proof applies the data-processing inequality for the Kullback–Leibler divergence to the indicator of a Hahn set and reduces to two Bernoulli laws.

    TheoremAn event's probabilities differ by at most the total variation#✓fully proved

    Let \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) be probability measures on a measurable space and \(B\) a measurable set. Then \(|\mu(B) - \nu(B)| \le \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)\), where \(\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu) = \sup_B |\mu(B) - \nu(B)|\).

    theorem abs_sub_le_totalVariationDist [IsProbabilityMeasure μ] [IsProbabilityMeasure ν]

    TheoremCommon part of two probability measures#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.MeasurePinsker.exists_le_le_measure_univ_eq

    For probability measures \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) on a measurable space there is a measure \(m\) with \(m \le \mu\), \(m \le \nu\) and

    \[ m(\Omega) = 1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu), \]

    namely \(m = \mu|_s + \nu|_{s^c}\) for a Hahn set \(s\). This is the overlap \(\mu \wedge \nu\), whose density with respect to \(\mu + \nu\) is the minimum of the two densities, so \(\int \min(p, q) = 1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)\).

    theorem exists_le_le_measure_univ_eq (μ ν : Measure Ω) [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] :
    ∃ m : Measure Ω, m ≤ μ ∧ m ≤ ν ∧ m Set.univ = ENNReal.ofReal (1 - totalVariationDist μ ν)

    TheoremPinsker's inequality for the total variation norm#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.MeasurePinsker.ofReal_tvDist_sq_div_two_le_klDiv

    For probability measures \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) on a measurable space,

    \[ \frac{\|\mu - \nu\|^2}{2} \le D(\mu \,\|\, \nu), \]

    where \(\|\mu - \nu\|\) is the total variation norm (Mathlib's MeasureTheory.tvDist, with values in \([0, 2]\)) and \(D\) the Kullback–Leibler divergence with the natural logarithm. This is Pinsker's inequality rewritten with \(\|\mu - \nu\| = 2\,\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)\).

    theorem ofReal_tvDist_sq_div_two_le_klDiv (μ ν : Measure Ω) [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] :
    ENNReal.ofReal (tvDist μ ν ^ 2 / 2) ≤ InformationTheory.klDiv μ ν

    TheoremPinsker's inequality for probability measures#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.MeasurePinsker.ofReal_two_mul_totalVariationDist_sq_le_klDiv

    Let \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) be probability measures on an arbitrary measurable space. Then

    \[ 2\,\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)^2 \le D(\mu \,\|\, \nu), \]

    where \(\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu) = \sup_B |\mu(B) - \nu(B)|\) and \(D\) is the Kullback–Leibler divergence (Mathlib's klDiv, natural logarithm, valued in \([0, \infty]\)). No positivity, finiteness or absolute-continuity hypothesis is needed; \(D(\mu \,\|\, \nu) = \infty\) when \(\mu\) is not absolutely continuous with respect to \(\nu\).

    The proof follows Polyanskiy and Wu: the data-processing inequality for \(D\) applied to the indicator of a Hahn set \(s\) gives \(D(\mu \,\|\, \nu) \ge D(\mathrm{Ber}(\mu(s)) \,\|\, \mathrm{Ber}(\nu(s)))\), and the binary Pinsker inequality bounds the right side below by \(2 (\nu(s) - \mu(s))^2 = 2\,\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)^2\).

    theorem ofReal_two_mul_totalVariationDist_sq_le_klDiv (μ ν : Measure Ω) [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] :
    ENNReal.ofReal (2 * totalVariationDist μ ν ^ 2) ≤ InformationTheory.klDiv μ ν

    TheoremTwo-point testing bound#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.MeasurePinsker.one_sub_totalVariationDist_le_add_compl

    Let \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) be probability measures on a measurable space. For every measurable event \(A\) (the test that decides for \(\nu\) on \(A\) and for \(\mu\) on \(A^c\)), the sum of its two error probabilities satisfies

    \[ \mu(A) + \nu(A^c) \ge 1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu). \]

    Together with its attainment, the least total error of a test between \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) is exactly \(1 - \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu)\).

    theorem one_sub_totalVariationDist_le_add_compl [IsProbabilityMeasure μ] [IsProbabilityMeasure ν]
    {A : Set Ω} (hA : MeasurableSet A) :
    1 - totalVariationDist μ ν ≤ μ.real A + ν.real Aᶜ

    TheoremTotal variation is attained at a Hahn set#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.MeasurePinsker.totalVariationDist_eq_of_isHahnDecomposition

    Let \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) be probability measures on a measurable space and let \(s\) be a Hahn set for \((\mu, \nu)\): \(s\) is measurable, \(\mu(t \cap s) \le \nu(t \cap s)\) and \(\nu(t \cap s^c) \le \mu(t \cap s^c)\) for every set \(t\). Then

    \[ \mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu) = \sup_B |\mu(B) - \nu(B)| = \nu(s) - \mu(s). \]

    A Hahn set exists for any two finite measures, so the supremum over events is always attained.

    theorem totalVariationDist_eq_of_isHahnDecomposition [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] {s : Set Ω} (h : IsHahnDecomposition μ ν s) :
    totalVariationDist μ ν = ν.real s - μ.real s
    noncomputable def totalVariationDist {Ω : Type*} [MeasurableSpace Ω]
    (μ ν : Measure Ω) [IsFiniteMeasure μ] [IsFiniteMeasure ν] : ℝ :=
  ⨆ s : {s : Set Ω // MeasurableSet s}, |(μ s.1).toReal - (ν s.1).toReal|

    TheoremTotal variation norm is twice the supremum over events#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.InformationTheory.MeasurePinsker.tvDist_eq_two_mul_totalVariationDist

    For probability measures \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) on a measurable space, the total variation norm \(\|\mu - \nu\|\) (Mathlib's MeasureTheory.tvDist, with values in \([0, 2]\)) satisfies

    \[ \|\mu - \nu\| = 2 \sup_B |\mu(B) - \nu(B)| = 2\,\mathrm{TV}(\mu, \nu). \]

    This identifies two conventions for the total variation distance: Mathlib's MeasureTheory.tvDist and the repository's supremum over events, which is the convention of Polyanskiy and Wu, \(\mathrm{TV}(P, Q) = \tfrac12 \int |dP - dQ|\). A third convention, the repository's MarkovMixing.tvDist \(= \tfrac12 \sum_y |\mu(y) - \nu(y)|\) on a finite set, has the same short name as Mathlib's but equals \(\mathrm{TV}\), half of Mathlib's tvDist.

    theorem tvDist_eq_two_mul_totalVariationDist (μ ν : Measure Ω) [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    [IsProbabilityMeasure ν] :
    tvDist μ ν = 2 * totalVariationDist μ ν
    noncomputable def totalVariationDist {Ω : Type*} [MeasurableSpace Ω]
    (μ ν : Measure Ω) [IsFiniteMeasure μ] [IsFiniteMeasure ν] : ℝ :=
  ⨆ s : {s : Set Ω // MeasurableSet s}, |(μ s.1).toReal - (ν s.1).toReal|
