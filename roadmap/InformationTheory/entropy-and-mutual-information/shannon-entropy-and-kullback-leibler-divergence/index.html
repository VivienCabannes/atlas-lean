    Shannon entropy and Kullback–Leibler divergence

    DefinitionShannon entropy#✓fully proved

    InformationTheory.shannonEntropy

    The Shannon entropy in bits of a probability mass function \(p\) on a finite type \(\alpha\) is \(H(p) = -\sum_{a \in \alpha} p(a) \log_2 p(a)\), where a term with \(p(a) = 0\) contributes \(0\).

    noncomputable def shannonEntropy {α : Type*} [Fintype α] (p : PMF α) : ℝ :=
  (∑ a, Real.negMulLog (p a).toReal) / Real.log 2

    TheoremKullback–Leibler divergence on a finite space#✓fully proved

    InformationTheory.toReal_klDiv_eq_sum

    Let \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) be finite measures on a finite type \(\alpha\) in which every singleton is measurable, with \(\mu \ll \nu\) and \(\mu(\alpha) = \nu(\alpha)\). Then the Kullback–Leibler divergence \(\mathrm{KL}(\mu \parallel \nu)\), taken as a real number, equals \(\sum_{a \in \alpha} \mu(\{a\}) \log (\mu(\{a\}) / \nu(\{a\}))\), where \(\log\) is the natural logarithm and a term with \(\mu(\{a\}) = 0\) is \(0\).

    theorem toReal_klDiv_eq_sum [Fintype α] [MeasurableSpace α] [MeasurableSingletonClass α]
    {μ ν : Measure α} [IsFiniteMeasure μ] [IsFiniteMeasure ν] (hμν : μ ≪ ν)
    (huniv : μ Set.univ = ν Set.univ) :
    (klDiv μ ν).toReal = ∑ a, μ.real {a} * log (μ.real {a} / ν.real {a})

    TheoremKullback–Leibler divergence of finite probability laws#✓fully proved

    InformationTheory.toReal_klDiv_toMeasure

    Let \(p\) and \(q\) be probability mass functions on a finite type \(\alpha\) in which every singleton is measurable, such that \(p(a) = 0\) whenever \(q(a) = 0\), and let \(P\) and \(Q\) be the measures they define. Then the Kullback–Leibler divergence \(\mathrm{KL}(P \parallel Q)\), taken as a real number, equals \(\sum_{a \in \alpha} p(a) \log (p(a) / q(a))\), where \(\log\) is the natural logarithm and a term with \(p(a) = 0\) is \(0\).

    theorem toReal_klDiv_toMeasure [Fintype α] [MeasurableSpace α] [MeasurableSingletonClass α]
    {p q : PMF α} (hpq : ∀ a, q a = 0 → p a = 0) :
    (klDiv p.toMeasure q.toMeasure).toReal =
      ∑ a, (p a).toReal * log ((p a).toReal / (q a).toReal)

    DefinitionSurprisal#✓fully proved

    InformationTheory.surprisal

    The surprisal (information content) in bits of an outcome of probability \(q\) is \(-\log_2 q\), with value \(\infty\) when \(q = 0\). Formally \(q\) ranges over the extended nonnegative reals \([0, \infty]\): the surprisal of \(0\) is \(\infty\), the surprisal of \(q \in (0, \infty)\) is \(\max(0, -\log_2 q)\), and the surprisal of \(\infty\) is \(0\).

    noncomputable def surprisal (q : ℝ≥0∞) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  if q = 0 then ∞ else ENNReal.ofReal (-logb 2 q.toReal)

    DefinitionCross-entropy#✓fully proved

    InformationTheory.crossEntropy

    For probability mass functions \(p\) and \(q\) on a finite alphabet, the cross-entropy of \(q\) relative to \(p\) is \(H(p, q) = \sum_a p(a) \cdot (-\log_2 q(a))\) in bits, a value in \([0, \infty]\). Here \(-\log_2 q(a)\) is the surprisal of \(a\) under \(q\), which is \(\infty\) when \(q(a) = 0\), and a term with \(p(a) = 0\) contributes \(0\). So \(H(p, q)\) is \(\infty\) exactly when \(p\) charges an outcome that \(q\) rules out.

    noncomputable def crossEntropy [Fintype α] (p q : PMF α) : ℝ≥0∞ := ∑ a, p a * surprisal (q a)
    noncomputable def surprisal (q : ℝ≥0∞) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
  if q = 0 then ∞ else ENNReal.ofReal (-logb 2 q.toReal)

    TheoremCross-entropy is entropy plus divergence#✓fully proved

    InformationTheory.crossEntropy_eq_shannonEntropy_add_klDiv

    For probability mass functions \(p\) and \(q\) on a finite alphabet, the cross-entropy \(H(p, q)\) in bits equals \(H(p) + D(p \parallel q) / \ln 2\), where \(H(p)\) is the Shannon entropy of \(p\) in bits and \(D(p \parallel q)\) is the Kullback–Leibler divergence of \(p\) from \(q\) with the natural logarithm. The identity holds in \([0, \infty]\) with no support hypothesis: when \(p\) charges an outcome that \(q\) rules out, both sides are \(\infty\).

    theorem crossEntropy_eq_shannonEntropy_add_klDiv [Fintype α] [MeasurableSpace α]
    [MeasurableSingletonClass α] (p q : PMF α) :
    crossEntropy p q = ENNReal.ofReal (shannonEntropy p) +
      klDiv p.toMeasure q.toMeasure / ENNReal.ofReal (log 2)
