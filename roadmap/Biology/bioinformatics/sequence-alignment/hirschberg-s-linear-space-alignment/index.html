    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.splitScore_le_nwScore_ys

    Let \(\sigma\) be an integer-valued substitution score on an alphabet, let \(g\) be an integer gap score, and write \(\mathrm{NW}(x, y)\) for the optimal Needleman–Wunsch global alignment score of sequences \(x\) and \(y\) under \(\sigma\) and \(g\). For sequences \(x\), \(y\) and natural numbers \(k\), \(m\), let \(x_{\lt k}\) be the first \(k\) letters of \(x\) (all of \(x\) if \(k\) is at least its length) and \(x_{\ge k}\) the remaining letters, and define \(y_{\lt m}\) and \(y_{\ge m}\) likewise. Then \(\mathrm{NW}(x_{\lt k}, y_{\lt m}) + \mathrm{NW}(x_{\ge k}, y_{\ge m}) \le \mathrm{NW}(x, y)\).

    The bound holds because optimal alignments of the two halves concatenate to an alignment of \(x\) and \(y\).

    theorem splitScore_le_nwScore_ys (xs ys : List α) {mid k : ℕ} :
    nwScore σ gap (List.take k xs) (List.take mid ys) +
      nwScore σ gap (List.drop k xs) (List.drop mid ys) ≤ nwScore σ gap xs ys
    def nwScore (xs ys : List α) : ℤ := nwScoreAux σ gap (xs.length + ys.length) xs ys

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.smallAlign

    Fix a substitution score \(\sigma\) assigning an integer to each pair of letters and an integer gap score \(g\), and write a column as a pair whose missing entry, a gap, is marked \(\bot\). The base-case alignment of sequences \(x\) and \(y\) is the empty alignment if both are empty; the single column \((\bot, b)\) if \(x\) is empty and \(y = [b]\); the single column \((a, \bot)\) if \(x = [a]\) and \(y\) is empty; if \(x = [a]\) and \(y = [b]\), it is the single column \((a, b)\) when \(\sigma(a, b) \ge 2g\) and the two columns \((a, \bot)\), \((\bot, b)\) otherwise; and it is the empty alignment when \(x\) or \(y\) has length at least \(2\).

    def smallAlign : List α → List α → Alignment α
  | [], [] => []
  | [], [b] => [(none, some b)]
  | [a], [] => [(some a, none)]
  | [a], [b] => if σ a b ≥ 2 * gap then [(some a, some b)]
      else [(some a, none), (none, some b)]
  | _, _ => []
    abbrev Alignment (α : Type*) := List (Option α × Option α)

    Cslib.Algorithms.Lean.hirschbergAlign

    Fix a substitution score \(\sigma\) assigning an integer to each pair of letters and an integer gap score \(g\), and write \(\mathrm{NW}\) for the Needleman–Wunsch score. For a sequence \(s\), write \(s_{\lt j}\) for its first \(j\) letters and \(s_{\ge j}\) for the remaining letters. The Hirschberg reconstruction of sequences \(x\) and \(y\) with fuel \(n\) is the alignment defined by recursion on \(n\). With fuel \(0\) it is the empty alignment. With fuel \(n + 1\): if \(|x| \le 1\) and \(|y| \le 1\), it is the base-case alignment of \(x\) and \(y\); otherwise, if \(|y| \ge |x|\), let \(m = \lfloor |y|/2 \rfloor\) and let \(k\) be the first index \(j \in \{0, \dots, |x|\}\) that maximizes \(\mathrm{NW}(x_{\lt j}, y_{\lt m}) + \mathrm{NW}(x_{\ge j}, y_{\ge m})\), and the result is the concatenation of the reconstructions with fuel \(n\) of \(x_{\lt k}\) and \(y_{\lt m}\) and of \(x_{\ge k}\) and \(y_{\ge m}\); otherwise, let \(m = \lfloor |x|/2 \rfloor\) and let \(k\) be the first index \(j \in \{0, \dots, |y|\}\) that maximizes \(\mathrm{NW}(x_{\lt m}, y_{\lt j}) + \mathrm{NW}(x_{\ge m}, y_{\ge j})\), and the result is the concatenation of the reconstructions with fuel \(n\) of \(x_{\lt m}\) and \(y_{\lt k}\) and of \(x_{\ge m}\) and \(y_{\ge k}\).

    Hirschberg's alignment algorithm runs this reconstruction with fuel \(|x| + |y|\).
