    Cslib.Bioinformatics.GeneNetworks.Aracne.aracne

    The network ARACNE reconstructs from symmetric pairwise mutual informations I with independence threshold I₀ and DPI tolerance zero (Margolin et al. 2006): {i, k} is an edge iff i ≠ k, I₀ < I i k, and no third gene j with I₀ < I i j and I₀ < I j k has I i k < min (I i j) (I j k).

    def aracne (I : V → V → ℝ) (hI : ∀ i k, I i k = I k i) (I₀ : ℝ) : SimpleGraph V where
  Adj i k := i ≠ k ∧ I₀ < I i k ∧
    ∀ j, j ≠ i → j ≠ k → I₀ < I i j → I₀ < I j k → min (I i j) (I j k) ≤ I i k
  symm := ⟨by
    rintro i k ⟨hik, h0, h⟩
    refine ⟨hik.symm, hI i k ▸ h0, fun j hjk hji hkj hji' ↦ ?_⟩
    rw [hI k j, hI j i] at *
    rw [hI k i, min_comm]
    exact h j hji hjk hji' hkj⟩
  loopless := ⟨fun _ h ↦ h.1 rfl⟩

    Cslib.Bioinformatics.GeneNetworks.Aracne.le_aracne_of_isAcyclic

    If the true network T is a forest whose edges all carry mutual information above I₀, and I a b ≤ min (I a c) (I c b) whenever c separates a from b in T (every walk from a to b visits c), then ARACNE keeps every edge of T.

    Cslib.Bioinformatics.GeneNetworks.Aracne.aracne_eq_of_isTree

    ARACNE recovers tree networks exactly (Margolin et al. 2006, Theorem 1): if the true network T is a tree whose edges all carry mutual information above I₀, and the strict data-processing inequality holds on every separated triple, then ARACNE returns T. Strictness is the explicit form of the paper's exclusion of exact cancellations; with equality ARACNE can keep an indirect edge (see the tests).

    Cslib.Bioinformatics.GeneNetworks.Aracne.le_aracne_of_isMaxWeightSpanningTree

    Chow–Liu trees survive ARACNE (Margolin et al. 2006, Theorem 2): a spanning tree T of maximum total weight ∑_{{i, k} ∈ T} I i k among all spanning trees, whose edges all carry mutual information above I₀, is a subnetwork of the ARACNE network.
