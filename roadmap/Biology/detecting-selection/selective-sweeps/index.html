    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.SelectionScan.sweepDuration_full

    A complete sweep in a population of N diploids, from a single copy 1 / (2N) to 1 - 1 / (2N), takes exactly (2 / s) log (2N - 1) generations in the deterministic logistic model. (The familiar (2 / s) log (2Ns) is the conditional expected duration of the corresponding stochastic sweep, a different quantity.)

    theorem sweepDuration_full {s N : ℝ} (hs : s ≠ 0) (hN : 1 < N) :

    The probability that two carriers of a swept allele are identical over a window of genetic length d, in the star-genealogy model of Voight et al. (2006): all carriers coalesce at the start of the sweep, a time T ago, and the pair is identical exactly when neither of its two lineages recombined, each at rate r * d per unit time.
