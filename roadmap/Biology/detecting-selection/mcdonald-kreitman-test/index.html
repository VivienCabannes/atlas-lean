    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.McDonaldKreitman.MKModel.tendsto_alphaHat

    Let Dn i, Ds i, Pn i, Ps i be the counts at locus i; suppose each of the four sequences consists of pairwise independent, identically distributed, integrable variables whose means are the expected counts of M (the loci are exchangeable copies of the model). Then almost surely α̂ computed from the counts summed over the first m loci converges to α = A / E D_n as m → ∞.
