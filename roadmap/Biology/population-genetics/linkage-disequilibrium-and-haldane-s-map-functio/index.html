    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Linkage.recombine_eq_sub

    The classical form of the recursion: coupling gametes (AB, ab) lose r D and repulsion gametes (Ab, aB) gain r D. This identity holds for every real r; the biological domain is 0 ≤ r ≤ 1/2.
