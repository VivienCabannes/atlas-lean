    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.LuriaDelbruck.ldPmf

    The Luria–Delbrück probability of n mutants with expected mutation number m: the compound-Poisson law ∑_k e^{-m} m^k / k! · cloneProb^{*k}(n). Since clones have size at least 1, only k ≤ n contribute.

  ∑ k ∈ range (n + 1), exp (-m) * m ^ k / k.factorial * convPow cloneProb k n
