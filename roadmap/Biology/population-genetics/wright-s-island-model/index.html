    Wright's island model

    DefinitionIdentity-by-descent recursion in Wright's island model#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.IslandModel.islandStep

    One generation of Wright's island-model identity-by-descent recursion for a deme of M gene copies (M = 2N for N diploids) receiving a fraction m of migrants from an infinite pool: F' = (1 - m) ^ 2 * (1 / M + (1 - 1 / M) * F). Biologically meaningful for M ≥ 2 and 0 ≤ m ≤ 1; M = 1 is an algebraic extension (no pair of distinct copies exists), and at M = 0 Lean's 1 / 0 = 0 gives a junk value.

    noncomputable def islandStep (M : ℕ) (m F : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  (1 - m) ^ 2 * (1 / M + (1 - 1 / M) * F)

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.IslandModel.islandEquilibrium

    The equilibrium identity-by-descent probability (Wright's F_ST) of the island model: F* = (1 - m) ^ 2 / (M - (M - 1) (1 - m) ^ 2). Biologically meaningful for M ≥ 2 and 0 ≤ m ≤ 1; the denominator is positive already for M ≥ 1 (islandEquilibrium_denom_pos), where M = 1 is an algebraic extension.

    noncomputable def islandEquilibrium (M : ℕ) (m : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  (1 - m) ^ 2 / (M - (M - 1) * (1 - m) ^ 2)
