    DefinitionOne generation of the neutral Wright–Fisher chain#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Diffusion.WrightFisherSemigroup.wfStep

    One generation of the neutral Wright–Fisher chain with M gene copies, acting on continuous functions of the allele frequency: (wfStep M f)(x) = ∑_k f(k/M) C(M, k) x^k (1 - x)^(M - k), which for M ≥ 1 is the expected value of f at the next frequency Bin(M, x) / M (the M-th Bernstein operator, Mathlib's bernsteinApproximation; the expectation is ProbabilityTheory.integral_binomial). It is a linear contraction. At M = 0 it returns the constant f 0 (Lean's k / 0 = 0; no population).

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Diffusion.WrightFisherSemigroup.sum_binomial_mul_div_pow
