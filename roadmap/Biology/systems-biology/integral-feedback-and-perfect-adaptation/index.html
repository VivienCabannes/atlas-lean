    MathlibExt.Biology.Dynamics.IntegralFeedback.output_eq_zero_of_integral_feedback

    Integral feedback gives robust perfect adaptation (nonlinear form). Let F p be the vector field and y p the output of a system with parameters p, and let z be a differentiable state function with derivative z' x satisfying z' x (F p x) = y p x for all p, x (so ż = y along every trajectory, for every parameter). Then the output vanishes at every equilibrium of every F p.
