    DefinitionBalanced minimum-evolution length#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.Phylogenetics.BalancedMinimumEvolution.UTree.bmeLength

    The balanced minimum-evolution length of the topology of T for a dissimilarity d (Desper and Gascuel 2002): ∑_{i<j} 2^{1-τ_ij} (d(i, j) + d(j, i)) / 2. It depends on T only through the topological distances τ_ij. Each term is symmetrized, so the value does not depend on the orientation T.pairs happens to assign to a pair: swapping the children of a node, or the two sides of the root edge, leaves bmeLength unchanged.
