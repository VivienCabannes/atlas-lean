    Cslib.Bioinformatics.Phylogenetics.FelsensteinPruning.weight

    The weight of a labeling given root state s: the product over all edges of the transition weight from the parent's state to the child's state, times the leaf weight obs l at the state of every leaf l.

    TheoremIndependent sites#✓fully proved

    Cslib.Bioinformatics.Phylogenetics.FelsensteinPruning.sum_prod_siteWeight_eq_prod_likelihood

    For an alignment whose sites k : ι evolve independently along the same tree with leaf data obs k, the joint likelihood (the sum over all choices of a root state and a labeling at every site of the product of the per-site weights) is the product of the per-site likelihoods.
