    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Coalescent.integral_meanPairDiff

    Expected pairwise differences (Tajima 1983): if, for each pair s of sampled genes, the coalescence time τ s of the pair is exponential with rate 1 and the number D s of differences has conditional mean θ τ s given the genealogy σ-algebra m (mutations at rate θ / 2 on each of the two branches of length τ s), then E π = θ.

    μ[meanPairDiff n D] = θ
