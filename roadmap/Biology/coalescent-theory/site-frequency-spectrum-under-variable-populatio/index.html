    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Coalescent.VariableSFS.integral_sfs_of_indep

    Expected SFS under a variable population size (Griffiths & Tavaré 1998; Polanski & Kimmel 2003). Let the genealogy of a sample of n copies consist of holding times T k (integrable for 2 ≤ k ≤ n, otherwise arbitrary) and an independent block-size history τ with the random-joining law. If, given the genealogy, the count ξ i is integrable with conditional mean (θ / 2) L_i, then E ξ_i = (θ / 2) ∑_k k C(n-i-1,k-2)/C(n-1,k-1) E T_k for 1 ≤ i ≤ n - 1. Independence of the holding times and the topology holds for the time-changed Kingman coalescent of any deterministic population-size history (a standard property of that model; here it is a hypothesis).
