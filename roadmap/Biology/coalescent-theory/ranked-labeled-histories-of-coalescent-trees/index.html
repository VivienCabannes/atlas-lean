    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Coalescent.blocksAfter

    The lineages (as blocks of sampled genes, indexed by lineage label) after the first t coalescence events of a history, starting from the singletons {0}, …, {n - 1}.

    def blocksAfter {n : ℕ} (h : RankedHistory n) (t : ℕ) : List (Finset (Fin n)) :=
  ((List.finRange (n - 1)).take t).foldl
    (fun bs i =>
      let s : Finset ℕ := (h i).1.map Fin.valEmbedding
      mergeBlocks bs (s.min.getD 0) (s.max.getD 0))
    ((List.finRange n).map fun j => {j})

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Coalescent.randomJoining

    Random joining (Edwards 1970; Kingman 1982): the pair merging at each step is uniform among the current (n - i).choose 2 pairs and independent of the earlier steps. Kingman (1982) shows that this is the law of the tree topology of the coalescent.
