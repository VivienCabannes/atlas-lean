    The holding-time model

    StructureHolding times of Kingman's coalescent#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.Coalescent.KingmanTimes

    Kingman's coalescent for a sample of size n, through its holding times: T k is the time (in units of 2N generations) during which the genealogy has exactly k lineages, for 2 ≤ k ≤ n. These are independent, and T k is exponential with rate k.choose 2. The values of T k for k ∉ [2, n] are irrelevant.

    structure KingmanTimes (μ : Measure Ω) (n : ℕ) where
  /-- `T k` is the holding time while the genealogy has `k` lineages. -/
  T : ℕ → Ω → ℝ
  /-- `T k` is exponential with rate `k.choose 2`, the number of pairs of lineages. -/
  hasLaw : ∀ k ∈ Icc 2 n, HasLaw (T k) (expMeasure (k.choose 2 : ℕ)) μ
  /-- The holding times are mutually independent. -/
  indep : iIndepFun (fun k : Icc 2 n => T k) μ
      T : ℕ → Ω → ℝ
