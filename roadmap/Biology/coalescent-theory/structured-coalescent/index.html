    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.StructuredCoalescent.rate

    The transition rates q(s, t) (s ≠ t) of the two-lineage structured coalescent in the finite island model with d demes and M = 4Nm, time in units of 2N generations: same deme → coalesced at rate 1, same deme → different demes at rate M, different demes → same deme at rate M / (d - 1); all other rates (and the diagonal) are 0. Meaningful for d ≥ 2 and M ≥ 0.

    noncomputable def rate (d : ℕ) (M : ℝ) : PairState → PairState → ℝ
  | same, coalesced => 1
  | same, different => M
  | different, same => M / (d - 1)
  | _, _ => 0

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.StructuredCoalescent.FirstStepEquations

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.StructuredCoalescent.meanCoalescenceTime_eq_firstStep

    At each transient state s the exit rate is positive and the mean coalescence time is the mean holding time 1 / qₛ plus the mean time from the next state, h s = 1 / qₛ + ∑ₜ (q(s, t) / qₛ) h t.

    theorem meanCoalescenceTime_eq_firstStep {d : ℕ} (hd : 2 ≤ d) {M : ℝ} (hM : 0 < M)
