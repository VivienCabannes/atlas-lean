    StructureRandom-effects model of LD-score regression#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.LDScore.Model

    The random-effects model of LD-score regression (Bulik-Sullivan et al. 2015), stated at the level of raw second moments, for N individuals and M markers with heritability h2.

    • beta k is the (random) effect of marker k, with ∫ β k * β l = h2 / M * [k = l];
    • eps i is the environmental noise of individual i, with ∫ ε i * ε i' = (1 - h2) * [i = i'];
    • effects and noise are orthogonal in L².

    The measure is arbitrary and no means are assumed; for a probability measure and mean-zero effects and noise these are the covariance conditions of the cited model. The genotype matrix is not part of the model: all expectations are conditional on it.

    TheoremLD-score regression#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.LDScore.Model.integral_chiSq_of_standardized

    LD-score regression (Bulik-Sullivan et al. 2015): for a standardized genotype matrix (IsStandardized, so that ldCorr is the sample correlation and ldScore the LD score) and N ≠ 0, 𝔼[χ² j] = N h² / M * ℓ j + (1 - h²). The expected association statistic is affine in the LD score, with slope N h² / M and intercept 1 - h². The proof uses only that column j has sum of squares N; the rest of the standardization is what makes ℓ j an LD score.
