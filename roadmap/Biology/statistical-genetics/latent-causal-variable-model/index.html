    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.LatentCausalVariable.Model

    The latent causal variable model of O'Connor and Price (2018, Equation 3) on a probability space: α_k = q_k π + γ_k with π independent of (γ₁, γ₂), the law of (γ₁, γ₂) invariant under each sign flip, π of mean zero and unit variance, and both α_k of unit second moment. Fourth moments are assumed finite.

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.LatentCausalVariable.gcp

    The genetic causality proportion gcp = (log|q₂| - log|q₁|) / (log|q₂| + log|q₁|) (O'Connor and Price 2018, Equation 4). Meaningful for 0 < |q₁|, |q₂| ≤ 1 and ρ_g² < 1; for |q₁| = |q₂| = 1 the denominator vanishes and the value is 0 by the convention x / 0 = 0.

    noncomputable def gcp (q1 q2 : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  (Real.log |q2| - Real.log |q1|) / (Real.log |q2| + Real.log |q1|)
