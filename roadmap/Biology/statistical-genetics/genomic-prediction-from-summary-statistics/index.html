    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.GenomicPrediction.integral_score_mul_score

    In a target population whose marker genotypes x j have raw second moments ∫ x j * x k = [j = k] (standardized, uncorrelated markers), the scores xᵀ a and xᵀ b of two effect vectors have raw cross moment aᵀ b. With mean-zero genotypes and a probability measure this is the covariance of the genetic value xᵀ β and the polygenic score xᵀ β̂.

    theorem integral_score_mul_score {Ξ : Type*} {mΞ : MeasurableSpace Ξ} {ν : Measure Ξ}

    ∫ ξ, (∑ j, a j * x j ξ) * (∑ k, b k * x k ξ) ∂ν = ∑ j, a j * b j

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.GenomicPrediction.PRSModel

    The effect-estimate model behind polygenic-score accuracy (Daetwyler et al. 2008; Dudbridge 2013), at the level of raw second moments, for M independent markers: true effects beta k with ∫ β k β l = (h² / M) [k = l], estimation errors err k with ∫ e k e l = s² [k = l], orthogonal to the effects. The estimates are β̂ = β + e (betaHat). For a probability measure and mean-zero effects and errors these are the covariance conditions of the cited model; s² is the sampling variance of each estimate (1 / N in the cited papers, (1 - h²) / N in ofLDScore).
