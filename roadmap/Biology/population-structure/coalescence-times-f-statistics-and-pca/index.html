    StructureCoalescent model linking heterozygosity and coalescence times#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.CoalescentFStatistics.CoalescentModel

    The relation between allele frequencies and coalescence times under neutral infinite-sites mutation, taken as the model assumption: the expected heterozygosity between populations X and Y is θ times the expected coalescence time coalTime X Y of a gene from X and a gene from Y (two genes drawn independently, so with replacement, when X = Y). Time is in units of 2N generations and θ = 4Nu. The measure μ is finite: it ranges over the evolutionary randomness and, in practice, over a finite set of loci. For θ = 0 the coalescence times are not determined by the frequencies, and the identities below then say that the f-statistics vanish.

    structure CoalescentModel (μ : Measure Ω) (P : Type*) where
  /-- Allele frequencies of the populations. -/
  freq : P → Ω → ℝ
  /-- The scaled mutation rate `θ = 4Nu`. -/
  theta : ℝ
  /-- Expected pairwise coalescence times `𝔼[T X Y]`. -/
  coalTime : P → P → ℝ
  /-- Frequencies are square integrable. -/
  memLp_freq : ∀ X, MemLp (freq X) 2 μ
  /-- Infinite sites: `π X Y = θ 𝔼[T X Y]`. -/
  meanHet_eq : ∀ X Y, meanHet μ freq X Y = theta * coalTime X Y
      freq : P → Ω → ℝ

    StructureMcVean's genealogical model for PCA#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.CoalescentFStatistics.McVeanModel

    McVean's genealogical setting for PCA, taken as the model assumption: random genotypes Z i s (sample i, locus s), square integrable, whose expected numbers of pairwise differences are θ times the expected pairwise coalescence times coalTime i j (neutral infinite sites, time in units of 2N generations, θ the total scaled mutation rate of the L loci). For θ = 0 the coalescence times are not determined by the genotypes.

  /-- Random genotypes. -/
  geno : ι → S → Ω → ℝ
  /-- The scaled mutation rate. -/
  theta : ℝ
  /-- Expected pairwise coalescence times of the samples. -/
  coalTime : ι → ι → ℝ
  /-- Genotypes are square integrable. -/
  memLp_geno : ∀ i s, MemLp (geno i s) 2 μ
  /-- Infinite sites: `𝔼[d i j] = θ t i j`. -/
  integral_pairDiff : ∀ i j, ∫ ω, pairDiff (fun k s => geno k s ω) i j ∂μ = theta * coalTime i j

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.CoalescentFStatistics.McVeanModel.integral_genCov

    McVean (2009): the expected genotype covariance is θ / (2 L) times the double centring of the expected pairwise coalescence times, 𝔼[C i j] = θ / (2 L) * (t̄ i· + t̄ ·j - t i j - t̄ ··).

    theorem McVeanModel.integral_genCov {μ : Measure Ω} (M : McVeanModel μ ι S) (i j : ι) :
    ∫ ω, genCov (fun k s => M.geno k s ω) i j ∂μ =
      M.theta / (2 * Fintype.card S) * doubleCenter M.coalTime i j
