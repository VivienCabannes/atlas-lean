    TheoremqpAdm model equation#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.ADMIXTOOLS.f4_affine_combination

    qpAdm model equation (Haak et al. 2015, Supplementary Information): if the target T is an admixture of sources S k with weights w k summing to 1 (p T = ∑ k, w k * p (S k) pointwise; the weights may be negative, as in fitted qpAdm models), then for all X, Y, Z, f4 T X Y Z = ∑ k, w k * f4 (S k) X Y Z.     TheoremqpWave rank bound#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.ADMIXTOOLS.rank_f4Matrix_le

    qpWave rank bound (Reich et al. 2012, Supplementary Information; Patterson et al. 2012): if the frequency of every left population L i is an affine combination ∑ l, a i l * p (S l) (∑ l, a i l = 1, coefficients of any sign) of the frequencies of k + 1 source populations, then the matrix f4 (L i) (L 0) (R j) (R 0) (i, j ≥ 1) has rank at most k; with k sources, at most k - 1. The right populations are arbitrary. The converse (rank k forces k + 1 streams) and the statistical rank test are not covered.

    theorem rank_f4Matrix_le (hp : ∀ X, MemLp (p X) 2 μ) {n r k : ℕ} (L : Fin (n + 1) → P)
    (R : Fin (r + 1) → P) (S : Fin (k + 1) → P) (a : Fin (n + 1) → Fin (k + 1) → ℝ)
    (ha : ∀ i, ∑ l, a i l = 1) (hL : ∀ i ω, p (L i) ω = ∑ l, a i l * p (S l) ω) :
    (f4Matrix μ p L R).rank ≤ k
    noncomputable def f4Matrix {n r : ℕ} (L : Fin (n + 1) → P) (R : Fin (r + 1) → P) :
    Matrix (Fin n) (Fin r) ℝ :=
  Matrix.of fun i j => f4 μ p (L i.succ) (L 0) (R j.succ) (R 0)
