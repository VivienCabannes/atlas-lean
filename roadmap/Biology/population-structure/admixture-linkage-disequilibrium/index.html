    ALDER weighted LD

    DefinitionExpected ALDER weighted linkage disequilibrium#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.PopulationGenetics.AdmixtureLD.weightedLD

    The expected ALDER two-site weighted-LD statistic 𝔼[z(x, y) w(x) w(y)] (Loh et al. 2013, Appendix A, without distance binning). The loci x and y drift independently: ω.1 and ω.2 are independent draws from μ, and p X ω is the frequency of the '1' allele in population X. The covariance z(x, y) is that of the population formed n generations ago as an α : 1 - α mixture of A and B (genotypeCov_admixed), and the weights are the frequency differences w = p R - p S between two reference populations R and S.

    noncomputable def weightedLD (α r : ℝ) (n : ℕ) (A B R S : P) : ℝ :=
  ∫ ω, genotypeCov ((recombine r)^[n] (sourceMix α (p A ω.1) (p A ω.2) (p B ω.1) (p B ω.2))) *
      ((p R ω.1 - p S ω.1) * (p R ω.2 - p S ω.2)) ∂μ.prod μ
