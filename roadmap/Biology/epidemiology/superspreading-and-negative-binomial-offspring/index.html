    MathlibExt.Biology.Epidemiology.Superspreading.nbPmf

    The negative-binomial offspring probability with mean R and dispersion k: P(Z = n) = C(k + n - 1, n) p^k (1 - p)^n with p = k / (k + R) (Lloyd-Smith et al. 2005). Meaningful for k > 0, R ≥ 0.

    noncomputable def nbPmf (R k : ℝ) (n : ℕ) : ℝ :=
  Ring.choose (k + n - 1) n * (k / (k + R)) ^ k * (R / (k + R)) ^ n
