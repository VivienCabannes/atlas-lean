    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.DominanceLD.dominanceEncoding

    The dominance encoding of a biallelic genotype g ∈ {0, 1, 2} (count of the allele with frequency p): p², -p (1 - p), (1 - p)². Dividing by -p (1 - p) gives the encoding -p / (1 - p), 1, -(1 - p) / p of Palmer et al. (2023).

    noncomputable def dominanceEncoding (p g : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  if g = 0 then p ^ 2 else if g = 1 then -(p * (1 - p)) else (1 - p) ^ 2
