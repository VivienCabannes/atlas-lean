    TheoremLande's equation#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.SelectionGradient.selectionDifferential_eq_mul_deriv_log

    Lande's equation for one normally distributed trait (Lande 1979): the selection differential is the phenotypic variance times the derivative of log mean fitness with respect to the trait mean, S = v · d/dμ log w̄(μ).

    theorem selectionDifferential_eq_mul_deriv_log {W : ℝ → ℝ} (hWm : Measurable W)
    (hWb : ∃ C, ∀ z, |W z| ≤ C) {v : ℝ≥0} (hv : v ≠ 0) {μ : ℝ} (hw : meanFitness W μ v ≠ 0) :
    selectionDifferential W μ v = v * deriv (fun m => Real.log (meanFitness W m v)) μ
    noncomputable def meanFitness (W : ℝ → ℝ) (μ : ℝ) (v : ℝ≥0) : ℝ :=
  ∫ z, W z ∂gaussianReal μ v

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.SelectionGradient.selectionDifferential_div_eq

    The Lande–Arnold selection gradient for one normally distributed trait: for a differentiable bounded fitness function with integrable derivative, β = S / v = 𝔼[W' z] / w̄, the average slope of relative fitness W / w̄ (Lande–Arnold 1983).

    MathlibExt.Biology.StatisticalGenetics.SelectionGradient.selectionDifferentialMV_eq

    Lande's equation (Lande 1979): for a multivariate normal trait vector, the selection differential is the phenotypic covariance matrix applied to the gradient of log mean fitness with respect to the trait means, S = P ∇_μ log w̄(μ).

    theorem selectionDifferentialMV_eq {W : EuclideanSpace ℝ ι → ℝ} (hWm : Measurable W)
    (hWb : ∃ C, ∀ z, |W z| ≤ C) {P : Matrix ι ι ℝ} (hP : P.PosDef) {μ : EuclideanSpace ℝ ι}
    (hw : meanFitnessMV W P μ ≠ 0) :
    selectionDifferentialMV W P μ =
      toEuclideanCLM (𝕜 := ℝ) P (gradient (fun m => Real.log (meanFitnessMV W P m)) μ)
