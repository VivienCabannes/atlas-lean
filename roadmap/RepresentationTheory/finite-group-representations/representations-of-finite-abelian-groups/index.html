    FDRep.rho_eq_character_smul_of_simple

    Let \(k\) be an algebraically closed field, \(G\) a monoid with commutative multiplication, and \(V\) a simple finite-dimensional representation of \(G\) over \(k\) (a simple object of the category of finite-dimensional representations), with action \(\rho\) and character \(\chi_V(g) = \operatorname{tr} \rho(g)\). Then for every \(g \in G\) the map \(\rho(g)\) is scalar multiplication by \(\chi_V(g)\), that is, \(\rho(g) = \chi_V(g) \cdot \mathrm{id}_V\).

    FDRep.character_mul_of_simple

    Let \(k\) be an algebraically closed field, let \(G\) be a commutative monoid, and let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional representation of \(G\) over \(k\) that is simple as an object of the category of finite-dimensional representations of \(G\) over \(k\). Let \(\chi_V : G \to k\) be the character of \(V\), where \(\chi_V(g)\) is the trace of the action of \(g\) on \(V\). Then \(\chi_V(gh) = \chi_V(g)\,\chi_V(h)\) for all \(g, h \in G\).

    V.character (g * h) = V.character g * V.character h

    TheoremCharacters of simple representations of a finite abelian group are homomorphisms#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.AbelianRepresentationWanted.abelian_simple_char_is_hom

    Let \(G\) be a finite abelian group and let \(V\) be a simple finite-dimensional complex representation of \(G\). Then there is a monoid homomorphism \(\varphi : G \to \mathbb{C}\) such that the character of \(V\) equals \(\varphi\).

    Proof: Simplicity of \(V\) makes it irreducible, and complex irreducible representations of an abelian group are one-dimensional (the corollary in Wikipedia, “Schur's lemma”), so \(V\) is one-dimensional. Endomorphisms of a one-dimensional space are scalars, and their traces give the multiplicative character.

    public theorem abelian_simple_char_is_hom (G : Type*) [CommGroup G]
    [Finite G] (V : FDRep ℂ G) [Simple V] :
    ∃ φ : G →* ℂ, V.character = ⇑φ

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.AbelianRepresentationWanted.abelian_hom_is_simple_char

    Let \(G\) be a finite abelian group and let \(\varphi : G \to \mathbb{C}\) be a monoid homomorphism. Then there is a simple finite-dimensional complex representation \(V\) of \(G\) whose character equals \(\varphi\).

    Proof: Let each \(g \in G\) act on the one-dimensional space \(\mathbb{C}\) by the scalar \(\varphi(g)\); its trace is \(\varphi(g)\). One-dimensional modules are simple. Wikipedia, “Representation theory of finite groups”, notes that the irreducible characters of an abelian group are its homomorphisms to \(\mathbb{C}^\times\).

    TheoremCharacters determine simple representations of a finite abelian group#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.GroupTheory.AbelianRepresentationWanted.abelian_simple_iso_of_char_eq

    Let \(G\) be a finite abelian group and let \(V\) and \(W\) be simple finite-dimensional complex representations of \(G\), with characters \(\chi_V\) and \(\chi_W\). If \(\chi_V = \chi_W\), then \(V\) and \(W\) are isomorphic.

    Proof: For functions \(\chi, \psi : G \to \mathbb{C}\) write \(\langle \chi, \psi \rangle = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} \chi(g)\,\psi(g^{-1})\), where \(|G|\) is the order of \(G\). The orthonormality of irreducible characters gives \(\langle \chi_V, \chi_V \rangle = 1\). Replacing the second character by the equal character \(\chi_W\) gives \(\langle \chi_V, \chi_W \rangle = 1\), which forces \(V \cong W\). This is the orthonormality of irreducible characters in Wikipedia, “Character theory”.

    public theorem abelian_simple_iso_of_char_eq (G : Type*) [CommGroup G]
    [Finite G] (V W : FDRep ℂ G) [Simple V] [Simple W]
    (h : V.character = W.character) : Nonempty (V ≅ W)
