    CharacterTheory.wedderburn_sum_sq_eq_card

    If |G| is nonzero in an algebraically closed field, the group algebra of a finite group is a product of matrix algebras with nonzero block sizes whose squares sum to |G|.

    theorem wedderburn_sum_sq_eq_card
    (k : Type u) [Field k] [IsAlgClosed k]
    (G : Type u) [Group G] [Fintype G] [NeZero (Nat.card G : k)] :
    ∃ (n : ℕ) (d : Fin n → ℕ), (∀ i, NeZero (d i)) ∧
      (∑ i, (d i) ^ 2 = Nat.card G) ∧
      Nonempty (MonoidAlgebra k G ≃ₐ[k] Π i, Matrix (Fin (d i)) (Fin (d i)) k)
