    MathlibExt.Topology.Knot.RegularProjection.DartDiagram

    A dart diagram consists of a finite set \(V\) of crossings, a fixed-point-free involution \(\mathrm{pair}\) of the set \(V \times \{0, 1, 2, 3\}\) of darts, and a natural number counting isolated crossingless components.

    Darts \(0, 1, 2, 3\) at a crossing are read in counterclockwise order, darts \(0\) and \(2\) form the over-strand and darts \(1\) and \(3\) the under-strand, and \(\mathrm{pair}\) glues darts across edge segments. The structure does not by itself assert planarity.
