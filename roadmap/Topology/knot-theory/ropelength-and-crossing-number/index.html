    MathlibExt.Geometry.Euclidean.Ropelength.circumradiusENN

    For points \(p, q, r\) of Euclidean 3-space, the extended circumradius of \((p, q, r)\) is the following value in \([0, \infty]\). If \(p, q, r\) are affinely independent, it is the circumradius of the triangle with vertices \(p\), \(q\) and \(r\). Otherwise, that is, when the three points are collinear (in particular when two of them coincide), it is \(\infty\).

    This matches the convention that collinear points determine a circle of infinite radius.

    MathlibExt.Geometry.Euclidean.Ropelength.thickness

    The thickness of a set \(s\) in Euclidean 3-space is the infimum, in \([0, \infty]\), of the extended circumradii of \((p, q, r)\) over all points \(p, q, r \in s\).   ⨅ p ∈ s, ⨅ q ∈ s, ⨅ r ∈ s, circumradiusENN p q r

    MathlibExt.Geometry.Euclidean.Ropelength.contourLength

    Let \(\gamma\) be a map from \(\mathbb{R}\) to Euclidean 3-space and let \(a, b\) be real numbers. The contour length of \(\gamma\) on \([a, b]\) is the total variation of \(\gamma\) on \([a, b]\): the supremum, over all finite sequences \(t_0 \le t_1 \le \dots \le t_n\) of points of \([a, b]\), of \(\sum_{i < n} \lVert \gamma(t_{i+1}) - \gamma(t_i) \rVert\). It is a value in \([0, \infty]\).

    For continuously differentiable curves it equals the integral of the speed.

  eVariationOn γ (Set.Icc a b)

    MathlibExt.Geometry.Euclidean.Ropelength.ropelengthRatio

    For a curve \(\gamma : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^3\) and reals \(a, b\), the ropelength ratio of \(\gamma\) on \([a, b]\) is the contour length of \(\gamma\) on \([a, b]\) divided by the thickness of the trace \(\gamma([a, b])\).     MathlibExt.Topology.Knot.TightKnot.IsAdmissible

    A real number \(c\) is admissible for the tight-knot inequality when \(c \ge 0\) and, for every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), every parametrized link \(L\) with \(n\) components and every \(k \in \mathbb{N}\) such that the crossing number of \(L\) equals \(k\), the ropelength of \(L\) is at least \(c \, k^{3/4}\), the inequality being read in \([0, \infty]\). Here the crossing number and the ropelength of \(L\) are those of its oriented labeled ambient-isotopy type.

    Link types of infinite crossing number, that is, types none of whose representatives admits a regular projection, impose no constraint.

    def IsAdmissible (c : ℝ) : Prop :=
