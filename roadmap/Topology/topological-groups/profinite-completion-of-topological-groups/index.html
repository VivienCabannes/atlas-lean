    ProfiniteGrp.OpenProfiniteCompletion.Index

    For a group \(G\) with a topology, this is the set of normal subgroups \(H\) of \(G\) that have finite index and are open in \(G\), ordered by inclusion and viewed as a category: there is a morphism \(H \to K\) exactly when \(H \subseteq K\).

    ProfiniteGrp.OpenProfiniteCompletion.finiteGrpDiagram

    For a group \(G\) with a topology, this is the functor from the category of open finite-index normal subgroups of \(G\) to finite groups that sends \(H\) to the quotient group \(G/H\) and sends an inclusion \(H \subseteq K\) to the projection \(G/H \to G/K\), \(gH \mapsto gK\).

  obj H := FiniteGrp.of <| G ⧸ H.1.toSubgroup

  map_comp f g := by ext ⟨x⟩; rfl

    ProfiniteGrp.OpenProfiniteCompletion.diagram

    For a group \(G\) with a topology, this is the functor that sends each open finite-index normal subgroup \(H\) of \(G\) to the finite quotient group \(G/H\) with the discrete topology, regarded as a profinite group, and each inclusion \(H \subseteq K\) to the projection \(G/H \to G/K\), \(gH \mapsto gK\). It is the diagram of finite quotients followed by the inclusion of finite groups into profinite groups.

    DefinitionOpen profinite completion of a group#✓fully proved

    ProfiniteGrp.OpenProfiniteCompletion.completion

    For a group \(G\) with a topology, the open profinite completion of \(G\) is the limit, in profinite groups, of the diagram of finite quotients \(G/H\), where \(H\) runs over the open finite-index normal subgroups of \(G\) and the transition maps are the projections \(G/H \to G/K\) for \(H \subseteq K\). Concretely, it is the group of families \((x_H)_H\) with \(x_H \in G/H\) that are compatible under these projections, with the topology of a subspace of the product of the discrete groups \(G/H\).

    def completion (G : Type u) [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] :
    ProfiniteGrp.{u} :=
  ProfiniteGrp.limit (diagram G)

    Let \(G\) be a group with a topology. For every \(x \in G\) and every open finite-index normal subgroup \(H\) of \(G\), the \(H\)-coordinate of the image of \(x\) under the canonical map into the open profinite completion of \(G\) is the coset \(xH \in G/H\).

    ProfiniteGrp.OpenProfiniteCompletion.etaFn_continuous

    Let \(G\) be a group with a topology in which multiplication is separately continuous: \(x \mapsto ax\) and \(x \mapsto xa\) are continuous for every \(a \in G\). Then the canonical map from \(G\) to its open profinite completion is continuous.
