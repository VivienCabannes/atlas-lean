    RestrictedProduct.DirectLimitXS

    Let \((X_i)_{i \in \iota}\) be topological spaces with subsets \(U_i \subseteq X_i\), and for a finite set \(S \subseteq \iota\) let \(X_S\) be the set of tuples \(x \in \prod_i X_i\) with \(x_i \in U_i\) for all \(i \notin S\), with the subspace topology of the product topology.
