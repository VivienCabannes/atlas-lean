    TopologicalSpace.DirectLimit.Rel

    Let \((X_i)_{i \in I}\) be a family of topological spaces indexed by a preorder \(I\), with maps \(f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j\) for \(i \le j\) forming a directed system (\(f_{ii}\) is the identity and \(f_{jk} \circ f_{ij} = f_{ik}\) for \(i \le j \le k\)). This relation on the disjoint union \(\bigsqcup_{i \in I} X_i\) relates \((i, x)\) to \((j, f_{ij}(x))\) for all \(i \le j\) and \(x \in X_i\), and relates no other pairs.

    def Rel [DirectedSystem X (fun ⦃i j⦄ (h : i ≤ j) => f i j h)] :
    (Σ i, X i) → (Σ i, X i) → Prop :=
  fun a b => ∃ (i j : ι) (h : i ≤ j) (x : X i),
    a = ⟨i, x⟩ ∧ b = ⟨j, f i j h x⟩

    TopologicalSpace.DirectLimit.Space

    Let \(I\) be a preorder, \((X_i)_{i \in I}\) a family of topological spaces, and \(f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j\) for \(i \le j\) continuous maps forming a directed system (\(f_{ii}\) is the identity and \(f_{jk} \circ f_{ij} = f_{ik}\) for \(i \le j \le k\)). The direct limit \(\varinjlim X_i\) is the quotient of the disjoint union \(\bigsqcup_{i \in I} X_i\) by the equivalence relation generated by \((i, x) \sim (j, f_{ij}(x))\) for \(i \le j\) and \(x \in X_i\), with the quotient topology of the disjoint union topology.

    The directed-system laws and the continuity of the transition maps are hypotheses of the construction: the underlying quotient does not use them, but without them it need not be a direct limit of topological spaces.

    abbrev Space {ι : Type*} [Preorder ι]
    (X : ι → Type*) [∀ i, TopologicalSpace (X i)]
    (f : ∀ i j, i ≤ j → X i → X j)
    [DirectedSystem X (fun ⦃i j⦄ (h : i ≤ j) => f i j h)]
    (_hf_cont : ∀ i j (h : i ≤ j), Continuous (f i j h)) : Type _ :=
  @Quotient (Σ i, X i) (directLimitSetoid X f)
