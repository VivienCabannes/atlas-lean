    DefinitionMoves of the Accelerated Zeckendorf Game#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.AccelZeckMove

    Index the Fibonacci numbers as \(F_1 = 1\), \(F_2 = 2\) and \(F_{i+1} = F_i + F_{i-1}\), and call a finitely supported function \(s : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\) a state, \(s(i)\) being the number of copies of \(F_i\). A move takes a state \(s\) to a state \(t\) if for some multiplicity \(m > 0\) one of the following holds: the move \(m \cdot C_i\) with \(i \ge 2\), where \(s(i-1) \ge m\), \(s(i) \ge m\) and \(t\) is obtained from \(s\) by removing \(m\) copies each of \(F_{i-1}\) and \(F_i\) and adding \(m\) copies of \(F_{i+1}\); the move \(m \cdot C_1\), where \(s(1) \ge 2m\) and \(t\) is obtained by replacing \(2m\) copies of \(F_1\) by \(m\) copies of \(F_2\); the move \(m \cdot S_2\), where \(s(2) \ge 2m\) and \(t\) is obtained by replacing \(2m\) copies of \(F_2\) by \(m\) copies each of \(F_1\) and \(F_3\); or the move \(m \cdot S_i\) with \(i \ge 3\), where \(s(i) \ge 2m\) and \(t\) is obtained by replacing \(2m\) copies of \(F_i\) by \(m\) copies each of \(F_{i-2}\) and \(F_{i+1}\).

    public def AccelZeckMove (s t : ℕ →₀ ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ m, 0 < m ∧
    ((∃ i, 2 ≤ i ∧ m ≤ s (i - 1) ∧ m ≤ s i ∧
        t = s - Finsupp.single (i - 1) m - Finsupp.single i m + Finsupp.single (i + 1) m) ∨
      (2 * m ≤ s 1 ∧ t = s - Finsupp.single 1 (2 * m) + Finsupp.single 2 m) ∨
      (2 * m ≤ s 2 ∧
        t = s - Finsupp.single 2 (2 * m) + Finsupp.single 1 m + Finsupp.single 3 m) ∨
      (∃ i, 3 ≤ i ∧ 2 * m ≤ s i ∧
        t = s - Finsupp.single i (2 * m) + Finsupp.single (i - 2) m +
          Finsupp.single (i + 1) m))

    The losing and the winning states of the Accelerated Zeckendorf Game are defined by simultaneous induction: a state is losing when every move from it leads to a winning state, and a state is winning when some move from it leads to a losing state.     DefinitionZeckendorf decomposition as a game state#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.zeckendorfState

    Index the Fibonacci numbers as \(F_1 = 1\), \(F_2 = 2\) and \(F_{i+1} = F_i + F_{i-1}\), and record a game state as a finitely supported function \(s : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}\), where \(s(i)\) is the number of copies of \(F_i\). Every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) has a unique Zeckendorf decomposition \(n = F_{i_1} + \cdots + F_{i_r}\) with \(r \ge 0\), indices \(i_1, \dots, i_r \ge 1\) and \(i_t \ge i_{t+1} + 2\) for \(1 \le t < r\).     TheoremThe Accelerated Zeckendorf Game terminates at the Zeckendorf decomposition#●ready to prove

    MetaMathlibExt.accelZeckGame_terminates_at_zeckendorf

    Index the Fibonacci numbers as \(F_1 = 1\), \(F_2 = 2\), \(F_{i+1} = F_i + F_{i-1}\).
