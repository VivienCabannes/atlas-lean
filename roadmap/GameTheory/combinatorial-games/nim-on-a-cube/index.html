    DefinitionRotation move on the cube#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.CubeRotationMove

    Label the faces of a cube \(f_a, f_b, f_c, f_A, f_B, f_C\), where \(f_A\), \(f_B\), \(f_C\) are opposite \(f_a\), \(f_b\), \(f_c\), and record a position as the tuple \(p = (a, b, c, A, B, C)\) of numbers of tokens on these faces. A rotation is one of the three belts of four faces \(\{f_a, f_b, f_A, f_B\}\), \(\{f_a, f_c, f_A, f_C\}\) and \(\{f_b, f_c, f_B, f_C\}\). In the game \(C_R\) there is a move from a position \(p\) to a position \(q\) when, for some rotation, \(q\) agrees with \(p\) on the two faces outside the rotation, \(q \le p\) on every face, and \(q \ne p\); that is, at least one token in total is removed from the four faces of the rotation.

    def CubeRotationMove (p q : Fin 6 → ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ i j : Fin 6, ((i = 0 ∧ j = 1) ∨ (i = 0 ∧ j = 2) ∨ (i = 1 ∧ j = 2)) ∧
    q ≤ p ∧ q ≠ p ∧
    ∀ k : Fin 6, k ≠ i → k ≠ j → k ≠ i + 3 → k ≠ j + 3 → q k = p k
