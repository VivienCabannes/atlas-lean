    For \(k \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(p \in \mathbb{N}^k\), the Princess and the Roses game (P&R) with \(k\) labelled piles started at \(p\) is the impartial game whose positions are the \(q \in \mathbb{N}^k\), with \(q_i\) objects on pile \(i\), and whose moves remove one object from one nonempty pile, or one object from each of two distinct nonempty piles.
