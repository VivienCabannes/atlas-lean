    SimpleGraph.contractOutside

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph on a vertex set \(V\) and let \(D \subseteq V\). Contracting the complement of \(D\) to a single vertex gives the simple graph whose vertices are the elements of \(D\) together with one new vertex \(\ast\), in which two vertices \(a, b \in D\) are adjacent exactly when they are adjacent in \(G\), and a vertex \(a \in D\) is adjacent to \(\ast\) exactly when \(a\) has a neighbour in \(G\) outside \(D\). The new vertex \(\ast\) is present even when \(D = V\), and is then isolated.

    public noncomputable def contractOutside {V : Type*} (G : SimpleGraph V) (D : Set V) :
    SimpleGraph (Option D) :=
  G.map (collapseOutside D)

    SimpleGraph.HasStrongSquareCycle

    Let \(G\) be a graph on a finite vertex set \(V\) and let \(x \in V\). Then \(G\) has a strong square cycle at \(x\) if the square \(G^2\) of \(G\) has a Hamiltonian cycle that starts and ends at \(x\) and whose two edges at \(x\), its first and its last edge, are edges of \(G\).

    public def HasStrongSquareCycle {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V]

    G.Adj x p.snd ∧ G.Adj p.penultimate x

    TheoremFleischner's theorem#✓fully proved

    MetaMathlibExt.fleischner

    Let \(G\) be a finite simple graph that is 2-vertex-connected, that is, \(G\) has at least three vertices and stays connected after deleting at most one vertex. Let \(H\) be the square of \(G\): the graph on the same vertices in which two distinct vertices are adjacent if and only if they are adjacent in \(G\) or have a common neighbour in \(G\). Then \(H\) is Hamiltonian: it has a cycle that passes through every vertex exactly once.

    theorem fleischner : ∀ {V : Type*} [Fintype V] (G H : SimpleGraph V),
    G.IsVertexConnected 2 →
    (∀ u v : V, H.Adj u v ↔ (u ≠ v ∧ (G.Adj u v ∨ ∃ w, G.Adj u w ∧ G.Adj w v))) →
    @SimpleGraph.IsHamiltonian V (fun a b => Classical.propDecidable _) _ H
