    StructureEdge-indexed multigraph#✓fully proved

    SimpleGraph.EdgeIndexedMultigraph

    An edge-indexed multigraph with vertex type \(V\) and edge type \(E\) assigns to each edge \(e \in E\) an unordered pair of endpoints in \(V\), such that no edge has two equal endpoints. Distinct edges may have the same endpoints, so parallel edges are distinguished by their elements of \(E\).

    public structure EdgeIndexedMultigraph (V : Type*) (E : Type*) where
  /-- The unordered endpoints of an edge. -/
  ends : E → Sym2 V
  /-- No edge has two equal endpoints. -/
  loopless : ∀ e v, ends e ≠ s(v, v)
      ends : E → Sym2 V

    SimpleGraph.EdgeIndexedMultigraph.IndexedWalk

    In an edge-indexed multigraph, an indexed walk is a walk that records which edge it traverses at each step. Indexed walks are defined inductively: for every vertex \(v\) there is the empty indexed walk from \(v\) to \(v\), and if \(e\) is an edge with endpoints \(u\) and \(v\) and \(p\) is an indexed walk from \(v\) to \(w\), then traversing \(e\) from \(u\) to \(v\) and then following \(p\) is an indexed walk from \(u\) to \(w\).

    SimpleGraph.EdgeIndexedMultigraph.IndexedWalk.support

    The support of an indexed walk \(p\) from \(u\) to \(v\) in an edge-indexed multigraph is the list of the vertices that \(p\) visits, in order and with repetitions, including both endpoints: the empty walk at \(w\) has support \([w]\), and a walk that starts at \(u\) with an edge and continues with an indexed walk \(q\) has support \(u\) followed by the support of \(q\).

    public def support {u v : V} : M.IndexedWalk u v → List V
  | .nil w => [w]
  | .cons _ _ p => u :: p.support

    Given edge-indexed multigraphs \(M\) and \(N\) on the same vertex type \(V\), with edge types \(E\) and \(F\), their disjoint sum is the edge-indexed multigraph on \(V\) whose edge type is the disjoint union \(E \sqcup F\), where an edge coming from \(E\) has its endpoints in \(M\) and an edge coming from \(F\) has its endpoints in \(N\).
