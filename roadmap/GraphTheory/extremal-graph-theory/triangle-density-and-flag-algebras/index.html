    Triangle density and flag algebras

    How few triangles can a graph with a given edge density have? Write \(n\) for the number of vertices, \(m\) for the number of edges and \(T\) for the number of triangles. Goodman's bound \(3nT \ge m(4m - n^2)\) is proved by summing the common neighbourhood bound \(|N(u) \cap N(v)| \ge \deg u + \deg v - n\) over the ordered triangles and applying Cauchy–Schwarz to the degrees (ordered form, homomorphism-count form). With no triangles it gives Mantel's theorem, \(4m \le n^2\).

    The objects of Razborov's flag algebras are induced densities: \(p(H, G)\) is the probability that a random \(|H|\)-subset of the vertices of \(G\) induces a copy of \(H\). Their basic identity is averaging: for \(|H| \le l \le |G|\), \(p(H, G)\) is the average of the densities inside the \(l\)-subsets of vertices, a double count of \(k\)-sets inside \(l\)-sets. Grouping the \(l\)-subsets by the isomorphism type \(F\) of the graph they induce gives the chain rule \(p(H, G) = \sum_F p(H, F)\,p(F, G)\), so that inequalities between finitely many densities, proved once for all graphs on \(l\) vertices, hold for every large graph; flag-algebra proofs combine such identities with Cauchy–Schwarz inequalities.

    In the language of graph limits, Goodman's bound says \(t(K_3) \ge \rho(2\rho - 1)\) for homomorphism densities, and Razborov's flag-algebra calculus derives it as the inequality \(K_3 \ge \rho(2\rho - 1)\) (Flag algebras, J. Symbolic Logic 72 (2007), Example 11 in the author's version). The bound is tight only at the edge densities \(1 - 1/t\) of balanced complete multipartite graphs. The exact asymptotic minimum is Razborov's function \(h(a)\), the triangle density of complete \((t+1)\)-partite graphs with \(t\) equal large parts; it vanishes up to density \(1/2\) and equals \(t(t-1)/(t+1)^2\) at the Turán densities. Razborov's minimum triangle density theorem (2008), one of the first major applications of flag algebras, shows that no graph does better (statement only, proof wanted).

    The finite statements use homomorphism-count normalisations (\(n^2\), \(n^3\)). The induced-density form \(T/\binom{n}{3} \ge \rho(2\rho - 1)\) with \(\rho = m/\binom{n}{2}\) holds only as \(n \to \infty\); the four-cycle violates it.

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.InducedDensity.inducedDensity

    For a graph \(H\) on \(k\) vertices and a finite graph \(G\), the induced density \(p(H, G)\) is the probability that a uniformly random \(k\)-subset of the vertices of \(G\) induces a copy of \(H\). It is \(0\) when \(G\) has fewer than \(k\) vertices. These densities, written \(p(M, N)\) for finite models of a universal theory, are the basic quantities of Razborov's flag algebras; the averaging identity is their basic relation.

  inducedDensityIn H G univ

    TheoremAveraging identity for induced densities#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.InducedDensity.inducedDensity_eq_average

    For a graph \(H\) on \(k\) vertices, a graph \(G\) on \(n\) vertices and \(k \le l \le n\), the induced density of \(H\) in \(G\) is the average of its induced densities in the subgraphs of \(G\) induced on the \(l\)-subsets of vertices:

    \[p(H, G) = \binom{n}{l}^{-1} \sum_{|L| = l} p(H, G[L]).\]

    The proof is the double count of \(k\)-sets inside \(l\)-sets together with \(\binom{n}{l}\binom{l}{k} = \binom{n}{k}\binom{n-k}{l-k}\). Grouping the \(l\)-sets \(L\) by the isomorphism type \(F\) of \(G[L]\) turns the identity into the chain rule \(p(H, G) = \sum_F p(H, F)\,p(F, G)\) of Razborov's flag algebras, which expresses every density of a small graph as a linear combination of densities of \(l\)-vertex graphs.

    theorem inducedDensity_eq_average {k l : ℕ} (H : _root_.SimpleGraph (Fin k))
    (G : _root_.SimpleGraph V) (hkl : k ≤ l) (hln : l ≤ Fintype.card V) :
    inducedDensity H G =
      (∑ L ∈ (univ : Finset V).powersetCard l, inducedDensityIn H G L) /
        ((Fintype.card V).choose l : ℚ)
    noncomputable def inducedDensity {k : ℕ} (H : _root_.SimpleGraph (Fin k))
    (G : _root_.SimpleGraph V) : ℚ :=
  inducedDensityIn H G univ

    The ordered triples \((u, v, w)\) of vertices of a finite simple graph \(G\) that are pairwise adjacent.     TheoremCommon neighbourhood bound#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.GoodmanTriangleBound.degree_add_degree_le

    For all vertices \(u\) and \(v\) of a finite simple graph on \(n\) vertices,

    \[\deg u + \deg v \le |N(u) \cap N(v)| + n,\]

    because \(|N(u)| + |N(v)| = |N(u) \cup N(v)| + |N(u) \cap N(v)|\) and \(N(u) \cup N(v)\) has at most \(n\) vertices. Summed over the ordered edges \((u, v)\), the left side gives \(2\sum_v \deg(v)^2\) and the common neighbourhoods count the ordered triangles; this is the first step of Goodman's bound.

    theorem degree_add_degree_le (u v : V) :
    G.degree u + G.degree v ≤ #(G.neighborFinset u ∩ G.neighborFinset v) + Fintype.card V

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.GoodmanTriangleBound.goodman_orderedTriangles_nat

    For a finite simple graph with \(n\) vertices, \(m\) edges and \(T_0\) ordered triangles,

    \[8m^2 \le nT_0 + 2mn^2.\]

    TheoremGoodman's bound on the number of triangles#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.GoodmanTriangleBound.goodman

    A finite simple graph with \(n\) vertices, \(m\) edges and \(T\) triangles (\(3\)-cliques) satisfies

    \[m(4m - n^2) \le 3nT,\]

    as an inequality of integers. In particular a graph with more than \(n^2/4\) edges has a triangle.

    The bound is due to A. W. Goodman. Dividing by \(n^4/6\) turns it into the inequality \(t(K_3) \ge t(K_2)(2t(K_2) - 1)\) between the homomorphism densities \(t(K_2) = 2m/n^2\) and \(t(K_3) = 6T/n^3\). Razborov derives its limit form \(K_3 \ge \rho(2\rho - 1)\) in the flag algebra of graphs and attributes it to Goodman.

    The proof counts ordered triangles, which are six per triangle, and applies Goodman's bound in homomorphism-count form.

    theorem goodman :
    (#G.edgeFinset : ℤ) * (4 * #G.edgeFinset - Fintype.card V ^ 2) ≤
      3 * Fintype.card V * #(G.cliqueFinset 3)

    DefinitionRazborov's triangle density function#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.RazborovTriangleFunction.razborovTriangleDensity

    The function \(h\) of an edge density \(a\) with \(h(a) = 0\) for \(a \le 1/2\), \(h(a) = 1\) for \(a \ge 1\), and, for \(1 - 1/t < a \le 1 - 1/(t+1)\) with \(t \ge 2\) (so that \(t = \lceil 1/(1-a) \rceil - 1\)),

    \[h(a) = 6\left(\binom{t}{3}c^3 + \binom{t}{2}c^2(1 - tc)\right), \qquad c = \frac{t + \sqrt{t(t - a(t+1))}}{t(t+1)}.\]

    It is the triangle density of the complete \((t+1)\)-partite graph with edge density \(a\) whose \(t\) largest parts have equal relative size \(c\) (the last part has size \(1 - tc\)). Razborov's minimum triangle density theorem states that no graph sequence of edge density \(a\) has asymptotically fewer triangles. At the Turán densities \(h\) equals \(t(t-1)/(t+1)^2\).

    noncomputable def razborovTriangleDensity (a : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  if a ≤ 1 / 2 then 0
  else if 1 ≤ a then 1
  else
    let t : ℕ := ⌈1 / (1 - a)⌉₊ - 1
    let c : ℝ := (t + Real.sqrt (t * (t - a * (t + 1)))) / (t * (t + 1))
    6 * ((t.choose 3 : ℝ) * c ^ 3 + (t.choose 2 : ℝ) * c ^ 2 * (1 - t * c))

    Related topics

    • Turán density of complete graphs: \(\pi(K_{r+1}) = 1 - 1/r\), of which Mantel's theorem is the case \(r = 2\).

    TheoremRazborov's minimum triangle density theorem#●ready to prove

    MathlibExt.GraphTheory.RazborovTriangleDensityWanted.razborov_minimum_triangle_density

    For every edge density \(a \in [0, 1]\) and every \(\varepsilon > 0\) there is \(N\) such that every simple graph on \(n \ge N\) vertices with at least \(a\binom{n}{2}\) edges has at least \((h(a) - \varepsilon)\binom{n}{3}\) triangles, where \(h\) is Razborov's triangle density function.

    Equivalently, \(g_3(a) \ge h(a)\) for the limit \(g_3(a) = \lim_n g_3(\lfloor a\binom{n}{2}\rfloor, n)/\binom{n}{3}\), where \(g_3(m, n)\) is the least number of triangles in a graph with \(n\) vertices and \(m\) edges; the \(\varepsilon\)–\(N\) form follows because \(g_3(m, n)\) does not decrease when \(m\) grows. Together with the complete multipartite constructions behind \(h\), this determines \(g_3(a) = h(a)\), answering the question of Erdős (1955) in the form conjectured by Lovász and Simonovits. Razborov's proof was one of the first major applications of flag algebras. Nikiforov (2011) did the same for \(K_4\), and Reiher (2016) for every \(K_r\).
