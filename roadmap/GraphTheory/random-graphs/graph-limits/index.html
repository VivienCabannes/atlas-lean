    Graph limits

    Dense graph limits in the sense of Lovász and Szegedy: homomorphism densities \(t(F, G)\) of a graph \(F\) in a (weighted) graph \(G\), the cut norm and cut distance, the counting lemma, the Frieze–Kannan weak regularity lemma, convergent graph sequences, graphons as their limits, and compactness of the graphon space.

    DefinitionHomomorphism density#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.GraphLimits.homDensity

    For a simple graph \(F\) on a finite vertex set \(\alpha\) and a weighted graph \(w : \operatorname{Sym}^2 V \to \mathbb R\) on a finite vertex set \(V\) (unit node weights, real edge weights, loops allowed), $\(t(F, w) = \frac{1}{|V|^{|\alpha|}} \sum_{\varphi : \alpha \to V} \prod_{ij \in E(F)} w(\varphi(i), \varphi(j)).\)$ For the \(0/1\) weight of a simple graph \(G\) this is the probability that a uniformly random map \(V(F) \to V(G)\) is a homomorphism, and it equals \(\hom(F, G) / |V(G)|^{|V(F)|}\).

    A sequence of graphs converges when \(t(F, \cdot)\) converges for every \(F\); the limit object is a graphon, whose densities are given by an integral instead of a sum.

    noncomputable def homDensity (F : SimpleGraph α) [DecidableRel F.Adj] (w : Sym2 V → ℝ) : ℝ :=
  (∑ φ : α → V, homWeight F w φ) / (Fintype.card V : ℝ) ^ Fintype.card α
    def homWeight (F : SimpleGraph α) [DecidableRel F.Adj] (w : Sym2 V → ℝ) (φ : α → V) : ℝ :=

    DefinitionCut norm#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.GraphLimits.cutNorm

    The cut norm of a symmetric kernel \(u : \operatorname{Sym}^2 V \to \mathbb R\) on a finite vertex set is $\(\|u\|_\square = \frac{1}{|V|^2} \max_{S, T \subseteq V} \Big| \sum_{x \in S} \sum_{y \in T} u(x, y) \Big|.\)$ For two weighted graphs \(w, w'\) on the same vertex set, \(\|w - w'\|_\square\) is their cut (rectangle) distance \(d_\square\): two independent random graphs \(G(n, 1/2)\) are close in cut distance with high probability (the distance tends to \(0\)), although they differ in about half of their pairs.

    noncomputable def cutNorm (u : Sym2 V → ℝ) : ℝ :=
  cutMax u / (Fintype.card V : ℝ) ^ 2

    TheoremCounting lemma#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.GraphLimits.abs_homDensity_sub_homDensity_le

    Let \(F\) be a simple graph and let \(w, w'\) be weighted graphs on the same finite vertex set with all edge weights in \([0, 1]\). Then $\(|t(F, w) - t(F, w')| \le |E(F)| \cdot \|w - w'\|_\square .\)$ So homomorphism densities are Lipschitz in the cut distance: graphs that are close in the cut norm have close subgraph densities. The proof changes one edge weight at a time; each step is the integral of \((w - w')(x_i, x_j)\) against factors in \([0,1]\) that depend on \(x_i\) alone or on \(x_j\) alone, which the cut norm bounds after integrating out the two endpoints.
