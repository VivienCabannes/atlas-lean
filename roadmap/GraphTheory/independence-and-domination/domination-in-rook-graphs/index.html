    SimpleGraph.rookGraph

    For natural numbers \(n\) and \(m\), the rook graph on the \(n \times m\) board is the simple graph whose vertices are the cells \((i, j)\) with \(0 \le i < n\) and \(0 \le j < m\), in which two cells are adjacent if and only if they are distinct and lie in the same row (same \(i\)) or the same column (same \(j\)).

    def rookGraph (n m : ℕ) : SimpleGraph (Fin n × Fin m) where
  Adj x y := x ≠ y ∧ (x.1 = y.1 ∨ x.2 = y.2)

    TheoremDominating sets of the rook graph#✓fully proved

    SimpleGraph.rookGraph_isDominating_iff

    For natural numbers \(n\) and \(m\), a finite set \(S\) of cells of the \(n \times m\) board dominates the rook graph on that board if and only if for every cell \((i, j)\), some cell of \(S\) lies in row \(i\) or some cell of \(S\) lies in column \(j\).

    theorem rookGraph_isDominating_iff {n m : ℕ}
    {s : Finset (Fin n × Fin m)} :
    (rookGraph n m).IsDominating ↑s ↔
      ∀ i : Fin n, ∀ j : Fin m,
        (∃ j' : Fin m, (i, j') ∈ s) ∨ ∃ i' : Fin n, (i', j) ∈ s

    MathlibExt.GraphTheory.RookDominationPolynomialWanted.rookDominationPolynomial

    For natural numbers \(n\) and \(m\), the rook domination polynomial of the \(n \times m\) board is the integer polynomial \(D_{n,m}(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{nm} d_{n,m}(k)\, x^k\), where \(d_{n,m}(k)\) is the number of \(k\)-element sets of cells of the \(n \times m\) board that are dominating sets of the rook graph on that board.
