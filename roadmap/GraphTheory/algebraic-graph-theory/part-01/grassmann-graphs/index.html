    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.PseudorandomGrassmannWanted.PseudorandomGrassmann.SubGr

    For natural numbers \(k\) and \(\ell\) and linear subspaces \(A\) and \(B\) of \(\mathbb{F}_2^k\), \(\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)[A, B]\) is the finite set of vertices \(L\) of the Grassmann graph \(\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)\), that is of \(\ell\)-dimensional subspaces of \(\mathbb{F}_2^k\), with \(A \subseteq L \subseteq B\). If \(A \not\subseteq B\) the set is empty.

    This is the set \(\mathrm{Gr}_{k,\ell}[A, B]\) on page 13 of Khot, Minzer and Safra, Pseudorandom sets in Grassmann graph have near-perfect expansion.

    noncomputable def SubGr (k ℓ : ℕ) (A B : Submodule 𝔽₂ (Fin k → 𝔽₂)) : Finset (GrVertex k ℓ) :=
  {L | A ≤ L ∧ L ≤ B}

    DefinitionEdge expansion of a vertex set#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.PseudorandomGrassmannWanted.PseudorandomGrassmann.Φ

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph on a finite vertex set \(V\) and let \(S \subseteq V\) be nonempty. Let \(d\) be the degree in \(G\) of a fixed vertex of \(S\), chosen once for each \(S\). The expansion of \(S\) is the rational number \(\Phi_G(S) = |E(S, V \setminus S)| / (d \cdot |S|)\), where \(E(S, V \setminus S)\) is the set of edges of \(G\) with one endpoint in \(S\) and the other in \(V \setminus S\); it is \(0\) when \(d = 0\).

    For a \(d\)-regular graph, \(d\) is the common degree and this is Definition 1.8 of Khot, Minzer and Safra, which also asks that \(|S| \le |V|/2\); neither regularity nor that bound is assumed here.

    noncomputable def Φ {V : Type*} [Fintype V] (G : SimpleGraph V) [DecidableRel G.Adj]
    (S : Finset V) (h : S.Nonempty) : ℚ :=
  #(G.interedges S Sᶜ) / (G.degree h.choose * #S)

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.PseudorandomGrassmannWanted.PseudorandomGrassmann.theorem_1_12

    For every real \(\alpha\) with \(0 < \alpha < 1\) there are a real \(\varepsilon > 0\) and a natural number \(r\) such that for all sufficiently large natural numbers \(\ell\) and, for each such \(\ell\), all sufficiently large natural numbers \(k\), the following holds. For subspaces \(A\) and \(B\) of \(\mathbb{F}_2^k\), let \(\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)[A, B]\) be the set of vertices \(L\) of the Grassmann graph \(\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)\) with \(A \subseteq L \subseteq B\). Let \(S\) be a nonempty set of at most half of the vertices of \(\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)\) whose edge expansion in \(\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)\) satisfies \(\Phi(S) \le \alpha\). Then there are subspaces \(A \subseteq B\) of \(\mathbb{F}_2^k\) such that the dimension of \(A\) plus the codimension \(k - \dim B\) of \(B\) is at most \(r\) and \(|S \cap \mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)[A, B]| / |\mathrm{Gr}(k, \ell)[A, B]| \ge \varepsilon\).
