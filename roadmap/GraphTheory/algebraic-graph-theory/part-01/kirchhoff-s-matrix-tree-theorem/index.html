    DefinitionInteger Laplacian of a graph#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.MatrixTreeWanted.laplacianInt

    For a simple graph \(G\) on a finite vertex set \(V\), the integer Laplacian of \(G\) is the \(V \times V\) integer matrix \(L\) with \(L_{uu} = \deg u\) for every vertex \(u\), \(L_{uv} = -1\) when \(u \ne v\) are adjacent, and \(L_{uv} = 0\) when \(u \ne v\) are not adjacent.

    def laplacianInt {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] (G : _root_.SimpleGraph V)
    [DecidableRel G.Adj] : Matrix V V ℤ :=
  Matrix.of fun u v => if u = v then (G.degree u : ℤ) else if G.Adj u v then -1 else 0

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.MatrixTreeWanted.reducedLaplacian

    For a simple graph \(G\) on a finite vertex set \(V\) and a vertex \(r \in V\), the reduced Laplacian of \(G\) at \(r\) is the integer Laplacian of \(G\) with the row and the column of \(r\) deleted, a matrix indexed by the vertices of \(G\) other than \(r\).

    def reducedLaplacian {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] (G : _root_.SimpleGraph V)

    TheoremThe reduced Laplacian is a principal submatrix#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.MatrixTreeWanted.reducedLaplacian_eq_lapMatrix_submatrix

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph on a finite vertex set \(V\) and let \(L = D - A\) be its Laplacian matrix over \(\mathbb{Z}\), where \(D\) is the diagonal matrix of vertex degrees and \(A\) is the adjacency matrix of \(G\). For every vertex \(r \in V\), the reduced Laplacian of \(G\) at \(r\) equals the submatrix of \(L\) obtained by deleting the row and the column of \(r\).

    theorem reducedLaplacian_eq_lapMatrix_submatrix {V : Type*} [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V]

    reducedLaplacian G root =

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.MatrixTreeWanted.spanningTreeSubgraphEquiv

    For a simple graph \(G\) on a vertex set \(V\), there is an equivalence between the spanning trees of \(G\) (subgraphs of \(G\) that contain every vertex and are trees) and the simple graphs \(H\) on \(V\) that are trees with \(H \le G\), that is, every edge of \(H\) is an edge of \(G\). It sends a spanning tree to the graph on \(V\) with the same edges.

    TheoremKirchhoff's matrix-tree theorem#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.MatrixTreeWanted.kirchhoff_matrix_tree

    Let \(G\) be a simple graph on a finite nonempty vertex set \(V\) and let \(r \in V\). Then the determinant of the reduced Laplacian of \(G\) at \(r\), that is, the integer Laplacian of \(G\) with the row and the column of \(r\) deleted, equals the number of spanning trees of \(G\).

    In particular, this determinant is \(0\) when \(G\) is disconnected.

    theorem kirchhoff_matrix_tree :
    ∀ {V : Type*} (G : _root_.SimpleGraph V) [Fintype V] [DecidableEq V] [DecidableRel G.Adj]
      [Nonempty V] (root : V),
      (reducedLaplacian G root).det = (numSpanningTrees G : ℤ)
