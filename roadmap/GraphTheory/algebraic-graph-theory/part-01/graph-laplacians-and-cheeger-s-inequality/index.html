    Graph Laplacians and Cheeger's inequality

    The weighted Laplacian L = B diag(w) Bᵀ of a finite multigraph and its quadratic form xᵀLx = ∑_e w_e (x(a) - x(b))², following D. A. Spielman, Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory (Chapters 3, 20 and 21). Positive semidefiniteness and the Loewner order on Laplacians come from linear algebra; the Courant–Fischer theorem turns the second-smallest eigenvalue λ₂ into a minimum of Rayleigh quotients over vectors orthogonal to the constants, which gives connectivity (λ₂ > 0 iff connected) and the easy direction of Cheeger's inequality. The hard direction, for conductance, is in the conductance topic. Spectral clustering and Laplacian eigenmaps in machine learning use exactly these eigenvectors.

    For endpoint maps end₁ end₂ : E → V and edge weights w : E → ℝ, the matrix B · diag(w) · Bᵀ where B is the oriented incidence matrix; it equals ∑_e w_e (δ_{end₁ e} - δ_{end₂ e})(δ_{end₁ e} - δ_{end₂ e})ᵀ (D.     noncomputable def laplacian (w : E → ℝ) : Matrix V V ℝ :=
  orientedIncidence end₁ end₂ * diagonal w * (orientedIncidence end₁ end₂)ᵀ
    def orientedIncidence : Matrix V E ℝ :=
  Matrix.of fun v e => (if end₁ e = v then 1 else 0) - if end₂ e = v then 1 else 0

    TheoremEasy direction of Cheeger's inequality#✓fully proved

    EndpointMapMultigraph.mul_card_mul_card_compl_le_of_forall

    Let λ be a lower bound for the Rayleigh quotient xᵀ L x / xᵀ x over all nonzero x orthogonal to the constant vector (the second-smallest Laplacian eigenvalue is the best such bound, by Courant–Fischer). Then for every vertex set S, λ · |S| · |V \ S| ≤ |V| · w(∂S), where w(∂S) is the weight of the cut of S. With s = |S|/|V| this is w(∂S)/|S| ≥ λ (1 - s), D. A. Spielman, Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory, draft of 2 April 2025, Theorem 20.1.1 (stated there for unit weights); in particular θ_G ≥ λ/2.

    theorem mul_card_mul_card_compl_le_of_forall (w : E → ℝ) {lam : ℝ}

    (S : Finset V) :
    lam * S.card * (Finset.univ \ S).card ≤
      (Fintype.card V : ℝ) * ∑ e ∈ cut end₁ end₂ S, w e
