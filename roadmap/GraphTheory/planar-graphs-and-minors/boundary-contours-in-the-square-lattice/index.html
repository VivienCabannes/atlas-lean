    StatMech.Lattice.dartNext

    The successor of the dart e along the boundary of K. Write r for e.dir rotated clockwise by a quarter turn. If e.head + r ∈ K (a reflex corner of K), the successor shares the head of e; if only e.tail + r ∈ K, it is the translate of e by r; otherwise (a convex corner of K) it starts at e.tail in direction r.

    noncomputable def dartNext (K : Set (Site 2)) (e : Dart) : Dart := by
  classical
  exact
    if e.head + (-rot90Fun e.dir) ∈ K then
      mkDart (e.head + (-rot90Fun e.dir)) (rot90Fun e.dir) (unitWt_rot90Fun_dir e)
    else if e.tail + (-rot90Fun e.dir) ∈ K then
      mkDart (e.tail + (-rot90Fun e.dir)) e.dir (unitWt_dir e)
    else
      mkDart e.tail (-rot90Fun e.dir) (unitWt_neg_rot90Fun_dir e)
