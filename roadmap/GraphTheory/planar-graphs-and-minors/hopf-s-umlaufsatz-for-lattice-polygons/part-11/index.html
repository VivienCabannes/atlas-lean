    TheoremDiscrete Umlaufsatz for lattice walks without right turns#✓fully proved

    StatMech.Onsager.UmlaufsatzFinal.cc_eq_four_of_no_right_turn

    Discrete Umlaufsatz (theorem of turning tangents) for lattice walks without right turns: a simple closed walk on ℤ² whose every turn is straight or a left quarter turn has exactly four left turns, i.e. its direction makes one full counterclockwise revolution. Right turns and reversals are excluded by hypothesis, so the walks covered are exactly counterclockwise boundaries of lattice rectangles (no reflex corner); the general signed statement is StatMech.Onsager.GeneralUmlaufsatz.turnSum_eq_four_or_neg_four.
