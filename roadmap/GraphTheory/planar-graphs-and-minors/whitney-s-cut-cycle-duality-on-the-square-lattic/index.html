    DefinitionWhitney's cut–cycle duality#✓fully proved

    StatMech.Lattice.whb_CorrectWhitney

    Whitney's cut–cycle duality for subgraphs of ℤ² (H. Whitney, Planar graphs, 1933): for every H and lattice edge pq not in H, with fg the dual edge across pq, the endpoints p and q are connected in H iff f and g are disconnected in the face-region graph of H with fg deleted.

    def whb_CorrectWhitney : Prop :=
  ∀ (H : SimpleGraph (Site 2)) (p q f g : Site 2),
    (hypercubicLattice 2).Adj p q → s(p, q) ∉ H.edgeSet →
    (hypercubicLattice 2).Adj f g → sharedPrimalEdge f g = s(p, q) →
    (H.Reachable p q ↔
      ¬ ((whb_faceRegion H).deleteEdges {s(f, g)}).Reachable f g)
    abbrev Site (d : ℕ) : Type := Fin d → ℤ
