    A strict total order on a type \(\alpha\) is a binary relation \(<\) on \(\alpha\) that is irreflexive (\(a < a\) holds for no \(a\)), transitive (\(a < b\) and \(b < c\) imply \(a < c\)) and total on distinct elements (\(a \ne b\) implies \(a < b\) or \(b < a\)). In the Gale–Shapley development it models strict preference.

    abbrev StrictTotalOrder := MathlibExt.Order.StrictTotalOrder

  lt : α → α → Prop
  irrefl : ∀ a, ¬ lt a a
  trans : ∀ {a b c}, lt a b → lt b c → lt a c
  total : ∀ {a b}, a ≠ b → lt a b ∨ lt b a

    DefinitionBlocking pair#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.Combinatorics.Matching.GaleShapley.IsBlockingPair

    Let \(M\) and \(W\) be sets. Suppose that each \(m \in M\) has a strict total order \(\lt _m\) of preference on \(W\) and each \(w \in W\) has a strict total order \(\lt _w\) of preference on \(M\), where \(x \lt _m y\) means that \(m\) prefers \(x\) to \(y\), and likewise for \(\lt _w\). For a perfect matching \(\mu\) between \(M\) and \(W\), a pair \((m, w) \in M \times W\) is a blocking pair when \(w \lt _m \mu(m)\) and \(m \lt _w \mu^{-1}(w)\), that is, when \(m\) prefers \(w\) to \(\mu(m)\) and \(w\) prefers \(m\) to \(\mu^{-1}(w)\).

    (mp : MPrefs M W) (wp : WPrefs M W) (μ : PerfectMatching M W) (m : M) (w : W) : Prop :=
  (mp m).lt w (μ m) ∧ (wp w).lt m (μ.symm w)
