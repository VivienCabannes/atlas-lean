    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.FaltingsWanted.AlgebraicGeometry.Faltings.Place

    Let \(F\) be a field and \(K\) a field with \(F\) a \(K\)-algebra. A place of \(F/K\) is a valuation ring of \(F\) containing \(K\) and different from \(F\): a \(K\)-subalgebra \(\mathcal{O}\) of \(F\) with \(\mathcal{O} \neq F\) such that \(z \in \mathcal{O}\) or \(z^{-1} \in \mathcal{O}\) for every \(z \in F\). See [Stichtenoth, Definition 1.1.4 and 1.1.8].

    Stichtenoth calls the maximal ideal of \(\mathcal{O}\) the place, which determines \(\mathcal{O}\), and also requires \(\mathcal{O} \neq K\); that condition is omitted since it is automatic.

    @[ext] structure Place extends ValuationSubalgebra K F where
  ne_top : toSubalgebra ≠ ⊤

    Let \(F\) be a function field over a field \(K\), that is, a finite extension of the rational function field \(K(t)\). For every \(x \in F\), there are only finitely many places \(P\) of \(F/K\) whose valuation ring \(\mathcal{O}_P\) does not contain \(x\), that is, \(x\) has only finitely many poles.     MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.FaltingsWanted.AlgebraicGeometry.Faltings.Adele

    Let \(F\) be a field and \(K\) a field with \(F\) a \(K\)-algebra. The adele ring \(\mathcal{A}_F\) of \(F/K\) is the restricted product of copies of \(F\) indexed by the places \(P\) of \(F/K\), with respect to their valuation rings \(\mathcal{O}_P\) and the cofinite filter: its elements are the families \(\alpha = (\alpha_P)_P\) with \(\alpha_P \in F\) for every place \(P\) and \(\alpha_P \in \mathcal{O}_P\) for all but finitely many \(P\). See [Stichtenoth, Definition 1.5.2].

    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.FaltingsWanted.AlgebraicGeometry.Faltings.Adele.principal

    Let \(F\) be a function field over a field \(K\), that is, a finite extension of the rational function field \(K(t)\). The principal adele map is a ring homomorphism from \(F\) to the adele ring \(\mathcal{A}_F\) of \(F/K\). It is meant to be the diagonal embedding sending \(x \in F\) to the adele whose coordinate at every place \(P\) of \(F/K\) is \(x\), which is well defined since \(x\) has only finitely many poles; this coordinate formula is stated separately.

    Let \(F\) be a field and \(K\) a field with \(F\) a \(K\)-algebra.     TheoremGenus as the dimension of the adele class space#●statement formalized

    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.FaltingsWanted.AlgebraicGeometry.Faltings.genus_eq

    Let \(F\) be a function field over a field \(K\), that is, a finite extension of the rational function field \(K(t)\). The genus \(g\) of \(F/K\) equals the dimension over \(K\) of the quotient \(\mathcal{A}_F / (\mathcal{A}_F(0) + F)\), where \(\mathcal{A}_F\) is the adele ring, \(\mathcal{A}_F(0)\) the integral adeles, and \(F\) is embedded in \(\mathcal{A}_F\) by the algebra map of the \(F\)-algebra structure on the adeles (stated separately to be the principal embedding). See [Stichtenoth, Corollary 1.5.5].

    The dimension is the finite rank, taken to be \(0\) if the quotient is infinite-dimensional.

    TheoremFaltings' theorem (Mordell conjecture)#●statement formalized

    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.FaltingsWanted.AlgebraicGeometry.Faltings.faltings

    Let \(K\) be a number field and \(F\) a function field over \(K\), that is, a finite extension of the rational function field \(K(t)\). If the genus of \(F/K\) is at least \(2\), then \(F/K\) has only finitely many places of degree one, that is, places \(P\) whose residue field (the quotient of the valuation ring of \(P\) by its maximal ideal) has dimension \(1\) over \(K\).
