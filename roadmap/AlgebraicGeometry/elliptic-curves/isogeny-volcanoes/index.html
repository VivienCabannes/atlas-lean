    Multigraph

    A multigraph on a vertex set \(V\) is given by an edge multiplicity function \(m\) that assigns to every two vertices \(v, w \in V\) a natural number \(m(v, w)\) and is symmetric: \(m(v, w) = m(w, v)\) for all \(v, w \in V\). Loops are permitted: \(m(v, v)\) may be positive.

    IsogenyVolcano

    Let \(\ell\) be a prime. An \(\ell\)-isogeny volcano consists of a finite vertex set \(V\), a multigraph on \(V\) with edge multiplicities \(m(v, w)\), a depth \(d \in \mathbb{N}\), a surjective level map \(\mathrm{lev} : V \to \{0, 1, \dots, d\}\) and a surface degree \(s \in \mathbb{N}\) with \(s \le 2\), subject to the following conditions, where the surface is the set of vertices of level \(0\). Every surface vertex \(v\) satisfies \(\sum_{w \in V,\ \mathrm{lev}(w) = 0} m(v, w) = s\). Every vertex \(v\) of positive level has a unique parent: \(\sum_{w \in V,\ \mathrm{lev}(w) + 1 = \mathrm{lev}(v)} m(v, w) = 1\). Whenever \(m(v, w) > 0\), either \(v\) and \(w\) both lie on the surface or their levels differ by exactly one. Every vertex \(v\) with \(\mathrm{lev}(v) < d\) has degree \(\sum_{w \in V} m(v, w) = \ell + 1\), where a loop at \(v\) contributes its multiplicity once. Finally, the multigraph is connected: any two vertices are joined by a path along which consecutive vertices have positive edge multiplicity.
