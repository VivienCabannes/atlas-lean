    MetaMathlibExt.galPointHom

    Let \(W\) be a Weierstrass curve over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and let \(W(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})\) be the group of nonsingular points of \(W\) with coordinates in the algebraic closure \(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}\), together with the point at infinity. For \(\sigma\) in the absolute Galois group \(G_{\mathbb{Q}}\), this is the homomorphism of additive groups \(W(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}) \to W(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})\) that sends the point \((x, y)\) to \((\sigma(x), \sigma(y))\) and fixes the point at infinity.

    For each natural number \(p\), restricting this action to the \(p\)-torsion points gives the mod-\(p\) Galois representation.

    noncomputable def galPointHom (σ : Field.absoluteGaloisGroup ℚ) :
    Affine.Point (W.baseChange (AlgebraicClosure ℚ)) →+
      Affine.Point (W.baseChange (AlgebraicClosure ℚ)) :=
  Affine.Point.map (galAlgEquiv σ).toAlgHom

    AlgebraicClosure ℚ ≃ₐ[ℚ] AlgebraicClosure ℚ := σ
