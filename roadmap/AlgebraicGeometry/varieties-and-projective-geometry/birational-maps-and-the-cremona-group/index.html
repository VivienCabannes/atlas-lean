    AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.BirationalMap

    Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be irreducible schemes. A birational map from \(X\) to \(Y\) consists of dominant rational maps \(f \colon X \dashrightarrow Y\) and \(g \colon Y \dashrightarrow X\) that are mutually inverse: \(g \circ f\) is the identity rational map of \(X\) and \(f \circ g\) is the identity rational map of \(Y\).

    DefinitionComposition of birational maps#✓fully proved

    AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.BirationalMap.trans

    Let \(X\), \(Y\) and \(Z\) be irreducible schemes, let \(f\) be a birational map from \(X\) to \(Y\) and let \(g\) be a birational map from \(Y\) to \(Z\). The composite of \(f\) and \(g\) is the birational map from \(X\) to \(Z\) whose forward rational map is the forward map of \(f\) followed by the forward map of \(g\), and whose inverse rational map is the inverse of \(g\) followed by the inverse of \(f\).

    @[simps]
noncomputable def trans (f : X.BirationalMap Y) (g : Y.BirationalMap Z) :
    BirationalMap X Z where
  hom := f.hom.comp g.hom
  inv := g.inv.comp f.inv

    AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.BirationalMap.IsOver

    Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be irreducible schemes over a scheme \(S\). A birational map from \(X\) to \(Y\) is over \(S\) when its forward rational map \(X \dashrightarrow Y\) is compatible with the structure maps to \(S\).

    Its inverse rational map is then compatible with the structure maps to \(S\) as well.

    abbrev IsOver (S : Scheme.{u}) [X.Over S] [Y.Over S] (f : X.BirationalMap Y) : Prop :=
  f.hom.IsOver S

    AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.birationalAutOver

    Let \(X\) be an irreducible scheme over a scheme \(S\). The birational automorphisms of \(X\) over \(S\) form the subgroup of the group of birational self-maps of \(X\) consisting of those birational maps whose forward rational map \(X \dashrightarrow X\) is compatible with the structure maps to \(S\).

    def birationalAutOver (S : Scheme.{u}) [X.Over S] : Subgroup (X.BirationalMap X) where
  carrier := { f | f.IsOver S }
  one_mem' := inferInstanceAs ((RationalMap.id X).IsOver S)
  mul_mem' {f g} (_ : f.IsOver S) (_ : g.IsOver S) := inferInstanceAs ((f.hom.comp g.hom).IsOver S)
  inv_mem' {f} (_ : f.IsOver S) := inferInstanceAs (f.inv.IsOver S)

    Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be irreducible schemes and let \(f\) be a partial isomorphism from \(X\) to \(Y\), that is, an isomorphism from a dense open subscheme of \(X\) onto a dense open subscheme of \(Y\). The birational map from \(X\) to \(Y\) given by \(f\) has as forward map the rational map \(X \dashrightarrow Y\) represented by \(f\), and as inverse the rational map \(Y \dashrightarrow X\) represented by the inverse of \(f\).

    StructurePseudo-isomorphism#✓fully proved

    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.MotivicInvariantsWanted.MotivicInvariants.IsPseudoIso

    Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be schemes. A partial isomorphism from \(X\) to \(Y\), that is, an isomorphism \(U \cong V\) between dense open subschemes \(U \subseteq X\) and \(V \subseteq Y\), is a pseudo-isomorphism if it is an isomorphism in codimension one: every point of \(X\) outside \(U\) and every point of \(Y\) outside \(V\) has coheight at least \(2\) in the specialization order of points.

    structure IsPseudoIso
    {X Y : Scheme.{u}} (i : PartialIso X Y) : Prop where
  codim_source : ∀ x ∉ i.source, 2 ≤ Order.coheight x
  codim_target : ∀ y ∉ i.target, 2 ≤ Order.coheight y

    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.MotivicInvariantsWanted.MotivicInvariants.PseudoRegularization

    Let \(X\) be a variety over a field \(k\) and let \(\varphi\) be a birational automorphism of \(X\). A pseudo-regularization of \(\varphi\) consists of a variety \(X'\) over \(k\), a birational map \(\alpha \colon X \dashrightarrow X'\) over \(k\), and a partial isomorphism \(\gamma\) from \(X'\) to itself over \(k\) that is a pseudo-isomorphism, such that \(\varphi = \alpha^{-1} \circ \gamma \circ \alpha\), where \(\gamma\) is viewed as a birational map.

    MathlibExt.AlgebraicGeometry.MotivicInvariantsWanted.MotivicInvariants.IsPseudoRegularizable

    Let \(X\) be a variety over a field \(k\). A birational automorphism \(\varphi\) of \(X\) is pseudo-regularizable if there exist a variety \(X'\) over \(k\), a birational map \(\alpha \colon X \dashrightarrow X'\) over \(k\), and a partial isomorphism \(\gamma\) from \(X'\) to itself over \(k\) that is a pseudo-isomorphism (an isomorphism in codimension one), such that \(\varphi = \alpha^{-1} \circ \gamma \circ \alpha\), where \(\gamma\) is viewed as a birational map.
