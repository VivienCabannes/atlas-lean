    Projectivization.mk_smul_eq

    Let \(V\) be a module over a division ring \(K\). For every nonzero scalar \(a \in K\) and every nonzero vector \(v \in V\), the points of the projectivization \(\mathbb{P}(V)\) represented by \(a v\) and by \(v\) are equal.

    theorem mk_smul_eq {a : K} (ha : a ≠ 0) {v : V} (hv : v ≠ 0) :
    mk K (a • v) (smul_ne_zero ha hv) = mk K v hv

    TheoremOrthogonality of projective points via representatives#✓fully proved

    Projectivization.orthogonal_iff_rep_dotProduct_rep

    Let \(K\) be a field and \(m\) a finite set. Let \(p, q\) be points of the projective space \(\mathbb{P}(K^m)\) and let \(\hat p, \hat q \in K^m\) be the fixed nonzero representatives chosen for \(p\) and \(q\). Then \(p\) and \(q\) are orthogonal (the dot product of a representative of \(p\) with a representative of \(q\) vanishes, which does not depend on the representatives) if and only if \(\hat p \cdot \hat q = 0\).

    theorem orthogonal_iff_rep_dotProduct_rep {v w : ℙ K (m → K)} :
    v.orthogonal w ↔ v.rep ⬝ᵥ w.rep = 0

    MvPolynomial.orderAt

    Let \(R\) be a commutative semiring, let \(F\) be a polynomial over \(R\) in the variables \((X_i)_{i \in \sigma}\) and let \(v \in R^\sigma\). The order of vanishing \(\operatorname{ord}_v F \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}\) of \(F\) at \(v\) is the order at the origin of the Taylor expansion \(F(v + X)\), the polynomial obtained from \(F\) by substituting \(X_i + v_i\) for each \(X_i\): the least total degree of a monomial with nonzero coefficient in \(F(v + X)\), or \(\infty\) if \(F(v + X) = 0\). In particular it is \(\infty\) for \(F = 0\).

    noncomputable def orderAt (F : MvPolynomial σ R) (v : σ → R) : ℕ∞ :=
  ((aeval fun i => (X i + C (v i) : MvPolynomial σ R)) F : MvPowerSeries σ R).order

    MvPolynomial.multiplicityAt

    Let \(K\) be a field and \(F\) a polynomial over \(K\) in the variables \((X_i)_{i \in \sigma}\). The multiplicity of \(F\) at a point \(p\) of the projective space \(\mathbb{P}(K^\sigma)\) is the order of vanishing of \(F\) at the chosen representative \(\bar p \in K^\sigma\) of \(p\), as a natural number, with the value \(\infty\) read as \(0\).

    For homogeneous \(F\) it does not depend on the representative. The order of vanishing is \(\infty\) only for \(F = 0\), so the multiplicity of the zero polynomial is the junk value \(0\).

    noncomputable def multiplicityAt (F : MvPolynomial σ K) (p : ℙ K (σ → K)) : ℕ :=
  (F.orderAt p.rep).toNat
